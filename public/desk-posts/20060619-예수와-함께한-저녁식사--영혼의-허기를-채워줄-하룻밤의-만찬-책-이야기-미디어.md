**Rating**

Initially, I bought this book simply because it was a bestseller, not even realizing it was a religious book.

There was a reason it became a bestseller!

A clear explanation of Christianity that even Christians don't fully know~!
The Bible already knew all the answers.

It's just that people didn't try to know.

People's churches merely tried to go against Jesus.

As he always did,
not loudly like today's churches,

very quietly.
But he speaks a powerful truth.

"This is why I have no choice but to believe in you."
