0. FOOL
New beginnings
Divine grace
Enthusiasm
Purity
Immaturity
Foolish actions
Rashness

2003.12.17
**2015. 08. 06**

The very first card of the Tarot
The FOOL card, which is also the origin of the Joker in playing cards.
At the time, I learned Tarot cards as a hobby.
My situation at the time was exactly like the FOOL card.
My new, pure, and passionate love
signified foolishness and rashness.
I already knew the situation, but I couldn't escape it.

After that, I stopped using Tarot cards,
the reason being that they were too accurate.
I was confused whether it was prophecy or self-suggestion.
