3차 NHN의 공채 일정은 거의 3개월에 걸쳐 진행이 되었습니다.
그동안 좀 지쳐갔죠.

합격자 발표 하루전, 카페에는 이상한 글이 떴습니다.
네이버에서 합격자 케익과 떡을 받았다는 글이었죠.
사람들은 케익 배송처(티니케잌)를 알아내 전화를 걸어
미리 합격을 전화하기 시작했고

네이버는 급히 배송처의 입단속과 배송중지를 시킨듯했습니다.
아마도 합격 발표이전에 합격사실을 안다는것은
합격자에게는 기쁜일이지만, 불합격자에게는 실망이 큰 일이고
네이버의 통제를 벗어나는 일이었기 때문이었으리라고 봅니다.

합격자는 한번에 발표가 나야 맞겠죠.

케익이 배송되기전에는 보통 전화가 갑니다.
하지만, 발표 당일날 저녁도 제게는 아직 전화가 오지 않았고
사실 많이 실망하고 있었습니다.

케익 회사에 전화걸어볼 용기 조차 나지 않았고
차후에 알아보니 네이버에서 배송회사의 입단속을 시켰기 때문에 알려주지 않았다고 하더군요.

그런데, 본래 2006년 6월 12일 5시에 발표하기로 했던 사이트가 네이버의 사정으로 인해
그 다음날로 발표가 연기 되었고
그 다음날이 아닌 7시 20분에 발표가 되었습니다.

저도 혹시나 하는 마음에 사이트에 들어갔다가
합격소식을 알게 되었구요.

그날 실망하고 있었던 마음이 환희로 바뀌었습니다.
실망해서 술이나 마시려 했던 여자친구에게는
합격했다라는 메시지를 보냈고, 여자친구도 기뻐해주었습니다.

아무튼, 이렇게 합격자 소식을 들었습니다.

케익 배송은 합격자 발표 다음날 왔구요.
정작 저는 그날 집에 못들어가서 케익을 구경하지 못했습니다.
때문에 위의 사진은 합격자 발표 이틀후에 찍은 사진입니다.

실은 제가 과거에 SK컴즈 지원한적이 있어서 아는데
SK컴즈는 꽃배달을 해주고, 탈락자에게는 싸이월드 머그컵을 배송했습니다.
전 머그컵 받았죠 ㅜㅜ
그것도 탈락의 상처를 잊을만 하는데 배송되어서..
시간도 안맞아서 우체국까지 두번이나 찾아가서 받았습니다.

미니미가 그려진 머그컵은
예쁘기는 했지만, 그것도 깨지는 머그컵을 주는 싸이월드가 속상했습니다.
던져버릴까.. 했을정도로.. ㅜㅜ

아무튼, 탈락자에게 그런 위험한 물건을 주는 싸이월드는 생각 좀 다시해봐야 할듯.
NHN의 탈락자에게는 어떻게 처리하는지는 잘 모르겠습니다.

케익의 맛은 보통.
디자인은.. 이쁜데..
색감은.. 영. ㅡㅡ;
누가 형광색 쓸 생각을 한건지.
재료가 그런거겠죠. 아마두..

한게임 지원은 G의 한게임 마크가 도착했다하더군요.

아무튼 꽃보다도 현실적으로 먹을수 있는 맛있는 케이크가와서 좋았습니다.
저희 아버지는  그 다다음날이 생신이셨는데
친척들이 올때까지 두었다가 자랑하셨다더군요 ㅡㅡ;

합격자 후기 끝입니다.

The 3rd NHN public recruitment process lasted for almost 3 months.
I got quite exhausted during that time.
 
A day before the announcement of successful candidates, a strange post appeared on the cafe.
It was a post saying that they had received cakes and tteok (rice cakes) for successful candidates from Naver.
People found out the cake delivery service (Tiny Cake) and called them,
starting to call and confirm their acceptance in advance.
 
Naver seemed to have quickly silenced the delivery service and halted deliveries.
This was probably because knowing the acceptance status before the official announcement,
while joyful for successful candidates, would be a great disappointment for unsuccessful ones,
and it was an issue beyond Naver's control.
 
It's only right that successful candidates are announced all at once.
 
Usually, a call comes before a cake is delivered.
However, even on the evening of the announcement day, I hadn't received a call yet,
and I was actually very disappointed.
 
I didn't even have the courage to call the cake company,
and I later found out that Naver had told the delivery company to keep quiet, so they wouldn't disclose anything.
 
However, the website, which was originally scheduled to announce results at 5 PM on June 12, 2006, was delayed due to Naver's circumstances,
and the announcement was postponed to the next day.
It was then announced at 7:20 PM on that day.
 
I also went to the website just in case,
and found out the good news of my acceptance.
 
My disappointment that day turned into joy.
To my girlfriend, who had been disappointed and was about to drink,
I sent a message saying "I passed," and she was also happy for me.
 
Anyway, that's how I heard the news of my acceptance.
 
The cake delivery arrived the day after the announcement of successful candidates.
However, I couldn't go home that day, so I didn't get to see the cake.
Therefore, the photo above was taken two days after the announcement.
 
Actually, I know this because I applied to SK Comms in the past.
SK Comms delivered flowers, and for unsuccessful candidates, they sent Cyworld mugs.
I received a mug ㅜㅜ
And it was delivered just when I was starting to forget the pain of rejection...
The timing was also inconvenient, so I had to go to the post office twice to pick it up.
 
The mug with the Minimi drawing
was pretty, but I was upset that Cyworld gave a mug that could break.
To the point where I considered throwing it away... ㅜㅜ
 
Anyway, Cyworld should probably reconsider giving such a "dangerous" item to unsuccessful candidates.
I'm not sure how NHN handles unsuccessful candidates.
 
The cake's taste was average.
The design... was pretty...
But the colors... not at all. ㅡㅡ;
Who thought of using fluorescent colors?
It must be the ingredients, probably...
 
For those who applied to Hangame, I heard they received a Hangame G logo.
 
Anyway, I was happy to receive a delicious cake that could actually be eaten, rather than flowers.
My father's birthday was two days after that,
and I heard he kept it until the relatives arrived and showed it off. ㅡㅡ;
 
End of the successful candidate review.
