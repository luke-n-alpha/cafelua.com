After meeting you, I lost everything I had, even myself... Where is the real me? 2003.07.01

**Experiencing love for the first time, I felt so unfamiliar with myself. I couldn't control myself; I was either excessively cold or hot, and I was confused because I didn't even know what to cut away or where to look. All I felt was confusion and a pain in my chest.** **2015.11.22**
