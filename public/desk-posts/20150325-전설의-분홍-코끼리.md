**The Legendary Pink Elephant**

{{IMG:1}}

2002. 05. 03
During WWII, the US requested India to dispatch troops. So India sent the legendary pink elephant. Just imagine it. An elephant wearing a turban, gracefully dodging anti-aircraft fire, showering Hitler's head with watermelon seeds.
However, unfortunately, that elephant didn't last long. Every time it achieved a feat, it was given a medal, and on the day it received its 1000th medal, it was hit by German anti-aircraft guns due to the heavy weight of the medals.
Anyway, that elephant had historical value. It's just that it wasn't recorded because it wasn't human.

by Forest Baby

**2015. 03. 25**

Today, my wife went out in the evening, so I had to put the children to bed.

My second son looked for his mom while falling asleep, so I made up a story on the spot and told it to him. It was a story about Cocomong from Refrigerator Land crash-landing in Broom's Town on Robocong, and how the rescue team, including Poli, together defeated Germ King's giant robot.
Since it was a story I made up for my child on the spot, I guess the advantage is that I don't have to worry about copyright issues, right?

However, far from falling asleep, my son kept making "shwoosh, bang-bang" sounds, so I had to hold him to sleep.
