이런 온라인 극단화는 보수·진보 모두에게 나타난다. 보수 성향 커뮤니티는 더욱 강경한 반대파 배척을, 진보 성향 커뮤니티는 더욱 급진적인 분배·인권·환경 담론을 주장하며, 서로 간 대화나 타협의 여지가 줄어드는 것이다.​**3. 기존 보수·진보가 놓친 것: 단선적 해법으론 안 된다**- **보수의 맹점**- ‘성장’과 ‘생존(안보)’만 강조하면, 장기적 환경·복지 문제를 간과하기 쉽다. 결국 사회적 불평등이 심화되거나 기후위기가 악화되면, 더 큰 위기에 직면할 수 있다.
- **진보의 맹점**- ‘분배 정의’와 ‘노동 vs. 자본’ 구도에만 머무르거나, ‘환경’을 추상적 가치로 내세우다가, 전쟁·감염병·실업·치안 위기 같은 현실 문제를 도외시하면 대중적 설득력을 잃는다.
​한편, SNS를 통해 각자의 관점만 강화되는 상황에선, 더 쉽게 양극화되고, 더 자주 극단적 대치 국면이 벌어진다.​**4. 역대 정부의 비전·정책 실패가 가져온 위험**- 한국 현대사에서 각 정부가 **특정 기치**만 강조하다가 시대 변화에 적응하지 못해 큰 혼란을 겪는 장면을 여럿 봤다. 이제는 전쟁·팬데믹·기후위기·AI·SNS 극단화 같은 **복합 위기**가 동시에 닥칠 가능성이 높다.
- **이명박 정부(녹색성장)**: 성장과 환경을 함께 잡겠다고 했지만, 4대강 사업 등으로 부작용이 컸고, 결국 녹색성장 구호는 토건 정책으로 변질.
- **박근혜 정부(창조경제)**: 공허한 구호만 남고, 세월호 참사·국정농단으로 ‘재난 대처·정부 신뢰’에 치명타.
- **문재인 정부**: ‘탈원전’ 등 친환경 정책 시도, 코로나 초기 대응 선방. 그러나 부동산·고용 문제, 백신 갈등, SNS 상 극단적 찬반 대립 등으로 사회 갈등이 커졌다.
- **윤석열 정부**: ‘공정과 상식’을 표방했지만, 실제 정책에서 공정성 논란이 계속되고, 법조·언론 등 여러 갈등이 SNS를 통해 증폭되는 상황.
앞으로 정부가 ‘환경 vs. 생존’ 중 한 쪽에만 치우치거나, SNS 갈등을 방치한다면, 그 **실패 위험**과 **사회 충격**은 훨씬 심각해질 수 있다.**​****5. 한국 진보가 유지해야 할 가치, 그리고 대응 과제**보수화 흐름이 강화되고, SNS로 인해 갈등이 과열되는 환경 속에서 **한국 진보**가 어떻게 기존 가치를 지키면서도 시대변화에 대응할 것인가?

**1) 사회적 연대와 포용**

- 전쟁, 감염병, 기후위기, SNS 극단화—all 약자·취약계층이 더 큰 피해를 본다.
- 진보가 전통적으로 강조해온 ‘소수자 보호’, ‘포용 정책’은 오히려 지금처럼 혼란스러운 시기에 더 중요해진다.

**2) ‘장기 생존(환경·복지)’과 ‘단기 생존(안보·경제)’의 균형**

- 환경 정책이 단순히 ‘미래 세대를 위한 의무’가 아니라, **지금 경제와 안보에도 긍정적 효과**가 있음을 설득력 있게 제시해야 한다(예: 에너지 자립, 녹색 일자리 등).
- 동시에 북한·미중 갈등 등 현안에 대한 현실적 대안을 함께 제시해야 ‘이상론’ 비판에서 벗어날 수 있다.

**3) AI·디지털 시대의 복합 문제 해결**

- AI로 인한 실업·불평등 확대, 디지털 개인정보·사이버 공격, 플랫폼 독점 등 다양한 문제를 복합적으로 다뤄야 한다.
- 진보가 강조하는 공공성·사회 안전망을 디지털 분야로 확장해, **‘디지털 복지’, ‘플랫폼 공정’** 등을 구체화해야 한다.

**4) 온라인 갈등 관리 방안 모색**

- SNS 극단화로 인해 사회가 급속히 분열되고, 정책 추진력이 떨어진다.
- 가짜뉴스 규제, 알고리즘 투명성, 숙의 민주주의 플랫폼 등 새로운 제도적 장치가 필요하다.
- 진보적 가치는 “공론화와 대화”에 강점이 있으므로, 이를 디지털 시대에 맞게 진화시켜야 한다.
**​****6. 결론: 진보 가치를 지키되, ‘복합 위기’를 통합적으로 다룰 현실적 로드맵 필요**- **단순 이분법**: “환경 vs. 성장”, “진보 vs. 보수”, “우리 편 vs. 네 편” 식의 구도는 SNS를 통해 더 극단화되어 갈등을 키울 뿐, 문제 해결엔 도움이 안 된다.
- **새 시대 과제**: 전쟁·팬데믹·기후위기·AI·SNS 극단화를 한꺼번에 바라보고, 각 이슈가 서로 얽혀 있다는 점을 인식해야 한다.

**1. Conservatives and Progressives, What's the Problem?**
Looking at recent global trends, progressives appear to be steadily losing ground, while conservatives are gaining strength. Even after South Korea's impeachment crisis, there was a brief period that seemed like ‘progressive dominance,’ but the inauguration of the Yoon Suk-yeol administration exposed its limitations. However, whether the Yoon Suk-yeol administration represents ‘true conservatism’ is ambiguous, and the fundamental problem is that the **traditional conservative-progressive framework** as a whole is failing to adequately respond to the rapidly changing challenges of our era.
- **Traditional Conservatives**: Prioritize economic growth, national security, and survival.
- **Traditional Progressives**: Based on distributive justice and the logic of class struggle between labor and capital.
While both sides achieved some success during the industrialization and democratization phases of the past, their limitations are becoming increasingly clear now, with the convergence of the **AI era**, **low birth rates and aging populations**, **climate crisis**, **geopolitical conflicts**, and **online polarization**.
​
**2. War, COVID-19, and Conservatism, Plus SNS Polarization**
**(1) War and Infectious Diseases: An Era Where 'Survival' Takes Priority**
- Through conflicts like the **Ukraine-Israel War**, people have felt the immediate threat that ‘my life could be in danger.’
- The **COVID-19 pandemic** paralyzed economies and societies worldwide, demanding ‘survival-oriented’ policies from nations.
In such situations, a **conservative stance** emphasizing short-term survival and security tends to gain support more easily than long-term issues (climate crisis, environmental protection, distribution, etc.). South Korea, too, cannot be free from this trend, as tensions with neighboring powers like North Korea, China, and Japan are ever-present.
​
**(2) The Development of the Internet and SNS, and 'Extreme Confrontational Structures'**
- As **social networks** developed, people experienced the **‘filter bubble’** effect, where like-minded individuals gather.
- As a result, **individualized thoughts** frequently lean towards extreme poles, losing **common values**.
- News, communities, and YouTube algorithms reinforce ‘confirmation bias,’ increasing **conflict costs** and threatening social safety and integration.
This online polarization appears in both conservatives and progressives. Conservative communities advocate for more rigid exclusion of opponents, while progressive communities push for more radical discourse on distribution, human rights, and the environment, thereby reducing the room for dialogue or compromise between them.
​
**3. What Traditional Conservatives and Progressives Missed: Linear Solutions Are Not Enough**
- **Blind Spot of Conservatism**
- If only ‘growth’ and ‘survival (security)’ are emphasized, long-term environmental and welfare issues are easily overlooked. Ultimately, if social inequality deepens or the climate crisis worsens, a greater crisis may be faced.
- **Blind Spot of Progressivism**
- If one remains solely focused on ‘distributive justice’ and the ‘labor vs. capital’ dynamic, or champions ‘environment’ as an abstract value while neglecting real-world issues like war, infectious diseases, unemployment, and public safety crises, public persuasion will be lost.
​
Meanwhile, in a situation where only individual perspectives are reinforced through SNS, polarization occurs more easily, and extreme confrontations happen more frequently.
​
**4. The Risks Brought by Past Governments' Vision and Policy Failures**
- In modern Korean history, we have seen numerous instances where governments, by emphasizing only a **specific banner**, failed to adapt to changing times and experienced great confusion. Now, there is a high probability that **complex crises** such as war, pandemics, climate crisis, AI, and SNS polarization will strike simultaneously.
- **Lee Myung-bak Administration (Green Growth)**: Claimed to pursue both growth and environment, but suffered significant side effects from projects like the Four Major Rivers Project, ultimately transforming the ‘Green Growth’ slogan into a civil engineering policy.
- **Park Geun-hye Administration (Creative Economy)**: Left only empty slogans, and dealt a fatal blow to ‘disaster response and government trust’ with the Sewol ferry disaster and state affairs manipulation.
- **Moon Jae-in Administration**: Attempted eco-friendly policies like ‘nuclear phase-out,’ and performed well in early COVID-19 response. However, social conflicts grew due to real estate and employment issues, vaccine disputes, and extreme pro-con confrontations on SNS.
- **Yoon Suk-yeol Administration**: Advocated ‘fairness and common sense,’ but controversies over fairness continue in actual policies, and various conflicts in legal circles, media, etc., are amplified through SNS.
If future governments lean only towards one side between ‘environment vs. survival’ or neglect SNS conflicts, the **risk of failure** and **social impact** could become far more severe.
**​**
**5. Values Korean Progressives Must Uphold, and Corresponding Tasks**
In an environment where conservative trends are strengthening and conflicts are overheating due to SNS, how can **Korean progressives** uphold their existing values while responding to the changes of the times?

**1) Social Solidarity and Inclusion**

- War, infectious diseases, climate crisis, SNS polarization—all cause greater harm to the vulnerable and disadvantaged.
- The ‘protection of minorities’ and ‘inclusive policies’ traditionally emphasized by progressives become even more crucial in chaotic times like these.

**2) Balance Between 'Long-Term Survival (Environment·Welfare)' and 'Short-Term Survival (Security·Economy)'**

- Environmental policies must be persuasively presented not merely as ‘an obligation for future generations,’ but as having **positive effects on the current economy and security** (e.g., energy independence, green jobs).
- At the same time, realistic alternatives for current issues such as North Korea and the US-China conflict must be presented to avoid criticism of being ‘idealistic.’

**3) Solving Complex Problems in the AI and Digital Era**

- Various issues such as unemployment and widening inequality due to AI, digital personal information and cyberattacks, and platform monopolies must be addressed comprehensively.
- The publicness and social safety net emphasized by progressives must be extended to the digital sphere, concretizing **‘digital welfare’ and ‘platform fairness.’**

**4) Exploring Ways to Manage Online Conflicts**

- Due to SNS polarization, society is rapidly fragmenting, and policy momentum is decreasing.
- New institutional mechanisms are needed, such as fake news regulation, algorithm transparency, and deliberative democracy platforms.
- Progressive values have strengths in “public discourse and dialogue,” so these must be evolved to suit the digital age.
**​**
**6. Conclusion: Upholding Progressive Values, but Needing a Realistic Roadmap to Address 'Complex Crises' Integratively**
- **Simple Dichotomy**: A framework of “environment vs. growth,” “progressive vs. conservative,” or “us vs. them” only exacerbates conflicts through SNS polarization and does not help solve problems.
- **New Era Challenges**: We must view war, pandemics, climate crisis, AI, and SNS polarization all at once, recognizing that each issue is intertwined with the others.
