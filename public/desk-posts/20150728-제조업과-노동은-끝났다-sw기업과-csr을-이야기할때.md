Some people consider Xiaomi a manufacturing star, but Xiaomi is an example that shows manufacturing is over.

**How Did Xiaomi Become a Price Disruptor? 10 Secrets** Recently, while reviewing Xiaomi's products at The Gear, I had a question: "Isn't this price actually normal?" Xiaomi first launched a smartphone in August 2011. And within three years of entering the smartphone market, it became number one in the Chinese smartphone market and third in the global smartphone market. It's hard to say that this happened solely due to the power of price. However, many still view Xiaomi with questions about its pricing. Last year, Lee Myung-jin, a former Samsung Electronics executive, also discussed Xiaomi at the 'Samsung Investors Forum' held in the US. thegear.co.kr

In hardware, efficiency and zero-margin are pursued, while profit is sought in services. This is why we must find answers in software and services. Labor needs to be redefined; instead of stable labor, the labor market should discuss equal opportunities, dynamism, and efficient, rational welfare and CSR. In Korea, CSR is often misunderstood as wealthy people donating to the poor after working hard, but the true nature of CSR is a debt to society. Without society, there are no businesses. Of course, some companies or sectors might think of moving overseas, wondering what help they've received... (But even in this case, let's reconsider. Customers helped.) Without that, this world would be a terrible place for ordinary people. Amazon and Xiaomi could become even more exploitative companies than Samsung Electronics.
