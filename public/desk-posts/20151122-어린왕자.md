어린왕자야.넌 외로울때면 노을을 보곤 한다고 했지.노을을 보며 무슨 생각을 하니.정말로 그러면 좋은거야 ?노을을 보면 좋은거냐고..넌 어쩌면 알고 있는거지.모든것에는 끝이 있음을..언젠가는 네 꽃도 시들테고 네 상자속 종이 양이진짜 양이 아님을 알지도 몰라.차라리 울 수 있을때 울어버려.어차피 네 별에는 너 혼자 잖아.그리고 강해지렴.​2003.07.30

가장 인상깊게 읽은 책을 뽑으라면 나는 어린왕자를 뽑았다. 그의 순수함이 좋았다. 하지만, 커버린 지금은 그 아이가 성장할까봐 염려가 들었다. 무척 고통스러울텐데..**2015.11.22**

Little Prince. You said you watch the sunset when you're lonely. What do you think about when you watch the sunset? Is it really good then? Is it good to watch the sunset? Perhaps you already know. That everything has an end. Someday your flower will wither, and you might realize that the paper sheep in your box isn't a real sheep. You might as well cry when you can. After all, you're alone on your planet. And be strong. 2003.07.30

If I had to pick the most impressive book I've read, I would choose The Little Prince. I liked his innocence. However, now that I've grown up, I worry about that child growing up. It would be very painful...**2015.11.22**
