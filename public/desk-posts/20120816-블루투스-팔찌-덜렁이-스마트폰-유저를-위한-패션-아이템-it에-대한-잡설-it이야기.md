**1. The Electronic Watch, the Origin of Wearable Computers**

[Wearable Computer](http://terms.naver.com/100.nhn?docid=770186)
A special computer that can be worn on the body like clothing. Primarily used for military purposes in developed countries like the US...

Source: [Doosan Encyclopedia EnCyber](http://www.encyber.com/)

What will be the next hot IT item after the smartphone era? Google created IT glasses called Project Glass. This device actually falls into the category of wearable computing technology. This concept of a "wearable computer" is slightly different from smartphones, which originated from handheld PCs, or "computing in your hand." The biggest feature of these devices is that they are always attached to the body. Even though people nowadays are glued to their smartphones while awake, they at least put them down in situations where their hands aren't free, like when eating or driving. However, there is one device that is the progenitor of such wearable computers: the electronic watch. I remember being incredibly envious of other kids' electronic watches that had calculators or Galaga back in the day.
**2. Adding Smartness to Electronic Watches?**
There have already been attempts to smartify such watches, like the watch provided with LG's Prada phone. I remember people being more enthusiastic about the watch than the phone back then.
The device I'm introducing this time is such a smartwatch. As phones gained Bluetooth and universal OS, these devices began to appear even from non-handset manufacturers. I also think that handset manufacturers might soon start producing such smartwatches in the future, if only for smartphone differentiation. Moreover, since they have a strong character as fashion items, one person might own multiple watches, and prices can vary greatly depending on the design, suggesting a new market could open up. Someone once included watches among the devices that would disappear due to smartphones, but I believe that the smartification of watches will actually lead to an era where even those who didn't wear watches before will start wearing them. Watches should also be seen as part of the n-screen concept.
**3. Beyond Watches: A Smart Bracelet with Added Ideas and Bling!**
Now, let's get back to the main topic and introduce a product. First, its specs might seem a bit modest compared to the Bluetooth smartwatch recently introduced by blogger Ham Young-min. That watch could even receive KakaoTalk messages. However, the differentiating factor of the watch I'm introducing is that it's equipped with truly essential functions and clever ideas. Furthermore, by enhancing its jewelry aspect, it's a beautiful watch that's closer to a bracelet than a traditional watch. Its name is actually Bluetooth Bracelet.

**2) Don't leave me behind! A great idea!**
My father recently lost his smartphone. Thanks to that, my old S2 went to him... This watch uses the distance characteristic of Bluetooth as an idea: if the Bluetooth connection with the smartphone is lost, it vibrates. About 10m? It even vibrates if you go to the restroom, so you must always carry your phone. When I showed this watch to my father, he said he almost left his phone behind recently and really liked it. In the end, I decided to give him the review product. Even though my father has thick wrists and it doesn't quite fit him. ㅡㅡ
**Advantage 2. It's pretty. It just *has* to be pretty.**
The design is closer to jewelry, as its name suggests. Regarding the watch's design, everyone said, "That's a cool design, unlike you!" ㅜㅜ (What's wrong with me... ㅜㅜ) I heard "It's pretty. Where did you buy it?" a lot. Then, when I say, "It connects to your smartphone!" the additional reaction is, "Wow~! That's awesome~!" It really made me think that 80% of a product is its design. A long time ago, when others didn't have smartphones, and I carried a brick-like smartphone, I heard "Why use such a phone?" I've also seen a cool all-in-one Bluetooth smartwatch, but the reaction to that watch was "It's not pretty..." It seems it's not easy to achieve both.
**Disadvantage 1. Battery**
The battery doesn't last very long. Of course, if you disconnect Bluetooth, it can last up to 72 hours, which is quite a long connection. However, when Bluetooth is connected, it's a bit erratic. Sometimes it lasts about 8 hours, and other times it lasts over 12 hours. Because of this, I have to remember to charge it every single day. This was a bit annoying.
**Disadvantage 2. Durability**
The durability of the charger clip is weak, likely because the clip is plastic and constantly subjected to force in its clamp-like design. Also, after about a month of use, the rubber gasket on the back of the watch fell off, indicating somewhat weak durability.{{IMG:3}} However, the watch body itself is sturdy. The gasket is a hidden part, so it can be ignored, and if you use the clip carefully, it's fine. In fact, I'm also the type who uses things very roughly, **to the point of breaking a sturdy tempered glass watch by hitting it against a pedestrian bridge railing while walking. (This is by no means a normal standard. When a product comes to me, it undergoes a hardcore test.)**
**Disadvantage 3. Size not suitable for children and muscular men**
Fortunately, I'm not a muscular man, so I didn't have much trouble using it... but it's a bit tight for men with thick wrists. My father, who has thick wrists, still said he liked this watch and would use it, so user preference is clear, but it is tight for muscular men. Conversely, for young children with excessively thin wrists, it's too loose. I also felt that a wider range of sizes would be good.{{IMG:4}} **[ But it fits me! lol ]** If I say, "This is a bracelet!" then... I have nothing to say... (sigh)...
**Overall Review. A Fashion Item for the Clumsy**
This is it exactly. For those who often forget their phone, miss calls and get scolded... (That's exactly me. ㅜㅜ) It's an essential item for them. Plus, it gives a slight feeling of being ahead of the times? Anyway, I always make sure to carry it. Because my wife is scary... ^^ If your wife is scary, go for it.
