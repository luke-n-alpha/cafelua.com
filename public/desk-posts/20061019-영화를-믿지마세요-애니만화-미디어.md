**평점**

지식인 만화가 그에게 어울리는 말이 아닐까 ?

더군다나 최근북핵문제로 뜨거운 가운데

'그날이후'란 편에서는 핵에 대한 그의 의견을 잘 보여주고 있다.
정확한 비평..
그리고 현실을 바라보는 그의 정확하고 따뜻한 시선이 좋았다.

#### [Don't Believe the Movies](http://book.naver.com/bookdb/book_detail.php?bid=1946456)

By Park Moo-jik
Geobukibooks
**Rating**

I like books like this.
Because they stimulate imagination.

Also, due to the homages, a strong scent of Park Moo-jik's personal preference for Korean comics emanates.

A book difficult to write unless you're a cartoonist who majored in physics.
Isn't "intellectual cartoonist" a fitting description for him?

Moreover, amidst the recent heated North Korean nuclear issue,

in the chapter titled 'After That Day,' he clearly shows his opinion on nuclear issues.
Accurate criticism..
And I liked his precise and warm perspective on reality.
