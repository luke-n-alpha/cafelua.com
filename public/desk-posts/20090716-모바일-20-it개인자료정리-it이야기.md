**I. Concept of Mobile 2.0**
- Services implementing Web 2.0 features on mobile platforms
**- Similarities and Differences with Web 2.0**

| **Similarities** | ** Differences** |
| --- | --- |
| - Maximization of collective intelligence (sharing and participation) - Enriched user experience (RIA) - Technology standardization (Ajax, HTML5) | - Seamless network service - Real-time nature of information creation and sharing - Importance of mobility, location-based information - Personalization with one device per person |

**III. Obstacles to Mobile 2.0 Diffusion and Solutions**

| **Obstacles** | ** Solutions** | ** Examples** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Closed wireless network | Network opening | Jan 2002: Ministry of Information and Communication specifies obligation to open wireless internet. Oct 2005: Korea Communications Commission orders corrective action for mobile carriers' negligence in network opening. |
| High network usage fees | Development of new pricing models | Flat-rate plans |
| Lack of business models | Development of killer applications | App Store |
| H/W-dependent S/W | Development of standardized platforms | OHA, Open Mobile OS, WIPI |
| Dualized content for PC and mobile | Full browsing | HTML 4.0, CSS, Flash |
| Existing operators concerned about reduced revenue | Activation of strategic alliances between operators | AT&T's market share increase through APPLE's iPhone sales |
