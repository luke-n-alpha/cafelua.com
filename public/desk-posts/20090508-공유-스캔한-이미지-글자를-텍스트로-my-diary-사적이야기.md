{{IMG:3}} **타이핑 노가다(?)에 지치셨다면...**
****{{IMG:4}}
종이에 인쇄된 책이나 논문을 텍스트로 저장하거나 편집해야 할 경우가 종종 발생하죠? 내용의 일부분을 발췌해야 하는 경우도 있고, 친구에게 메일로 보내거나, 텍스트 내용을 사전이나 웹에서 찾아봐야 할 경우도 있습니다. 이럴 때 OCR이라는 기술이 없었다면, 사람들은 일일이 손으로 그 많은 텍스트를 타이핑 해 컴퓨터로 옮겨야 했을 거예요.

광학 문자 인식 즉, OCR(Optical Character Recognition)은 기계에 의해서 인쇄된 문자를 광학적 수단으로 판독하는 장치를 뜻합니다. 이 OCR 기술은 이미 많은 분야에서 활용되고 있는데요. 대표적인 곳이 바로 우체국입니다. 우체국에는 하루에도 수없이 많은 양의 우편물이 쏟아져 들어오는데, 이 때 이 수 많은 우편물의 우편번호를 인식해 자동으로 분류하는 데 쓰이는 것이 바로 OCR 기술이죠. 네이버 책 서비스에서도 OCR 기술을 활용해 책의 본문을 스캐닝 받아 여러분이 검색할 수 있는 형태의 텍스트로 변환해 본문 검색 기능을 제공하고 있습니다. 사람이 그 많은 텍스트를 모두 손으로 입력한다고 생각해 보세요? 정말 굉장한 일이겠죠?

{{IMG:3}} **If you're tired of manual typing...**

There are often times when you need to save or edit books or papers printed on paper as text, right? Sometimes you need to extract a part of the content, send it to a friend via email, or look up text content in a dictionary or on the web. If OCR technology didn't exist, people would have had to manually type all that text into a computer.

Optical Character Recognition, or OCR, refers to a device that optically reads printed characters by machine. This OCR technology is already being used in many fields. A prime example is the post office. Countless pieces of mail pour into post offices every day, and OCR technology is used to automatically sort these many pieces of mail by recognizing their zip codes. Naver Books service also utilizes OCR technology to scan book content and convert it into searchable text, providing a full-text search function. Imagine if a person had to manually input all that text? That would be an incredible feat, wouldn't it?

 
 **From character segmentation to skew correction**
****
However, securing such OCR technology is not an easy task. First, you have to extract only the text parts from an image and separate them character by character. Then, you need to distinguish whether the separated character is English, Korean, or a number and match it with a specific character, right? Since computers are not yet as accurate as humans in recognizing characters, this process must consider the condition and resolution of the scanned paper, the color of the characters and background, and the skew of the text. Even when documents are edited in multiple columns or contain inserted images, the text area must be processed to be recognized correctly. Sometimes, even the same character might not be recognized properly by the computer depending on the font used in the document. Anticipating all these various scenarios, processing and correcting files to provide them in a format that the computer can best recognize is one of the most important parts of OCR.

To utilize such OCR technology, OCR software is needed. Various software is available for English OCR, and its recognition rate is known to be quite high. However, for Korean, there is still not much software available, and the recognition rate is not very good. While some Korean recognition programs exist, they must be purchased as commercial programs.
