**1. 안드로이드로 인한 구글의 모바일 환경선점 **
얼마전부터 국내포탈들이 모바일 환경에서 구글의 서비스가 기본탑제 되는데 불만을 토로하기 시작했습니다. 안드로이드폰을 사면 처음에 해야하는것이 바로 구글의 계정을 등록하고 구글의 주소록과 sync를 해야했습니다. 홈화면에는 언제나 구글의 검색창이 가장 먼저 있었고요. 여기에 구글의 검색창과 동등한 수준의 네이버검색창이 들어있는 수준외에는 크게 구글의 아성에 도전할 수 있는바가 없었습니다. 분명 이러한 환경은 국내에서 라이트유저들에게는 듣보잡에 불과하던 슈퍼구글이 각인될수 있는 좋은기회였습니다.

**2. 정보유통을 제대로 하기위해서는 Os를 만들어야 하는건가?
**앱스토어부터 정보 유통 플랫폼의 미래는 Os를 선점하면 유리하다는 교훈은 곧 구글의 안드로이드가 나오는 계기가 되었고 Ms에서는 윈도우모바일7과 윈도우즈8을 만들게 만들었습니다. 여기에 삼성의 바다, 블랙베리등 Os의 경쟁이 본격화가 되었죠. 그래서 정보유통 플랫폼을 만드는 기업들은 Os를 만들어야 하는것인가 하는 고민에 빠질 수 밖에 없는 상황이된것 같습니다. 그러나 Os를 만드는 작업은 엄청난 노력이 소요되고 성공한다는 보장 또한 많지 않습니다.

**3. Os없이도 플랫폼을 만드는 서비스
**물론 Os가 없이 플랫폼을 만들어 성공하는 가장 좋은 사례는 바로 페이스북입니다. 사실상 웹이라는 가상 Os위에 구축된 페이스북 서비스 자체가 플랫폼이자 Os가 되는 개념이죠. 마찬가지 개념의 도전을 할 것 처럼 보이면서 의욕적으로 런칭을 한것이 국내에는 네이버me입니다.

**4. 모바일에서도 웹플랫폼인가?
**이전의 포스팅에서도 이야기를 한 적이 있었지만 모바일에 웹이 어울릴지 앱이 어울릴지 하는 포스팅을 한적이 있습니다. 이때 웹이 어울리는 시스템과 앱이 어울리는 시스템을 이야기 했었는데 현재 웹이 지원하지 못하는 많은 모바일의 리소스를 활용하기 위해서는 역시 앱일 수 밖에 없죠. 무엇보다도 ux적 측면에서만 접근한다면 기민한반응성을 요구하기에는 앱만한것이 없습니다. 그래서 다음은 네이버가 웹에서 플랫폼을 만들기를 시도했듯이 이번 다음앱은 앱으로 플랫폼의 형태를 만들었습니다. 이른바 앱플랫폼이라고 할 수 있는거죠. (그러나 플래시나 SK같은 미들웨어적 성격은 없는듯합니다.)

**1. Google's Preemption of the Mobile Environment Due to Android**
Recently, domestic portals began to complain that Google's services are pre-installed as default in the mobile environment. When you bought an Android phone, the first thing you had to do was register a Google account and sync it with Google Contacts. The Google search bar was always the first thing on the home screen. Beyond having a Naver search bar at the same level as Google's, there was little that could challenge Google's stronghold. Clearly, this environment was a great opportunity for "Super Google," which was previously unknown to light users in Korea, to become ingrained.

**2. Do We Need to Create an OS to Properly Distribute Information?**
The lesson that it's advantageous to preempt the OS for the future of information distribution platforms, starting with the App Store, soon led to Google's Android and prompted MS to create Windows Mobile 7 and Windows 8. Here, OS competition intensified with Samsung's Bada, BlackBerry, and others. Thus, companies creating information distribution platforms seem to have been forced into the dilemma of whether they need to create an OS. However, creating an OS requires immense effort, and there's no guarantee of success.

**3. Services That Create Platforms Without an OS**
Of course, the best example of successfully creating a platform without an OS is Facebook. In essence, the Facebook service itself, built on the virtual OS of the web, becomes both a platform and an OS. Naver Me in Korea was launched with similar ambitious intentions, seemingly challenging with the same concept.

**4. Is It a Web Platform Even on Mobile?**
I've discussed this in a previous post, whether web or app is more suitable for mobile. At that time, I talked about systems where web is suitable and systems where apps are suitable. Currently, to utilize many mobile resources that the web doesn't support, it inevitably has to be an app. More than anything, from a UX perspective, nothing beats an app for demanding agile responsiveness. So, just as Naver attempted to create a platform on the web, this Daum app has created a platform in the form of an app. It can be called an app platform. (However, it doesn't seem to have middleware characteristics like Flash or SK.)

**5. Daum App Possesses All Attributes of a Mobile OS**
**1) App Management Function**
The Daum app allows you to register shortcuts not only for its own services but also for other apps installed on Android. And the user experience is identical to the basic Android usage, so there's no sense of unfamiliarity.
{{IMG:1}}
**[ You can register applications on the Daum app screen. ]**
****
**2) Potential to Surpass the Basic OS UI**
For some reason, the Daum app's screen transitions are faster and smoother than LG Home on Optimus Q. Not only screen movement, but if it's an app, you can make it however you want anyway. It would be entirely possible to create new UIs or interfaces not supported by the existing OS.
**3) Theme Function**
The Girls' Generation theme provided by default implies that other themes, or user themes, could be provided in the future.

{{IMG:2}}
**[ Girls' Generation theme, more powerful than any killer app. Naver urgently needs to introduce an IU theme. ]**

**The above attributes mean that Daum App can completely replace the mobile home screen, which is Google's strongest advantage.**

**6. However, it should have been a launcher, not an app. Android has launchers.**
The Daum app is just an app. If it had been developed as a home screen, it could have been applied to the home button. But pressing the home button takes you to LG Home. There are alternatives to this, such as ADW Launcher, one of the most famous launchers on Android.

**[ Launcher Pro Demo Video: Source [http://cpu29k.blog.me/90099066876](http://cpu29k.blog.me/90099066876) ]**

Thus, it seems that the solution for portals to catch Google has already been sufficiently presented in Android. **Perhaps Naver's launcher will come out? The competition to capture the browser's first screen will now shift to the competition for launcher installation. And this competition for launcher installation will soon increase the diversity and localizing nature of Android UX, allowing for the development of good services perfectly suited for Korea.**
