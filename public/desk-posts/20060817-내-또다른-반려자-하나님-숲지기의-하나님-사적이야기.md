Things were crazy.
After moving my job to NHN (Naver) and as my wedding day approached, I was just trying to keep up with life.
I'm still busy, though...

Recently, I've been receiving good messages by email.
It's like Go Do-won's Morning Letter, but it contains good words from God, and even includes short streaming messages.

Who sent it?

Who recommended it for me to receive this?

{{IMG:1}}
It suddenly occurred to me, and I wanted to recommend this email to my girlfriend as well.
However, to recommend it, I had to sign up for O2vill.

So, as I was about to sign up,
I tried typing in my girlfriend's ID and password.

It logged in.

And when I went to the recommendation list...

{{IMG:2}}

Under 'Relationship,' it said 'Partner.'

.....

Yesterday, I went to Shinwoohoe, an in-house club at NHN.
It was a place where people sang hymns and shared messages for a short time during lunch every Wednesday.
The number of people was about 30-40?
Since I only went once, I don't know the exact number, but...

I was surprised that there was such a place at the company.

It seems God is everywhere in life.
And He goes with us.

Just as my girlfriend and I are partners,
He also seems to be such a partner.

I'm posting an article after a long time.

These days, I'm mostly active on Naver Blog.
