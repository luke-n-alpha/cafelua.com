**
****Han (Deep-seated Sorrow)**

 
Park Jae-sam

 

Could it be a persimmon tree,
Ripening with the sorrowful light of the sunset,
The tree bearing the fruit of love in my heart?

It seems there's no place for this to properly extend but the afterlife,
And even then, extending behind the back of the person I thought of,
Perhaps finally blossoming above that person's head.

But that person,
Wanted to plant in their inner courtyard,
It might become a fruit of deep feeling!

To put it another way, the color of that fruit
Is all my sorrow, all my hope from a past life.

 
They might or might not realize it!

No, that person too, this world,
Whether they lived it with sorrow or not,
I don't know that either, I don't know that either!

Decades ago, someone who embezzled hundreds of billions and committed murder said,
"Why didn't you hold on a bit longer...?"
'Hey. No matter what, you're not in a position to say that, are you?'
 
 Busy envying him, would the one who was acquitted for lack of a subject feel good now?
Listening to the nauseating words of politicians from both ruling and opposition parties, who claim to sincerely mourn his death, is as bitter as strong coffee.
 
 Nevertheless, he lived a magnificent life, and his retreating figure, who almost remained the only president by the people's side until the very end, was incredibly lonely.
 What were the world he last saw and his dreams?
 
'You left this flawed world with only your admirable self, and that's good. From now on, people will miss you forever. I had hoped you would fight the world to the very end, enduring both good and bad...
 Still, I liked your human-like appearance, getting angry and sad about obvious things just like us. How can you leave so vainly like this? You've ultimately become a saint and a hero...
 
 How hard it must have been? The great hopes and dreams of those who supported you, and the sharp blades that pierced you.
Go well to a good place. You lived truly well, among the politicians I've seen.
 
The survivors will someday clean up the remaining mess you couldn't finish, so go well.
 
Don't do politics in heaven.
And thank you.'
