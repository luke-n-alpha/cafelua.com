Searching for Gear VR, I found a truly absurd article and would like to share it. It's an article comparing Gear VR and Oculus. Since the content is very wrong, I'd like to first meticulously debunk those parts, and then explain the relationship between Gear VR and Oculus.
http://news.naver.com/main/read.nhn?mode=LSD&mid=sec&oid=092&aid=0002070154&sid1=001[[Five Reasons Why Gear VR is Better Than Oculus RiftQHD-class ultra-high resolution screen… providing better realism (ZDNet Korea = Reporter Bong Seong-chang) For those whose job is to experience new IT devices every day, IT devices alone...news.naver.com](http://news.naver.com/main/read.nhn?mode=LSD&mid=sec&oid=092&aid=0002070154&sid1=001)

The gist of it is the nonsense claim that Gear VR is hardware-superior to Oculus. I will explain it properly.
■ **QHD-class ultra-high resolution screen… providing better realism**
"The display resolution used in DK2 is 1920×1080 pixels, which is Full HD. On the other hand, the resolution of the Galaxy Note 4, which acts as the display for Gear VR, is QHD-class at 2560×1440 pixels, making it more detailed. In other words, Gear VR is much more advantageous in terms of realism."
However, **DK2's display is 960x1080 per eye**. Where did they get 1920x1080 from? The Galaxy Note 4's **resolution is halved when it goes into Gear VR, making it 1280x1440**. Of course, that's slightly higher than DK2. Are they saying they used a Note 3 display in terms of specs? 960x1080?? **(Further correction: I've confirmed that the entire panel is used. Not the whole device, but just the panel. Since it's half of 1920x1080, 960x1080 is correct. I miscalculated. The reason for using the same panel is presumed to be due to the cost of AMOLED panels. I thought using individual displays would be cheaper and more efficient, but it seems they used the Note 4 as is due to mass production and HDMI. In the end, Cardboard, VR, and DK2 all use the same single panel with just a divider.)**
■ **20ms lower latency… no motion sickness felt**
???? I was breathless the moment I saw this. **DK2's latency is 2ms, 3ms**. It supports 60, 72, 75 Hz. At 20ms, you can't even see properly. "Experts believe that if screen latency drops below 20ms, most people will not experience motion sickness." Which experts did you ask? **LCD response time is not that slow. It's around 2ms, similar to AMOLED.**
**Addition (2015.04.06): It has been confirmed as a fact that Gear VR is faster with a latency of 20ms.** What confused me in the article was that it stated "minimum latency due to AMOLED," making me think the latency referred to display latency. However, the latency mentioned here refers to the total latency from when motion occurs until it is reflected on the screen, which has nothing to do with the screen itself. Therefore, **while DK2 is superior in rendering speed, it has been confirmed that the total time, including the time for the screen to be transmitted via HDMI to the head-mounted display (Screen Latency = Motion Input Time + Rendering Time + HDMI Transmission Time + VSync Wait Time), results in a time of 20ms or more.** However, the claim that AMOLED provides an advantage, as stated in the article, is false. **Among the tens of milliseconds of latency, the effect of AMOLED, even at best, shows a difference of less than 1ms in screen display time.**
■ **Wireless freedom… enjoy VR anytime, anywhere**
This part is correct. This technology, called Gear VR or mobile VR, allows you to freely view 360 degrees, and its advantage is being wireless. However, Oculus has a reason for giving up 360 degrees. It's motion recognition. **They said you don't get closer when you move your body, but Oculus does.** That's why a camera is attached to the PC, Oculus requires more performance, and the viewing angle is limited compared to VR. **It performs motion sensing like Kinect with the PC's camera. I can't understand how they wrote such an article without even trying it once.**

■ **Reasonable price for enjoying virtual reality**
They calculated DK2 at 388,000 won and VR at 220,000 won... but they missed the Note 4 price for VR. ㅡㅡ; If you're going by that, comparing it to a 20,000 won Cardboard would be more appropriate.

■ **The relationship between Oculus and Gear VR… Gear is actually not Samsung technology.**
And finally, it seems they think Gear VR and Oculus are made by different companies... Gear VR is more of an Oculus product than a Samsung one. The SDK is also distributed by Oculus.
