There was a child. That child was very forgetful. Even when receiving a gift, they would quickly forget that they had received it. So, they constantly complained about not having enough. There were always tear stains around their eyes. That's why they were hateful. Moreover, they were very careless and didn't know how precious things were. They would play with things and quickly break them. And then they would pester for new ones. The child would cry and throw a tantrum again, and he (the giver) would tear off another part of his heart. The child didn't know the value of what they received. They didn't know how precious the blood stained on it was. That child is me. ㅜㅜ 2004.10.13
