**1. 방송말고 할게 많다 ! 부가서비스**
iptv를 쓰면서 좋은 점 중 하나는 다양한 부가서비스가 존재한다는 것입니다. Mylgtv의 경우, 교육, 레저, 노래방, 게임등을 제공 합니다. 과금 방식은 하루 이용에 얼마 이런식으로 운영되죠.
{{IMG:1}}
[ My LG TV 노래방의 과금 방식 ]

이러한 프로그램을 구동시 라이브 방송에서 부가서비스 전환시 약간의 시간이 소요됩니다.

이러한 시간은 iptv가 방송 이라기보다는 컴퓨터와 마찬가지로 로딩시간을 써야한다는 의미입니다. 이러한 어플리케이션은 사실 컴퓨터와 동일한 Java나 플래시 어플리케이션으로 구현됩니다. 이러한 범용 플랫폼을 구동 시킬수 있는 iptv는 사실 어떠한 응용 프로그램이든 사양만 받추어준다면 제공이 가능하다는 의미입니다. 문제는 현재 iptv사양은 pc나 핸드폰에 비해서도 열악하다는 단점이 있긴 하지만 말입니다.

**1. There's more to do than just broadcasting! Value-added services**
One of the good things about using IPTV is the variety of value-added services available. In the case of MyLGTv, it offers education, leisure, karaoke, games, and more. The billing method is typically a daily usage fee.
{{IMG:1}}
[ My LG TV Karaoke billing method ]

When running such programs, there is a slight delay when switching from live broadcast to a value-added service.
{{IMG:2}}
[ Of course, it's much shorter than Windows boot time, but it would have been better if they showed an advertisement. ]

This delay means that IPTV, rather than being just a broadcast, requires loading time, similar to a computer. These applications are actually implemented as Java or Flash applications, just like on a computer. The fact that IPTV can run these general-purpose platforms means that it can provide any application, as long as the specifications support it. The problem, however, is that current IPTV specifications are inferior to those of PCs or mobile phones.

**1) Games and Karaoke on IPTV**
{{IMG:3}}
[ My LG TV's Karaoke service ]

Among the programs I've used on IPTV, games and karaoke had high satisfaction. While flashy 3D games don't run yet, the environment was sufficient for board games like Go-Stop.
And if you're using IPTV as a media bay, as I mentioned before, you can enjoy karaoke with excellent sound quality. However, the lack of built-in microphone support is a drawback for using it solely as a karaoke machine. It was quite difficult to sing without anything when sober. Song updates are almost real-time as they are provided by Kumyoung, a professional karaoke company, and you can find songs on their website or search using the IPTV remote control, which allows text input just like a mobile phone.

http://www.kumyoung.net/kym/html/kym_02_search.asp
Above all, the potential for games seems even greater, and it will see the most development and change in the future. The board game market still accounts for the largest share of the overall game market. IPTV, with its good accessibility, seems poised to grow into the best business model for light users to enjoy games without installation.
{{IMG:4}}
[ My LG TV Baduk (Go) game, free games are also available. ]

**2. Disadvantages of Value-added Services**
**1) Low Accessibility**
Currently, the most difficult aspect of using MyLGTv's value-added features is that the menus are too deep, and it's hard to inform users about new features. Besides channels like number 8, which transmits VOD content in an nVOD format during live broadcasts, it would be good to have a channel that advertises value-added services. Also, these value-added features should be able to be registered as shortcuts.
{{IMG:5}}
[ I only found out about the music learning value-added service after deliberately searching for it. ]

**2) Slow Remote Control and Non-intuitive UX**
In addition to complex menus, user accessibility would be further improved if icon-based or widget support were provided.
Recently, Samsung released a TV with a gyro sensor built into the remote control. If IPTV supported this, it could even rival the possibilities of the Wii.
**3) Why not multitasking with live broadcasts?**
Multitasking with live broadcasts also seems necessary. The concept of true interactive broadcasting will emerge when users can enjoy a Go game while watching a Go broadcast.

**3. Development Potential of IPTV Value-added Services**

**1) Convergence with General Video Game Consoles**
I dream of a win-win strategy if IPTV and general video game consoles converge. A large portion of video game console revenue comes from content sales, and combining it with always-online IPTV would make online game implementation and software distribution much easier. What if you could enjoy Mario or Final Fantasy on IPTV? One of IPTV's strengths is also the ability to control children's access. Uncontrolled game playing by children could also be managed.

**2) Various Interactive Services - Shopping, Banking, Medical, Communication**
{{IMG:6}}
[ MyLGTV Shopping Value-added Service ]
As shown above, there are ways to make purchases through some advertising VODs, but they seem limited. You have to select the service directly. The products are not as diverse as Naver Shopping, and I doubted if any user would choose this value-added service just to see this.
I still haven't seen scenarios like shopping for a celebrity's clothes while watching an IPTV drama. (Despite this being a demonstration video always shown when discussing the IPTV market.) The concept of shopping on IPTV is quite old, and despite many discussions and attempts at implementation, the fact that such content and STBs are not yet provided means that IPTV still has a long way to go. Recently, I heard about medical services using IPTV, but given that shopping isn't even properly implemented yet, I'm skeptical. Of course, if desired, VOIP services could be added to IPTV for high-resolution video calls.

Anyway, I hope more IPTV-specific services emerge, and that content providers who earn money from IPTV establish it as a software platform. Next, I plan to post about IPTV's potential as a software platform.
