**1. Real-time Stat Change System**
1) Existing System:
 Basically, in games, stats are fixed according to each Level.
 Alternatively, upon leveling up, a certain value is given, which can then be used to change individual stats.
2) Improved System:
 In addition to basic stats, upon leveling up, a certain bonus value is held as a fluid value.
 During combat, desired stats can be weighted or reduced in real-time.
3) Goal:
 To make user combat richer and enable more diverse control.
ex) Potential 100: (A virtual stat name that can change individual abilities)
    Defense 10:
    Attack 30:
    Speed 20:
     
 If the user has a powerful close-range weapon and is an offensive unit,
 and the opponent has a medium-power long-range weapon,
 the user temporarily changes stats to:
    Potential 0
    Defense 10+30
    Attack 30 
    Speed 20+70   
 and closes the distance.
 After the distance is closed,
  
    Potential 0
    Defense 10+30
    Attack 30+70
    Speed 20
 and annihilates the enemy.
The opponent can also employ a contrasting strategy.

4) Stat Change Delay: Changing stats takes time.
  This stat change time can also be affected by Potential.
5) Hotkeys: Defensive stats and offensive stats can be changed by pre-assigning hotkeys before combat.
6) Effects: Like strategies in RTS games, players can employ strategies based on the opponent's immediate changes.
 Control becomes very important, and situational judgment becomes crucial.
