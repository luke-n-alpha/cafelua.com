'작은 아이'란 소재는 제가 글이나 그림으로 어릴적부터  많이 썼던 소재입니다.
저는 키가 작은편에 속했기 때문인지큰 사람들의 세상이 부럽고 제 자신이 초라하게 느껴질 때가 많았습니다.
그것이 마음이던 키던 말입니다.때문에 무척 조급한 마음이나 자격지심도 느꼈죠.지금도 여전히 크다고 생각하지 않습니다.벌써 서른중반이 넘었고, 더 많이 알고
세상의 많은 일에 조금은 더 담담해졌지만,아직도 깨금발로 버스에서 큰 사람들 사이에서 통학하던 중,고시절과 크게 자란것 같지는 않습니다.아직도 바람에도 흔들리지 않는 마음이 큰 사람들이 부럽습니다.
그런데 아이의 동화책을 읽어주다보니큰 동물을 부러워하던 쥐에게 개미가 이렇게 말합니다." 난 네가 커서 너무 부러워! "아이들도 배우는 단순한건데  마음만큼은 잘 안되나 봅니다. 그렇기에 모두들 끊임없이 욕심을 부리는 거겠죠.

****A Small Person****
**{{IMG:1}}
2002. 05. 15
There once lived a very small person. He had been small since childhood. He looked at tall people and wished he could grow as tall as them.**
**by Forest Child

**2015. 03. 27**
The theme of 'a small child' is something I've often used in my writing and drawings since I was young.
Perhaps because I was on the shorter side, I often envied the world of tall people and felt insignificant.
Whether it was about my heart or my height. Because of this, I often felt very impatient or had an inferiority complex. Even now, I don't consider myself tall. I'm already past my mid-thirties, I know more, and I've become a little more composed about many things in the world, but I still don't feel like I've grown much from my middle and high school days, when I used to commute on tiptoes among tall people on the bus. I still envy people with big hearts that aren't swayed by the wind.
However, while reading a children's storybook to my child, an ant said to a mouse who envied larger animals, "I'm so jealous of how big you are!" It's a simple lesson even children learn, but it seems our hearts don't quite grasp it. That's probably why everyone constantly harbors desires.
But, perhaps because they resemble my wife and me, our children are also on the smaller side, and I hope they don't inherit our height. Is this also a desire?
