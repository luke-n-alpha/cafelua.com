I often use Bluetooth earphones while driving. I mainly use them for listening to music, and they can also be used hands-free, making them a helpful item while driving. With a Bluetooth earphone I reviewed previously, a benefit was being able to check incoming SMS messages on a small LCD screen. However, a drawback(?) is that trying to check that small screen while driving could lead to trouble. The item I'm introducing this time solves this problem by reading out text messages and KakaoTalk messages. You need to install an app on your smartphone, and this app reads SMS and KakaoTalk messages through speech synthesis. It's what's known as a trendy "appcessory" these days.

Compared to Samsung's HS-3000, which was very popular in a similar price range, this product seems far superior in terms of design. Another advantage of this product over the HS3000 is that it has tangle-free flat earphones. I also used the HS3000, and tangled earphones were definitely a problem. In terms of sound quality, I'm not an audiophile, so I can't say if it's exceptionally good. Generally, the sound quality of Bluetooth earphones tends to be significantly degraded, but the Samsung HS-3000 was well-regarded for having decent sound quality. While it's true that some Bluetooth earphones claiming to be Hi-Fi and costing hundreds of thousands of won have recently appeared, the CS-300, which uses the same apt-x as the HS3000, is considered to have good sound quality. I used the HS-3000, but I don't currently own it, so I couldn't compare the two products.
Above all, the biggest feature of these earphones seems to be their ability to read "KakaoTalk" messages when the 'Crips Message Reader' app is installed.
[https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.edicon.talksms](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.edicon.talksms){{IMG:6}}

I installed this app and tried it with other Bluetooth earphones. The app read SMS messages well with other Bluetooth earphones, but KakaoTalk messages only played through these earphones. Being able to listen to KakaoTalk messages while driving seems like the biggest advantage. Of course, it might be a slight drawback that it also reads game push notifications like "**님이 ***로 초대했어요. (You've been invited to *** by **.)"
**{{IMG:7}}** **[ My mom sent "Saebom, I love you" via KakaoTalk, and it read it aloud automatically, which she loved. ]**
It would be even better if it could reply by voice like Google Glass, but the unique feature of being an earphone that reads KakaoTalk messages alone seems to create a significant differentiation from other earphones.
While watches and glasses are currently gaining attention with talk of IoT, Bluetooth earphones, which were already at the forefront of IoT, are quietly adding interesting smart features. How much smarter can earphones get?
