이제 쓸데가 생겼네요 ㅎㅎ

메일만 잘 쓰면, 한달에 30개의 콩을 누적할수 있으니, 매달 3000원씩 기부가 가능합니다. @.@

I've also added it.
You see the Kong Piggy Bank on the left, right? ^^;
First, you collect Kongs there, and then you can donate them all at once to a Happy Log you like.

Happy Beans, which used to be mostly collected through events, can now be gathered with a simple method...
Which is writing "Kong Mail."

But, when I actually tried to write one... I had no one to send it to.
Normally, I'd draw a Christmas card picture and send it...
But I've been too busy...

"Mail to myself," by the way, doesn't accumulate Kongs ㅜㅜ

Mileage, which I never knew where to use when just using it.
Now it has a purpose ㅎㅎ
I immediately exchanged the leftover mileage this month for 10 Kongs.

If you write emails well, you can accumulate 30 Kongs a month, so you can donate 3,000 won every month. @.@
In a year, that's 36,000 won in cash {{IMG:1}}

It seems NHN has made a lot of money...
I wonder if they'll give out more incentives... {{IMG:2}}

Anyway, it's a good feeling that they're offering such a service.
If Naver has 10 million users, and even if we only count 1 million of them as mail users,
36 billion won could be donated every year...
(Of course, assuming everyone diligently writes emails...)
