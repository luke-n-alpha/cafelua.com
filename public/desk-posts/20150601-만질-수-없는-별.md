**Untouchable Stars**

{{IMG:1}}

The stars in the night sky, seen while trapped within cold walls—not everything visible can be touched.
Because I am living in a world called reality.

2003. 02. 13
2015. 06. 01

It seems that when I drew this picture, it was before I confessed.

She, who was like a twinkling star, didn't seem to be within my reach,
and the wall was merely an illusion of hesitation within reality.

Ultimately, the only people who reach the stars are those who reach out their hands towards them.
