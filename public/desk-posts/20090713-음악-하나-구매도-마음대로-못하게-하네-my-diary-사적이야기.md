All of them are unavailable for purchase, and it's even impossible to listen to them on Naver Music. Looking at the comments, it seems they were available for purchase and listening before, so why is this happening?
 
Online Music Providers "Suspend Music Supply to Naver"
[http://economy.hankooki.com/lpage/industry/200906/e2009062917442470260.htm](http://economy.hankooki.com/lpage/industry/200906/e2009062917442470260.htm)
Could this article be the answer? I can only think of it as music providers trying to tame Naver.
Is there any other platform that protects copyrights as aggressively as Naver, to the point of inconveniencing users?
 
I finally found some music I liked and wanted to listen to, but there are all sorts of obstacles.
Nowadays, there are many more paid users, and I'm so afraid of copyright law that I can't even post a single link, so I don't know what kind of noise this is.
Just be honest. Say you want to raise the price of music.
 
I keep making negative posts, so I tried to make a positive one, but the world isn't cooperating.
