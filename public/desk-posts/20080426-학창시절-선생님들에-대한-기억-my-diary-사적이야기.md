3. 초등학교 6학년 때 담임 선생님 (여) 나이 40 이상
: 1년 내내 단 한번 아이를 때렸다. 기독교인이셨는데, 종례시간에 기도를 하고 보내실 정도 였으니까. 매일 돌아가면서 점심시간에 아이들과 한명씩 식사를 했고, 말썽꾼 아이를 한번 때리고 복도로 뛰어나가셔 우셨다. 처음 봤다. 그리고 아이들한테 자기를 때리라고 했던 정말 거짓말 같은 영화같은 일을 실제로 했던 분. 6학년이나 되는 아이들이 선생님을 때리며 우는 것이 정말 가능할지 몰랐다.
진심으로 대하면 진심이 통한다고 했던가? 가장 말썽꾼이었던 내 친구는 그 선생님만 생각하면 너무 고마웠다고 말했다.

4. 중학교 2학년때 담임 선생님 (남) 나이 40이상 - 담당 체육
학교 최고의 호랑이 선생님, 걸리면 죽는다. 그러나 자신의 반 아이들은 끔찍히 생각하셨던 분. 엄격하셨고 40세 이상임에도 불구하고 근육질 몸매를 자랑하셨던 체육선생님. 내가 다리가 갑자기 아파 응급실에서 걷지 못했을때, 운동안해서 그런다고 한번에 와서 다리를 풀어주셨던 분. 공인 육상경기 심판도 겸임하셨던 분.
그 날 이후로 구로구에서 가장 큰 운동장 외곽으로 땀복을 입고 방학때까지 10~15바퀴를 돌아야 했다. 중학교 3학년때까지 그 약속은 유효해서 전교에서 가장 유명한 땀복 소년이 되었는데, 내가 지나가면 여학생들이 모두 박수를 쳐서 무척이나 부끄러운 기억이지만, 건강의 소중함을 알게 해주신분. 그 해 난생 처음 오래달리기를 만점을 받아봤고, 다른 체육 점수도 후하게 주셨다. 체육시간에 다른 친구들은 축구할때, 난 여전히 운동장을 돌고 있었으므로, 어떤 친구들도 불만은 없었다.

5. 고등학교 3학년때 담임 선생님 (남) 나이 40이상 - 담당 국어
학생주임선생님으로서 담배피다 걸리면, 빠따로 딱 100대만 때리는 분. 100대 빼주거나 넘는법 없다. 있는 힘껏 치시는데, 맞다 못맞겠으면, 다음에 와서 또 맞으라신다. ㅡㅡ;
학교에서 가장 먼저 출근하셔서 가장 늦게 퇴근하시는 분이고, 지하독서실에서 공부를 할 때, 퇴근후에도 수시로 들어와 우리를 괴롭히셨던 분. 덕분에 반의 성적은 늘 탑. 대학에서 시간강사를 뛰시며, EBS에 출연할 정도로 실력은 최고. 강의는 그리 오래 안하시는데, 순식간에 진도나가서 나머지는 다른 과목 자습.
공부를 하던 안하던 성적이 항상 규칙적으로 오르락 내르락하는 내 스타일을 파악하시고, 전 학교의 컴퓨터를 맞기셨던 분.
" 너 공부하나 안하나 똑같지? 와서 컴퓨터좀 고쳐라. "
수능이후, 선생님의 일을 돕기 위해 새벽 1시까지 야근도 해봤고, 아이들의 성적을 쳐넣으면 과목 가중치에 따라 예상 학교를 출력하는 프로그램을 주문형 개발을 해봤음. 첫 내 고객 ㅡㅡ; ??
수능을 망친 저 한테는 재수를 적극 권하셨으나, 운 좋게 그나마 원하던 학교 학과에 들어가 가장 기뻐해주신 분임.
그분의 말 중 또 기억에 남는 말이 있는데..
" 선생님 중에도 악역은 꼭 필요한거다. 그리고 그걸 내가 하고 있다. "
정말 그 말이 너무 멋있게 보였던 분.

6. 고등학교 3학년때 기술 선생님
반장과 함께 몰래 지하독서실에서 도망가던 날, 학교 현판앞에 널부러져 술이 떡이 되서 드러누우셨던 선생님.
마누라 이름을 부르며 울고 계셨음. ㅡㅡ;
지나가는 어른들이 수근거리는 소리에 우리는 차마 그냥 가지 못하고, 선생님을 들쳐 엎고 지하독서실로 복귀.
지하독서실을 감시하던 선생님께 인계 후 이런 말을 들었음
" 선생님들도 다 사람이다. 힘든때도 있는거야. 너희들 이 일은 비밀이다. 그건 그 일이고, 몰래 도망갔으니 두대씩 맞고 들어가라. "

우리 어머님은 선생님께 항상 이렇게 적어 보내주셨죠.
" 잘못하면 때려주십쇼. "
맞아도 추억이 되고 교훈이 되는 매도 있는 법입니다.
요즘 뉴스를 보면 참 씁슬하더군요. 군에가기전에 고3때와 중3때 담임 선생님을 만나뵜습니다. 기뻐하시더군요. 그리고 요즘 애들 참 힘들다는 말씀도 빼놓지 않으셨습니다.

3. In 6th grade of elementary school, homeroom teacher (female), over 40 years old
 : She hit a child only once throughout the entire year. She was a Christian, to the extent that she would pray before dismissing us at the end of the day. Every day, she would take turns eating lunch with one student, and once, after hitting a mischievous child, she ran out into the hallway and cried. I had never seen anything like it. And she was someone who actually did something unbelievable, like in a movie, telling the children to hit her back. I didn't know if it was truly possible for 6th graders to hit a teacher and cry at the same time.
 They say sincerity begets sincerity, don't they? My friend, who was the most mischievous, said he was incredibly grateful whenever he thought of that teacher.

4. In 2nd grade of middle school, homeroom teacher (male), over 40 years old - PE teacher
 The school's fiercest teacher; if you got caught, you were dead. However, he cared immensely for the students in his class. He was strict and, despite being over 40, was a PE teacher who boasted a muscular physique. When my leg suddenly hurt and I couldn't walk in the emergency room, he came right over, saying it was because I didn't exercise, and massaged my leg. He also served as a certified track and field referee.
 From that day on, I had to wear a sweatsuit and run 10-15 laps around the perimeter of the largest sports field in Guro-gu until summer vacation. That promise remained valid until 3rd grade of middle school, making me the most famous 'sweatsuit boy' in the entire school. It's a very embarrassing memory because all the girls would clap when I passed by, but he was the one who taught me the importance of health. That year, for the first time in my life, I got a perfect score in long-distance running, and he also gave me generous scores in other PE activities. During PE class, while other friends played soccer, I was still running laps, so none of my friends had any complaints.

5. In 3rd grade of high school, homeroom teacher (male), over 40 years old - Korean language teacher
 As the head of student affairs, if you were caught smoking, he would hit you exactly 100 times with a bat. Never less than 100, never more. He'd hit with all his might, and if you couldn't take it, he'd tell you to come back and get hit again later. ㅡㅡ;
 He was the first to arrive at school and the last to leave, and when we studied in the underground reading room, he would frequently come in and bother us even after work. Thanks to him, our class's grades were always top-notch. He worked as a part-time lecturer at a university and was so skilled that he even appeared on EBS. His lectures weren't very long; he'd cover the material quickly, and then we'd self-study other subjects.
 He understood my style of grades always fluctuating regularly whether I studied or not, and entrusted me with all the school's computers.
 "Whether you study or not, it's all the same, isn't it? Come fix the computers."
After the Suneung (college entrance exam), I even worked overtime until 1 AM to help the teacher, and custom-developed a program that would output predicted schools based on subject weighting when students' scores were entered. My first client ㅡㅡ; ??
 He strongly advised me to retake the Suneung after I messed it up, but when I luckily got into the school and department I wanted, he was the happiest for me.
 Another memorable quote from him was...
 "Among teachers, a villain is absolutely necessary. And I am that villain."
 He was someone whose words truly seemed so cool.

6. Technology teacher in 3rd grade of high school
 One day, when the class president and I were secretly escaping from the underground reading room, we found the teacher sprawled out in front of the school sign, completely drunk.
 He was crying, calling out his wife's name. ㅡㅡ;
Hearing the whispers of passing adults, we couldn't just leave him, so we carried the teacher on our backs and returned to the underground reading room.
 After handing him over to the teacher supervising the underground reading room, we heard this:
 "Teachers are people too. There are times when they struggle. This incident is a secret among you. That's that, but since you secretly ran away, each of you will get two hits before going in."
 
 My mother always wrote this to my teachers:
"If they do wrong, please hit them."
 There are also punishments that, even when received, become memories and lessons.
Watching the news these days makes me feel quite wistful. Before going to the military, I met my homeroom teachers from 3rd grade of high school and 3rd grade of middle school. They were happy. And they didn't fail to mention that kids these days have it really tough.
