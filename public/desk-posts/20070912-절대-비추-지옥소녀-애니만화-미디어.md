그리고, 자신도 죽으면 지옥을 가야 한다.
그래도 받아들이고 싶은 원한이 있는가 ?

똑같은 대사~

그저 앞의 이야기 전개만 다른...

언제 좀 다른 전개가 되나 해서 끝까지 봤으나
지옥소녀에 대한 배경과 이야기의 진행은 24,25,26화 만 보면 된다. ㅡㅡ;
차라리 총 4화짜리 애니메이션이었다면 만족했을것이다.

도대체 어떻게 이런게 3기까지 인기를 끌고 있는건지..
그렇게 사람들은 죽이고 싶은 사람이 많은겐가 ?

이 애니의 대사대로 정말..
지옥소녀..
한번 죽어볼래 ? 그만 나와주~

I watched it because it was said to be fun on the internet, but I was trolled.
The premise was novel.
The existence of the Hell Girl, who sends the other person to hell on behalf of someone who harbors a grudge.
And, if you die, you also have to go to hell.
Is there still a grudge you'd be willing to accept that for?

If the story had been well-developed, it would have been excellent content.
The problem is the same pattern in every episode.
The same lines~

Ugh... It's like watching *Chikyu Sentai Flashman*.
The same pattern where a small monster appears first, then a Kraken, and it's finished off with a Rolling Vulcan.
Only the preceding story development is different...

I watched it to the end, hoping for a different development, but
you only need to watch episodes 24, 25, and 26 for the background of Hell Girl and the story's progression. ㅡㅡ;
I would have been satisfied if it had been a 4-episode anime instead.

How on earth did something like this become popular enough for 3 seasons...?
Do people really have that many people they want to kill?

Just like the lines from this anime, truly...
Hell Girl...
"Want to try dying once?" Please stop appearing~
