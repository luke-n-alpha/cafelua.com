 ps. For those who ask why I dislike SKT... as you can see from the rates above, if you look closely, it's expensive. There's no other reason.
Of course, there are advantages such as a wider range of membership card usage. In terms of price comparison, LGT is unrivaled.
However, the disadvantage is that due to the limitations of Ev-Do, it has a disadvantage in data communication speed, and due to its scale, it hasn't secured enough network coverage. If you're in urban or road areas (metropolitan areas, highway areas), don't worry. Sound quality is the same for all three carriers as signals are digitized. With the switch to 3G, the frequency band changed, so SKT no longer has an advantage.
In the case of HSDPA, it is theoretically faster, and there is room for speed improvement with future LTE application... but current tests show it's not even as good as WiBro. I'm not sure if it's significantly faster than LGT, and I also wonder if a smartphone's browser rendering time can even keep up with that speed anyway.
 
** So, to be honest, I would currently choose LGT. However, the reasons to choose KTF lie more in WiBro and Netspot. Netspot is a condition that cannot be ignored. WiBro is practically an added bonus in the Seoul metropolitan area. Furthermore, through these plans, we can see the carriers' true intentions, such as the prohibition of tethering and VoIP.**
 
 It was an open secret that SKT pressured handset manufacturers to release certain devices exclusively for SKT. In my case, I'm using SKT for the Xperia model. And although I'll pay a few thousand won more, SKT seems usable enough now. Furthermore, the emergence of these plans was virtually non-existent until recently. While the iPhone itself isn't extraordinary, the mere fact that it led to the introduction of these plans and changed the carrier environment seems like a great benefit to all smartphone users.
