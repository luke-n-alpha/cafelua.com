While browsing the internet, I came across someone's self-introduction.

Among the items there,

there was "Favorite Program : "...
The first things that came to mind were Java and C...

Looking again... it was TV programs... ㅡㅡ;
But most people don't say "TV program,"
and even if they just think "program,"
it suddenly occurred to me that they would probably still think of TV.
