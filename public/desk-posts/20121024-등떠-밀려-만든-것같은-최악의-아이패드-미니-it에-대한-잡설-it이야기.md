**1. 성공적이었던 뉴아이패드 전략** 최고의 방어는 공격이라는 이야기가 있습니다. 잡스의 뉴아이패드는 전 회심의 일격이라고 말씀드렸습니다. 거센 아마존 킨들 파이어의 공세를 멈춘 가장 효과적인 전략이었다고 평가했죠. 자신의 강점을 부각하고, 상대의 강점을 손못쓰게 만든 전략이었습니다. 먼저 레티나 디스플레이는 자신의 강점인 하이엔드와 혁신의 이미지를 부각했고, 두번째 구아이패드의 가격하락으로 아마존의 가격경쟁력을 깎아내렸습니다.  **2. 안드로이드 태블릿과 비교되는 순간, 이미 실패한 아이패드 미니** 제가 아이패드 미니에게 기대했던 것은 사실 바로 뉴아이패드의 후자전략이 있을거라는 점이었습니다. 이미 아이패드는 앱들의 우수성으로 인해 차별성은 있는 상황입니다. 그러나, 그 차별성을 얼마까지 쳐줄거냐는 다른 이야기죠.  같은 가격이라면 분명 아이패드가 훨씬 좋습니다. 저는 때문에 사용자가 근사하다고 느낄 가격인 299$~250$ 선에 결정될거라고 예상했습니다. 그런데, 다른 가격이라면? 여기서 사용자들은 합리적인 생각을 시작합니다. 애플이 실패하는 순간입니다. 애플의 제품은 그동안 생각할 시간을 주지 않을 만큼 쿨했습니다. 그러나, 이번 아이패드미니를 보면 누구나 넥서스7과 킨들파이어와 비교할것입니다. 이로서, 아이패드는 태블릿이 아니다. 라고 말했던 잡스의 전략이 팀쿡에 의해서 망가졌습니다. 삼성과 비교당한 애플이 어떤꼴을 겪었는지는 이미 다들 아실겁니다.**

The news about the iPad Mini that came this morning: the part that matched my expectations was the 1024*768 resolution, not the Retina display, and the part that didn't match my expectations was the iPad Mini's price, which amounts to 420,000 won.

**1. The Successful New iPad Strategy**
There's a saying that the best defense is offense. I said that Jobs's New iPad was a decisive blow. I evaluated it as the most effective strategy to halt the fierce offensive of the Amazon Kindle Fire. It was a strategy that highlighted its own strengths and rendered the opponent's strengths useless. First, the Retina display emphasized its strength in high-end and innovative image, and second, the price drop of the old iPad undercut Amazon's price competitiveness.

**2. The moment it's compared to Android tablets, the iPad Mini has already failed**
What I expected from the iPad Mini was actually the latter strategy of the New iPad. The iPad already has differentiation due to the excellence of its apps. However, how much that differentiation is worth is another story.

If the price were the same, the iPad would clearly be much better. That's why I expected the price to be set around $299-$250, a price users would find appealing.
But what if the prices are different? This is where users start thinking rationally. This is Apple's moment of failure. Apple's products until now have been cool enough not to give people time to think. However, anyone looking at this iPad Mini will compare it to the Nexus 7 and Kindle Fire. Thus, Jobs's strategy, which stated that the iPad is not a tablet, has been ruined by Tim Cook. You all already know what happened to Apple when it was compared to Samsung.

**3. Reasons why the iPad Mini lacks competitiveness**
**1) If you're an Apple enthusiast, go for the 9.7-inch with Retina...**
Your eyes are already accustomed to Retina. You'll pay a bit more and go for the 9.7-inch model. For Apple enthusiasts, it's a disappointing product from the first impression.
(Personally, I don't think Retina is absolutely necessary for a 7-inch model. I think creating a lower price is more meaningful.)

**2) If you're a user wanting a low-priced second device, Nexus 7?**
Ultimately, it gets compared to Android tablets. You can't help but consider Android tablets with higher resolution and a much lower price (299,000 won).

**3) Weakening of iPad's strength due to diminished app influence**
I plan to post separately on this later, but the boundary between web and app, which Jobs wanted to clearly define, is gradually blurring due to the high specifications of devices. Looking at Naver Me or Naver Webtoon's Smart Webtoon, it's hard to distinguish whether it's a web page or an app. Furthermore, it's true that user fatigue with app installation is increasing, and users who want a low-priced second device have a stronger need for content consumption like web and video rather than apps. This means that the iPad's advantage over Android is gradually weakening. Therefore, the price merit might not be as high as Tim Cook thinks.

**4. Tim Cook's iPad Mini, which boosted the Nexus 7. It should have never been born.**
The announcement of the iPad Mini ended up boosting the Nexus 7. Nexus 7 appeared in real-time search along with the iPad Mini, and the related search term for iPad Mini is Nexus 7.
The Nexus 7, which had relatively less promotion because mobile carriers weren't actively distributing it, ironically gained attention thanks to the iPad Mini.

Personally, I evaluate it as the worst product to come out of Apple since the iPhone. The concept of the iPad Mini feels like it was forcibly put together just to match the Android competitors. There's no differentiation, no merit.
Moreover, unlike Jobs, who strictly maintained clear boundaries between devices, Tim Cook is creating intermediate devices and making various compromises, which makes me wonder if he's shaking Apple's identity to its core. Is this "Tim Cook's Apple"?
Steve Jobs's prediction of a 7-inch DAO (Dead on Arrival) wasn't about the iPad Mini, was it?
