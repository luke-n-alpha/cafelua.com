** 2. 여성 유저의 니즈와 감성을 자극할 패드**
디지털 코쿤족이라면 예쁜 여성보다 예쁜 아이패드에 눈이 갈지 모르겠지만, 보통은 예쁜 이성한번 쳐다보고 그녀가 하고있는 다른 액션을 보게된다는 점이죠. 요즘 카페를 보면 넷북과 스마트폰을 들고 앉아있는 분들을 심심치 않게 볼 수 있습니다. 바로 여기서 생각해볼수 있는게 넷북의 휴대성은 여전히 좋지 않다는것과 상당수는 문서작업보다는 싸이월드같은 sns를 즐기는데 이용한다는 것이죠.

** 3. 7인치 안드로이드 패드가 갖추어야 할 조건**
그렇다면 각이 딱 나옵니다. 만들어야할 기기는
1) 여성의 가방에 들어갈정도의 휴대성을 지닐것
3) 디지털 카메라와 연동이 용이할것
4) sns게임과 캐쥬얼게임이 구동될것
5) 최소 720p 그 이상의 HD급 동영상 구동이 가능할 것
6) 그리고 마지막으로 중요한 예쁠것.

사실 아이패드는 타겟 자체가 홈에서 사용하는 디지털 엔터테이먼트 단말이었던데 반해, 7인치 안드로이드탭은 목적 자체가 상당히 다릅니다.
한 조사에 의하면 아이패드유저의 90%이상이 이동시에 사용하지는 않는다고 합니다. (사실 이동성을 강화한 넷북도 마찬가지였죠.)

** 4. 7인치 안드로이드 패드가 몰고올 변화**
** 1) 넷북구매율 감소**
아이패드가 영향을 준건 PC구매율이 아니라 넷북이었습니다. 그나마 넷북이 가지고 있던 유리한점, 싸이월드가 된다던지 하는 점 마저 안드로이드패드가 흡수할 가능성이 높습니다.

** 2) 캐쥬얼 게임들의 재조명**
최근 터치가 대세가 되면서 과거 퀴즈퀴즈에 있었던 그림 연상 게임이 다시 인기를 끌더군요. 라이트한 경험을 위주로한 이러한 기기에는 캐쥬얼 게임들이 딱입니다. 당연히 결국은 온라인으로 이어지겠죠. 다시한번 캐쥬얼 게임이 뜰듯합니다.

** 3) SNS 게임 활황**
지금도 잘되고 있는 SNS게임이 더 탄력을 받을듯합니다. 여기에는 FaceBook과 싸이월드 앱스토어가 한몫을 하겠죠. 최근 네이버에서도 뭔가 한다고 합니다.

** 4) PC방 타격**
이렇게되면 Pc방의 캐쥬얼게임 유저들이 안드로이드 패드로로 빠져나가면서 좀 더 칙칙해질것 같네요. 어차피 탭으로 카페에 앉아 스타2를 한다는것은 분위기가 안맞습니다. ㅋ

** 6) 디지털 카메라에 블루투스 확대**

** 1. The Meaning of a 7-inch Size**
My wife recently received a gift of a baby's spacesuit for our soon-to-be-born child, and it was made in the US. But its size was unbelievably large for a newborn. No matter how much Koreans' physical development has improved, there's bound to be a difference in average height and weight compared to Westerners. This phenomenon can be felt in Japanese laptops, which emphasized "smaller," and American laptops, which emphasized "powerful." What I felt while looking at these Android tablet machines was exactly that.
10 inches might be small enough for Westerners. But for East Asians, a 10-inch device weighing over a geun (680g) of beef is bound to be burdensome.

The Galaxy Tab was developed with the image of a diary in mind, and diaries mostly go into women's bags. This is precisely the important point. Targeting female users has several advantages; simply by them sitting in a cafe and touching this tab, they garner considerable attention.

** 2. A Pad to Stimulate the Needs and Sensibilities of Female Users**
Digital cocooners might look at a pretty iPad rather than a pretty woman, but usually, people glance at an attractive person and then observe other actions she's performing. These days, you can often see people sitting in cafes with netbooks and smartphones. What we can infer from this is that netbooks still don't offer great portability, and a significant number of people use them more for enjoying SNS like Cyworld than for document work.

** 3. Conditions a 7-inch Android Pad Must Meet**
Then the answer is clear. The device to be made must:
1) Have enough portability to fit into a woman's bag.
2) Be capable of full browsing, including Flash, without issues, considering Korea's web environment (at least a netbook-level resolution).
3) Be easy to link with a digital camera.
4) Be able to run SNS games and casual games.
5) Be capable of playing HD-quality videos, at least 720p or higher.
6) And finally, and most importantly, be pretty.

In fact, while the iPad's target audience was digital entertainment terminals for home use, the 7-inch Android tab has a significantly different purpose.
According to one survey, over 90% of iPad users do not use it while on the go. (In fact, the same was true for netbooks, which emphasized portability.)

** 4. Changes a 7-inch Android Pad Will Bring**
** 1) Decrease in Netbook Purchase Rate**
The iPad's impact was not on PC purchase rates but on netbooks. Even the advantages netbooks had, such as being able to access Cyworld, are likely to be absorbed by Android pads.

** 2) Re-evaluation of Casual Games**
As touch screens have become mainstream recently, picture association games, similar to those in the old Quiz Quiz, are gaining popularity again. Casual games are perfect for these devices that focus on light experiences. Naturally, this will eventually lead to online gaming. Casual games seem poised for another boom.

** 3) Boom in SNS Games**
SNS games, which are already doing well, are likely to gain even more momentum. Facebook and Cyworld app stores will play a significant role here. Recently, Naver also announced something in this area.

** 4) Impact on PC Bangs (Internet Cafes)**
If casual game users from PC bangs migrate to Android pads, PC bangs might become even gloomier. After all, playing StarCraft II on a tab in a cafe doesn't quite fit the atmosphere. lol

** 5) Need for Full HD Application, Cloud VOD Service**
While 1024*600 resolution, similar to netbooks, might be popular in the short term due to its price-performance ratio, the size of Android pads will soon increase to Full HD-level, such as 1366*768. The wide aspect ratio adopted by Android devices is compatible with the broadcasting industry.

** 6) Expansion of Bluetooth in Digital Cameras**
No matter how good phone cameras get, recent point-and-shoot cameras have also become incredibly powerful. And they've become really convenient and light. However, the inconvenience lies in removing and uploading memory cards, so I believe Bluetooth modules will eventually be integrated into them, making it easier to link with Android pads or netbooks. It's even highly possible that Android OS will be embedded in digital cameras. (Perhaps it's already being attempted...)

** 5. Announcement of the Start of Price Competition**
While the Galaxy Tab's price is clearly set very high, the price-breaking trend for this tab will soon begin, just like with netbooks. It could become a WiBro subsidy or a free gift when changing internet providers. It might even be used by online bookstores as a means to sell e-books, with subsidies going to the bookstores.
