**Believer and Quest Edutech Begin Targeting Southeast Asian and Global B2B Metaverse Markets with XRCloud**

**SEOUL, South Korea – June 16, 2024** – At the 2024 NEXT RISE event, a stage for various startup collaborations, **Believer Co., Ltd.** (CEO Yang Byung-seok), a leading company in extended reality (XR) technology, and **Quest Edutech Incorporated** (CEO Kirk Pathumanun), an innovative company in metaverse-based education solutions, announced the signing of a strategic Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) to target the Southeast Asian and global B2B markets. This agreement is stated to be a significant milestone, combining the global market expansion potential by leveraging the technological synergy of both companies, which have been developing and operating platforms based on **open-source Mozilla Hubs**.

{{IMG:1}}

**Strengthening Global Collaboration for Innovative XRCloud Distribution and Expansion of Educational Solutions**

**Believer**'s **XRCloud** web metaverse construction platform boasts powerful scalability, enabling the creation of web metaverse virtual environments without installation, thanks to its web standard compatibility and real-time update features. Through this agreement, **Quest Edutech** will distribute the XRCloud solution in the Southeast Asian B2B market and provide innovative metaverse solutions in the education sector. Both companies are creating opportunities to expand XRCloud into the global market through close cooperation with the Hubs Foundation. **Mozilla Hubs** technology, the foundation of XRCloud, is continuously expanding its use overseas, and **Quest Edutech** is a company generating substantial revenue in the educational metaverse market in Thailand.

**Quest Edutech** stated, "Through Believer's XRCloud, we have acquired full-body avatar and large-scale concurrent access technologies that Mozilla Hubs could not support, and Believer has discussed business expansion into Southeast Asia and plans for attracting overseas investment through Quest Edutech. This agreement is an important starting point for us to leap forward together. **Believer** is targeting **overseas markets** where the metaverse has been actively utilized by the private sector, rather than domestically where government-led construction has occurred, and plans to actively enter **various B2B markets** overseas that desire web metaverse technology easily accessible from anywhere in the world."

**Kirk Pathumanun, CEO of Quest Edutech**, said, "We are very pleased that our metaverse solution, combined with Believer's XRCloud, can help us expand in the Southeast Asian and global markets. Furthermore, we will spare no effort in supporting Believer's global market expansion by assisting with investment attraction."

**Key Goals and Plans for Collaboration**

With the signing of this MOU, **Believer** and **Quest Edutech** will promote growth in the Southeast Asian and global B2B markets through the following goals and plans:
- **Distribution and Expansion of XRCloud in the Southeast Asian Market**: **Quest Edutech** will pursue a strategic plan to distribute and expand **Believer**'s **XRCloud** solution to B2B customers in Southeast Asia.
- **Innovative Expansion of Educational Solutions**: **Quest Edutech** will provide immersive educational experiences through XRCloud, utilizing **full-body avatars and large-scale concurrent access features**.
- **Support for Attracting up to $400,000 in Investment**: **Quest Edutech** plans to provide necessary support to **Believer** for attracting up to $400,000 in funding from Southeast Asian investors for technology development and market expansion.
- **Collaboration with Hubs Foundation**: Both companies plan to expand XRCloud into the global market through collaboration with the Hubs Foundation.
- **Negotiation of Exclusive Agreement**: Based on the MOU, both companies plan to negotiate an exclusive distribution and resale agreement for XRCloud in the Southeast Asian B2B market.

**Expected Outcomes and Future Plans of the Collaboration**

The signing of this MOU will be a crucial starting point for **Believer** and **Quest Edutech** to accelerate growth in the Southeast Asian and global B2B markets for XRCloud and strengthen global competitiveness. Both companies aim for the following expected outcomes through this collaboration:
- **Provision of Innovative B2B and Educational Solutions**: Combining XRCloud's technology with Quest Edutech's distribution capabilities to provide immersive and scalable metaverse solutions to B2B customers and the education sector in Southeast Asia and globally.
- **Investment Attraction and Market Expansion**: Accelerating technology development and market expansion through support for attracting up to $400,000 in investment.
- **Negotiation of Exclusive Distribution and Resale Agreement**: Expanding market share through negotiation of an exclusive distribution and resale agreement in the Southeast Asian market.

**Company Overviews**

**Believer INC.**
**Believer** provides **XRCloud**, an innovative XR service construction platform for enterprises and developers, aiming for expansion in Southeast Asian and global markets.

**Quest Edutech Incorporated**
**Quest Edutech** utilizes **Mozilla Hubs**-based technology to provide **XRCloud** as an educational and business metaverse solution, already generating revenue through successful educational metaverse cases in the global market.
