The title is \"마음에 달\". \"마음\" means \"heart\" or \"mind\". \"에\" is a locative particle, meaning \"in\", \"on\", \"at\". \"달\" means \"moon\". So, literally, it's \"Moon in the heart\" or \"Moon on the mind\". Let's consider the nuance for a blog title. \"Moon in the heart\" sounds poetic and perhaps a bit abstract. \"Moon on the mind\" is also possible. Other interpretations: - \"The Moon in My Heart\" (adding \"my\" for naturalness) - \"Moon of the Heart\" (a common poetic structure) - \"Heart's Moon\" (more concise) Given it's a blog title, it should be evocative and easy to understand. \"Moon in the Heart\" is a very direct and common poetic phrase. \"The Moon in My Heart\" adds a personal touch which is often present in blog titles.
