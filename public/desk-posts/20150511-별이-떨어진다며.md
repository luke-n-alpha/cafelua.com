​They said a star was falling, so I bundled up and went up to the roof of my house. Damn.. The weather feels like it's going to snow. It's cloudy and as bright as a baseball stadium.. --; (Reinstalled Windows and the sketch board is working. This time FTP isn't working, so I'll have to reinstall again tomorrow. ㅠㅠ)​2002. 11. 19
2015. 05. 11​It was around 2002, I think, when I developed a hobby of going to see stars. I still have a vague longing for stars. Because it's probably the farthest place my gaze can reach.. My children are still young, so we can't go to places like astronomy villages, but I often show them books about stars. They say the reason stars aren't visible in cities is more due to light pollution than smog. I heard that in other countries, there are days when lights are turned off to see stars, but South Korea is always brilliantly bright 24 hours a day. It's no wonder the 2014 Nobel Prize went to the inventors of blue LED material, which emits bright light with little power, is it? Sometimes I want to look at the sky, but I'm always mindlessly staring at the monitor. I've been incredibly busy lately and haven't even been able to write personal posts... It seems like something needs to be sorted out.
