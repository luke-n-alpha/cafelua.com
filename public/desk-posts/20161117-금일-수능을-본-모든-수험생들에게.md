아직, 있다 뮤직비디오(세월호 참사 2주기 추모 영상)

돈도 실력이라고 말하며 말타고 대학가신 어떤 분이 있는가 하면
금일 많은 학생들이 치른 수능을 기다렸으나 끝내 치지 못했던 어떤 아이들도 있었습니다.
아직 있는 우리가 꼭 바로 잡아야 하는 이유입니다.

금일 수능을 보신 모든 분들에게 격려의 박수를 보냅니다.
수능을 잘 쳤던 못쳤던 금일은 누군가가 그토록 기다리던 그날 이었습니다. 힘내세요.
대신 돈도 없고 말도 사줄수 없지만 여러분 곁을 묵묵히 지켜주신 부모님께 고맙다 한마디만 해주세요.

And, JTBC's closing music was Lucid Fall's "Still There."

                        While there are some who say money is skill and went to university by riding horses,
there were also some children who waited for the Suneung that many students took today, but ultimately couldn't take it.
This is why we, who are still here, must set things right.

I send a round of applause to everyone who took the Suneung today.
Whether you did well or not, today was the day someone had been waiting for so eagerly. Cheer up.
Instead, please just say "thank you" to your parents who silently stood by you, even if they couldn't give you money or buy you a horse.
