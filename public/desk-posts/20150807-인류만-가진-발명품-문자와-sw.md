I believe software, along with writing, is humanity's greatest invention. There are animals that control fire, animals that make tools (machines), and animals that count numbers (mathematics) or enjoy singing (art). However, humanity is unique in communicating with symbols (writing) and, based on this, creating machines (computers) capable of creative thought (software) just like themselves.
