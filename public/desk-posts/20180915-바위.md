On a day so profoundly melancholic it's hard to breathe... I wish I could become a cold stone, unable to feel. If I were to become such a rock, I would be alright even alone... Or I would be alright even if broken. Scattered into countless pieces, becoming even sand, my heart would be swept away to the shining sea.
