** 1. 음질, 막귀를 뚫어준 하이파이 이어폰**이번에 이어폰을 소개시켜 드리기 위해 포스팅을 쓰지만, 사실 저는 막귀를 자랑하며, 음악에 무지한 편입니다. 귀에 전기가 올라 딱딱거리는 3500원짜리 이어폰도 잘 쓰는 편이죠. 스마트폰의 이어폰은 갤럭시S2의 번들도 매우 만족하며 쓰는 그저 그런 사용자입니다. 무슨 파워블로거가 그러냐 라고 말씀하실지 모르겠지만, 전 리뷰 블로거도 아니고 (오히려 구매 제품을 가감없이 까는지라 블랙리스트에 안들어있다면 다행), 수만원짜리 사는 것도 마눌님의 윤허를 받아야 하고, 철저히 가성비에만 목을 메는 그런 서민남입니다. 그나마 치렁치렁한 선이 싫어서 구매한 HBS-3000시리즈의 삼성 블루투스 이어폰도 정말 감격하며 사용하고 있었죠.  HBS-3000도 그럭저럭 들을만한 음질에 무엇보다도 범용 이어폰 아무거나 꽂을 수 있는 점도 마음에 들었고, 쓸데 없는 기능 없이 심플한게 가장 마음에 들었습니다. 그러던 중에 GBH-S400이라는 블루투스 이어폰의 리뷰를 부탁받았습니다. 전 편견을 없애기 위해.... (아니 사실은 게을러서) 아무 준비 없이 이 이어폰을 갤럭시S2에 연결하여 음악을 틀었습니다.
순간, 헉.. 정말 좋더군요. ㅜㅜ  삼성 HBS-3000으로 듣던 음질은 60km제한 2차선 도로로 달리다가, GBH-S400을 듣는 것은 8차선 고속도로를 달리는 기분이군요. 베이스가 틀려요. 베이스가.. 귀가 순식간에 고급이 되버린 기분이었습니다.

**1. Sound Quality: Hi-Fi Earphones That Opened Up My 'Tin Ears'**
I'm writing this post to introduce earphones, but honestly, I pride myself on having 'tin ears' and being quite ignorant about music. I even use 3,500 won earphones that crackle with static electricity quite well. When it comes to smartphone earphones, I'm just an average user who is perfectly satisfied with the bundled earphones of the Galaxy S2. You might wonder what kind of power blogger says that, but I'm not a review blogger (in fact, I'm lucky if I'm not on a blacklist because I criticize purchased products without hesitation), and I need my wife's permission to buy anything worth tens of thousands of won. I'm just an ordinary guy who is thoroughly obsessed with cost-effectiveness. Even the Samsung Bluetooth earphones from the HBS-3000 series, which I bought because I disliked dangling wires, I was using with great satisfaction.

The HBS-3000 also had decent sound quality, and what I liked most was that I could plug in any universal earphones, and its simplicity without unnecessary features. Then, I was asked to review a Bluetooth earphone called GBH-S400. To eliminate prejudice... (or rather, because I was lazy) I connected these earphones to my Galaxy S2 without any preparation and played music.
Suddenly, wow... it was really good. ㅜㅜ The sound quality I heard with the Samsung HBS-3000 felt like driving on a 60km/h limited two-lane road, but listening to the GBH-S400 felt like driving on an eight-lane highway. The bass is different. The bass... My ears instantly felt like they had become high-class.

**2. Product Design**
**1) Earphones: Two Types, Neckband and Clip-on!**
The neckband type can be worn around the neck without any burden. The cable is also tangle-resistant... it's a 'kal-guksu' (flat noodle) shape. (Someone please tell me in the comments what this is called...) Most importantly, it comes with two earphones. One is clip-type, and the other is straight-type, which is a difference, but for me, the fact that there are two is more significant than the difference in type. It seems like a consideration for people like me who use them roughly and often break them...

**3. Features**
As expected from an expensive product, it also offers various features.
**1) Bluetooth Earphones**
- Its inherent function, needless to say, and the sound quality of portal music apps is also good.
**2) Radio**
- FM radio can also be listened to. It's a surprisingly useful feature to have. You can even listen to Cultwo Show with just earphones without consuming data..
**3) MP3 Player**
- Additionally, you can put MP3 files on a microSD card and listen to music. The sound quality is better than Bluetooth, but it's a bit difficult to insert and remove. Still a useful feature for those who enjoy listening to music offline.
**4) Call Send/Receive**
- When making or receiving calls, you can check the caller's name on the OLED before answering. I couldn't properly test voice dialing because I don't know how to use it well. Is it a Galaxy S2 issue? The app seemed to run properly... If it works well, it would be surprisingly convenient, but I couldn't properly use the Galaxy S2's Google voice dialing, so PASS.
**5) Message Reception**
- It's said to have this function, but there's no detailed explanation. Testing on the Galaxy S2 showed it didn't work. I thought it would be good to have a more user-friendly explanation. What is MAP? Is it a map?

**4. Overall Review**
It can be called an all-in-one model in the Bluetooth earphone world, with a wide range of features and excellent sound quality. They say it captures both hi-fi and Bluetooth, and as an existing HBS-3000 user, I thought, 'So this is what hi-fi is like...' It's really good. Since I'm someone who doesn't usually spend money on sound quality... it's hard for me to definitively say if it's as good as true hi-fi. **What's certain is that if I were to give it as a gift to a girlfriend who uses just any regular Bluetooth earphones, I think she might be deeply impressed.** However, if a slightly lower-priced model were to be released later, with the OLED, radio, and MP3 player removed, I think it would be a thoughtful consideration for those who crave good sound quality but also have a thirsty wallet. Thank you for the wonderful music gift. This concludes the review of the Hi-Fi GBH-S400.
