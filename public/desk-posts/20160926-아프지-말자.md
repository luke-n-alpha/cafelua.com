On days when I'm not feeling well, if I look at a street bathed in strong light, colors disappear and only light and shadow remain. Let's not get sick.. If you get sick, you become weak. Let's not get sick, because if you become weak, you might remember things again and get depressed. Let's find the colors of our hearts.
2003.08.12

It's true that if your heart hurts, your body gets sick more often.
2016.09.26
