**1. The Unfortunate WiBro Technology**
Do you remember WiBro, the 4G technology developed in Korea? Perhaps some of you, now so accustomed to LTE, might have only vague memories of it. WiBro was primarily serviced by KT. In a previous post I wrote, I mentioned that the prejudice of WiBro being slower than LTE was not an appropriate comparison, as WiBro was shackled by frequency band allocation issues. Anyway, WiBro's biggest strength was that it was much cheaper than LTE. In fact, WiBro's technical background can be seen as adding mobility to Wi-Fi. While being cheap is an advantage for customers, it can be a disadvantage for mobile carriers seeking profit, and the market standard technology is usually determined by a complex interplay of many factors beyond just technical superiority. As a result, WiBro effectively lost the standard competition, and it's true that it became burdensome for mobile carriers to continue investing in it.

**2. WiBro's Biggest Strength: Price! And its Weakness: Dead Zones. A Godsend to Overcome Weaknesses: Hybrid!**
However, for those who have used WiBro, the ability to use 10GB of internet at a speed good enough to watch YouTube videos without interruption, albeit slightly slower than LTE, for less than 20,000 won including VAT, was a huge attraction. In fact, there were smartphones that incorporated both 3W WiBro technology and 3G. But as I mentioned earlier, at a time when continuous investment in WiBro is not happening, WiBro, with its many dead zones compared to LTE, often leads to frustrating situations. What's the point of having a lot of data if it doesn't connect?

But now, a product has emerged that solves this problem. It's called the Hybrid Egg, a new product from Infomark, which always produces Eggs as beautifully as Apple. Finally, it has a built-in USIM and automatically switches to LTE in WiBro dead zones. If you're in an area where WiBro doesn't work, the Egg is essentially the same concept as U+ LTE router. The difference is that it's much cheaper. You can use 10GB of internet for 16,500 won including VAT. By using this Egg, I even lowered my phone plan from 62,000 won to 34,000 won. A quick calculation shows I save about 12,000 won per month while increasing my internet data from 6GB to 10GB. Furthermore, for someone like me who uses Wi-Fi devices for tablets and navigation, and even frequently needs laptop internet for fieldwork, this was the optimal choice.

In fact, I once left the Egg on my desk with streaming music playing and went outside, using tens of megabytes at once ㅜㅜ And you can't ignore apps running in the background.
If you go to Settings > Connections > Data Usage, you can set your smartphone to be used only for Wi-Fi (excluding calls), set a limit for 750MB usage, or prevent apps from using data in the background (when not actively in use). Using these features can help you save on your phone bill.
In my case, after using 300MB in 4 days, I completely turned off the data network settings to use it only for Wi-Fi. Since I'm mostly in Wi-Fi areas and just carry the Egg, it seems like a good approach after a few days of use. I'm even thinking of lowering my plan below 34,000 won and increasing voice minutes.
This concludes the Hybrid Egg review. I hope that with the Hybrid Egg, WiBro and LTE will develop into technologies that complement each other appropriately.
