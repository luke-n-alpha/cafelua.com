며칠전에 카카오스토리라는 모바일 소셜네트워크서비스가 오픈했습니다. 카카오톡의 형제서비스라는 점 하나만으로도 주목을 끌만합니다.{{IMG:1}}

** 1. 위기의 카카오톡의 역전 만루홈런?** 개인적으로는 카카오톡은 위기라고 생각하고 있었습니다. 높아지는 사용량만큼 뾰족한 수익이 나오지 않고 있고, 그렇다고.해외의 이용자가 팍팍 늘어나고 있지도 않았으니까요. 플러스친구에대한 반응은 좀 별로 였고, 이모티콘 팔아서 연명하는것은 싸이월드의 사례를 보듯 한계가 분명합니다. 이미 국민앱이 된 카카오톡은 이제 수익을 실현하지 않으면 빛좋은 개살구가 될지도 모른다는 생각과, 결국, 남은것은 잘 파는것이라고 생각하고 있었습니다. 그러던와중에 틱톡이 sk에 인수되고, 왠만한 대형 it회사들은 독자의 톡서비스를 하려고 하고 있는 상황이라 매각할만한데가 있을끼 하는 의문이들고 있었죠. 그러던 와중에 카카오스토리라는 소셜네트워크가 나왔습니다. 소셜네트워크는 많은 기업들이 눈독들이고 있는 분야로 이는 각종 정보를 유통하는 플랫폼이자 빅데이터의 키로 여겨지고 있는 서비스입니다. 여기에서는 페이스북이 증명했듯이 확실히 돈 줄이 보이는 분야기도 합니다. 카카오스토리가 성공한다면 제 예측은 완전히 빗나가고 제대로된 비즈니스모델을 잡아낸겁니다.
** 2. 얼마나 새로운 sns길래 이렇게 뜨거운 반응일까?** 친구의 사진을 타임라인 하나로 볼수 있고, 빠른 속도, like버튼이 아닌 좀 더 세분화된 의사표현, 카카오톡 아이디를 통한 간편한가입, 주소록기반의 실명 네트워크가 장점이긴 하지만, 사실 새로운것은 없습니다. 먼저 전체적인 모습을 보면 다들 하던이야기가 패쓰네. 라는 거 였습니다. 사실입니다. 카카오톡 아이디로 가입하는 간편함은 이미 네이버의 간편로그인과 크게다르지 않은 기능입니다. 네이버의 sns의 경우, 정작 미투데이에서는 이원화된 회원체계로 간편로그인을 쓸수는 없지만 이번 카톡때문이라도 곧 적용이 되겠죠. 결국은 새로운게 하나도 없어 별로라는게 초기의 주위 반응이었습니다.
** 3. 전혀 새롭지 않다. 그러나 새롭다.** 그러나 유저들의 폭발적인 반응을 알리는 기사들이 쏟아졌습니다. 왜일까요? 이는 제 주변이 it종사자와 일반유저간의 괴리감입니다. 페이스북에도 관심이 없던 사촌동생들이 카카오스토리에 모습을 보였습니다. 그들은 패쓰를 알지 못합니다. 그러니 그들에게는 새로울수 밖에요.
** 5. 아직까지도 비즈니스모델은 불명확하지만 카카오의 힘과 가능성을 보여준 사례** 물론 sns를 카카오톡이 접수한다하더라도 비즈니스모델은 아직 불명확하고, 현재도 수익은 커녕 투자가 더 많이드는것이 현실입니다. 하지만, 이번 카카오스토리에서 보여준 카카오의 가치는 분명합니다. 그리고, 국내 모바일의 강자는 카카오임을 증명해줬습니다. 과연 카카오가 또다른 네이버가 될수 있을지 지켜봐야할것 같습니다.

A few days ago, KakaoStory, a mobile social network service, launched. The mere fact that it's a sibling service to KakaoTalk is enough to attract attention.{{IMG:1}}

**1. KakaoTalk in Crisis: A Reversal Grand Slam?** Personally, I thought KakaoTalk was in crisis. Despite increasing usage, there wasn't a clear profit, and overseas users weren't rapidly increasing either. The reaction to Plus Friend was rather lukewarm, and relying on selling emoticons, as seen with Cyworld, clearly has its limits. I thought that KakaoTalk, already a national app, might become a "pretty but useless" app if it didn't start generating profit, and ultimately, what remained was to sell it well. Meanwhile, TicToc was acquired by SK, and most major IT companies were trying to launch their own chat services, raising the question of whether there would be anywhere to sell it. It was then that KakaoStory, a social network, emerged. Social networks are an area many companies are eyeing, as they serve as platforms for distributing various information and are considered key to big data. As Facebook has proven, this is also an area where a clear revenue stream is visible. If KakaoStory succeeds, my predictions will have been completely wrong, and they will have found a proper business model.
**2. How New is This SNS to Elicit Such a Hot Reaction?** Being able to see friends' photos on a single timeline, fast speed, more nuanced expressions of opinion instead of just a 'like' button, easy sign-up via KakaoTalk ID, and an address book-based real-name network are advantages, but in reality, there's nothing new. First, looking at the overall picture, everyone was saying, "It's just Path." That's true. The ease of signing up with a KakaoTalk ID is not significantly different from Naver's simple login feature. In the case of Naver's SNS, Me2day currently has a dual membership system that doesn't allow simple login, but it will likely be applied soon, perhaps because of KakaoTalk. Ultimately, the initial reaction from those around me was that it wasn't great because there was nothing new.
**3. Not New at All. Yet, It Is New.** However, articles reporting explosive user reactions poured out. Why? This is due to the gap between IT professionals and general users in my circle. My cousins, who weren't even interested in Facebook, appeared on KakaoStory. They don't know Path. So, for them, it's bound to be new.
**4. The Power That Makes KakaoTalk New: Formidable Reach** According to data released by KoreanClick, KakaoTalk's reach exceeds 95%. This means 95% of smartphone users use KakaoTalk (it's usage, not just installation). Did Cyworld, once a national service, ever reach such a figure? In Naver's case, there are people who intentionally avoid using only Naver, and Line, positioned as a real-time communication tool like a chat app, still doesn't have much power in Korea, making it difficult to achieve this level of reach. Even assuming 90% market share in search, its reach would inevitably be lower than a real-time chat app. With this formidable reach, KakaoTalk was able to deliver advanced services to very casual users. Of course, a photo community service like Path was definitely needed by Korean smartphone users. Many, including myself, spoke about photo-based communities being key. Just as KakaoTalk, created after seeing WhatsApp, wasn't new, KakaoStory isn't new either. However, it was needed by many users, appeared new, and had a channel through which it could approach them.
**5. Although the Business Model Is Still Unclear, a Case That Showed Kakao's Power and Potential** Of course, even if KakaoTalk takes over SNS, the business model is still unclear, and currently, it's a reality that investments outweigh profits. However, the value Kakao demonstrated with KakaoStory is clear. And it proved that Kakao is the powerhouse of mobile in Korea. It remains to be seen whether Kakao can truly become another Naver.
