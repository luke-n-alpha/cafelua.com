I was surprised that skins and personal icons became free.
Free stuff is great, as expected.
On top of that, they even refunded everything I had bought previously...
I was once again surprised by Naver's generosity.
 
* Difficulty finding the silver coin purchase and gift buttons
 
I struggled for a long time trying to find the silver coin purchase and gift buttons.
How about placing the silver coin top-up station and silver coin gifting options below the "View Silver Coin Usage History"?
They are the same gray color and located at the bottom of the screen, making them hard to see.
The "Silver Coin Usage History" is the most prominent, but when I clicked it,
it took me to the blog, and I couldn't top up there.
 
I had been grumbling about fonts being expensive...
And buying a font with a set duration was... well, a bit iffy...
But this time, I bought a font with the refunded money.
Now, if I just buy fonts and music, I'll have less need to spend real money on blogging ^^
 
I also like that there's an Item Factory Cafe,
and that they'll upload items if you create them yourself...
 
I'd like to make suggestions regarding skin management.
 
1. Random Skins
2. Weather-specific Skin Options
 
 - Since they became free, there are many skins, so it would be nice to show favorite skins randomly or
  to be able to show them according to the weather.
 
* Blog Banner Feature
- It would be great to have a feature to display banners for clubs or Happy Log that one belongs to.
 Wouldn't it create a sense of belonging?
 
 The location could be below the photo in the prologue...
Or assigned to the far right.
