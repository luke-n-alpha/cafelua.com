**Pigs That Don't Fly**

{{IMG:1}}

A pig that doesn't fly is just a pig. Porco Rosso 2003. 01. 30 **2015. 05. 27**

Porco Rosso is one of the masterpieces among the anime I've seen. I also think it's the most Miyazaki Hayao-esque work. And perhaps, it's the gaze of an enlightened observer, watching love unfold. I probably wanted to be that way, and I wanted to chase the dream of flying rather than love.
