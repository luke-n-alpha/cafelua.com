The weather was still chilly before the Lunar New Year.
I stopped by the winter sea.

As soon as I unpacked at the accommodation, I thought, "Since I came to the sea, shouldn't I eat raw fish?"
So... I stopped by Sokcho Port nearby.

Although it wasn't as big as Daepho Port nearby, the port I just randomly entered because it was close to the accommodation was noisy and chaotic with the hawking of the ajummas...

I was busy snapping away, trying to capture a few photos.
