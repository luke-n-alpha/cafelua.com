머리를 하고온지 꽤 시간이 지났는데 여러일이 정신없다보니 이제야 포스팅을 발간하네요. 아시는분은 아시겠지만, 상업성은 배제하는 블로거라고는 하지만, 소정의 소개료로 제가 원하는 것(?)이면 포스팅을 발간합니다. {{IMG:1}}﻿ It블로거 주제에 왠 미용실 포스팅이냐 말하겠지만, 일단은 지역상점이 it블로거에 포스팅을 요청하여 블로그마켓팅을 생각했다는 것 자체가 it가 아닐까요? 아전인수라고 생각하실지 모르겠지만, 제 블로그는 제껍니다. 태클 금지. 네이버에 파워블로그 달라고 부탁한적 없습니다.
이무튼 사실 전 외모에 그닥 관심없는 순수 공돌이 ﻿﻿출신입니다. 화창한 봄날 커플이 득실거리는 교정에서도, 머리는 블루클럽과 학교이발관에서 착실히 돈을 모아 램업그레이드에 기뻐하던 칙칙한? 공돌이였습니다. 잡스옹은 자신만의 유니폼을 만들고 싶었다지만, 전 유니폼의 정체성도 없었습니다. 처음 외모에 관심을 가진건 나이 스물절반에 한 아가씨에게 미쳤을때 즈음, 처음 홍대까지 찾아가 염색이란것도 해보고 머리에 그래픽카드보다 더 비싼돈을 쳐발쳐발해봤죠. 그때 서툰 연애감정으로 그 아가씨와는 틀어졌습니다.
그러나. 디행히 2년후에 다시만나.  새봄이의 엄마가 되어있습니다. 하지만, 여전히 제 외모에 대한 무개념으로 시간이 없다는 저를 아내가 미용실로 끌고갑니다.
그런데 최근 아침마다 머리하기 어렵다니 아예 펌을 하라더군요. 그러던중에 반가운 리뷰요청. 승낙했습니다. 이왕이면 아내도 부탁을... 어차피 미용실 고객은 대부분 여성일테니까요.

먼저 위치는 범계역에서 한 200미터정도 떨어져있습니다. 로데오거리에서 중앙공원쪽 뒷쪽 건물, 봄빛병원근처입니다. 흠... 미용실에 대해 어떻게 리뷰해야할지 잘 모르겠네요.
좋았던건, 만화책이 많습니다.  {{IMG:2}}

It's been quite a while since I got my hair done, but with so many things going on, I'm only now publishing this post. As some of you may know, although I'm a blogger who generally excludes commercial content, I do publish posts for a small referral fee if it's something I want (?). {{IMG:1}} You might ask why an IT blogger is posting about a hair salon, but isn't the very act of a local business requesting a post from an IT blogger to consider blog marketing, IT itself? You might think I'm being self-serving, but this is my blog. No tackling allowed. I never asked Naver to make me a power blogger.
Anyway, I'm actually a pure engineer by background, with little interest in my appearance. Even on a bright spring day, amidst a campus teeming with couples, I was a drab engineer who diligently saved money from Blue Club and school barbershops to happily upgrade my RAM. While Jobs wanted to create his own uniform, I didn't even have a uniform identity. I first became interested in my appearance around the age of twenty-five, when I was smitten with a girl. I even went all the way to Hongdae to get my hair dyed for the first time and spent more money on it than on a graphics card. Back then, due to clumsy romantic feelings, things didn't work out with that girl.
However, fortunately, we met again two years later. And she became Saebom's mother. But, still clueless about my appearance, my wife drags me to the hair salon, despite my protests of having no time.
Recently, she told me it's hard to do my hair every morning, so I should just get a perm. Just then, a welcome review request came in. I accepted. While I'm at it, I asked for my wife too... since most hair salon customers are probably women anyway.

First, the location is about 200 meters from Beomgye Station. It's in the building behind Rodeo Street, towards Jungang Park, near Bombit Hospital. Hmm... I'm not sure how to review a hair salon.
What I liked was that there were many comic books. {{IMG:2}}
I don't remember if there was wireless internet, but there's a PC. And some tea and a large table? Huh? Was I reviewing a cafe? Shall I say it's equipped with items for men waiting for their girlfriends? It's not a fancy or very stylish interior, but it has a spacious lounge area compared to other hair salons (my wife vouched for the "compared to other hair salons" part).

After getting my hair done, I heard a lot of comments about how good it looked on my first day back at work. For me, with my thinning hair on top that makes me look bald, it was an excellent choice? I felt like I'd been upgraded a level, even if I'm no Gu Jun-pyo. I hope I can maintain this hairstyle.
I once read in a book that appearance is actually the number one factor in a man's success. I don't know if it's true, but it's certainly true that appearance creates an image, and that image is an important factor in persuading others. Also, neglecting one's appearance can give the impression of being sloppy in self-management. More than that, I feel that physical management is necessary in terms of physical fitness.
