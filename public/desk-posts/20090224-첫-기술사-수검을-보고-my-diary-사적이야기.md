2월 22일 공부한지 한달도 안되서 맛배기나 보려고 첫 기술사 시험을 보러 갔습니다.

정말 8:30분까지 가야하더군요. 10분만 늦었어도 큰일날뻔했습니다.
고사장인 수원 삼일공고 생각보다 멀더군요. 다음에는 15분만 더 일찍 나가야지.
학교 앞에는 리어카 한대가 나와있어서 떡볶이, 김밥등이 있어서 굳이 김밥을 안사왔어도 됬을것 같더군요.

처음에는 제법 그럴듯하게 표도 그리고, 구분도 해보고 하려 했지만,
이건 뭐 아는 것도 없고, 아는건 그냥 주저리주저리 되는데로 써버렸습니다.
솔직히 쓰면 안될 사견들도 마구마구. "웹2.0은 허구다."라고 써버렸습니다.
합격할 마음이었다면 아마도 이렇게 쓰지 않았겠죠.
모르겠으면 찍찍 그어버리고.. 최악의 답안이라면 이렇게 작성하는거다. 라고 보여주고 왔습니다.

점심시간에는 잠깐 한겜이나 할까해서 PC방이나 찾아볼까 했는데.. 근처에 PC방도 없더군요.
그래서 주위의 수원성을 구경했습니다. 경치는 정말 끝내줬습니다.
그리고 또 시험장에 들어가 시험을 치뤘습니다.
1,2,3교시에는 거짓말을 섞어서 어캐어캐 쓰기는 했습니다만,
4교시는 정말 2문제 정도밖에 쓸수가 없더군요 ㅜㅜ 완전 gg.
전혀 추측도 안가는 문제라 거짓말도 칠수 없었습니다. 결국은 중간에 먼저 나와버렸습니다.

그렇게 아주 가볍게 가벼운 마음으로 시험을 쳤다고 생각했는데,
집에 갈때는 팔목도 아프고 무지하게 피곤하더군요.
결국은 그 다음날까지 피로가 갔습니다.

정말 가벼운 마음으로 볼게 아닌거란 생각만 들었습니다.
얻은게 있다면, 이런 분위기구나.. 하는 것만 보고 왔습니다.

8월에는 정식입니다. 그때까지는 철저히 준비해야겠습니다.

<!-- en -->
On February 22nd, less than a month after I started studying, I went to take my first professional engineer exam, just to get a feel for it.

I really had to be there by 8:30 AM. If I had been just 10 minutes late, it would have been a disaster.
Sam-il Technical High School in Suwon, the test venue, was farther than I thought. Next time, I'll have to leave 15 minutes earlier.
In front of the school, there was a pushcart selling tteokbokki, gimbap, etc., so I probably didn't need to have bought gimbap beforehand.

At first, I tried to make it look decent, drawing tables and making distinctions, but
I didn't know anything, and what I did know, I just rambled on and wrote whatever came to mind.
Honestly, I even wrote down a lot of my personal opinions that shouldn't have been there. I even wrote, "Web 2.0 is a fiction."
If I had intended to pass, I probably wouldn't have written like that.
If I didn't know, I just scribbled it out... I went there and showed them, "This is how you write the worst possible answer."

During lunchtime, I thought about playing a quick game and looked for a PC bang (internet cafe)... but there weren't any nearby.
So I explored Suwon Hwaseong Fortress nearby. The scenery was absolutely stunning.
And then I went back into the test venue and took the exam again.
For the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd periods, I managed to write something, mixing in some lies, but
for the 4th period, I could only answer about 2 questions ㅜㅜ Total GG (game over).
The questions were completely beyond my guessing ability, so I couldn't even make things up. In the end, I left early.

I thought I had taken the exam very lightly, with a carefree attitude,
but on the way home, my wrist hurt and I was incredibly tired.
The fatigue lasted until the next day.

I realized that it's definitely not something to take lightly.
If there's anything I gained, it was just seeing what the atmosphere was like.

The official exam is in August. Until then, I need to prepare thoroughly.
