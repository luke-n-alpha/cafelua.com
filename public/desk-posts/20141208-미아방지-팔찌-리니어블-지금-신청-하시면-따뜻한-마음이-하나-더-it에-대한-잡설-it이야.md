**1. Remember Lineable, the $5 Innovation?** I once introduced Lineable, a $5 anti-lost wristband for children. It was a wearable device that used beacons to collectively track children's locations. What made it innovative was the idea of "protecting together." At the time, it was crowdfunding through Indiegogo, far exceeding its initial goal of $30,000, reaching $40,000, spreading through social networks over 3,500 times, being featured in TechCrunch on September 24th, and winning first place at D.camp's D.day on September 26th. Many people commented on my blog, and many were curious about when and where to buy it. It seems all parents feel the same way.

**2. Finally, Concrete News About Lineable is Here.** So, here's an update. Detailed purchase information for Lineable has been released. **1) Sizes and Colors** Sizes are available in S, M, and L for ages 3 to 12, and a variety of color options are now available. {{IMG:1}} **2) "One Heart" Package with Customizable Logo and Text** The "One Heart" package (250,000 won), likely for use in daycare centers or schools, comes in units of 50 and allows for customizable text and logos. {{IMG:2}}

**3) Schedule** The app is scheduled for release in February, and products will be shipped sequentially starting March 15th.
**4) Price** The Korean price is 5,000 won, with a separate shipping fee of 3,000 won.

**3. Where to Buy? Pre-order Now for a Warm 1+1 Deal.** Protect your child, and give one more to a child in need. {{IMG:3}} And there's more. Following Indiegogo overseas, Lineable is also running a crowdfunding campaign in Korea through Wadiz. Crowdfunding might sound complicated, but you can think of it simply as a pre-order. A slight difference is that if the goal is not met, the project is canceled and refunded; if it is met, the product is manufactured and delivered to customers. The target amount is 20 million won, with a deadline of January 31st, and 20% has already been achieved. At this rate, it seems likely to reach 20 million won without difficulty by January 31st. The reason for this crowdfunding campaign is not just to raise funds for product manufacturing. For every one purchased, one will be delivered to children in a special education school for the disabled. If you're planning to buy anyway, why not participate in this project a little earlier? Not only can you get Lineable the fastest, but you can also help children with disabilities. The reason this project started was an email from a female student majoring in special education in Chungnam. Children with disabilities are more likely to go missing during outdoor events, and the risks are greater. Earlier this year in Changwon, a child with disabilities was found dead 15 days after going missing, and it was pointed out that the accident was due to inadequate initial response. What if a product like this had existed then? This project began before the product was even released, thanks to the beautiful letter from the student expressing this wish.
