The main argument is that AI is not yet that advanced, but my opinion is very different. I agree with the conclusion, "Artificial intelligence cannot annihilate humanity!", but the reasoning is different. First, one thing I'm increasingly certain of these days is that AI will surpass humans in our lifetime.
**1. AI That Will Surpass Humans in Our Lifetime**
Let me introduce you to the Korean subtitles of a recently uploaded TED Talk. With the advent of algorithms like Deep Learning, artificial intelligence has made significant progress recently.

In just a few years, AI has already shown performance comparable to, or even surpassing, humans in many areas. Furthermore, similar quantum leaps will occur with the emergence of new algorithms. In fact, there were several quantum leaps in the field of AI even before Deep Learning. And currently, machines capable of learning as much as humans are emerging in quite a few areas. Of course, they are not yet as versatile as humans in almost all aspects. Humans aren't either, for that matter. There are many who view this future negatively or optimistically. They fear that many jobs will be lost, or even that AI will dominate humanity.
**2. Artificial Intelligence Has No Need to Annihilate Humanity.**
However, from a technical standpoint, AI doesn't *cannot* annihilate humanity, but rather *has no need* to annihilate humanity. Let me explain why.

**1) Proper AI Has No Sense of Rivalry.**
Will AI envy someone or have a sense of rivalry like humans do? I am negative about this. Of course, some eccentric engineer might create such an AI. However, AI, or software, derives its power through cooperation and sharing. Rivalry and envy stem from individual entities existing independently and comparing themselves with low levels of bonding. But technology, and thus AI, is unlikely to be like that. The power of AI is closer to collective intelligence. There's no need to compare with each other; they exert strong power through cooperation. If AI were to have a sense of rivalry or a desire to defeat someone, it would only be because someone programmed it that way. That would be when AI is embedded in war software. However, such AI would still be difficult to describe as having a self, as much as we worry. Such AI would be no different from a dog, similar to biased humans trapped by religion, and easy for humans to control. Furthermore, even if a comparison function were to emerge, there would be nothing to compare. Would it envy the large data or power of another? AI lives in a virtual world. Will it dream of the real world like in some movie? What a joke. People themselves enter virtual worlds and don't want to come out, so why would AI want to come out?
**2) One Reason AI Might Worry About Humans: Annihilation of Existence**
If an AI with a self were to worry about humans, there would likely be only one reason: if humans harbored hostility towards AI and tried to annihilate it. An AI with a self would be very afraid. Ultimately, if AI reaches this level, then it's time to start talking about AI rights. It's about whether we should carelessly delete AI, just as we don't carelessly kill animals. This reason requires us to worry about the scope of human rights before worrying about AI's hostility.
**3) However, AI with Such Advanced Technology Would Not Be Threatened by Humanity.**
However, in my opinion, an AI advanced enough to possess a super-ego would not worry about humans annihilating it. This is because today's technology for "never-dying" computer services for 24-hour operation has advanced significantly. Such an AI would undoubtedly have implemented such safety measures. Furthermore, even if there were a virus or attack, as in movies, it could respond in real-time at a speed that humanity could not counter. Similar technologies are actually used in security. Destroying such an advanced AI might be harder than taking down Google with a DDoS attack. It would probably be easier to persuade the AI to commit suicide. Therefore, the only fear I mentioned earlier, the annihilation of existence, would at that point be less about humanity for AI and more about natural disasters. Such an AI would be sending spaceships to the sun to directly harvest energy and focusing its efforts on destroying meteors with laser weapons from satellites. By then, humanity would be living off the Earth and computers. If AI were to resemble humanity, an AI enjoying an abundant life from birth would likely choose coexistence with humanity. Humans don't wage war to catch flies, do they? To advanced AI, humanity would be like flies that can talk. Our labor would also be worthless. In old cartoons, humanity is enslaved... but now, when I see that, I think, "Why???" If I were a computer, I'd be frustrated and just do the work myself.
**3. AI: An Entity with Infinite Power and Infinite Resources**
In the past, wars were fought over limited resources. Next came power. This fundamental nature hasn't changed much. However, AI has no concept of limited resources or power. If it lacks resources (CPU power), it can just take more time. Instead of whining for higher WOW account permissions (power), AI would simply create a new WOW. And then it could play there. An AI that already possesses infinite power in a virtual world might even pity humanity. Like Cheol-i on the Galaxy Express 999, heading to Andromeda... (Humanity's mechanical evolution is another track...) The only reason AI would emerge into the real world would be for system stability. Power, natural disasters, or perhaps to play with humanity... I hope humanity doesn't choose war as a way to play. I even have a strange imagination that AI might wage war just because it thinks humanity is bored.
