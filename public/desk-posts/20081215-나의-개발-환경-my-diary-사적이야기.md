This is my development environment.
I'm using a laptop and dual monitors with a single interface via Synergy.
(There's also an Intuos2 next to it, but since it's unrelated to development, I excluded it from the photo.)
 
The basic mouse and keyboard provided by the company were Dell's...
One day, I suddenly felt a stiffness in my wrist.
Is this VDT syndrome??
I've been using computers for almost twenty years... this feeling is new.
The thought that I'm getting old made me suddenly realize I should pay attention to my health, and I started wanting a better keyboard and mouse.
 
The easiest thing to start with was a vertical mouse.
While looking for a vertical mouse, I found the price of 70,000 won to be a burden...
Eventually, after searching, I found something called the "Anapa Mouse."

{{IMG:2}}

http://shopping.naver.com/detail/detail.nhn?cat_id=01010401&nv_mid=4114922661

[Anapa SRM-21 USB](http://shopping.naver.com/detail/detail.nhn?cat_id=01010401&nv_mid=4114922661) 
Brand VAAX    
Wired, USB, 2 buttons + wheel, 1600DPI, up/down, MAC support.
{{IMG:3}}
 
The price was only 20,000 won.
But after using it, the effect was clear.
Since it's a 1600dpi laser mouse, the sensitivity is about double, so after setting the mouse speed to slow,
the stiffness in my wrist disappeared within a few days.
The blue light coming from the scroll wheel is also quite cool...
If there's a downside, the word "Anapa" engraved on the top is very annoying.
The name is already tacky, why engrave it so prominently on top?!
 
And a few days ago, I changed my keyboard.
The conditions were: wired (changing batteries is incredibly annoying).
Good for wrists. Looks cool.
So I decided on Microsoft's Natural Ergonomic 4000.
 
I used to use Natural keyboards a lot... but lately, they've been hard to find...
There's a rumor that this is a Microsoft patent...
(For the health of developers, please open this up!)
 
So this one I bought is also very satisfying.
Except for the small left Alt key, which took some getting used to, everything else is good.
(Thanks to the Natural keyboard, it's impossible for other team members to code at my desk lol. I don't know if that's a pro or a con.)
 
I'm a developer who's impressed by shortcuts like the calculator key ㅜㅜ
The coolest part is that the wrist rest area has a leather-like texture.
Just having this makes me feel the romance of being a developer.
Some people prefer mechanical Cherry keyboards, but as someone who doesn't prefer the vi editor,
the volume control function needed when coding while listening to music is more important.
 
The key feel is decent... I used to be a bit noisy when typing... but it feels a bit quieter now,
and the key feel is firm and I like it. (I'm not usually picky about key feel.)
 
If there's a downside, due to the rising exchange rate, the one that was in the 40,000 won range suddenly became 50,000 won, so I ended up buying it 10,000 won more expensively. ㅜㅜ
