궁금해진다.
이놈의 영화는 감독이 대체 뭘 생각하고 만든건지 모르겠다.
다만, 지독히도 현실적인 그래픽과 더운 여름에 시원할만한 요소들..

드라마 기대해서 안돼구, 주제성 역시 기대해서는 안돼는것 같다.
공룡을 만들었던 실험실에서의 섬뜩한 장면이나..
마지막에 렙터에게 알을 돌려줌으로서 화해(?)를 하는 장면이나
"신의 섭리를 거스리는 짓은 악"이라고 주제의식을 표명하는듯
하지만..
마지막에 그 결과물인 초식동물을 보면서 감탄하는 짓거리나..
특히 마지막에는 미제국주의의 원산인 미해군과 해병대가 상륙하는
장면... 은 도대체 뭘 얘기하는지 모르겠다.

섬을 불바다로 만들 생각인가 ?

없었던것 같다.

#### [Jurassic Park 3](http://movie.naver.com/movie/bi/mi/review.nhn?code=30776)

Director Joe Johnston
Production 2001 USA, 0 min
**Rating**

Today was my day off from my part-time job, so I watched Jurassic Park 3 (at the theater) and The Emperor's New Groove (on video).

"Jurassic..." sounds like "Japanese-style," "Korean-style," "American-style," "hip-hop-style," "modern-style"...
Anyway, it's a bland movie that doesn't really make you think, "Wow, that was great!" after watching it. Neither 1 nor 2 did. --;
3 feels like a combination of 1 and 2. Those who have read the books *Jurassic Park* and *The Lost World* will know that scenes missing from the books appear in 3. Like the Pterodactyls... or the final scene with the US Marines deploying.
(Ultimately, all the islands are engulfed in flames.)
The consistent elements across 1, 2, and 3 are...
Women always cause problems...
And why are children always geniuses who solve problems? I'm curious about the director's leanings towards women.
I don't know what the director was thinking when he made this movie.
Only, the extremely realistic graphics and elements that would be refreshing on a hot summer day?
Don't expect drama, and don't expect a strong theme.
The eerie scenes in the lab where the dinosaurs were created...
Or the scene where they make peace (?) by returning the eggs to the raptors at the end, seemingly expressing the theme that "defying God's providence is evil"...
But then, marveling at the herbivores, which are the result of that defiance...
Especially the final scene where the US Navy and Marines, the origin of American imperialism, land... I don't understand what that's trying to say.
They returned the eggs, so they've "made peace," and then they're going to burn the island down next?
And then act as if that's a good thing... Would a true scientist tolerate wiping out such creatures with modern weapons?
Are the US military always cool, no matter what they do? --;
Even in the trailer for *Planet of the Apes* before this movie...
The scene where someone proudly says, "Where are you from?" "US Air Force!" (I'm disappointed that a Tim Burton movie has a scene like this... TT)
I tried to recall if the original *Planet of the Apes* had such a scene, but I don't think it did.
I remember a strange video I once saw where aliens conquered Earth, civilization disappeared, and humanity was enslaved. Later, humans learned how to operate their ancestors' weapons (US Marine Harriers) in a few days (learning to fly a fighter jet in just one week... Is it some kind of crash course? How much money and time does it take to train one pilot? --;)
And bravely destroyed the alien spaceship.
I had some regrets after watching Jurassic Park. Well, anyway, I enjoyed the cool screen and air conditioning while watching the movie, so I guess I'll have to be satisfied with that.

The Emperor's New Groove, first of all, as a Disney movie, its rhythm is outstanding.
It feels like a musical film, you could say... Although there were no scenes where characters directly sang (it's quite surprising for a Disney movie not to have that), the dialogue has a rhythmic pace.
And the characters' personalities, well, it ultimately became a story of good triumphing over evil, but the fact that they became quite eccentric might be a surprising development for Disney.
It feels like characters like the dragon or the horse from Mulan became the protagonists.
Maybe next, Disney animation will use Eddie Murphy as a voice actor, like Shrek's donkey...
Or a character as twisted as Bruce Willis.
Ultimately, I held onto these hopes, but the ending was, as expected, Disney.
It's not good to trample on children's dreams and hopes, so I'll just say it was okay.
I think it's time for Disney to change direction a bit.
