**1. 개인 컨설턴트 서비스의 발달**
21세기에 들어 최근 화두가 된것은 개인 컨설턴트 서비스입니다.  금융에서는 private banking이 뜨고 있고, 의료행위도 주치의 개념이 도입되고 있습니다.  산업이 발달함에 따라 경쟁력을 위해 금융과 의료 서비스가 다양해졌고, 따라서 개인이 선택할 수 있는 서비스 또한 선택의 폭이 넓어졌습니다. 고객을 위한 서비스들로 나온것이지만 그 다양성이 지나치게 증가해버리는 바람에 공부를 하지않으면 오히려 안하느니만 못한 상황이 와버렸습니다. 개인별로 최적화된 서비스를 찾아주는 개인화 서비스가 출현하게 된것이죠. 또한 범용 서비스의 한계가 가지는 문제점을 해결하기 위해서란 이유도 존재합니다. 산업이 점점 고객중심의 비즈니스 행동이 경쟁력이란 것이 증명된 영향이라고도 봅니다.

**2. IT에도 개인화 서비스가 필요한 시대가 도래**
그러나 점점 복잡해지고 다양해지는 산업분야가 있는데 바로 it입니다. 난 금융과 아무 상관없다고 말하시는 분들도 종이로 된 통장 하나즈음은 가지고 있듯이 it와 관계없다고 말하시는 분들도 핸드폰, 혹은 tv, 카메라, 자동차까지 어느하나 it기기가 아닌 것이 없게 되어가고 있습니다. 본래 it에 푹 빠져 지내는 분들이야 즐거운 일이겠지만, 별 상관없이 살던 분들은 무척 곤혹 스러운 일입니다. 그렇다고 it를 간과하기에는 그 기회비용이 꽤 큽니다. 요즘은 제 1금융권의 낮은 금리의 일반 통장 하나만 가지고 계신분은 거의 없을 것입니다. 기업의 경우도 마찬가지인데 it가 기업환경을 개선할 수 있는 기회에 대한 막연한 인식은 있으나 어떻게 할지 모르는 경우가 존재합니다. 그래서 나온것이 it 컨설턴트 기업들이고, 보다 적극적인 기업은 기업의 전략과 매칭하여 CTO를 도입하기도 합니다. 이러한 환경은 어느새 개인에게 까지 도달하기 시작했습니다. 개인의 많은 주요정보들이 다양한 it기기에 저장되기 시작했고, 하루가 멀다하고 나오는 신제품들과 신기술에 어리둥절합니다.
어떤 신기한 기기를 보고 저건 내게 필요할지 모른다고 생각합니다. 그러나 그 기기보다 더 적합한 기기가 있다는 것을 그 기기를 다 익히고 난 이후에나 알게 되는 경우도 있죠. 이는 결국은 디지털 친화도에 따라 정보격차로 바뀌게 될수도 있는 것이겠죠.

** 2) 관련 법, 제도 고민 필요**

**1. The Development of Personal Consultant Services**
In the 21st century, personal consultant services have become a recent hot topic. In finance, private banking is on the rise, and the concept of a primary care physician is being introduced in medical practice. As industries developed, financial and medical services diversified to maintain competitiveness, and consequently, the range of services individuals could choose from also expanded. While these services were introduced for the benefit of customers, their excessive diversification has led to a situation where, if one doesn't study them, it's almost worse than not having them at all. This is where personalized services, which find optimized services for each individual, emerged. Another reason for their appearance is to solve the problems inherent in the limitations of general-purpose services. I also see this as a result of the growing evidence that customer-centric business behavior is a competitive advantage in increasingly customer-focused industries.

**2. The Era Where IT Also Needs Personalized Services Has Arrived**
However, there's an industry that is becoming increasingly complex and diverse: IT. Just as those who claim to have nothing to do with finance still likely have a paper bankbook, those who say they have no relation to IT are finding that there's hardly anything that isn't an IT device, from cell phones to TVs, cameras, and even cars. While this is a joy for those who are deeply immersed in IT, it's quite perplexing for those who previously lived without much involvement. Yet, the opportunity cost of neglecting IT is quite high. Nowadays, hardly anyone only has a low-interest general savings account at a primary financial institution. The same applies to businesses; there's a vague awareness of IT's potential to improve the corporate environment, but often, they don't know how to go about it. This led to the emergence of IT consulting firms, and more proactive companies even introduce CTOs to align with their corporate strategy. This environment has now begun to reach individuals. A lot of personal key information is being stored on various IT devices, and people are bewildered by new products and technologies that emerge almost daily.
You might see a fascinating new device and think it could be useful for you. However, sometimes you only realize there's a more suitable device after you've fully mastered the first one. This could eventually lead to a digital divide based on digital literacy.

**5. Key Challenges in the Personal IT Consultant Market**
While I've listed the advantages above, are there no problems with such services? I'll discuss the issues I perceive.

**1) Concentration of personal information**
{{IMG:5}}
[Source: Personal Information Protection Act Legislative Notice, Kyunghyang Shinmun 2008.08.12]

Even if it's just device information, that device can contain critical personal information, and the device information itself might be considered personal information. MAC addresses would fall into this category, and they could be matched with a company's personal information and utilized. Of course, due to the Personal Information Protection Act, easy utilization is impossible, but the possibility of legal violations or accidental leaks still exists.

**2) Recommendation of affiliated company devices**
If LG provides the service, they will likely recommend LG products, or even just for ALYac installation, they might recommend devices from their affiliated companies. However, in my case, I prefer iptime for wireless LAN equipment and PC Green, which uses the Kaspersky engine, or the lightweight V3Lite for antivirus. Therefore, portals and various IT device companies will likely accelerate their efforts to partner with such personal IT consultant companies. **(Please, please don't change the start page... Don't change it to Daum when installing Powercom.)** In personal finance, FPs affiliated with specific financial companies also face such situations, and commissions might arise from this. This could limit the user's range of choices. That said, it would be better than choosing completely blindly, and a business model could be built in this way.

**3) Lack of experience, low awareness**
As this service concept has only recently begun, both the companies providing it and the customers will likely face many difficulties. The business model for the performing company will also be a major concern. How much profit can be earned from internet fees alone, and how much will labor costs be, are aspects that need consideration.

**6. The Path the Personal IT Consultant Market Should Take**
**1) Separation of service companies**
It seems necessary to separate the operations currently performed by TPS companies, even if they are subsidiaries. There's a saying that Samsung Electronics' AS Center can provide such good service because it's a different company from Samsung Electronics itself. This is because providing service is directly linked to performance. If not, service would be seen as a cost and expense within the company, leading to reluctance and even more severe recommendations for their own products or affiliated company devices.

**2) Need to consider related laws and systems**
New laws, such as those against unfair trade practices like recommending one's own products or affiliated company devices, might be necessary. However, these laws must be carefully crafted, as they could inadvertently destroy the business model and eliminate the market itself. Furthermore, the issue of personal information remains a key topic.

**3) Related Services, Technology Development**
Various services and new technologies may be required, such as remote PC inspection, repair, on-site visit processes, and SLAs.
