**Kendo**

{{IMG:1}}

The exercise I'm doing these days, it's been about 4 months since I started...? The bogu alone cost 620,000 won. ㅠㅠ 6 months of credit card debt.
2003.03.12**2015.08.06**
It was an exercise I wanted to try, and I wanted to look somewhat cool? I liked the symbolism of the sword, which is to cut something away. After that, during my wandering, countless blisters burst on the soles of my feet, and I broke many shinai. Kendo was an outlet where I could relieve my frustration, and I am still a proponent of it.

The problem is that now the children's daycare fees are a priority, and there are too many things I want to do, so it keeps getting pushed back.

Always saying "someday...", my bogu and sword have been stored away for a long time. I really plan to take them out again someday. When I have the time...

But what is this feeling that the older I get, the less time I have...?
