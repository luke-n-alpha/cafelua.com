I did nothing today.
In the morning, a bit of studying and
didn't do any of the company work I was assigned..
just enjoyed games in the club room.
And after coming home, listening to the songs Lee Soo-young remade,
ended up drawing another picture filled with useless emotions.

Old songs Lee Soo-young remade..
Songs I've liked since long ago..

But
only now.. do I understand these songs.
Though it's not a particularly well-drawn picture or well-written text,
I feel like only now can I create proper drawings and writings.

It has definitely changed..
My drawing style.
Shadows have fallen upon my foolish drawings that were always just light.
Thanks to that.. should I be grateful..?

It's truly ironic.
