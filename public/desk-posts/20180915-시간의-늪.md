Trapped in the swamp of time..
unable to pull my feet out of sticky, dirty emotions
I wish I could just cut down all living things,
and the rusty, creaking screws of my heart,
with a chainsaw that makes a metallic laughing sound,
and go completely mad like that..
Clearly.. unlike before, a part of my heart must have broken somewhere..
There's no going back, is there....
Ah.. so pathetic and annoying..... So loud..

2004. 01.11
