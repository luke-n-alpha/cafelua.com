I don't usually write reviews, and I don't often get review requests, but an interesting product review came in, so I gave it a try. The compensation for this review is just this one cable. I haven't received any other compensation.

The components are simple, aren't they?

{{IMG:1}}

Generally, when charging, **you often have to charge from a PC or car with a USB port instead of a dedicated charger, and you experience slow charging or even just a delay in discharge. This product brings good news that it can improve charging speed even in such cases.** I found it selling for less than 10,000 won in the market.

So, now that I've received it, I'll share the review results. Since the components are simple and it's just a cable, let's not worry about the design or anything like that. It's neither bad nor exceptionally good. The connector part feels slightly thicker than a standard bundled USB cable.

What's important is the charging speed. To check the charging speed, I first installed an app called Ampare on the Galaxy Zoom 2. The baseline consumption was measured at -450mA. This means it consumes about 450mA of power per hour. With a 2450mA battery, it would be fully depleted in 5.4 hours. This time is based on having the screen on for measurement. This time varies greatly depending on whether the screen is off, browsing, or gaming.

**1. It became as fast as a real charger.**
Measurement App: Ampare / Unit: mA

| Product | Charge | Cable | Device Consumption | Charge Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Galaxy Zoom 2 | - | -450 | | | |
| Galaxy Zoom 2 | Computer USB (2.0) | Davinci | 1200 | 1650 | Recognized as AC |
| Galaxy Zoom 2 | Computer USB (2.0) | Bundle | 450 | 900 | Recognized as USB |
| Galaxy Zoom 2 | 2.5A Charger | Davinci | 1200 | 1650 | |
| Galaxy Zoom 2 | 2.5A Charger | Bundle | 1200 | 1650 | |

The reason the charge amount was limited to 1650 seems to be due to a limitation within the Galaxy Zoom 2 device or the cable itself. It really did get faster. It became as fast as a 2.5A charger. A unique point is that it doesn't function as a data cable; it's only recognized as AC.

**2. What about power-hungry monster tablets?**
This time, I decided to test my Chinese x98 air3g tablet. Its characteristic is that even when plugged into a regular charger, not USB, the power doesn't charge; it actually drops. Although Bay Trail is low-power, it's an 8.9-inch screen with 2048x1536, which is beyond Full HD. For that reason, it's a device with extreme battery consumption. When I installed Ampare, it said some SDK wasn't installed, so I installed a "Battery monitor" app and measured again, finding it consumed -1400mA, three times the power of the Zoom 2. The 8500mA battery is useless. It can be used for about 6 hours. So, what are the results for the Davinci charging cable?

Measurement App: Battery monitor / Unit: mA

| Product | Charge | Cable | Device Consumption | Charge Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| x98 air 3g | -1400 | | | | |
| x98 air 3g | Computer USB (2.0) | Davinci | -350 | 1050 | Recognized as AC |
| x98 air 3g | Computer USB (2.0) | Bundle | -950 | 450 | Recognized as USB |
| x98 air 3g | 2.5A Charger | Davinci | 800 | 2200 | |
| x98 air 3g | 2.5A Charger | Bundle | 450 | 1850 | |

Measurement results... failed to reach +350mA. However, it was proven that the charge amount remained above 1A, as guaranteed by the seller. The only problem is that the tablet consumes more than 1A. Interestingly, the charge amount from the bundled charger also increased with the Davinci cable. Compared to the Zoom 2, this Chinese tablet probably doesn't have a device-level limitation.

**3. Conclusion**
**Compared to the bundled cable, an additional charge of approximately 700mA was confirmed.** That means it can achieve more than double the charging speed, if not triple. For around 10,000 won, I thought it would be great if such a cable were included as standard with smartphones costing hundreds of thousands of won. Especially given that Android's battery consumption varies widely due to various apps polling, it would be good to carry such a cable.

Even in these cases, charging can be twice as fast.
