전 폰을 상당히 많이 사용합니다. 특히 쿼티에 목을 메게된 이유는 이동중 무언가 쓰는것을 좋아하기 때문이죠. 옵티머스큐가 출시한지 얼마 안되었지만 제 액정의 보호필름은 이미 걸레가되었습니다. 가방에서 열쇄등과 아무렇지않게 굴리는 성격때문인것 같습니다. 이번에 아이폰 4를 구매하면서 범퍼를 구매하시는 것을 보고 저도 좀 폰을 소중히 여겨야겠다는 생각이들었습니다. 그래서 케이스와 필름을 다시 구매하기로 했죠. 한간에는 아이폰이 악세사리시장을 키웠다느니 하지만, 해외는 몰라도 한국은 학생을 중심으로 한 핸드폰 악세사리시장은 이미 꽤 큰 상태였죠. 핸드폰 diy샵과 핸드폰 악세사리 전문매장도 존재합니다.

I use my phone quite a lot. The reason I'm particularly drawn to QWERTY is because I like writing things while on the go. Although the Optimus Q hasn't been out for long, my screen protector is already a mess. It seems to be because of my habit of just tossing it around in my bag with keys and such. Seeing people buy bumpers when they got the iPhone 4 made me think I should also cherish my phone a bit more. So, I decided to buy a case and a screen protector again. Some say the iPhone grew the accessory market, but even if that's true overseas, in Korea, the mobile phone accessory market, centered around students, was already quite large. There are even DIY phone shops and specialized phone accessory stores.
At the gift show I went to last weekend, there were phone cases designed with traditional Korean patterns, but they were all for iPhones and Galaxy S... As expected, the Optimus Q is marginalized. ㅜㅜ

So, I searched the friendly Gmarket, which welcomes even minorities, and found pretty cases at good prices. What I bought was 2 screen protectors for 3500 won, a case for 10900 won, with free shipping. For just 10,000 won, it claimed to have a pretty design and even be made of leather (?). So, when I received it, I was very satisfied. Peeling off the old film and applying a new one felt like getting new glasses. However, upon closer inspection, there was a slight crack in the film from the start. It was much better than the previous 'rag,' and I decided not to worry about it, thinking I could just replace the 1500 won film again if it became a mess soon. Besides, I still have one sheet left. A mirror film was also included, perhaps as a free gift, but I don't use it, so I'll pass.

{{IMG:1}}

And the case, which claims to be leather, I debated between black and this one due to its somewhat feminine aesthetic, but I ultimately chose the 'Romantic Bear Brown' just because it was cute, and I love it.
{{IMG:2}}

The inside is wrapped in plastic, so it won't pop out like my previous 30,000 won x1 case did after some use.
{{IMG:3}}

And it attaches easily with a magnet, and I like the color.
I reconnected the DMB antenna to the strap, along with the wife doll I made at the gift show, although I don't watch it much. I used to drop it often because I was clumsy without a strap.
{{IMG:4}}
When folded, it looks like a small dictionary called 'Chic' that I used as a child. They say LG smartphone users are diligent men without much money, and I bought an accessory with a completely satisfying price-to-performance ratio.

Yesterday, I even had a 'lucky' (?) drop test. My wife threw it on the sofa, but it slid off a book placed on the sofa and fell to the floor. When it dropped, the phone popped out of the case, and the battery also detached. In fact, whether the battery detaches or not makes a significant difference in impact durability. Just as cars have crumple zones to absorb impact, the loss of the battery's weight when it detaches means a loss of impact energy. I thought iPhones didn't have detachable batteries due to design, but looking at the Galaxy S, it seems possible to have a detachable battery even with a slim design if done well. Given that the Optimus Q has a built-in QWERTY keyboard and this thickness, the iPhone's non-detachable battery increasingly makes me suspect it's a policy to sell more chargers and devices. Anyway, fortunately, when this case dropped, it broke into four pieces – case, phone, battery cover, and battery – which seems to effectively absorb the impact. Of course, it might vary depending on the situation, but I was once again satisfied with the Optimus Q.
