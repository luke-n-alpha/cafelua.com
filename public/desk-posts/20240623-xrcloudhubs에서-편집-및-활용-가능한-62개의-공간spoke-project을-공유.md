1. XRCLOUD(웹 메타버스와 Hubs)에 바로 활용 가능한 62개 공간을 공유합니다.

XRCLOUD (Hubs) provides a web editor. The default map is a meteor crater, where you can combine building kits or rock kits, or freely utilize various 3D assets from SketchFab. However, you might want to use a more well-designed space. Before Mozilla Hubs' subscription service ended, the Spoke editor featured spaces, and generally good template spaces were publicly available for free. However, since Mozilla Hubs' subscription service has now ended, it's difficult to find and use that data.

1. We are sharing 62 spaces that can be immediately utilized in XRCLOUD (Web Metaverse and Hubs).

In response, our customers requested usable 3D spaces, and on this occasion, we are sharing 15 spaces we created and 47 spaces that were publicly available under CCL3.0 in Hubs Spoke.
