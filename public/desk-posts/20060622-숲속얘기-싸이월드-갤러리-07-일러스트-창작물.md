심장병에 걸렸다.

죽을것 같이 괴로웠던 화학요법과

몇차례의 수술로 제거했던 이 종양은

가슴을 열어보니

다시금 자라있었다.

도대체 얼마나 아파해야 낫는걸까.

얼마나 고통이란 약을 먹고

가슴을 찢는 수술을 더 해야

너를 치료할수 있을까

너라는 병은 너무나도

힘들고 슬프다.

진료기록에는 깨알만한 글씨로 이렇게 적혀있다.

- 당신은 생명조차 위협하는 무서운 병에 걸렸습니다.

치료의 기간이나 완치도 확신할 수 없습니다.

더군다나 이 병은 다른 병과 너무나도 다릅니다.

대개의 병은 희망을 가져야 낫지만

이 병은 희망을 버려야만 합니다. -

하지만

.

.

.

인간이란 희망을 버리면 그 순간 죽는다.

I have heart disease.

This tumor, which I removed through several surgeries and agonizing chemotherapy that felt like death,
was growing again when they opened my chest.

Just how much more pain do I have to endure to get better?
How much more of this medicine called pain do I have to take,
and how many more chest-splitting surgeries do I need
to cure you?
This disease, which is you, is so
difficult and sad.

In my medical records, it's written in tiny letters:

- You have a terrible disease that even threatens your life.
The duration of treatment or complete recovery cannot be guaranteed.
Moreover, this disease is so different from others.
Most diseases require hope to heal,
but this disease requires you to abandon hope. -

But
.
.
.
Humans die the moment they abandon hope.

I've been listening to Canon Variations for a very long time.
A song I've enjoyed since childhood,
a song I really wanted to share with someone and talk about.
Listening to it...
I realize this song wasn't joyful from the beginning.
Having been brainwashed by this song for such a long time,
I even get the illusion that my fate might have been predetermined from the start.
Its sadly beautiful melody,
which resembles my heart just a little,
brings back that beach and that white smile like a documentary film.
That image is clearly a picturesque scene,
and a memory of the happiest moment.
That memory, of which I don't even have a photo,
is imprinted in my heart more vividly than any camera could capture.
Tired... sleepy... tired... sleepy... tired... sleepy... tired... sleepy... tired... sleepy... tired... sleepy...
Lately... I'm having a hard time.
I should at least try to put my mind at ease.
