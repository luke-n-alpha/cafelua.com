Do you know Kids Note, where memories from 20,000 Korean daycare centers and kindergartens accumulate? It's a service that was recently acquired by Daum Kakao, famous for KakaoTalk. It's said to be used in a whopping 40% of daycare centers and kindergartens nationwide. As incidents in daycare centers and kindergartens have been reported one after another recently, many parents are feeling anxious. However, for centers that use Kids Note, activity logs are regularly sent with photos and videos, and comments can be added to these posts, making it easy to communicate with the center and its teachers, or to check medication requests and meal plans. Since my son's daycare center also uses Kids Note, I receive daily push notifications. While working, I sometimes get a push notification and feel pleased to see that my son is doing well.

{{IMG:1}}

I don't know why he thought the toilet was "Dad's" ㅜㅜ Anyway, Kids Note has launched a service called Storybook, which I'd like to introduce. They can't keep providing such a good service for free indefinitely, can they? They can't just dig up dirt to do business... Kids Note Storybook is a service that turns the photos accumulated in Kids Note into an album. It's a real album, not an online one. I was lucky enough to be selected for the experience group, so I'm leaving a review.

First, what arrived looked like this...

Videos have QR codes embedded. You can use portal apps like Naver App or Daum App, or a dedicated reader app for QR codes. Please note that Kids Note does not have a QR code reader function. Since search has recently been integrated into KakaoTalk, I wonder if it will be added there soon, but I'll talk about that in a future post.

Anyway, if there's an advantage, it's that memories accumulated in a book are good to look at all at once. The actual service has now launched.

To find it, click "Photo Store" in "More," then select "Kids Note Storybook." There's also "Snaps Photo Store," but I haven't tried that yet.

Select child. Select theme. Select content to create.

And then, wait, and ta-da...

If you'd like to see a sample directly, check out the URL below. You can also create a sample and share it.
[http://kn.bollogbook.com/html/preview/preview_m.html#201507162211576211](http://kn.bollogbook.com/html/preview/preview_m.html#201507162211576211)

The most important thing: the price! For about 300 pages, it's 93,000 won... Based on my experience, I got 160 pages for about 3.5 months. If I had paid the actual price, it would have been around 60,000 won. Calculating at about 45 pages per month, that's 540 pages for a year. Approximately 167,000 won... That's a bit more expensive than I expected.

I think it would be good to offer discounts for monthly or quarterly subscriptions, or perhaps allow daycare centers and kindergartens to customize them, adding more of their unique character, and then sell them directly. If daycare centers could customize and sell them for 30,000 won every two months, I think teachers would actively promote sales.
