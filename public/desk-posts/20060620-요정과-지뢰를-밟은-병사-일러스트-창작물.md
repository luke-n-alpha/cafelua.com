The M14 plastic landmine, upon being stepped on, creates an immense blast that severs the ankle of the person who steps on it. It's nicknamed the 'ankle mine,' and its claim to fame is that by severing an enemy's ankle, it causes greater loss to their forces than by killing them. This is because injured soldiers require evacuation.

I've never actually seen one. --; I wasn't even in a frontline unit...
I just wanted to draw it, though...
