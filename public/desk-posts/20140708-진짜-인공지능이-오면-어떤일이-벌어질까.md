**1. 2014년 튜링 테스트를 통과하다.**"진짜 인공지능"이란 무엇일까요? 먼저 얼마전 튜링 테스트를 통과 했다는 소프트웨어가 드디어 나왔습니다. 무작위로 선정한 사람들 중 1/3이 상대방이 소프트웨어인지 사람인지를 구분 하지 못한다면 인공지능으로 인정받는다는 간단한 테스트입니다. 이 테스트에는 우크라이나에 사는 영어가 익숙하지 않은 초등학생이라는 설정 때문에 사람들은 더욱더 속았다는 지적이 있어, 진짜 인공지능인가? 하는 반론이 있고, 이를 해결하기 위해 더 정교한 테스트가 논의되고 있는 중이지만, 튜링테스트의 통과 자체가 기념비적인 일인 것 만큼은 분명한 사실입니다.

**2. 인공지능의 발달** 우리가 생각하는 인공지능은 아톰이나 그러한 로봇이지만, 실제적으로 학문에서 다뤄지는 "지능"이란 우리의 뇌가 사고하는 다양한 방식을 각각의 방법으로 모델링하고 접근해가는 방식으로 발전해가고 있습니다. "구별", "추론", "학습", "그룹핑", "기억", "연산", "언어", "음성/비전 인식", "감정" 등이 각각 발전해나가고 있는 방식이죠. 그리고 일부 인공지능은 인간을 훨씬 앞서 있습니다. 대표적인것이 기억과 연산이죠. 기억과 연산이 인공지능이 아니라고 하실 분들도 있겠지만, 어쨌든 뇌가 하고 있는 역할 중에 일부이며, 이를 모델링 한 "생각하는 기계"는 컴퓨터가 최초이자 마지막이며 나머지 영역에서도 진전이 있습니다. **3. 인공지능은 딱딱한 기계에 유연성을 더한다.** 인공지능은 사람의 단순한 두뇌 작업들을 대체하고 있습니다. 기억은 이미 말할 것도 없으며, 최근에는 자동으로 신문기사를 작성해주는 소프트웨어도 출현했고, 사람 보다 더 많은 기사를 쓰고 있습니다. 소프트뱅크에서 만든 페퍼라는 로봇은 클라우드 기반의 감정인식 인공지능 로봇입니다. 이미 많은 부분에서 기계들은 사람보다 앞서 있고, 사람의 일을 대신하고 있습니다. 그런 중에 인공지능은 그런 딱딱하게 일만잘하는 기계에서 사람과 커뮤니케이션 하는 기계로 발전해 나갈겁니다.

[ 감정을 최대로 억제한 벌칸족, 페퍼가 더 인간적임 ] **4. 인공지능으로 무엇을만들 수 있을까?** 아이들이 즐겨보는 꼬마버스 타요의 설정을 보면, 타요는 인공지능 칩으로 동작하는 자동차라고 합니다. 재밌는 것은 초등학생인 인공지능 유진 처럼 타요는 어린아이로 구현된 인공 지능입니다. 그리고, 버스기사가 없이 달리죠. 실제로 버스기사가 없이 달리는 무인 자동차는 구글에서 개발이 완료되었습니다. 구글의 무인자동차와 페퍼의 감정인식, 그리고 유진의 지능이 합쳐지면 그대로 꼬마버스 타요가 현실로 구현이 가능한 시대가 온 겁니다. 버스의 인공지능이 비록 어린아이라고 할지라도, 운전 만큼은 구글의 엔진이 담당하고 있으므로 운전이 미숙하지는 않습니다.

**1. Passing the Turing Test in 2014.**
What exactly is "true artificial intelligence"? First, a software program that recently passed the Turing Test has finally emerged. It's a simple test where if one-third of randomly selected people cannot distinguish whether their interlocutor is software or a human, it is recognized as artificial intelligence. There's a criticism that people were further deceived in this test due to the setup of the AI being an elementary school student from Ukraine who wasn't fluent in English, leading to the question, "Is it really true AI?" To address this, more sophisticated tests are currently being discussed, but the very act of passing the Turing Test is undeniably a monumental achievement.
{{IMG:1}}
**2. The Development of Artificial Intelligence**
The artificial intelligence we often imagine is like Atom or similar robots, but in academia, "intelligence" is actually developing by modeling and approaching the various ways our brains think, each with its own method. "Distinction," "reasoning," "learning," "grouping," "memory," "computation," "language," "speech/vision recognition," and "emotion" are all developing in their own ways. And some artificial intelligences are far ahead of humans. Representative examples are memory and computation. While some might argue that memory and computation are not artificial intelligence, they are nonetheless part of the brain's functions, and a "thinking machine" that models these is the computer, which is both the first and the last, and there is progress in other areas as well.
**3. Artificial Intelligence Adds Flexibility to Rigid Machines.**
Artificial intelligence is replacing simple human brain tasks. Memory is already a given, and recently, software that automatically writes newspaper articles has appeared, writing more articles than humans. Pepper, a robot created by SoftBank, is a cloud-based emotion-recognition AI robot. In many areas, machines are already ahead of humans and are taking over human jobs. Amidst this, AI will evolve from rigid, task-efficient machines to machines that communicate with people.

**4. What Can We Create with Artificial Intelligence?**
If you look at the setting of Tayo the Little Bus, which children love, Tayo is a bus that operates with an AI chip. What's interesting is that, like the elementary school AI Eugene, Tayo is an AI implemented as a young child. And it drives without a bus driver. In fact, driverless cars that run without a bus driver have been fully developed by Google. When Google's driverless car, Pepper's emotion recognition, and Eugene's intelligence are combined, the era has arrived where Tayo the Little Bus can literally become a reality. Even if the bus's AI is that of a young child, the driving itself is handled by Google's engine, so the driving is not unskilled.

[ Little Bus Gani was newly created by inheriting the memory chip of veteran bus 'One'. ]
The Turing Test is conducted without seeing faces. Also, when a specific field is narrowed down, artificial intelligence becomes very natural (as do humans, in fact), which is why software that writes articles in specific fields has been put into practical use. What about telemarketers who constantly converse with people on specific topics? Recently, there was a report that numerous telemarketers were about to lose their jobs due to a card security breach. What about artificial intelligence? AI that doesn't get tired and isn't subjected to emotional labor. It's clearly already right around the corner.
**5. Can Machines Replace or Dominate Humanity?**
Those who dream of dystopia imagine AI dominating humanity or an era where everyone loses their jobs. First, regarding the issue of dominating humanity, the inherent nature of artificial intelligence is still far from allowing us to speak of "self-realization" or "self" for AI. If complexity continues to increase, it's not impossible that we might someday see the first such software in our lifetime, but thinking about such an era is still too premature. Before that, the shock of the changes in the world created by software that humans perceive as human-like will be considerable. As I mentioned, countless simple jobs will disappear. And in fact, these things have continuously happened throughout the development of technology. While it might be frightening for those who cling to existing jobs or paradigms, I am confident that it will be a positive development for human society, and given the frequent news of M&A of AI companies by global corporations, it is already an unstoppable trend.
There is also a theory that by cooking food, humanity reduced the energy needed to digest nutrients, which increased the energy capacity available to the brain, thereby making humanity more intelligent.
I firmly believe that by entrusting these simple tasks to software, humanity will be able to do more productive and intelligent work. **I hope that humanity and society will be reborn to be more relaxed, allowing us to look around and reflect more on ourselves. While this might be an overly vague utopian ideal, I believe we can also devote more thought to those who are left behind with the remaining resources and surplus, and I think we must do so.**
**"The 21st Century Age of Artificial Intelligence" is truly coming.**
