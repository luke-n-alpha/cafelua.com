얼마전에 네이버 북스가 오픈한 이후로 종종 네이버북스의 만화들을 즐겨보고 있습니다. 아직 도서대여점에 비하면 종류가 그리 많지 않다는게 불만이지만, 꽤 즐거운 경험입니다. 그런데 만화를 보고 있는 저의 소비 형태가 뭔가 바뀌었다는것을 알아챘습니다.

처음에는 인기있는 단행본을 보다가 두번째는 보던 만화의 짤막한 후속편을 보고 있는 저를 발견했습니다. 사실 이러한 액션은 과거에도 없었던것은 아니었습니다. 도서대여점에서 재밌는 단행본을 빌려본 이후에는 그 다음 내용이 너무 궁금해서 p2p의 번역본들을 화별로 다운받아 보곤 했으니까요. 그런데 이렇게 미디어를 소비하면 이후에는 한꺼번에 모아서 나오는 단행본을 안보는 효과가 있었습니다. 그리고 이제 보니 그 액션을 동일하게 ebook에서 하고 있는 저를 발견한겁니다. 다른점은 합법적이라는 거죠. 그리고 이러한 액션이 기존의 도서 소비와는 뭔가 다른 점이 있다는 것을 알아챘습니다.

**1. 외면받는 종합지, 주목받는 독립컨텐츠
** ebook에도 동일하게 종합지가 있습니다.
{{IMG:2}}
**[ 아. 코믹챔프도 아직 나오는구나. ]**

가격은 1000원으로 책정되었더군요. 과거에 종합지를 봐야했던 이유는 만화가 종합지밖에 없었거나 단행본 이후의 내용을 볼수 있는 방법이 종합지밖에 때문이었습니다. 그러면서 의외로 다른 재미있는 만화를 발견하는 재미와 함께, 보지도 않는 만화를 덤으로 가져가야하는 부담이 있었죠. 그나마 종합지는 병원이나 이발소등에 비치되어 다양한 니즈를 가진 독자들의 다양한 니즈를 맞출 수 있었습니다. 그리고 한편으로는 종합지는 작가들이 유일하게 독자들과 만날수 있는 통로로 작가들을 압박하거나 편집자들에 의해 독자의 수준을 강요받기도 했습니다.
그러나 ebook에서는 그럴 필요가 없어졌습니다. 500원짜리 단행본에 비해서는 분량당 단가는 더 비싸지만 100원씩 화별판매를 하니 1000원짜리 묶음 종합지를 사야하는 부담이 없어진거죠.

**2. 소비의 증가
**그러나 소비의 총량이 줄어들었다는 생각이 들지는 않습니다. 위에서 말씀드렸듯이 분량당 단가는 단행본이나 종합지에 비해 낮지 않기 때문입니다. 결론적으로 컨텐츠의 매력에 빠지면 거기에 돈 쓰는데 아까워하지는 않습니다. 더군다나 영화에 비하면 얼마나 저렴한 문화생활즐기기 입니까? 때로는 좋은 만화나 좋은 책은 값비싼 4D영화보다 훨씬 오래고 좋은 만족감을 줍니다.

**3. 컨텐츠 발굴의 역할은 종합지에서 플랫폼을 이용하는 대중에게

Ever since Naver Books launched recently, I've often enjoyed reading comics on Naver Books. I'm still a bit dissatisfied that the variety isn't as extensive as comic rental shops, but it's quite an enjoyable experience. However, I've noticed that my consumption pattern for comics has changed somewhat.

{{IMG:1}}

**[ Although paid, comics that are easy to read during commutes ]**

At first, I found myself reading popular single volumes, and then later, short sequels to comics I was already following. In fact, this action wasn't entirely new. In the past, after borrowing an interesting single volume from a comic rental shop, I would often download translated versions chapter by chapter via P2P because I was so curious about what happened next. However, consuming media this way had the effect of making me not buy the collected single volumes later. And now, I find myself doing the exact same thing with ebooks. The difference is that it's legal. And I've noticed that this action has some differences from traditional book consumption.

**1. Ignored General Magazines, Noticed Independent Content**
Ebooks also have general magazines.
{{IMG:2}}
**[ Ah, Comic Champ is still being published. ]**

The price was set at 1,000 won. In the past, the reason to read general magazines was either because comics were only available in general magazines, or because it was the only way to see content beyond the single volumes. While there was the unexpected fun of discovering other interesting comics, there was also the burden of having to take home comics you didn't even read. At least general magazines were placed in hospitals or barbershops, catering to the diverse needs of readers with various interests. And on the other hand, general magazines were the only channel for authors to meet readers, sometimes pressuring authors or forcing editors' standards upon readers.
However, in ebooks, that's no longer necessary. Although the unit price per volume is higher compared to a 500-won single volume, selling chapters for 100 won each eliminates the burden of having to buy a 1,000-won bundled general magazine.
{{IMG:3}}
**[ Each chapter costs 100 won. Baek Dong-soo has a lot more content per chapter than Yeolhyeolgangho ^^ lol ]**
**[ Note: After volume 5 of the single volume, you should read from volume 8 of the serialization. ]**

Ultimately, as a system for independent, small-amount sales of general magazine content emerged, the user's need to buy these general magazines in bundles decreased.

**2. Increase in Consumption**
However, I don't think the total amount of consumption has decreased. As I mentioned above, this is because the unit price per volume is not lower than that of single volumes or general magazines. In conclusion, if you're captivated by content, you won't hesitate to spend money on it. Moreover, how affordable is it as a cultural activity compared to movies? Sometimes, a good comic or a good book provides a much longer and better sense of satisfaction than an expensive 4D movie.

**3. The Role of Content Discovery Shifts from General Magazines to the Platform-Using Public**
As I mentioned above, content discovery was previously handled by general magazines. However, now, platform-provided recommended content, popular content reflecting user trends, and finally, reviews for each content (Naver Books currently lacks this feature) perform the role that traditional magazines couldn't: discovering hidden good content and searching for content that suits me. Authors are evaluated by the public, not by editors.

**4. What Else Will Change? The Answer Lies in the Music Market.**
In fact, this movement isn't new to books. The music market has already been hit hard by this change. Singers no longer force songs into their albums just to meet playtime requirements. That change is the single album. And this change accelerates the speed and transformation of media. With #1 songs changing frequently even within a week, it has become difficult to tally rankings for TV music programs. Some, like 2NE1, even seem to strategically utilize this market by releasing songs every three weeks.
The book market will change similarly. Short, independent content reflects trends in real-time. And in fact, blogs and webtoons are already showing this movement online. These contents spread via SNS and real-time search trends. Every week, at a specific time, webtoons or specific comic/drama episodes appear in real-time search trends. This trend has already been detected. The transformation of the book market is already complete. So, what other changes will there be in the future?
Will cross-media like "I Am a Writer" (similar to "I Am a Singer" in the music market) emerge? The change has begun.
