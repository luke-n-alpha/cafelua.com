When I opened my eyes, I saw the sea. The surging sea. The pungent salty smell and wind.. and blue.. I lay there alone, swaying in the endlessly stretching blue. Just swaying rhythmically, as if I had been there for thousands of years.. A sky that had forgotten humanity, filled only with longing. I simply saw what the sea had seen.

2004.08.01
