I saw a post on my timeline about the webtoon 'Bastard,' rated for all ages, expressing concern that children might watch it. I also raise a child, but because I remember encountering a lot of media and arguing with my parents when I was young, my feeling upon seeing this post was '꼰대' (old-fashioned/boomer).

[**평범한 아빠의 네이버 고소 이유? 웹툰의 전체이용가 진실 – Daum 아고라** m.bbs3.agora.media.daum.net](http://m.bbs3.agora.media.daum.net/gaia/do/mobile/petition/read?bbsId=P001&objCate1=1&articleId=187769&pageIndex=1)

The gist of it is that, based solely on the overall plot and expressions, it's harmful and shouldn't be watched. They mentioned having elementary and middle school children. I also raise a child. If I were to show this webtoon to Saebom, she would probably find it boring at first. There's too much text for children below elementary school age to read. And they might say it's too stimulating and scary. However, big and small stimuli are everywhere. When reading children's books, the range of expression is the issue; there are stories that deal with the death of parents, and children say they are sad.

[**후레자식** '내 집안에 살인마가 산다.' 본격 세대갈등 잔혹 스릴러 comic.naver.com](http://comic.naver.com/webtoon/list.nhn?titleId=628997)

This is not because children don't know about murder or killing, but because it's not within their scope of understanding. The moment it enters their scope of understanding, it can be scary or confusing. **No child will see the title 'Bastard' and think, 'Oh, I should try murdering too.' If they do, you've raised them poorly. You've turned your child into a robot.** It's like thinking that if they play games, they'll go outside and start shooting guns. On the contrary, the more you block external stimuli, the less they can tolerate even small stimuli, or they exhibit overreactions. This phenomenon also occurs with chocolate or games. I wonder if they read the 'Bastard' webtoon from beginning to end. **Demanding an R-rating based only on a captured image from the text is like demanding an R-rating for a wrestling screenshot because it looks suggestive (and it's even a homosexual work!).** **It's about the confrontation with a murderer father and twisted love-hate, not about the child being a bastard because the father is a murderer. I can't give you the detailed plot as it might be a spoiler, but please read it once.** If you want to stop your child, first watch/read it thoroughly yourself. It's a webtoon that offers a lot to think about. **I believe that media which provides food for thought, like this, can be more educational than spoon-fed educational materials. In my case, I actually developed my thinking skills through such media rather than textbooks.** **At the very least, play the game and read the webtoon before making a judgment.** **And, the first thing to do is not to sue, but to have a conversation with your child. Find out what your child felt and learned. If there's a problem with that, then it might truly be an issue with the work itself. (However, don't use leading questions.)** **If you did that without doing this, you've acted 100% like a '꼰대'. You've lost 10 points of trust with your children.**
