1. 블로그음악

I wish there was a music player on Naver Widgets.
Things that could be used in that music player:
 
1.  Blog music
2.  Music files on the desktop
3.  Winamp internet radio
 
I wish it would allow listening to things like these.
 
I'm trying out a service called Qbox, which someone I know introduced to me.
It goes around blogs and mini-homepages, creates a music list, and plays it.
 
Of course, if such an application were to be integrated into Naver Widgets, it would undoubtedly face significant legal issues...
At the very least, allowing users to listen to music they have purchased...
 
From an honest user's perspective, it would be great if they opened the widget's API and allowed the creation of things like Qbox.
