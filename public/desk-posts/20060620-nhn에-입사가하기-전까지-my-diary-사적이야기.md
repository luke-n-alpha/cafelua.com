NHN에 입사가하기 전까지 시도를 해보았던 곳..

1. 넥* (서류 -> 온라인필기시험 -> 면접  -> 불합격)
2. S*커뮤니케이션즈 (서류 -> 시험.면접  -> 불합격)
3. 넥* 모바일 (서류 -> 면접 -> 불합격)
4. L*전자 (서류 -> 시험  -> 불합격)
5. NHN (서류 -> 필기시험 -> 1차면접 -> 2차면접 -> 합격 )

여태껏 다녔던 직장에는 비밀이 었지만, 이렇게 저렇게 많은 시도를 해보았네요.
고민도 많았고.. 서류 썼던데는 물론 더 많았죠.

어떻게 보면 가장 좋은곳에 들어가게 되어서 잘되었다는 생각도 듭니다.

사실, 올해 11월에 결혼을 앞두고, 이직이란것을 반쯤 포기하고 있었습니다만,
아무생각없이 지원했던 NHN의 서류가 통과되고, 필기시험 일정이 토요일로 잡히면서

어차피 내 주제에 3차 면접까지 있는 그러한 바늘구멍에 시도해보기 싫다고 했었는데
여자친구의 권유에 의해 보았던 필기시험.

그 이후로 기대도 않게 필기 합격,
그리고 1차면접 합격.  그때부터는 욕심이 나기 시작하더군요.

그리고, 결국 (개발직군은 3차면접이 없슴) 2차면접합격이 되어서 입사결정이 되었습니다.

이제부터 시작인것 같습니다.
어렵게 간만큼 열심히 해야겠죠.

Places I tried before joining NHN...

1. Nex* (Resume -> Online Written Test -> Interview -> Failed)
2. S* Communications (Resume -> Test & Interview -> Failed)
3. Nex* Mobile (Resume -> Interview -> Failed)
4. L* Electronics (Resume -> Test -> Failed)
5. NHN (Resume -> Written Test -> 1st Interview -> 2nd Interview -> Passed)

It was a secret at all my previous workplaces, but I tried many things this way and that.
I had a lot of worries... and of course, there were many more places where I submitted my resume.

In a way, I think it turned out well because I got into the best place.

Actually, with my wedding coming up in November this year, I had half given up on changing jobs,
but then my NHN application, which I submitted without much thought, passed the resume screening, and the written test was scheduled for a Saturday.

I had initially said I didn't want to try for such a needle-in-a-haystack opportunity with a 3rd interview, given my circumstances,
but I took the written test at my girlfriend's urging.

After that, unexpectedly, I passed the written test,
and then the 1st interview. From then on, I started to get ambitious.

And finally, (there's no 3rd interview for development roles) I passed the 2nd interview and was decided for admission.

It feels like this is just the beginning.
Since I got here with such difficulty, I'll have to work hard.
