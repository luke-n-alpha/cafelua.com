**It's Okay to Be Alone**

{{IMG:1}}

I think it's okay to be alone. After all, a living human being has no choice but to pass through important doors alone...

2002. 12. 03
2015. 05. 14

Around this time, I seem to have reached the stage of resignation and self-rationalization as a solo person.

Looking back now, I wonder if it wasn't, paradoxically, a time when I was saying I was ready to love.
