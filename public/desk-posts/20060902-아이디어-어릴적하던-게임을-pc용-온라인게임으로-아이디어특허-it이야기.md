1. Playing House
 - When I was young, playing house was a quintessential children's game.
 I'm not sure if kids play house these days, but what if we made an online version of it?
 Where you take on the role of a mom or dad, raise children, cook meals, and decorate a house together.
 It might become a kind of avatar-raising game...
 
 I briefly joked about it with my teammates...
 It could even become an adult version.. ^^;
 Let's just say if you hold hands and sleep, a stork brings a baby. Haha.
 
 It might be similar to The Sims Online, but anyway, a game like that would be fun.
 
2. Territory Game (땅따먹기)
 - Was it in high school...? I once thought about making a game out of 'territory game' (땅따먹기), but still, no one has created it.
 It would be fun to implement childhood games like 'territory game' and 'donkatsu' into a video game.
 Even a Flash game would be fine.
 Territory game is somewhat similar to 'Alkkagi' (a Korean game played with Go stones), but if you expand on it, it could be made into a real-time game where you capture certain areas.
 
 If we think of it as a publishing game,
 First, flicking stones could be similar to 'Dangolwang' (another Korean game).
 If you flick a stone 3 times and it returns to your land, that becomes your territory. To prevent stalling, a time limit would be imposed,
and if the time limit is exceeded, the turn passes. Adding game elements like 'ppeom' (a hand span), special stones, or events triggered when specific areas are captured,
there would be many things to include.
