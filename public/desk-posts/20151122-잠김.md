My heart cried all day long again.
Even though my waking hours are gradually decreasing,
my fatigue is steadily increasing.
The taillights of late-night cars look like ghosts from another world, unrelated to me,
and the light love song on the radio becomes a funeral dirge for me.
My stomach burns, and my eyes gradually long for darkness.
All that welcomes me is
bitter instant coffee
and a foolish machine that can only add.
I'm sinking. Sinking deep into the darkness,
and I'm firmly locked in darkness.
At the end of the stars in that distant sky, will there be a place for me?
Will that person be there
to comfort a slightly weary me?

2003. 07. 01

At that time, I did something very foolish alone, suffered, and gradually sank deeper into despair. Even before I fell in love, I wrote some clumsy pieces, but they were entirely based on imagination. Everyone feels loneliness, but the loneliness felt when in love was different in degree. Some trace the origin of love to the mother-child relationship. I agree with this to some extent. The despair and sense of loss at that time were comparable to a child losing their mother. A very young child dies if they lose their parents.
**2015.11.22**
