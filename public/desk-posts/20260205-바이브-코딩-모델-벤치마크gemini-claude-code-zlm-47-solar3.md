**1. (흥미로운 결과) 최신 모델이 항상 좋은건 아니다. **

​구글의 Gemini와 upstage의 Sloar 모두 같은 결과를 나타냈습니다. 모델 최적화에 도움이 되시라고 벤치마킹 raw 데이터도 함께 공개했으니 활용해주시면 감사하겠습니다.​

**2 (한계) Hard suite조차 변별력이 떨어진다는 겁니다.**

​이미 최상위 모델은 보통 개발자 수준을 훨씬 넘었으며, 이제 경쟁은 단순 알고리즘 푸는 능력이 아니란걸 의미합니다. 이 부분을 더 고민해야 하는게 캐러티의 숙제 입니다. 이에 대한 기반을 마련하기 위해 다음 벤치마킹도 준비중입니다.​

At Careti, a Korean open-source-based coding agent that domestic developers prefer and Careti primarily supports, we conducted a practical model comparison of Gemini, Claude Code, Zlm, and Upstage by Dokpamo. Instead of solving problems in one go, we provided errors again to allow the models to solve them, creating a benchmark that more closely reflects an agent's performance. We also identified interesting results and test limitations.

**1. (Interesting Result) The latest models are not always the best.**

Both Google's Gemini and Upstage's Solar showed the same results. We have also released the benchmarking raw data to help with model optimization, so please feel free to use it.

**2. (Limitation) Even the Hard suite lacks discriminative power.**

Top-tier models already far exceed the average developer's skill level, meaning that competition is no longer about simply solving algorithms. Careti's task is to further deliberate on this aspect. To lay the groundwork for this, we are preparing the next benchmark.

**3. (Go, Korean AI!) Finally, as we plan to continue providing such data, we look forward to the improvement and development of domestic Dokpamo models. We will contribute with data and experiments. We appreciate your continued interest.**
