It's not yet certain if this is a scam, but in case others have experienced similar issues, I'm sharing this online first as it appears to be a fraud case, so they can check.
Mediation requested at [http://www.spayment.org/](http://www.spayment.org/mypage)
**Incident Summary ****1) August 17, 2013, 18:02 PM: SMS received for an unsolicited payment** ----- Content --(Go Animation) Super Jackpot Event 16,500 won payment complete Inquiry 050-5300-9998

Since there was no information about 'Go Animation' when searching online, I suspected smishing and did not call the number.

**2) September 13, 2013, 14:30 PM: Received August mobile payment details from KT**
Confirmed one unused transaction and requested KT to inquire about the company.
14:41 PM: Received the company's phone number from KT (1544-5553)

**3) Company Unreachable*** 1544-5533 busy ** Online ****Searching 1544-5553 revealed numerous posts about "InfoHub" auto-payment scams... *** 050-5300-9998 busy* No information about Go Animation found online

**4) Request for confirmation to KT**
KT said they would request the company to contact the customer within 3 days, and advised visiting the police station in case of fraud ㅡoㅡ

* Upon searching, I found an article about billions in damages from auto-payments affecting 130,000 people in Korea on August 28, 2013 [http://www.nocutnews.co.kr/Show.asp?IDX=2598152](http://www.nocutnews.co.kr/Show.asp?IDX=2598152)

**5) However, given the high possibility of damage, I first searched for ways to report it**
Found a method to request mediation at [http://www.spayment.org/](http://www.spayment.org/mypage)** KT, surprisingly, did not inform me about such a mediation request method. {{IMG:1}}**

** More than anything, I think it's important to identify the vulnerabilities in SMS micro-payments, and if these vulnerabilities are exposed, then users who were unaware should be found and compensated. It seems like elderly people would be helpless victims. By the way, it's only been 3 months since I changed my KT phone, could my personal information have been leaked? ㅡoㅡ To make an SMS payment, one needs to know the phone number, resident registration number, and telecom provider. Of course, there are only three telecom providers, so... let's assume that.**
