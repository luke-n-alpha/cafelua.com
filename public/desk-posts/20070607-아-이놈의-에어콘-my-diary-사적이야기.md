For me, someone with allergic rhinitis, air conditioning is a deadly weapon.
Sometimes it's even harder than winter.

And of all places, my seat seems to be located closer to the air conditioner...
It might be a privileged complaint, but I wish they'd turn down the air conditioner a bit...
But then, wouldn't the people further away get hot?

Anyway, summer is summer, and winter is winter,
I often have a hard time because of my nose.

My nose is raw and stinging...
Tissues pile up... .ㅡㅡ;
