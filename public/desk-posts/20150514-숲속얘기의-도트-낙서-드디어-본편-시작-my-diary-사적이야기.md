Stories of meeting my wife between 2002 and 2005. Everyone feels their own story is the most profound, but I've been gradually publishing doodles from 14 years ago, when I wandered the most, in Naver Post format. At the time, these drawings made me famous, getting featured on Cyworld's main page and becoming a best mini-homepage. Personally, I felt it was a waste for such precious content to just disappear, so I'm trying to reprocess it once again.

It's content like a picture diary from back then, combined with retrospective content from now. This 25th installment is a drawing I made two weeks after meeting my wife. Before meeting and after meeting... and my subsequent blunders. It feels like the main story has begun, so I'm leaving a record of it on my blog too. I'm not sure if it's okay to post this here for many people to see. From now on, it'll only be embarrassing stories.. ^^
