이번 정부들어 가장 유명한 구호라면 바로 창조경제 일겁니다. 창조경제에 대해 비아냥 거리는 이야기들도 많고 그 실체에 대해 말들이 많기는 하지만, 아이디어와 창조력이 중요한 시대임을 부정하는 분들은 그다지 없을 겁니다. 물론 대박의 꿈을 이룬 많은 기업들이 아이디어를 현실화 할 수 있는 능력을 갖추었던 SW기업들이고 해당 기업들이 상당히 많은 시행착오와 노력, 그리고 운등이 복합적으로 작용했다는 것 까지 말씀을 드리지 않아도, 그 시작은 아이디어란 것이 없었다면 존재조차 하지 않았을 것입니다.
그러한 아이디어, 곧 지식에 대한 중요성의 기치 아래에 정부 주도로 '창조경제타운'이라는 사이트가 있습니다. 일종의 아이디어 포털 같은 형태인데요. 아이디어들을 올리면 해당 아이디어를 사업화까지 연결 시켜주기 위해, 초기 투자자금을 대주고, 멘토링과 인프라를 제공하는 서비스를 하고 있습니다. 해외에서는 킥스타터나 인디고고등의 크라우드펀딩등이 유행하고 있다면, 국내에서는 여전히 많은 부분이 정부에 의한 자금이 스타트업에 큰 역할을 하고 있는 것이 분명한 사실입니다. 사실 국내에 와디즈같은 크라우드 펀딩 업체가 있긴 하지만, 시장 자체가 협소해서 크라우드 펀딩 만으로 사업을 진행한다는 것은 쉬운일이 아닙니다. 그렇기에 창조경제타운 같은 사업이 매우 중요한 역할을 할 수 있다고 할 수 있겠죠.
4월 20일 부터 미래창조과학부와 중소기업청이 공동개최하는 '2015 창조경제 대상: 아이디어. 창업경진대회' 가 시작합니다. 거창하긴 하지만, 간단히 말해 창업대회죠. 아이디어를 가지고 있다면 사업을 시작할 수 있다는 내용이 아주 매력적이게 들리지 않나요? 한편으로는 저는 천국과 지옥을 오가는 체험을 할것이라고 말씀드릴 수 있지만, 그 체험은 상당히 값 질 수도 있다고 감히 말씀드릴 수 있습니다.
조건과 내용은 아래와 같습니다.

사실 창업지원이 대부분 도심지역을 중심으로 많이 이루어집니다. 특히 제가 주로 다루는 SW의 경우, 거의 경기 서울권이라 해도 무방할 정도죠. 그러나, 이 프로그램의 경우에는 지역의 기업과 연계하는 프로그램으로 SW외의 기업들과의 연계가 주요한것 같습니다. 하지만, "아이디어"라고 생각하면, 요즘은 IoT등을 떠올릴테니, 사실 SW를 뺀다면 거의 시체인 사업이라고 할 수 있을겁니다.
여러분들도 생각해둔 좋은 아이디어 있으시면 속는셈 치고 한번 도전 해보시죠.특히 학생들이라면 도전을 적극 추천드립니다.

The most famous slogan of the current administration is probably "Creative Economy." While there are many cynical remarks about the creative economy and much debate about its true nature, few would deny that we live in an era where ideas and creativity are paramount. Of course, many companies that have achieved great success are software companies with the ability to turn ideas into reality, and it goes without saying that these companies benefited from a complex interplay of numerous trials and errors, effort, and luck. However, their very existence would not have been possible without an initial idea.

Under the banner of the importance of such ideas, or knowledge, there is a government-led website called 'Creative Economy Town'. It's a kind of idea portal. When ideas are submitted, the service provides initial investment funds, mentoring, and infrastructure to help commercialize them. While crowdfunding platforms like Kickstarter and Indiegogo are popular overseas, it's clear that in Korea, government funding still plays a significant role for startups. Although there are crowdfunding platforms like Wadiz in Korea, the market itself is small, making it difficult to proceed with a business solely through crowdfunding. This is why initiatives like Creative Economy Town can play a very important role.

Starting April 20th, the '2015 Creative Economy Grand Prize: Idea & Startup Competition,' co-hosted by the Ministry of Science, ICT and Future Planning and the Small and Medium Business Administration, will begin. While it sounds grand, it's simply a startup competition. Doesn't the idea of being able to start a business if you have an idea sound very appealing? On the one hand, I can tell you that you'll experience a journey between heaven and hell, but I dare say that experience can be incredibly valuable.

The conditions and details are as follows:
**◇ (Eligibility) Prospective entrepreneurs (teams) or entrepreneurs (teams) established within 3 years (after Jan 1, 2012) (No age limit)** - Excludes winners (teams) who have received cumulative prize money of 30 million KRW or more from other competitions with the same or similar items.
**◇ (Application Method) Creative Economy Town ([www.creativekorea.or.kr)](http://www.creativekorea.or.kr)/) website**
◇ (Schedule) Announcement & Recruitment (**Apr 20 - May 20, '15**) ⇒ Regional document & preliminary screening (May-June) ⇒ National main screening (July) ⇒ Grand Finale screening (early Aug) ⇒ Awards ceremony (late Aug)
◇ (Benefits) - Regional preliminary round qualifiers: Approx. 5 million KRW in support funds, intensive mentoring from Creative Economy Innovation Centers, etc. - Final winners: Awards*, exhibition at the Creative Economy Expo (scheduled for late Nov), investor connections, linkage to public and private startup support programs, marketing and sales channel support for creative products, etc. * Presidential Award (100 million KRW), Prime Minister's Award (30 million KRW), Minister of Science, ICT and Future Planning Award (10 million KRW, 2 winners), Small and Medium Business Administration Administrator's Award (10 million KRW, 2 winners), etc.{{IMG:1}}
First, the good news is that there's no age limit. In other words, if you haven't received money from other government programs and have a good item, it's worth looking into. 100 million KRW for the top winner doesn't seem like a huge or small amount. The Administrator's Award feels more like a 'good effort' recognition. In fact, the most important aspects of this program are the non-monetary support, such as mentor assistance and investor connections, and the title of being a winner. Therefore, it's unreasonable to evaluate this program solely by the prize money.

Startup support is mostly concentrated in urban areas. Especially for software, which I mainly deal with, it's almost exclusively in the Gyeonggi-Seoul region. However, this program seems to focus on connecting with regional businesses, including those outside of software. But if you think of "ideas," these days, IoT and similar concepts come to mind, so a business without software would practically be a dead end.

If you have a good idea, why not give it a try, as if you have nothing to lose? I especially recommend it to students.

I'm also thinking of putting some of my own ideas out there on Creative Economy Town. After all, I don't have the ability or conditions to execute them myself, and some of them have public-interest elements, plus they're pretty good ideas. Who knows? Maybe some good people will pick it up and make it a reality... ^^
