{{IMG:2}} Gently peeling off the product... just this? Is this excessive packaging?! {{IMG:3}} Oh. What is this? Where am I supposed to use it? Is it a keychain? The product's concept is to create a loop on the back of a smart device. It's a product for clumsy people like me who often drop their devices on the floor, conducting heartbreaking durability tests, by slipping a finger through the loop. ㅜㅜ So, for someone like me, a Defy would probably be the best fit...

Looking closely at the guide photos, it seems it can also be used to prop up a smart device, depending on the attachment location. But I use a wallet-style case for my Galaxy S2. Although this wallet case is already worn out... Most people use leather pouches, wallet-style cases, or plastic bumper cases, and I thought, "Not many people would want to stick this on their case..."

Then, I thought about attaching it to my 7-inch Flyer, which I proudly carry around. Whenever we went to a restaurant, the last resort to soothe Saebom, who would get restless, was to play Pororo or Juniver on the tablet. Each time, it was quite inconvenient, having to prop it up with a napkin case or something, and I thought this could solve that problem. I chose white and initially attached it to the center, just like the iPad in the manual photo.

{{IMG:4}} I just stuck it right in the middle without much thought.
Soon, the drawbacks came pouring out... While drafting a post vertically on Evernote on the bus, the hand holding it couldn't use its fingers. Crucially, the Smart Grip attached in the middle couldn't prop up the 7-inch tablet. Being in the center, it just became a seesaw, and while it wasn't lying flat, the angle was too low. ㅡㅡ;

So, a second attempt. I peeled it off and reattached it, shifted to one side.{{IMG:5}}

Finally, satisfaction increased! While holding it, my left thumb was positioned above the screen.{{IMG:6}} Most importantly, it stands up stylishly. I used to mobilize napkin holders and water cups to prop up the tablet at restaurants, but now that creativity is no longer needed. The angle is good, and the design matches well.{{IMG:7}} For frequent 7-inch tablet users like me, it seems like a good choice. One could buy a smart cover, but they are quite expensive, and the product selection is narrow, so a simple idea product like this doesn't seem bad. There are various colors: white, black, pink, blue, lime (yellow), and pearl, so choose one that matches your device.
