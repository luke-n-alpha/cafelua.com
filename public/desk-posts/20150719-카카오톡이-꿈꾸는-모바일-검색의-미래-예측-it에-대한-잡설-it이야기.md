최근 카카오톡이 검색사업을 시작했습니다. 제가 오래전에 이미 카카오가 언젠가는 필연적으로 검색 사업에 뛰어들 것이라고 예측했었습니다. 그러나, 모바일 검색이 사실 PC검색 만큼 만만한 것은 아닙니다. 모바일 검색광고는 PC광고대비 그렇게 낙관적인 시장이 아니기 때문입니다. 사용자가 쿼리를 입력하기 매우 불편한 환경이고, 화면 또한 매우 좁기 때문에 검색광고가 PC대비 썩 좋은 시장은 아니라고 할 수 있습니다. 그러나, 분명한 점은 사용자들의 PC사용 보다는 모바일 사용시간의 비중이 늘고 있다는 점이고, 거실에서 조차 PC앞에 앉기보다는 테블릿을 통해 가볍게 정보를 습득하는 사용자들도 늘었다는 점입니다. 따라서 기존의 PC검색 시장이 한계에 봉착한 것 만은 분명합니다. 그러면 카카오톡은 향후 어떻게 검색을 풀어갈까요? 잠깐 그 얘기를 하고 가겠습니다.
** 1. 대화창에 바로 샵 검색, 카드뷰 기반 섹션 검색** 현재 카카오톡은 대화창에서 샵을 누르면 바로 검색이 가능합니다. 또한 재밌는 것은 검색 결과를 카드뷰로 보여주면서 밀어서 각 섹션 검색 결과를 볼 수 있다는 점입니다. 메인 메뉴의 세번째인 채널에서는 기존의 포털형의 스크롤 방식을 유지하고 있지만, 샵검색의 경우에 훨씬 깔끔한 형태를 보여주고 있습니다. 어느쪽이 정답인지는 아직 모르겠습니다. 다만 카카오톡은 두가지 검색 결과에 대한 데이터를 가지고 어느쪽이 더 모바일 사용자에게 적합한지 혹은 사용패턴 별로 다를지를 분석해볼 수 있겠죠. 검색결과를 공유한다는 점은 한명의 검색 결과가 다수에게 전파될 수 있음을 의미합니다. 따라서 일반적인 검색 결과보다 더 높은 효과를 가질 수 있다는 의미도 될겁니다. 더군다나 도달율이 96%가 넘는 카카오톡은 전국민이 다쓰는 모바일상에서 가장 오래쳐다보는 검색 창이라고 할 수 있습니다.
여기까지는 다 아는 얘기죠. 그러나, 조금 더 다른 이야기를 해보겠습니다. 카카오톡이 최근 만들고 있는 o2o 라인업에 관한 이야기입니다. 물론, 아래 내용은 다음 카카오의 공식 발표가 아닌 제가 그려본 소설임을 참고하고 읽어주십쇼.**

KakaoTalk recently launched its search business. I had predicted a long time ago that Kakao would inevitably enter the search business someday. However, mobile search is not as easy as PC search. This is because the mobile search ad market is not as optimistic compared to PC ads. The environment for users to input queries is very inconvenient, and the screen is also very small, so search advertising cannot be considered a very good market compared to PCs. However, what is clear is that the proportion of users' mobile usage time is increasing compared to PC usage, and even in the living room, more users are acquiring information lightly through tablets rather than sitting in front of a PC. Therefore, it is clear that the existing PC search market has reached its limits. So, how will KakaoTalk approach search in the future? Let's talk about that briefly.
**1. Shop Search Directly in Chat Window, Card View-Based Section Search**
Currently, KakaoTalk allows immediate search by tapping the '#' (shop) button in the chat window. What's also interesting is that search results are displayed in a card view, allowing users to swipe to see results from different sections. While the third main menu item, 'Channel,' maintains the traditional portal-style scrolling method, the '#' search presents a much cleaner format. It's still unclear which approach is better. However, KakaoTalk can analyze data from both types of search results to determine which is more suitable for mobile users or if they differ by usage pattern. The ability to share search results means that one person's search result can be disseminated to many. This also implies that it can have a higher impact than typical search results. Furthermore, with an reach rate exceeding 96%, KakaoTalk can be considered the search window that the entire nation, using mobile devices, looks at the longest.

So far, this is all common knowledge. However, let's talk about something a little different. It's about the O2O lineup that KakaoTalk is currently building. Of course, please note that the following content is a fictional scenario I've envisioned, not an official announcement from Daum Kakao.
**2. My Predicted Kakao O2O Search Model**
Daum Kakao recently acquired Kim Ki-sa (a navigation app) for 62 billion won. They are also aggressively pushing Kakao Taxi, and Kakao Pay seems poised for a major showdown with Naver Pay in offline payments this year. As an example of O2O, Lineable, a child-tracking bracelet I once introduced, uses crowd GPS based on installed apps. Also, if you install the CU convenience store app, a pop-up appears with various benefits every time you enter a CU store. Recently, everything from laundry, car wash, to mobile phone battery charging... almost all aspects of daily life are gradually moving online, based on apps, to deliver services wherever you are. The characteristic of these businesses is that they are based on location and apps. What all companies wishing to engage in O2O business desire most is for all users to have their app installed. What does this mean? In the past PC era, when starting a business, companies would create a homepage and register their businesses with search engines like Naver, allowing users to receive services through search. That was the very foundation of the search business. But what about the mobile era? Do we have to register mobile web pages again? If we use mobile web, can we receive beacon services? It's impossible to solve this with just the existing search ad model of the web.

This point means that the mobile search market could be different, and this is where KakaoTalk's search opportunity lies. KakaoTalk can become both a search platform and an app platform for all companies wishing to engage in O2O business.
For example, let's say I type 'hand car wash' from my current location. Currently, car washes offering hand car wash services are marked on the map. But what if Yper, which I introduced before, is available in the service area?

**3. So, when will Kakao Search be considered a success?**
When did Naver enter its period of success? I believe it was when TV commercials started saying, 'Type OOO into the Naver search bar.' All advertisers placed ads on Naver and urged users to type something into that search bar. Kakao Search will be similar. However, its location won't be TV, but rather offline stores. After saying, 'Search for OO on KakaoTalk,' it will provide additional coupons or value to users who make payments or share with friends through it. Thus, I believe the moment advertisers start promoting it themselves will be the point when search advertising succeeds. Will we see messages like 'Search for OO on KakaoTalk' or NFC tags encouraging Kakao search while walking around? By this time next year, won't there be some resolution? I wonder if we can look forward to a strong counterattack from Daum Kakao.
