First, I'll briefly discuss the features of these three apps, and then focus on Polaris Office, which I was asked to review, to examine the current state of Android productivity tools.

| Price | Free | Paid (24,900 KRW) | Free |
| Cloud | Polaris's own Cloud, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, WebDAV, UCloud | 1Drive (links with Google Drive, Dropbox, Facebook, Evernote, etc.) | OneDrive, Office 365 |

As you can see from the table above, the biggest difference between MS and Korean companies is that MS aims to dominate the Windows platform. Also, the inability to write PowerPoint files is a significant difference at present. The biggest difference between Hancom and Polaris is the distinction between paid and free. Polaris's business model is based on premium services, while Hancom maintains a package sales model. If your purpose isn't to edit HWP files, I believe using the free version of Polaris is perfectly fine. The premium service also offers a basic free capacity of 100MB, and you can connect to other cloud services as much as you want, so Polaris should essentially be considered a free office suite. For reference, Hancom Office's 1Drive is a separate service unrelated to Microsoft's OneDrive. The advantage of 1Drive seems to be its integrated linkage with other clouds and services (I haven't used it, so I can't speak from experience).
