1. Purpose of Disclosure

South Korea uses two word processors. One is Hancom Office, used in public institutions and schools, and the other is Microsoft Word, used by the private sector. Recently, Google Docs has also been used, not prioritizing format, and in response, Naver released Naver Word using Synapsoft technology, but it doesn't seem to be gaining traction. 2. Ideas for Improving the Public Electronic Document Environment (First Draft: April 7, 2015)

Existing public document collaboration systems and policies have focused solely on digitizing paper documents. However, in an era centered on software, by developing a collaboration system based on documents that are digital from the creation stage and corresponding policies, we can achieve the values of Government 3.0: sharing, communication, and collaboration.

**□ Current Status and Problems of Public Electronic Documents**

ㅇ **(Closed Technology)** Public documents centered on HWP Hangeul files
- De facto standard use of a specific company's file format (HWP)
- ODF, an international standard, has been selected as a Korean industrial standard, but its use is not mandatory for public institutions.
- Concerns about forcing private companies that collaborate to use a specific company's product (Hangeul word processor).
- Concerns that a specific company's file format (HWP) has low international usage, leading to international isolation.
- ODF, supported by Hangeul and various other word processors, has limitations in actual use due to issues of reproducibility and system integration.

ㅇ **(Web Environment)** Low accessibility and difficulty in searching and sharing
- Public documents are uploaded as files and shared as attachments to specific web posts.
- Attached files are more difficult to search than web documents.
- Due to government security guidelines, the URL of web posts is not disclosed beyond the domain, making it difficult to share and disseminate on SNS sites that typically rely on fixed URLs (Permalinks) for sharing and communication.

ㅇ **(Underutilization)** Limited to use as a substitute for paper documents
- High cost and effort across all stages: creation, distribution, use, circulation, storage, and disposal.
- Advantages such as collaboration and version control are not utilized.
- Failure to achieve a paperless environment.
- Inability to guarantee access due to document changes or deletions.
- In mobile environments, the frequency of using separate document viewers is low, and the small screen size reduces the usability of existing digital documents.

**□ Research Topics for Solutions**

ㅇ **(Open Technology)** Defining and disseminating public document formats
- Defining and disseminating public document file formats considering market impact, global standards, implementability, and usability.
- Establishing a practical technical system through public-private cooperation.

ㅇ **(Web Environment Optimization)** Improving web accessibility and usability of public electronic documents
- Developing SEO strategies optimized for the domestic portal environment.
- Developing web environment improvement strategies for sharing and dissemination (e.g., Permalink).
- Improving accessibility for document changes and deletions.
- Developing mobile response strategies.

ㅇ **(Improving and Disseminating Utilization Environment)** Researching and promoting utilization systems suitable for electronic documents
- Reviewing utilization systems across all stages.
- Developing systems and methods to utilize advantages such as collaboration and version control.
- Analyzing and overcoming obstacles to achieving a paperless environment.

Diagnosis of current status and problems was conducted by dividing into two divisions: citizens and public institutions, to develop integrated improvement plans, which were then pursued as an integrated topic until completion. Irregular individual in-depth interviews were continuously conducted.

ㅇ **(Public Service)** Joint development by two groups: Open Technology and Web Environment Optimization
- **(Open Technology)** Hangeul and Computer, Microsoft, Infraware, Handysoft, Unidocs, Synapsoft, Open Source (OpenOffice) development group
- **(Web Environment Optimization)** Naver, Daum Kakao, Google, Synapsoft, Social Network Expert Group, etc.

ㅇ **(Public Institution Utilization)** Joint dissemination by groups: Open Technology and Utilization Environment Improvement & Dissemination
- **(Open Technology)** Hangeul and Computer, Microsoft, Infraware, Handysoft, Unidocs, Synapsoft, Open Source (OpenOffice) development group
- **(Utilization Environment Improvement & Dissemination)** Korea Electronic Document Industry Association, Web Document Experts, Korea Electronic Document Society, Public Officials, etc.

**□ Expected Effects**

ㅇ **(Establishment of Fair Market Order)** By establishing a document environment compliant with international standards, international compatibility and a fair market order in the document market can be achieved, leading to improved quality through competition in the public document market.
ㅇ **(Improved Job Satisfaction and Productivity)** Maximizing the advantages of digital documents to create a paperless environment will increase job satisfaction within public institutions and enhance productivity.
ㅇ **(Improved Public Satisfaction and Trust)** Creating a digital document environment that enables sharing, communication, and collaboration will improve public satisfaction with digital public documents and enhance trust in the government as a transparent entity.
