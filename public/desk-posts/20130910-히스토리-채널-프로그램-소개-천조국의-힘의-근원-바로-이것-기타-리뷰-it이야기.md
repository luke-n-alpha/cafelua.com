실리콘 밸리의 파워를 무엇이라고들 생각하시나요? 천재들이 모인곳? 벤쳐의 천국? 컴퓨팅과 인터넷 역사를 일으킨 종주국 파워? 여러가지가 있겠지만, 미국문화 특유의 "진지한 잉여"라고 생각합니다. 어떤 이야기로는 코끼리 똥만 연구해도 죽자살자 연구해도 먹고살수 있는 국가. 라는 얘기도 들었는데요. 바로 이 잉여를 엿볼 수 있는 프로그램을 소개해보고자 합니다.

소개를 요청받은 히스토리 채널의 두개의 신규프로그램은 한국인들의 생활을 생각하면 다소 낯설고 이국적인 프로그램입니다. "Duck Dynasty""와 "Counting Cars"인데요.

아무튼 공통점은 둘 다 백만장자와 괴짜라는 점 정도입니다. 사실 전 이 두 프로그램을 보지는 못했습니다. 최근 TV를 못봤습니다. 다만, 얼마전에 예고편만 좀 봤었죠. 소개서를 받아서 자세히 보니 미국 이니까 가능한, 그리고 한편은 부러운 설정이더군요.

**3. 이 두프로그램의 공통점, 바로 잉여와 여유! 이것이 천조국의 힘**
이 두프로그램의 공통점은 잉여를 즐기는 이들이라는 점입니다. 물론, 이로 인해 꽤 돈을 벌기도 하지만, 이들은 모두 그 일이 좋아서 그리고 가치 있다고 믿기 때문에 합니다. 다소 클래식하고 미련해보이지만 그 향수에 기꺼이 돈을 지불하고, 진정으로 삶을 즐길 줄 아는 모습입니다. 소위말하는 천조국의 위엄이나 덕중의 으뜸은 양덕이라는 농담은 괴짜 미국인들의 도전의 성과가 여유가 없는 우리들에게는 이해하기 힘든 결과물을 만들기 때문입니다. 그리고, 그것은 때로는 매우 강력한 상품가치를 갖게 되기도 하죠. 비록 당장 삶의 여유는 없더라도 이들의 이런 모습은 무척 부럽습니다. 다큐마저 시트콤 처럼 보이는 다소 과장되어보이는 미국인들의 제스츄어와 유머는 그들의 이런 자신감에서 나오는것이 아닐까 하는 생각도 들구요.
비록 당장 우리 마음에 여유는 없더라도, 히스토리 채널을 보면서 이러한 삶을 꿈꿔보며, 어린아이와 같은 순수한 꿈을 꿔보는 것은 어떨까요?
이상, 히스토리 채널의 신프로그램 소개였습니다.

What do you think is the power of Silicon Valley? A gathering place for geniuses? A venture paradise? The power of the originating country that sparked the history of computing and the internet? There could be many reasons, but I think it's the unique "serious surplus" of American culture. I've also heard it said that it's a country where you can make a living even if you dedicate your life to researching only elephant dung. I'd like to introduce a program that offers a glimpse into this very surplus.

**1. Why I Enjoy Documentaries**
**First, to begin, I'd like to state that this post was written with the sponsorship of History Channel Korea.**
{{IMG:1}}
I like watching documentaries. I have an unnecessary desire to know things. One of the joys of receiving a blog post request is being able to study the requested topic and interpret it from my own perspective. Among documentaries, I particularly like those about the vast universe, dramatic massive natural disasters that inspire awe, astronauts, mysteries, and such. As a result, one of my bookmarked IPTV channels is the History Channel. Since it's not a Korean broadcast, it usually comes with subtitles, which has the advantage of being able to watch it while putting my child to sleep. And these miscellaneous pieces of information are a great help when I write posts or daydream. Sometimes, I even connect them to my field, the IT industry.

**2. Duck Dynasty and Counting Cars?** (Duck Dynasty - Counting Cars)
The two new programs from the History Channel that I was asked to introduce are somewhat unfamiliar and exotic when considering the lives of Koreans: "Duck Dynasty" and "Counting Cars."

**1) Duck Dynasty**
A direct translation of Duck Dynasty would be "Duck Kingdom." There's an uncle who comes to mind when I hear "Duck Kingdom," but...
{{IMG:2}}
{{IMG:3}}
**They don't look alike at all...**

Anyway, the commonality is just that they are both millionaires and eccentrics. To be honest, I haven't watched either of these two programs. I haven't watched TV recently. However, I did see a preview a while ago. After receiving the introduction and looking closely, I realized it's a setting only possible in America, and in a way, an enviable one. A company was created by returning to farming and making duck hunting calls, earning a lot of money. It employs most of the local people, and features quirky, 4D-like characters straight out of a sitcom. This kind of setting is hard to achieve in South Korea, where large corporations have penetrated every corner of rural areas, and the country is geographically too small, so you can find big corporate franchises everywhere. Moreover, duck hunting? In Korea, hunting as a leisure sport is largely the exclusive domain of the very wealthy, which makes it feel quite exotic and enviable.

**2) Counting Cars**
{{IMG:5}}
Car modification, or tuning—this program is almost at the level of remodeling, but it's the story of people restoring vintage cars. In Korea, for reference, this is illegal. ㅡoㅡ Restoration itself might not be a problem, but developing the car's exterior beyond that is all illegal. Furthermore, there probably aren't many people with such a leisurely hobby. Korean vintage cars would probably only be the Pony and the old Grandeur, and their appeal as classic cars is lower compared to a Beetle or a Mini. This program makes you feel like an old classic car is time-warping into the 21st century in a time machine. Those who have an affection for retro and cars would probably like it... **3. The Commonality of These Two Programs: Surplus and Leisure! This is the Power of the 'Empire of Heaven'**
The commonality of these two programs is that they feature people who enjoy their surplus. Of course, they also earn a fair amount of money from it, but they all do it because they love the work and believe it has value. It might seem a bit classic and foolish, but they willingly pay for that nostalgia and truly know how to enjoy life. The so-called majesty of the 'Empire of Heaven' or the joke that 'otaku among otaku are Western otaku' stems from the fact that the achievements of eccentric Americans create results that are difficult for us, who lack leisure, to understand. And sometimes, these results gain very strong commercial value. Even if we don't have the immediate leisure in our lives, I deeply envy their way of life. I also wonder if the somewhat exaggerated gestures and humor of Americans, which make even documentaries look like sitcoms, come from this kind of self-confidence.
Even if we don't have immediate peace of mind, how about watching the History Channel, dreaming of such a life, and having pure, childlike dreams?
This concludes the introduction to the History Channel's new programs.
