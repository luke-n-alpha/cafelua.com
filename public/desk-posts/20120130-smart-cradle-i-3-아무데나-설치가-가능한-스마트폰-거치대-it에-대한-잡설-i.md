새봄(딸 15개월)이가 카시트에 앉아가다가 땡깡의 수위를 높일때즈음이면 마지못해 사용하게 되는 최종병기가 있습니다. 이른바 새봄이용 카씨어터에 뽀로로 상영. 아내와 제 스마트폰에는 각기 새봄이가 좋아하는 동영상이 몇편이 들어있는데, 우측 앞시트의 뒤에 부착된 스마트폰 거치대에 스마트폰을 얹어 상영을 시작하죠. 물론, 아이에게 스마트폰의 노출은 최소한으로 해줍니다. 보여주다 안보여주면 울음을 터뜨리는게 더 큰일이기도 하거든요.
아무튼 덕분에 T맵을 사용하기 위해 샀던 UFO거치대는 차 앞유리가 아닌 시트목받침대 사이에 껴두었습니다. 앞자리에 가끔 손님이 탈 경우 불편함을 감수해야 하죠.

해당 리뷰요청에서 처음에 눈에 띈것은 시트에 장착한 거치대였습니다.
블랙과 화이트 두가지 색상이 있습니다. 박스 뒤에는 qr코드가. 촬영해보니 PC사이트로 가네요. 설치법이 소개된 모바일 사이트로 갈 줄 알았는데.. m.site.naver.com으로 시작하는 QR코드이니, 중간에 모바일 페이지를 만들어서 소개와 사용법이 표기된 모바일 페이지로 만들어둠이 어떨까 싶습니다. (제가 관련자라서 요청드리는건 아니고.. ㅡㅡ; ㅋ)

블랙과 화이트를 받았으나, 저희 부부는 모두 갤럭시s2블랙이라 블랙만 개봉.

설명서의 시트에 장착한 모습을 참고하려 했습니다만, 하다보니 그냥 무시하고 내키는대로 자신의 창작력을 발휘하여 모양을 만들어주면 됩니다.

When Saebom (my 15-month-old daughter) is in her car seat and starts to escalate her tantrum, there's a final weapon we reluctantly resort to. It's what we call Saebom's car theater, showing Pororo. My wife's and my smartphones each have a few of Saebom's favorite videos, and we start the screening by placing a smartphone on the holder attached to the back of the front passenger seat. Of course, we minimize our child's exposure to smartphones. Because if we show it and then stop, she'll burst into tears, which is an even bigger problem.
Anyway, thanks to this, the UFO holder I bought for T-map is now tucked between the seat headrests, not on the car's windshield. If we occasionally have a passenger in the front, we have to put up with the inconvenience.

{{IMG:1}}
**[ Saebom's Car Theater Holder ]**

So, while I was looking for a holder that could be attached to the seat, a welcome review request came in.
**{{IMG:2}}**
**[ i-3 cradle on the homepage ]**

What first caught my eye in that review request was the holder mounted on the seat.
It comes in two colors: black and white. There's a QR code on the back of the box. When I scanned it, it led to the PC site. I expected it to go to a mobile site with installation instructions... Since it's a QR code starting with m.site.naver.com, I wonder if it would be better to create a mobile page in between that introduces the product and explains how to use it. (I'm not saying this because I'm involved... ㅡㅡ; lol)

I received both black and white, but since my wife and I both have black Galaxy S2s, I only unboxed the black one.
{{IMG:3}}

{{IMG:4}}
**[ Unboxing shot ]**

After unboxing, it looked completely different from a typical holder. If you release the clip, you can adjust the length of the three legs up and down and bend them freely to create a shape. Once the clip is fixed, the length becomes fixed. I tried to refer to the installation image on the seat in the manual, but I ended up just ignoring it and creating a shape as I pleased, using my own creativity.

{{IMG:5}}
**[ Finally, the neck part is down! **
**After installing it, it reminds me of an Alien facehugger..]**

The installed look. The thinness of the Galaxy S2 actually seemed to increase the possibility of it moving left and right in this type of holder.
{{IMG:6}}
**[ The surface is thin and smooth, so it might not hold well ]**

So, I installed it with the original case on. Then it held firmly.
{{IMG:7}}
**[ Works well with the case on, no need to remove the case for installation, actually more convenient. lol ]**

Alright. Finally, the front seat headrest can be properly adjusted.
And the UFO holder was moved back to the front window so I can use it for navigation.

{{IMG:8}}
**[ The old holder found its place ]**

Lastly, here's Ssan-i (the nickname for Tucson ix in the Tucson ix cafe) after a long-overdue hand wash, always grubby with the excuse of being busy.

{{IMG:9}}
So many scratches, you're already older than Saebom now.

ps. I've already bought a 7-inch tablet, and I think if I use my creativity, it might hold well. Creativity seems more important for this smart cradle ^^;

Thank you for letting me experience a good product.
Additionally, I'd like to say that I think the name i-3 might be too difficult to search for. For online sales, a unique product name is good for searchability. If you search for 'i3' in shopping, it's all CPUs.
