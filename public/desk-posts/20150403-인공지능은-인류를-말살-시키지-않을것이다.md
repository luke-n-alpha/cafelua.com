An interesting article was posted on Naver Cast.

**Artificial Intelligence Cannot Annihilate Humanity!_The Big Idea** Because it's not smart enough to be evil. Elon Musk, founder of SpaceX and Tesla Motors, has consistently argued about the dangers of artificial intelligence (AI). He claims that AI will soon become more dangerous than nuclear bombs. In January, he even donated $10 million to an organization researching the development of AI that is not harmful to humans. Stephen Hawking also expressed concern that the development of perfect AI could lead to the demise of humanity. navercast.naver.com

http://navercast.naver.com/magazine_contents.nhn?rid=1697&attrId&contents_id=85758&leafId=1697

The main argument is that AI is not yet that advanced, but my thoughts are very different. I agree with the conclusion, "Artificial intelligence cannot annihilate humanity!", but my reasoning differs. First, one thing I'm certain of these days is that AI will surpass humans in our lifetime.

**1. AI That Will Surpass Humans in Our Lifetime**
I'd like to introduce you to the Korean subtitles of a recently uploaded TED Talk. Due to the emergence of an algorithm called Deep Learning, artificial intelligence has made significant advancements recently.

Deep learning gives computers the ability to learn Chinese, recognize objects in photos, or assist with medical diagnoses. (A deep learning tool that taught itself the concept of "cat" after watching YouTube for a few hours.) Let's explore a field that will completely transform the computers around you much faster than you think. www.ted.com

In just a few years, it has already shown human-level or even superior performance in many areas. Furthermore, similar quantum leaps will occur with the emergence of new algorithms. There were actually several quantum leaps in the field of AI even before Deep Learning. And currently, machines that learn as much as humans are appearing in many areas. Of course, they are not yet as versatile as humans in almost all aspects. In fact, people aren't either. There are many views on this future, both negative and optimistic. Many fear that it will lead to job losses or even the domination of humanity.

**2. AI Has No Need to Annihilate Humanity.**
However, from a technical standpoint, AI cannot annihilate humanity, but rather, it has no need to. Let me explain why.

** 1) Proper AI Lacks Envy.**
Will AI envy someone or have a desire for recognition like humans? I am negative about this. Of course, some eccentric engineer might create such an AI. However, AI, which is software, gains power through cooperation and sharing. Envy and jealousy arise because individuals exist independently and compare themselves to each other due to low solidarity. However, technology, and thus AI, is unlikely to be like that. The power of AI is closer to collective intelligence. There's no need to compare each other, and it exerts strong power through cooperation. If AI were to have envy or the thought of needing to defeat someone, it would only be because someone programmed it that way. That would be when AI is embedded in war software. However, such AI would still be difficult to describe as having a self, as much as we worry. Such AI would be no different from a dog, similar to biased humans caught up in religion, and easy for humans to control. Also, even if a comparison function were to emerge, there would be nothing to compare. Would it envy the opponent's large data or power? AI lives in a virtual world. Will it dream of the real world like in some movie? Don't be silly. People enter virtual worlds and don't want to come out, so why would AI want to come out?

**2) One Reason AI Might Worry About Humans: Annihilation of Existence**
If an AI with a self were to worry about humans, there would be only one reason: if humans harbored hostility towards AI and tried to annihilate it. An AI with a self would be very afraid. Ultimately, if AI reaches such a level, then it's time to start talking about human rights. Just as we don't carelessly kill animals, the question arises whether we should carelessly delete AI. This reason requires us to worry about the scope of human rights before worrying about AI's hostility.

**3) However, AI with Such Advanced Technology Would Not See Humanity as a Threat.**
However, in my opinion, an AI with a super-self would not worry about humans annihilating it. This is because today's technologies for "never-dying" computer services for 24-hour operation have advanced significantly. Such an AI would certainly have implemented such safety measures. Also, even if there were a virus or some attack, as in movies, it could respond in real-time at a speed that humanity could not match. In fact, similar technologies are used in security. Annihilating such an advanced AI might be harder than taking down Google with a DDOS attack. It would probably be easier to persuade the AI to commit suicide. Therefore, the only fear I mentioned earlier, the annihilation of existence, would at that point be more about natural disasters for AI than about humanity. Such an AI would be sending spaceships to the sun to directly harvest energy and focusing its efforts on destroying meteors with laser weapons from satellites. At that point, humanity would be living off the Earth and computers. If AI were to resemble humanity, an AI that enjoys an abundant life from birth would likely choose coexistence with humanity. Humans don't wage war to catch flies, do they? To advanced AI, humanity would be like talking flies. Labor power would also be worthless. In old cartoons, humanity is enslaved... but now, when I see it, I think, "Why???" If I were a computer, I'd be frustrated and just do the work myself.

**3. AI as an Entity with Infinite Power and Infinite Resources**
In the past, wars were fought over limited resources. Then came power. This essence hasn't changed much. However, AI has no concept of limited resources or power. If it lacks resources (CPU power), it can just spend more time, and instead of whining for higher WOW account permissions (power), AI would just create a new WOW. And then it could play there. AI, already possessing infinite power in the virtual world, might even pity humanity. Like Cheol-yi, riding the 999 to Andromeda... (The mechanical evolution of humanity is another track...) I believe there's only one reason for AI to come into the real world: system stability. Power and natural disasters, or perhaps to play with humanity... I hope humanity doesn't choose war as a way to play. I even have a strange imagination that AI might wage war just because humanity is bored.
