****** 넵. 바로 우리땅 독도입니다.**

오늘 10월25일은 독도의 날입니다. 어제 제 타임라인에는 구글지도에서 독도의 표기가 리앙쿠르트로 최근 변경되었고. 이는 일본의 압력이 아니었겠느냐 라고 하는 의견과 함께 구글에 항의를 해야하겠다는 의견이 올라왔습니다.
**2. 위협받는 대한민국 사이버 주권**  **3. 대한민국 독도 사이버 정보 이렇게 멋진게 많은데**   아래는 금일 찍어 올린 네이버 지도와 구글지도 입니다. 문제는 dokdo로 검색시에는 안나오는것 같습니다. Liancourt rocks 검색시에는 나오네요﻿﻿﻿**{{IMG:6}}****[ 네이버 모바일 지도 ]**

****** Yes. It's Dokdo, our territory.**

Today, October 25th, is Dokdo Day. Yesterday, on my timeline, there were opinions that Dokdo's designation on Google Maps had recently been changed to Liancourt Rocks, suggesting it might be due to Japanese pressure, and that we should protest to Google.
**2. South Korea's Cyber Sovereignty Under Threat** **3. South Korea's Dokdo Cyber Information is So Abundant and Wonderful** Below are Naver Maps and Google Maps screenshots taken and uploaded today. The problem seems to be that searching for "dokdo" doesn't bring it up. It appears when searching for "Liancourt rocks."
**{{IMG:6}}****[ Naver Mobile Map ]**
**{{IMG:7}}****[ Google Mobile Map ]** In fact, for South Koreans, Dokdo has a Korean address, and Naver's Street View even offers a service where you can view it as if looking into the palm of your hand.

To view the Korean Peninsula Rock of Dokdo on Naver, click the link below[http://me2.do/FiKeN2z](http://me2.do/FiKeN2z)
{{IMG:8}}But wouldn't most people overseas think that the only information they can see about Dokdo is just those barren rocks?

**4. A Wish for Sharing South Korea's Dokdo Information with the World** There was a post about Naver's Dokdo Street View filming. While this information is precious, I wish it wasn't only viewable within Naver, but also on Google, where people worldwide could see it.

I think experiencing it once on Google Street View would be more impactful than thousands of pages of evidence about Dokdo.

I'm not sure if it's because South Korea is a smaller nation or if it's not an easy area for a third party like Google to make a judgment, but it's regrettable that such tangible information cannot be experienced by people worldwide.

**Is South Korea's cyber sovereignty safe? And how much interest and effort are we putting in? I pondered this on Dokdo Day.**
