**A Small Person**

{{IMG:1}}

2002. 05. 15
There lived a very small person.
He had been small since childhood.
He looked at the big people and wished to become as big as them.

by Forest Baby

**2015. 03. 27**

The theme of 'a small child' is a subject I've often written or drawn about since I was young.

Perhaps because I was on the shorter side, I often envied the world of tall people and felt insignificant.

Whether it was in terms of heart or height.
Because of this, I also felt a great sense of impatience and an inferiority complex.
Even now, I still don't consider myself big.
I'm already past my mid-thirties, I know more, and I've become a little more composed about many things in the world,
but I still don't feel like I've grown much from my middle and high school days, when I commuted by bus, standing on tiptoes among tall people.
I still envy people with big hearts that don't waver even in the wind.

However, while reading a children's storybook to my child, an ant says to a mouse who envied larger animals:
"I'm so envious of how big you are!"
It's a simple lesson even children learn, but it seems the heart doesn't quite follow. That's probably why everyone continuously harbors desires.

But, perhaps because they resemble my wife and me, our children are also on the smaller side, but I hope they don't inherit our height.
Is this also a desire?
