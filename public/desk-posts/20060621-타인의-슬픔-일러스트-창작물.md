슬픈 소식이 들려왔어.
난 습관처럼 또 우울해졌지.

어쩌면 난 타인의 슬픔 때문이 아닌지도몰라
그저 슬퍼할 기회만 기다리고 있었던게지.

내 슬픔은 모두 너때문이고 나때문이야.

그저 타인의 슬픔을 빌어
색깔을 달리해 찾아올 뿐이야

또 흔들리는 내마음은 너를 흔들까

바보처럼, 또 그렇게 바보마냥
지치지도 않고 또 저리는 시늉을해

아하하하. 우습지 ?
역시 이런건 하나도 멋있지 않아.

누가 사랑이 아름답데
그저 저주에 걸린듯
오랫동안 나를 조이기만 해온걸

젠장 기침이 심해졌어.

2004. 10. 30

Sad news arrived.
And I became depressed again, as if by habit.

Perhaps it's not because of someone else's sorrow.
Maybe I was just waiting for a chance to be sad.

All my sadness is because of you and because of me.

It merely borrows the sorrow of others
and comes in a different color.

Will my wavering heart shake you too?

Like a fool, again like a fool,
it pretends to ache again, tirelessly.

Ahahaha. Funny, isn't it?
As expected, there's nothing cool about this.

Who says love is beautiful?
It's just like being under a curse,
only tightening its grip on me for so long.

Damn, my cough has worsened.

2004. 10. 30
