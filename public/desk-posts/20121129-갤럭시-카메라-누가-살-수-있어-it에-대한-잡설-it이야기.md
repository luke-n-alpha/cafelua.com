**1. Who on earth would buy this device?**
**1) Early Adopters?**
- Compared to the camera performance early adopters want, the performance is too poor, and for gaming, the battery is worse than a phone. What's the reason to buy this?
**2) Light users like seniors?**
- For them, even existing cameras are complicated, and Android is hell. Do they have to see a launcher even on a camera?
**3) Female users who like compactness?**
- The Galaxy S3 already takes good enough photos. The Note 2 is already heavy enough.
**2. Samsung already has a combination that covers camera performance, wireless internet, and affordable rates.**
