Why does it hurt so much?
It's because you are loving, that's why it hurts.
You prayed to love like me, didn't you?
Just as I loved you, you are hurting from loving.
Love that resembles me is stained with blood, so it's natural for it to hurt.
It is loving even when rejected.
Your small wounds become greater wounds for you,
and I also hurt every day because of you. I pray with tears for you every day.
