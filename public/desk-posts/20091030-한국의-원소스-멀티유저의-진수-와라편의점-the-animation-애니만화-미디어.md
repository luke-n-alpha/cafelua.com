Ji Kang-min's "Wara Convenience Store" has been released as an animation.
[http://comic.naver.com/webtoon/detail.nhn?titleId=92106&no=2](http://comic.naver.com/webtoon/detail.nhn?titleId=92106&no=2)
The significance of this animation seems very great, as it's the first time an animation has started from an official portal webtoon. The quality is also good. This comic was already in a short episode format, so it's a perfect subject for a several-minute animation.
Not long ago, Wara Milk was also released...
