**The Far Side of the Moon**

{{IMG:1}}

2002. 05. 25. The moon. It is an unknown world. Countless humans have observed the moon and even set foot on it. But the far side of the moon, which our eyes on Earth can never see in a lifetime—what secrets could truly be hidden there?
-----------------------------------------------
If I were God, I think I would have made it this way.

by SoopSokAegi

**2015. 04. 05**

In fact, this drawing, inspired by 'Dalmyojeonseol' (Moon Cat Legend), was created with the hope that the world would be a bit more witty.

One of my bucket list items is a trip to the lunar surface. I wonder if it will come true before I die...
