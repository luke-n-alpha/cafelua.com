**Dolls with Emotions**

{{IMG:1}}

2002. 02. 06
If we create dolls with emotions, would humanity be committing a sin... or making a step forward...?
by Forest Child

**2014.05.05**

Anime (animated films) played the biggest role in my decision to major in computer science.

Like Astro Boy, who had a profound impact on the development of Japan's robot industry, I also wanted to create technology that serves and coexists with people.

Currently, I've slightly moved away from being a software developer, and even if I had become a software developer and studied AI, I know that it's not easy to create or challenge only what you want, like in childhood dreams. So, I'm well aware that realizing and pursuing those dreams from back then isn't easy. If I had continued to pursue that dream, if I had continued my studies, would it have been possible? In truth, such hypotheticals are meaningless. What's important is the present. It's important to do what I want to do now.

And it's also true that even if I'm not a developer, I'm still circling around technology.
