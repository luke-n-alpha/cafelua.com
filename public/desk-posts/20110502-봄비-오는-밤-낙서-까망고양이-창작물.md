{{IMG:1}}**Photoshop + TM2 (Tablet) Doodle**
The sound of rain, which I haven't heard in a very long time. I drew an old cat picture, then deliberately folded the tablet PC's keyboard and wrote character by character with a handwriting recognition tool.
Suddenly, old emotions resurfaced, making me feel a lump in my throat. If you add the depth of emotion embedded in each character, 150 characters is not a small amount of writing.

ps. I really wish there was a pen for tablets that could express emotions.

If I go too fast, I fear I might miss something precious.****
