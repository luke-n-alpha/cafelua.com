As part of OJT (On-the-Job Training), we started a pair programming session.
This is my mentor's seat right next to me.
(Not the constellation Sagittarius!)
 
We set up a clone LCD, two keyboards, and two mice on one computer,
where I work for 15 minutes, then my mentor works for 15 minutes, alternating.
I learned for the first time that you can connect multiple mice and keyboards to a single computer.
Of course, there's only one cursor ㅡㅡ;
 
The 15 minutes fly by so quickly, with no time to get bored...
The timer on the Naver widget next to me blinks.
 
If there's a downside, it's that it's impossible to do other things like web surfing during coding.
Since I'm taking up my mentor's time, it seems like we don't achieve 2MM.
If you ask if I even achieve 1MM before that, I'd pass.
Also, after a 15-minute interval, it's common to wonder, "Where was I?"
 
Because there's a schedule... not only is there the pressure of 15 minutes,
but I also feel the pressure of progress.
 
Anyway, it's an interesting experience, and I feel like I'm learning a lot.
 
Note) MM unit:
Man-month: Refers to the amount of work one person does in a month. The value varies depending on one's ability.
