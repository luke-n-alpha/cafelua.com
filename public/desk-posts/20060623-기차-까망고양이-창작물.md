떠나는것을 갈하며
몸부림치듯 달리지만

벗지 못하는 레일위에는

The train cutting through the evening's golden light
yearns to depart,
and runs as if struggling,

but on the rails it cannot escape,
having nowhere to rest its heart,
it cries a metallic wail, clattering and rattling.

2004. 06. 24
