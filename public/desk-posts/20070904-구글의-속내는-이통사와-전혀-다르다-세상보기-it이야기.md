Portals have a completely different revenue model from mobile carriers. If Google releases a phone, the device price will inevitably be higher than existing mobile carriers. This is because it won't be a structure that profits from communication fees. And they would never do something absurd like removing the browser to lower the price. This is because the browser is the entry point to Google and the driving force behind Google's revenue generation.

Basically, Google's correct revenue model would be to make communication fees free using VoIP, expand wireless internet users, and dramatically increase internet users, which is Google's traditional source of income.

While I'm not praising Google, and it won't be easy to get spectrum allocation in Korea, Google's actions will clearly ring an alarm bell for the landscape of the domestic wireless internet market.
