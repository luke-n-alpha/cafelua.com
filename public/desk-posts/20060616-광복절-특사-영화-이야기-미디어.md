#### [광복절 특사](http://movie.naver.com/movie/bi/mi/review.nhn?code=35099)

감독 김상진
제작 2002 한국, 0분
**평점**

기대하고 갔던만큼 재미있었습니다.
약간 어이없을정도로 잘짜여진 타이밍과 구도,
확실한 캐릭터성..

역시 설경구의 연기는 최고 였고.
신라의 달밤과 라이터를 켜라를 거치며 코믹연기를
보여준 차승원의 연기역시 설경구에 밀리지 않을정도로
죽이 잘맞았습니다.
저같은경우 신라의 달밤을 만든 감독이란 얘기에
조금 미심쩍었지만. (사실 전 신라의 달밤은 별로.. --;)

이 영화를 보고 난후에는 대 만족이었습니다.
한참이나 웃다보니 정신없이 영화는 끝나있었고,
그 안에 숨겨진 블랙코미디들도 볼만했습니다.
설경구의 명연설도 수많은 높으신 전과자들 앞이었기에
더욱더 설득력있게 들렸습니다.
아무려면 높으신 전과자들에 비해서 쓰레기겠습니까? ㅋㅋ
이 영화를 보면 후회 안할거라고 자신합니다. ^^

멋진 자동차 와이어액션도 덤으로..
(과학적으로는 본네트가 훨씬 무거우므로 수직으로 쳐박겠지만. --;
트렁크에 머라도 싣는게.. )

2002. 11. 24

#### [Jail Breakers](http://movie.naver.com/movie/bi/mi/review.nhn?code=35099)

Director Kim Sang-jin
Production 2002 Korea, 0 min
**Rating**

It was as fun as I expected.
The timing and composition were incredibly well-structured, almost to the point of being absurd,
and the characterization was solid.

As expected, Sol Kyung-gu's acting was superb.
Cha Seung-won, who showed his comedic acting through 'Kick the Moon' and 'Break Out', also matched Sol Kyung-gu perfectly, not falling behind at all.
In my case, I was a bit skeptical when I heard it was directed by the same person who made 'Kick the Moon'. (Honestly, I didn't really like 'Kick the Moon'.. --;)

After watching this movie, I was very satisfied.
I laughed for so long that the movie ended before I knew it,
and the black comedies hidden within were also worth seeing.
Sol Kyung-gu's brilliant speech was even more convincing because it was delivered in front of so many high-ranking ex-convicts.
How could they be trash compared to those high-ranking ex-convicts? Haha.
I'm confident you won't regret watching this movie. ^^

Plus, some cool car wire action...
(Scientifically, the hood is much heavier, so it would crash vertically. --; Maybe put something in the trunk...)

2002. 11. 24
