**Caret didn't just change the shell.**
We didn't simply apply a theme; we **completely replaced and improved the system prompts**. In fact, one of the reasons we started the Caret project was because we felt a lack in Cursor's system prompts and wanted to research a structure more suitable for coding, as well as my own sLLM.
Today's post aims to showcase those results and discuss why domestic AI, or more specifically, **Korean AI development**, like 'Sovereign AI,' must participate globally.

In an experiment conducted yesterday, we confirmed a **21.5% cost reduction based on Gemini 2.5 Pro**.

**Token Optimization, The Power of System Prompts**
The core of this experiment was **JSON-based system prompt structure optimization**. Through this, we attempted to **reduce prompt tokens by over 50%**, and **ultimately achieved an actual cost reduction of approximately 20%**.

Developing this project, I clearly felt that **global AI development is nothing special**. We may **lack money, but not skill.** We are truly grateful to Cline for releasing such a great open-source project, and at the same time, we also felt that there is still much room for improvement. **The level of Korean developers is not falling behind at all.**
However, Silicon Valley offers starting salaries of 300 million won, and Meta's AI talent war talks about 100 billion won. We would be honored to receive even 200 million won.

**The Essence of Vibe Coding is the 'Token Network'**
The essence of vibe coding is ultimately the problem of **how to connect** the neural networks of LLMs with software development activities (Problem Solving). LLMs are neural networks that learn the **associations between symbols, or tokens**. The key is to **extract the appropriate token network** for development activities at the right time. PRD, CLI, IDE plugins, etc., are all just attempts to refine this process. Ultimately, we believe that to create truly efficient tools, one **must understand the entire process from LLM training to actual development application.**
