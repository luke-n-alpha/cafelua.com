몇가지 키워드를 먼저 말씀 드리겠습니다. " 숏폼 미디어, 스낵 컬쳐, 에자일, 네이티브 광고, IoT "이 키워드를 들어보셨나요? 최신 트랜드에 관심이 많으신 분들이라면, 한 두 번씩은 들어봤음직한 용어들일 겁니다. 이러한 용어들은 등장의 시기는 모두 다르고, 분야도 다르지만 디지털 정보혁명을 관통하는 "작아지는 정보단위"라는 하나의 흐름 으로 해석 가능한 여지가 있어 이 이야기를 해볼까 합니다. 이 이야기를 이해하신다면, 위의 용어를 이해하는데도 크게 도움이 되실 겁니다.**** **** **1. 작아져가는 정보단위 ** 위에서 말씀드린 것의 공통점은 바로 정보단위가 작아졌다는 겁니다. 우선 체감적으로 생각할 수 있는 것은 컴퓨터 하드웨어의 크기가 작아진 것 만큼 정보의 단위도 작아지고 있다는 점입니다. PC에 비하면 스마트폰은 매우 작습니다. 이렇게 정보단위가 작아진 사용자적 이유는 정보를 습득하는 환경의 변화입니다. 작아진 디스플레이와 입력도구, 그리고 언제 어디서나 잠깐잠깐 자주 접속할 수 있는 스마트폰 환경은 콘텐츠와 미디어에서 다루는 정보들은 자주 그리고 작게 바꾸어가고 있습니다. 블로그 대신 SNS가 강세로 바뀐 숏폼 미디어, 웹툰, 웹소설, 유투브의 스낵 컬쳐들이 대표적인 사례 입니다.
하지만, 스마트폰은 최근 커지고 있습니다. 정보습득이 더 용이해지고 있죠. 그러면, 이러한 정보단위가 작아지고 있는 흐름에 영향을 줄까요? 저는 그렇지 않다고 생각합니다. 정보단위가 작아지고 있는데는 스마트폰 이상으로 디지털 정보가 가지는 근본적인 속성이 있기 때문입니다.   **2. 작아진 정보단위가 가지는 강점 양방향성 ** 디지털 정보가 가지고 있는 속성 중 하나는 양방향성 입니다. 작은 정보단위와 양방향성이 만나 갖게되는 시너지가 있습니다. 공급된 정보나 콘텐츠는 단순히 사용자가 읽기에 작아서 부담없다는 의미 뿐만 아니라, 양방향성으로 인해 그 작은 단위로 사용자의 반응을 수집하여 정보단위의 효율화를 꾀하는 것이 가능해집니다. 콘텐츠의 가치를 정보단위로 평가가 가능하게하여 최적화된 가치를 메기게 만듭니다. 예전에는 음반을 하나 사면 듣고 싶은 곡과 나머지 곡들을 모두 샀어야 했지만, 디지털 음원으로 바뀌면서 싱글앨범으로 변화되었습니다. 만화책 역시 과거에는 권단위에서 회단위로, TV드라마 역시 회차단위로 판매되어 회차별 사용자의 반응을 알 수 있게 됩니다. 시리즈물의 경우에는 동일한 가격을 유지하고는 있지만, 시리즈 물이 아니라면 사용자의 반응에 따라 최적화된 프로모션이 가능할 것입니다.
정보단위로 보았을때는 '사람'이 의미하는 것은 개인이라는 의미입니다. 사람은 더 이상 쪼개기 힘든 최소단위로서 잘게 쪼개진 정보들은 결국 개인에 최적화된 형태로 그룹핑 되어 개인에게 꼭 맞는 형태로 전달되어 최고의 만족도와 서비스를 추구하게 됩니다.    **5. 위의 키워드에 대한 추가 복습 ** 위에서 여러 키워드를 말씀드렸으나, 실제 설명 드리지 않은 것은 에자일, 네이티브광고, IoT가 있습니다. 에자일의 경우, 과업을 최소단위로 나누어 빠른 반복을 통해 빠르고 작은 실패를 경험하여 최적의 효과를 노리는 개발 방법입니다. 개발 방법외에도 기업단위로 더 크게 확대한 개념은이 리얼타임 엔터프라이즈라고 불린다고 합니다. 네이티브 광고는 콘텐츠에 녹아든 광고를 의미합니다. 콘텐츠에 녹아든 광고는 단순히 콘텐츠 내에서 사용자를 기만하기 위해서만 의미 있는 것이 아닙니다. 함께 개제된 콘텐츠가 가지고 있는 사용자 속성과 피드백 정보들을 함께 광고에 녹여 냅니다. 광고를 기준으로 했을때 사용자 타겟이 프로파일이나 사용자 그룹이 아닌, 사용자 정보 그자체로 세분화된 사용자의 개인화 광고입니다. IoT는 정보 뿐만 아니라 하드웨어도 최소단위로 나뉘어져 사용자의 환경에 녹아듭니다. 웨어에이블 컴퓨팅이라는 용어는 단순히 입는 컴퓨터를 의미하는 것이 아니라 사용자 환경에 보이지 않는 컴퓨터라는 의미도 포함합니다. 어쩌면 인비지블컴퓨팅이라는 용어를 만들어보면 더 그럴듯 할지 모르겠습니다.

Let me first mention a few keywords: "Short-form media, snack culture, agile, native advertising, IoT." Have you heard these keywords before? If you're interested in the latest trends, these are probably terms you've come across once or twice. While these terms emerged at different times and in different fields, they can all be interpreted as a single trend of "shrinking information units" that penetrates the digital information revolution, and I'd like to talk about this. Understanding this will greatly help you understand the terms above.

**1. Shrinking Information Units**
The commonality in what I mentioned above is that the unit of information has shrunk. What we can intuitively feel is that just as the size of computer hardware has shrunk, so too is the unit of information. Compared to a PC, a smartphone is very small. The user-side reason for this shrinking information unit is the change in the environment for acquiring information. Smaller displays and input tools, along with the smartphone environment that allows frequent, brief access anytime, anywhere, are causing the information handled by content and media to change frequently and become smaller. Short-form media where SNS has replaced blogs, webtoons, web novels, and YouTube's snack culture are prime examples.

However, smartphones are getting larger recently. Information acquisition is becoming easier. So, will this affect the trend of shrinking information units? I don't think so. The reason information units are shrinking goes beyond smartphones; it lies in the fundamental properties of digital information itself.

**2. Strengths of Shrinking Information Units: Interactivity**
One of the properties of digital information is interactivity. There's a synergy that arises when small information units meet interactivity. Supplied information or content isn't just easy for users to consume because it's small; due to interactivity, it becomes possible to collect user reactions in those small units and optimize the efficiency of the information unit. It allows the value of content to be evaluated in information units, leading to optimized valuation. In the past, if you bought an album, you had to buy all the songs, including the ones you wanted to listen to and the rest. But with digital music, it changed to single albums. Similarly, comic books, which used to be sold in volumes, are now sold in chapters, and TV dramas are sold by episode, allowing user reactions to be known for each episode. For series, the price might remain the same, but for non-series content, optimized promotions would be possible based on user reactions.
If it's difficult to understand this efficiency of small units in digital information, consider higher quality Mp3s or high-resolution bitmap images. The higher the resolution, the higher the bit-frame of the content, the higher its quality. If content units become more detailed, and the value of that content is fed back, the overall efficiency and satisfaction of the content will increase. This effect can be aimed for not only with a single image but also with content sets.

**3. Strengths of Shrinking Information Units: Real-time Capability**
Another property of digital information is real-time capability. These smaller information units cost less to process than processing the whole. They consume less network bandwidth, cheaper storage, less CPU, and less battery. For these reasons, when combined with higher computing power and higher network power, small information units become capable of real-time processing. Ultimately, the final destination of information processing, which has continuously sought higher speed and efficiency, moves towards real-time capability. It's a system where what I'm doing now, or what's happening externally, is reflected immediately and feedback comes instantly.

**4. The Destination of Shrinking Information: Personalization, or People**
However, there's a trap in the term "real-time." Systemically, true real-time doesn't exist. Whatever you input into a computer, it takes time for the system to operate, process, and provide feedback to the server. And if it's large-volume data, it takes even more time. But the real-time that users perceive is the level they expect, "right now." It means not feeling like your brain is waiting. It takes fractions of a second for the human eye to see and the brain to process. This can surprisingly be quite a long time from a machine's perspective, and this is what "real-time" means. The resolution of the display that Steve Jobs named "Retina" refers to a level where a person cannot distinguish pixels when looking at a smartphone from a normal distance, which is actually much lower than the resolution of the actual retina. What this implies is that the standard for technology is "people."

From the perspective of information units, "people" means individuals. People are the smallest indivisible units, and finely fragmented information is ultimately grouped into forms optimized for individuals, delivered in a way that perfectly suits them, aiming for maximum satisfaction and service.

**5. Additional Review of the Keywords Above**
I mentioned several keywords above, but I haven't actually explained agile, native advertising, and IoT. Agile is a development methodology that divides tasks into minimal units, aiming for optimal results through rapid iterations and experiencing quick, small failures. Beyond development methods, a larger concept expanded to enterprise level is called Real-time Enterprise. Native advertising refers to advertising embedded within content. Advertising embedded in content is not just meaningful for deceiving users within the content. It integrates user attributes and feedback information from the accompanying content into the advertisement. From an advertising perspective, it's personalized advertising for users, segmented not by user profiles or user groups, but by user information itself. In IoT, not only information but also hardware is divided into minimal units and integrated into the user's environment. The term wearable computing doesn't just mean computers that are worn; it also implies invisible computers in the user's environment. Perhaps coining the term "invisible computing" would be more fitting.
All these terms commonly aim for minimization of information units, utilizing interactivity for real-time, and optimization for specific targets (individuals).

**6. A World Enveloped by Tiny Information, What Future Will Unfold?**
Extending these properties of information change into the distant future allows for various imaginings. Your computer might know everything you expect better than you do, and it might be delivered in real-time, becoming as comfortable as air. Even when watching a movie, you could watch a story optimized for that individual. Recently, devices that recognize user emotions have been introduced, and we might see movies or games that recognize user reactions throughout the viewing experience to elicit optimal satisfaction. Two people might watch the same movie, one applauding a sad ending, and the other applauding a happy ending. What about educational services? Education will not be provided in subject units; the information units of education will be minimized. You might see a 3D diagram of the correlation tree between seemingly unrelated topics in theology, science, and philosophy, or a computer might determine and deliver the information that is perfectly suited for you, or what you lack and need most.

Such a future is wonderful but also carries risks. Being analyzed in real-time by someone to receive the best service is, in a way, a chilling future. However, that future is already here, and we are unknowingly enjoying its convenience. It's just asynchronous for now, but it's gradually moving towards real-time. That era has already arrived before us and will create many challenges and opportunities.
