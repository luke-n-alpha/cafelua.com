지난 주말에 간만에 대학로 나들이를 했습니다. 아직 아기가 어려서 부부동반이 어려운 관계로 사촌 동생을 대동한 나들이.일요일 저녁 6:30분 키득키득 아트홀,

머쉬멜로우 공연. 무엇보다도 오래간만에 문화생활을 즐기러 간다는것이 기분좋았습니다. 지하철을 타고 혜화역에 도착하니 어느새 제법 날씨가 쌀쌀해졌더군요. 이글을 쓰는 오늘은 정말 ㄷㄷㄷ한 한파가..
블로거라고 초대를 받은 덕분에 블로거 명함을 드리니 예약석이 적힌 티켓을 주시더군요. 파워블로거가 달리고 연극은 처음 초대 받아봤습니다. (난 IT블로거인데..! 왜 초대를?)
극장앞에는 일요일 주말인데도 많은 사람커플들이.. 남자 둘인 우리가 좀 이상해 보이는 ㅜㅜ 사내둘이서 극장앞에서 기다리려니 쌀쌀해서 들어가기전에 극장 앞에서 팔고 있는 붕어빵을 샀습니다. 3개 천원이던가.. 둘인데 3개라니!! 아무튼 아직 저녁을 못먹은 관계로 둘이 하나씩 먹고, 나머지를 허리를 잘라(잔인해!) 간만에 붕어빵으로 허기를 달래고, 곧 시간이 되어 입장.
**1. 깔끔한 소극장
** 키득키득 아트홀에 들어서자 소극장이라고는 믿어지지 않을 만큼 깔끔함에 깜짝 놀랐습니다.{{IMG:1}}
그동안 연극이나 공연을 안보러와서 그런건지, 아니면 이곳만 그런건지. 간이 의자를 기대했었는데, 의자는 극장의자에 준하는 등받이가 있는 폭신한 의자. 커플이 아닌관계로 팔걸이가 올라가는지는 확인을 안해봤습니다. 앞에는 대형 공기청정기가 딱~! ﻿﻿{{IMG:2}}

천장에는 LCD TV가 딱~! 이 극장만 그런걸까요 ? 극중에는 촬영 금지이므로 일단 찍어뒀습니다.
리뷰(?) 관람기에 사 진 한장 없으면 이상할것 같아서..

**2. 포토 타임** 극이 시작되기전에 분위기를 띄우고, 안내를 하기 위해 나오신 분이 전화기를 꺼달라고 부탁하시고... 어라. 극장안에서는 안테나가 한칸도 안뜹니다. 꺼달라고 하실 필요도 없겠더군요. 다행히 들어오기전에 SNS로 인증샷은 이미 날린상태.
연극중에는 사진을 찍지 말아달라고 부탁하셨습니다. 그리고 찍을라면 지금 찍어달라고. 포즈를 잡아주시더군요. 분위기를 띄우기위해 농담으로 한 말이시겠지만, 안찍어드릴수가 없었죠. 저는 카메라를 다시 꺼내들고 다시한번 포즈를 요청했습니다. 흔쾌히 다시 포토타임 포즈를.. 그렇게 찍은 이 사진을 첨부합니다. ﻿{{IMG:3}}
제 후기에 유일하게 인물이 나온 사진이 되겠군요.

**3. 연극 이야기 - 사랑이야~!** 아무튼 이제 진짜 연극 이야기를 해볼까 합니다. 연극은 참여형 연극을 표방하는 연애물입니다. 시놉시스는 아주 간단합니다. 사랑하여 결혼한 부부가 부부싸움을 시작하는데서 시작합니다. 실감나더군요. 보고 있으니 우리부부도 저런일이 있었지.. 란 생각이.. ㅡㅡ; 대부분의 관객들은 미혼커플일텐데 공감들 하실 수 있으려나... 그와중에 도둑이 들고 도둑이 이혼장을 발견하여 둘을 중재하고, 다시 화해한다는 이야기. 요약하고나면 지나치게 간단해지네요. 그래서인지 복잡한 해석이나, 각 극에 담긴 심각한 이야기를 남길것이 없습니다. 사실 극의 비중은 도둑의 비중이 상당히 큽니다. 겉으로는 위협적이지만, 사실은 알고보면 순둥이인 도둑의 모습 결국 제대로 턴적도 없는데, 어째서 현상금 1000만원이나 걸려서 쫓기는건지 좀 이해가 안가더군요. 아무튼 개그형 극이다보니 도둑의 개그 연기가 가장 중요했다고 볼 수 있었습니다. 아무튼 우여곡절 끝에 도둑이 화해를 시키고 마지막에는 남편이 남긴 거대한 초코파이같이 생긴 케이크에 불을  끄고 키스를 하며 끝. (진짜 키스임~~!! 오오~~)

**4. 머쉬멜로우의 뜻?** 보통은 제가 영화나 극을 리뷰할때는 제목과 극에 대한 해석을 많이 하는 편인데, 머쉬멜로우란 제목에 대한 요약은 극 중에 그냥 아예 해줘버립니다. 머쉬멜로우는 그 단 맛이 몸에 해로운줄 알면서 자꾸 먹게되고, 그것이 사랑과 같다고. 그리고 마지막에 나온 초코파이에 흰색이 없던데.. 머쉬멜로우가 빠진건가 ??? (좀 혼란 스러움) 극 대사 중에는 분명 당신이 좋아하는 머쉬멜로우를 만들었다고 했는데. 소품의 문제인건지. 머쉬멜로우를 뺀 케이크는 달콤하지만 않지만 몸에는 좋은 결혼을 의미하는건가 ??  이런 복잡한 연관을 짓기 좋아하는 저로서는 살짝 혼란 스럽게 만든 소품이었습니다.

**5. 결론은 사랑이지. 오래되고 뻔하지만, 질리지 않는 사랑 이야기!** 그러나, 실제로 가장 중요한 점은 이 극이 가장 죽고 못사는 커플들이 보면서 가상의 다툼을 겪어보고 화해하는 과정을 겪는다는것, 그리고, 혹은 다툼중에 있는 커플들을 화해하게 만든다는점이 아닐까 합니다. (나는 도대체 왜 사촌동생과 이 연극을 본것이었나! 근데 아내가 못오는 이상 누구와 봐도 문제가 됨) 결론은 이 극은 커플을 위한 사랑 얘기다... 라는 정도겠군요. 그리고 참여형 연극이라고 하였던것은 중간에 관객을 위에다 불러놓고선 극 중 역할을 시키게 됩니다.특히 부엉이역할 하셨던 분. 짜고치는 것 처럼 보일 정도로 능청스러운 대답과 액션을 해줘서 연극을 풍부하게 만들었습니다.
나중에 회사에 가서 팀장님하고 그 이야기를 했더니 팀장님께서 대학교때 그 연극을 봤다고 하시더군요. 머쉬멜로우... 정말 롱런한 극이었던것 같네요. 오래된 만큼 단조로운 시놉시스를 가진 듯 하지만,
반대로 잘 정리되고, 이해하기 쉬운 연극이었습니다.

오래 사랑을 하려면, 갈등을 넘길 줄도 알아야 합니다. 머쉬멜로우만큼 달콤하기만 한것이 사실 사랑이 아니죠.
사실 쓴 사랑이라면 누구보다도 제대로 했다고 생각하기에..  그렇게 극처럼 사랑하고 결혼했음에도 불구하고, 역시 현실은 현실. ㅎㅎ
또다른 2막이라는것. 그러나 가정과 부부의 근간은 사랑이란 것은 분명 변함 없을겁니다.
아내랑 봤으면 좋았겠다.. 란 생각이.

Last weekend, I went out to Daehak-ro for the first time in a while. Since the baby is still young, it's hard for my wife and I to go out together, so I brought my cousin along. Sunday evening at 6:30 PM, Kideuk Kideuk Art Hall,

The Marshmallow performance. More than anything, it felt good to enjoy some cultural life after a long time. When I arrived at Hyehwa Station by subway, the weather had already turned quite chilly. Today, as I write this, there's a truly bone-chilling cold wave...
Thanks to being invited as a blogger, when I presented my blogger business card, they gave me a ticket with reserved seats. I'm a power blogger, but this was my first time being invited to a play. (I'm an IT blogger..! Why was I invited?)
In front of the theater, even on a Sunday weekend, there were many couples... The two of us, being men, probably looked a bit strange ㅜㅜ Waiting in front of the theater as two men, it was chilly, so before going in, we bought bungeoppang (fish-shaped pastries) being sold there. Was it 3 for 1,000 won...? Three for two people!! Anyway, since we hadn't had dinner yet, we each ate one, and cut the remaining one in half (cruel!) to satisfy our hunger with bungeoppang after a long time. Soon, it was time to enter.
**1. A Tidy Small Theater**
Upon entering Kideuk Kideuk Art Hall, I was surprised by how tidy it was, almost unbelievable for a small theater.{{IMG:1}}
I wondered if it was because I hadn't been to a play or performance in a while, or if this place was just exceptionally clean. I had expected folding chairs, but the chairs were plush, with backrests comparable to those in a proper theater. Since we weren't a couple, I didn't check if the armrests lifted up. In front, there was a large air purifier~! ﻿﻿{{IMG:2}}

On the ceiling, an LCD TV~! Is it just this theater? Photography is prohibited during the play, so I took a picture beforehand.
I thought it would be strange to have no photos in a review (?) / viewing log...

**2. Photo Time**
Before the play began, someone came out to liven up the atmosphere and give instructions, asking us to turn off our phones... Oh. Inside the theater, there was no signal at all. There was no need to ask us to turn them off. Fortunately, I had already posted a verification shot on SNS before coming in.
They asked us not to take photos during the play. And if we wanted to take photos, to do it now. They struck a pose for us. Although it was probably said as a joke to lighten the mood, I couldn't *not* take a picture. I took out my camera again and requested another pose. They readily struck another photo time pose... I'm attaching the photo taken like that. ﻿{{IMG:3}}
This will be the only photo with people in my review.

**3. The Play's Story - It's Love~!**
Anyway, now I'll talk about the actual play. The play is a romance that claims to be an interactive play. The synopsis is very simple. It begins with a married couple, who married for love, starting a marital dispute. It felt very real. Watching it, I thought, 'My wife and I had something like that happen too...' ㅡㅡ; Most of the audience would be unmarried couples, I wonder if they could relate... In the midst of it, a thief breaks in, finds divorce papers, mediates between them, and they reconcile. That's the story. When summarized, it becomes overly simple. Perhaps because of that, there's no complex interpretation or serious story embedded in each act to discuss. In fact, the thief's role in the play is quite significant. The thief appears threatening on the surface, but is actually a gentle soul when you get to know him. He never really pulled off a proper heist, so I couldn't quite understand why there was a 10 million won bounty on his head and he was being chased. Anyway, since it's a comedic play, the thief's comedic acting could be seen as the most important. Anyway, after many twists and turns, the thief brings about reconciliation, and it ends with the husband blowing out the candles on a giant Choco Pie-like cake he left, and they kiss. (It was a real kiss~~!! Ohh~~)

**4. The Meaning of Marshmallow?**
Usually, when I review movies or plays, I tend to interpret the title and the play a lot, but the meaning of the title 'Marshmallow' is simply explained within the play itself. Marshmallows are sweet, and you keep eating them even though you know they're bad for you, and that's like love. And the Choco Pie that appeared at the end had no white part... Was the marshmallow missing?? (A bit confusing) In the play's dialogue, it was clearly stated that he made 'your favorite marshmallows'. Was it a prop issue? Does a cake without marshmallows mean a marriage that isn't just sweet but also good for you?? For someone like me who enjoys making such complex connections, it was a prop that caused a bit of confusion.

**5. The Conclusion is Love. An Old, Obvious, Yet Never-Tiring Love Story!**
However, the most important point is perhaps that this play allows couples who are head-over-heels in love to experience a simulated argument and reconciliation process, or helps couples who are currently arguing to reconcile. (Why on earth did I watch this play with my cousin! But as long as my wife couldn't come, it would be a problem no matter who I watched it with.) The conclusion is that this play is a love story for couples... that's about it. And what was called an interactive play involved calling audience members onto the stage and having them play roles in the play. Especially the person who played the owl. Their witty answers and actions made the play richer, almost as if it were pre-arranged.
Later, when I went to work and told my team leader about it, he said he had seen that play in college. Marshmallow... it seems like a truly long-running play. While it might seem to have a simple synopsis due to its age, conversely, it was a well-structured and easy-to-understand play.
To love for a long time, you must also know how to overcome conflicts. Love isn't just sweet like a marshmallow. In fact, if it's about bitter love, I think I've experienced it more properly than anyone else... Even though we loved and married like in the play, reality is still reality. Haha.
It's another second act. But the foundation of family and marriage, which is love, will surely remain unchanged.
I wish I had watched it with my wife... that thought.
As soon as I got home, I watched the baby, and my wife went out to see a late-night movie... so the situation isn't right now. Haha.
