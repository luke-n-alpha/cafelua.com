2002. 05. 03
**I guess I should have brought a ladder**
by Soopaegi
**2015. 03.24**
I didn't draw it with any particular thought in mind. In fact, drawings of putting a ladder to the moon or trying to catch it with a butterfly net were very common clichés in fairy tales. However, now it feels dry, as if this kind of leisure, breadth of imagination, and range of expression have narrowed. By the way, when was the last time I made up my mind to go stargazing or moon-gazing...? It's always in the sky, but I'm only looking at the ground.
