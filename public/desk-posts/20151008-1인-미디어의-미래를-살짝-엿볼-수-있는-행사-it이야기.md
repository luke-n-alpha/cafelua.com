I'm introducing an event after a long time. The Korea Bloggers Association has organized a small event with the sponsorship of the Ministry of Science, ICT and Future Planning. One-person media, which started with blogs, has continuously evolved through social networks and now into MCNs. The internet has changed the distribution channels for content, and as a result, it empowers individuals who create content. Under these circumstances, the continuous development of one-person media is inevitable. However, awareness of one-person media is not yet very high, and its effects don't seem to be well-known. Therefore, I believe this gathering could be very helpful.
