#### [화려한 휴가](http://movie.naver.com/movie/bi/mi/review.nhn?code=58018)

감독 김지훈
제작 2007 한국, 0분
**평점**

화려한 휴가...
보는 내내 가슴이 답답했던 한국의 아픈 역사.
누구를 위해 이들이 폭도가 되어야 했는가...

평범한 삶이 이념과 시대상황에 의해 송두리째 바뀌어 버린 이야기는
아주 먼 이야기가 아니란것이 더욱 충격적으로 다가올수밖에 없었다.

대학이란 곳을 다니면서 이념이나 사상과는 많은 거리를 두었었지만,
최소한 내가 알고 있던 것만으로도 광주는 그건 아니었다고 생각했다.

그리고, 그들을 폭도가 아니라는 목소리만큼만 기억하고 있으면 됬다라고 생각했다.

27만원이 전재산이란 전두환씨.
당신은 아직도 잘 살고 계시오 ?

그놈목소리를 보면서 그자식이 이 영화 꼭 봤으면 좋겠다고 생각했지만,
그때는 어떤놈인지도 몰랐었다해도,

이번엔 누가 나쁜놈인지 알아도 어쩔수 없는 사회현실에 분할뿐.

#### [May 18](http://movie.naver.com/movie/bi/mi/review.nhn?code=58018)

Director Kim Ji-hoon
Production 2007 South Korea, 0 min
**Rating**

May 18...
Korea's painful history that made my heart heavy throughout the viewing.
For whom did these people have to become rioters...?

The story of ordinary lives completely overturned by ideology and the circumstances of the era
was all the more shocking because it wasn't a story from a distant past.

While attending university, I kept a distance from ideologies and philosophies,
but at least from what I knew, Gwangju was not what they claimed.

And I thought it was enough to remember them simply as the voices that said they were not rioters.

Mr. Chun Doo-hwan, who claimed 270,000 won was his entire fortune.
Are you still living well?

Watching 'Voice of a Murderer,' I thought that culprit should definitely watch this movie,
even though I didn't know who he was back then,

this time, even knowing who the villain is, I'm just furious at the inescapable social reality.
