Even if someone else comes, it won't be him. Will I be able to love them as much as I loved him then? Has my heart already been emptied? Like a fool, I poured my everything into him. How much more time must pass until it's full enough to quench someone's thirst? In the deep night, dropping cold ice dew, filling the sand well every day, the empty well waits for the day someone comes again—or... for the day he returns again—and is filled by moonlight.
