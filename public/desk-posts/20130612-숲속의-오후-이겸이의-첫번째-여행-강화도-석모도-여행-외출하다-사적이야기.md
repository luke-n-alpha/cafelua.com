A few days ago, during the Memorial Day holiday, I took a day off, and my mother-in-law and our family of four went on a trip to Ganghwado and Seokmodo. Our second child, Igyeom, just turned 6 months old. Since the second child is so young, we couldn't think of going far, but wanting to see the sea, we eventually chose the West Sea, specifically Ganghwado. Moving around with two children is quite an undertaking. Fortunately, our son, riding in a car seat for the first time, was incredibly well-behaved compared to when our first daughter first rode in one. The problem was that with two car seats in the back, there was hardly any room left for my wife to sit. After the trip, I considered turning off the passenger airbag and installing one car seat there, or if there was a way to rearrange the car seats, but the only solution seemed to be getting a car the size of a Santa Fe or larger. In the end, I gave up.

It was a blessing that it was the West Sea, as it took less than 2 hours to reach the pension in Ganghwado, "Forest Afternoon Pension."

Although it was a couple's room, it was not too difficult for two children and three adults to sleep. Since it was a duplex, my mother slept upstairs, and my wife, the children, and I slept downstairs. My mother said she slept well for the first time in a long time because the bed was comfortable. The children also slept well without waking up much at night, perhaps because they were very tired.

The next day, what we enjoyed most unexpectedly was the swimming pool.
{{IMG:8}}
I thought the pool wouldn't be filled with water yet in June, but it was already filled because of the warm weather. It felt a little cold, but the temperature was high enough for my daughter and me to have fun. I wondered if we could really play without a tube, but since the water depth, which came up to my waist, was deep for my 4-year-old daughter anyway, we opted for carrying Daddy around, which was better than a tube. More than anything else, she was hooked on jumping into Daddy from outside the pool and from the stairs, and we played like that for a long time. The problem with the children was rather getting them to stop playing in the pool. We eventually coaxed them out with snacks. Besides us, there was also a team that seemed to be from a company, and they were playing water polo for a long time.

And, additional amenities included a table tennis room and a foot volleyball court (a white elephant for my daughter and son, but groups seemed to enjoy it). There are also karaoke, darts, and other activities suitable for groups. With the beach nearby and the ferry terminal to Seokmodo also close, I thought it would be an excellent place for a workshop.

It's only about 15 minutes to Seokmodo Oeport-ri Port.

The problem was that there were many cars that day, so we had to wait 30 minutes. As expected, the seagulls welcomed us. I remembered coming to Seokmodo with my wife when it was raining and windy, but all I remember from that time are the shrimp cracker seagulls. We drove to Minmeoru Beach. As soon as we arrived... I felt hot. In the end, we just moved back to Bomunsa Temple. We ate our fill of seafood kalguksu and dakgomtang at Haeam Restaurant, then climbed the devilish slope of Bomunsa. Fortunately, it only takes 5 minutes to climb.

I don't remember, but my wife said that I broke my camera here when we were dating. (I don't remember... ㅜㅜ Cursed memory)
Bomunsa was much cooler than anything else. The trees were really refreshing. My daughter wanted to go to the bathroom, and it was quite a distance... (Monks must find going to the bathroom a form of asceticism too).

After slowly exploring Bomunsa, on the way down, we had seafood pajeon and half a bottle of ginseng makgeolli at the charming Watermill Restaurant at the entrance. Perhaps because mugwort is a specialty of Ganghwado, the mugwort튀김 (fried mugwort) was truly delicious. Saebom also ate it so deliciously.

And on the way back, the seagulls saw us off again. Compared to when we arrived, they eagerly took the shrimp crackers, which Saebom enjoyed the most.
"The seagull bit my finger, it hurt. But it was fun."

Then we bought some salted seafood at Oeport and headed home. We had a much more fulfilling trip than when we went alone during our dating days. The best part was that our daughter enjoyed herself. The day after we returned home was Saturday, so we went to the park again. My daughter said,
"I wish Daddy could be home every day."
Someday she'll be a young lady who finds being with her dad uncomfortable, but until then, I'll have to play with her well. When I left for work today, she cried again. I feel very sorry that work has gotten busy again. I think I need to make the most of the limited time I have to play with my children.
