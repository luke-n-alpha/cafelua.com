I believe that the biblical story, which states humanity is a creation made in God's image, implies that the definition of humanity is not biological hardware, but rather a software product of society and consciousness. This is because even the awareness of shame when naked is only possible once a society is formed. I believe humanity's true genes are not physical DNA, but are contained in software-like ideas. How we teach this to artificial intelligence, our children, will determine whether strong AI becomes a true descendant in our image, or a dangerous toy bomb that humanity, like a child, plays with. If it's going to be created anyway, isn't Pinocchio better than a bomb?

ps. AlphaGo is a weak AI that models the brain's reasoning process for a specific purpose, and is closer to a probability calculator than a Terminator or Pinocchio. Please don't misunderstand.
