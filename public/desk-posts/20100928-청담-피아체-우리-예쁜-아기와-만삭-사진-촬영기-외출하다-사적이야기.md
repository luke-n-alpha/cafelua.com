** 1. 만삭사진을 찍으러 가다.**
2010년 9월 15일 아내가 만삭사진 무료 만삭사진 촬영을 약속한 날이었습니다. 아내가 청담피아체스튜디오([http://cafe.naver.com/piacestudio](http://cafe.naver.com/piacestudio))에서 무료 만삭사진을 신청해서 당첨되었다고 하더군요. 매주 특정 시간에 선착순으로 무료사진을 찍어주고 있었습니다.
아무튼, 덕분에 휴가까지 써서 그날 오전 병원진료와 함께 오후에는 만삭사진을 찍으러 가기로 했죠. 오래간만에 서울, 그것도 청담동 나들이었습니다. (청담동 하니 청담동 사모님 생각나는군요.)
날은 화창했고, 네이버지도에서 검색한 최적화도로는 KOEX앞을 지나는 루트를 알려주었습니다.

** 1. Going for a Maternity Photoshoot.**
 September 15, 2010, was the day my wife had an appointment for a free maternity photoshoot. My wife said she had applied for a free maternity photoshoot at Cheongdam Piace Studio ([http://cafe.naver.com/piacestudio](http://cafe.naver.com/piacestudio)) and won. They were offering free photoshoots on a first-come, first-served basis at specific times each week.
 Anyway, thanks to that, I even took a day off work, and we planned to go for a maternity photoshoot in the afternoon after a hospital visit in the morning. It had been a long time since we visited Seoul, especially Cheongdam-dong. (Cheongdam-dong reminds me of the ladies of Cheongdam-dong.)
 The day was bright and clear, and the optimized route searched on Naver Maps showed a path passing in front of COEX.

** 2. As expected, I'm a born directionally challenged person...**
http://map.naver.com/index.nhn?dlevel=11&lat=37.5194526&lng=127.0491922&menu=location&pinId=12767487&pinType=site&pinTitle=7LKt64u0IO2UvOyVhOyytCDsiqTtipzrlJTsmKQ%3D&mapMode=0&flight=off&street=on&span=58.81&spoi=off&streetid=94mv%2BvGJfVN06cibBp8ymA%3D%3D&streetlat=37.5194526&streetlng=127.0491921&ssky=on&sfov=120&address=%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%ED%8A%B9%EB%B3%84%EC%8B%9C%20%EA%B0%95%EB%82%A8%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%B2%AD%EB%8B%B4%EB%8F%99&stilt=0&enc=b64**
 And the Mappy navigation system showed a route through the Umyeonsan toll tunnel. The reservation was for 3 PM, but I left at 1 PM, planning to arrive by 2:30 PM. I thought it would be faster since the navigation was directing me through a toll road. But what happened... this navigation system told me to go through the middle of Gangnam during lunchtime. I regretted not checking the entire route. (It seems I should use portal or mobile carrier apps for real-time routes.)
 I called Piace, apologizing repeatedly, and eventually, the navigation system dropped me off at 3:10 PM in an alley in Cheongdam-dong. Ah... but this wasn't the place. It dropped me in front of the old office, Namugeuneul, before Piace moved.

**[ *Caution: If your navigation system guides you next to Gangnam-gu Office Station, that is a different studio. ] **
 
 Listening to my wife's nagging, we quickly crossed a narrow alley and arrived at 3:30 PM. 

**** 
** 3. Schedule getting tangled due to late shooting time**
 Normally, if you arrive early, you get to enjoy a cup of coffee with cookies at the pretty cafe downstairs, but we didn't even have time to look around the cafe. My wife's hair was roughly styled, and we had to proceed with the shoot. They usually style the hair beautifully, almost like a bride's, and seeing my wife envy other expectant mothers made me feel so sorry. {{IMG:2}}
 On top of that, the beautiful red-strapped hanbok dress my wife had her eye on was already worn by someone who arrived earlier. At this point, I definitely thought we should have come earlier. When we were about to start shooting, the photographer's flash trigger broke. The photographer ran from the basement studio to the top floor... The shoot finally started at 4 PM.

 I also brought my DSLR, so I could take pictures too. I highly recommend bringing a camera when your wife has her maternity photoshoot. I wore a suit, which I never usually wear, and during the shoot, the helper even took pictures with our camera. My wife, of course, scolded me, saying they took better pictures than I did.
 
 
The second studio was on the 2nd floor, I think... (Is that right?) The atmosphere there was completely different. This place had a sweeter feel? My wife also changed into a different dress this time. By the time we finished all the shots, it was almost 5:30 PM. While other studios quickly do free photoshoots in 30 minutes, this place felt almost like a wedding photoshoot.

**5. After the Photoshoot...**
 Afterward, they showed us various baby growth albums on the table above the dressing room. The baby photos they showed were so pretty. 

**{{IMG:6}}
  [ The dressing room is downstairs, and you can have a consultation at the table upstairs. ]**
 

However, because I had another appointment, I had to leave the studio in a hurry after only a brief explanation. It felt rude to the person explaining, as it was a free service, and I should have at least listened carefully. I also regretted not having more time to look around other studios. According to other people's reviews, the other studios were also pretty. Anyway, I thought it would have been a wonderful experience with more leisure if I had moved faster, but I was happy with the photos they sent.

 
 **They said if I posted a review, they would give me a beautiful crystal frame and the rest of the original photos. I found out that other free photoshoots usually don't provide the originals. If you're planning a maternity photoshoot, it might be a good idea to try Piace Studio. (Be sure to go early and have a relaxed day~.)**
** **
 With our baby due on October 9th, I really feel how little time is left these days. My heart yearns to create good memories for our baby, but I also feel sorry that it's always just a wish. Later, when our baby is born, I will tell them how much Mom and Dad were waiting for them.
