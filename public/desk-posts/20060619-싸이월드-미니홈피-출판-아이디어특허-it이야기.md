이쁠것 같은데요.

아니면 몇분이나 한분만 정해서 그 분들의 게시물만 모아서 출판한다던가..

저 같은 경우도.. 취미가 환쟁이면서 프로그래머고 IT쪽에 관심이 많다보니.
흘러가는 동향을 보는것이 취미입니다.
(말로는 일단 감성과 이성과 지성을 고루 높은 사람이 되고자 노력중.. )

저두 미니 홈피를 꾸미고 있고
블로그라는 것은 앞으로 하나의 문화가 확실히 될것 같네요.

문화라는것의 주도가 인터넷이구나 싶어집니다.

물론 출판이라는쪽은 싸이월드처럼 온라인 회사에서 쉽게 뛰어들기는 어렵겠지만..
출판의 컨텐츠는 이미 충분하다고 생각됩니다.
제가 아는 분들도 이미 감성적이거나 실용적인 좋은 내용을 미니 홈피에 많이 담고 있습니다.

제가 건의한 내용은 아마도 싸이월드가 최초가 아니더라도
곧 어딘가 하지않을까 싶네요. ^^
제 머릿속에서 나온걸 보면 말입니다.
지금 이순간에도 제 글을 다른 블로그 사이트에서 볼 수도 있겠죠.

아.. 글구 지난번에 커버스토리에 올려주신것 정말 감사합니다.
이제 투멤만하면 베스트 미니홈피를 포함해서 3관왕입니다. ^^
몇달후에 한번 도전하죠 ^^

노래값 500원으로 올라간건 조금.. 그렇지만..
돈많이 벌어서 좋은 서비스 해주세요.

저두 공부도 알바도 하면서 어떻게하면 돈벌까 이생각 저생각 궁리중입니다.
언젠가는 졸업하고 싸이월드가 맘에 들면 입사원서 낼지두. ^^

What if we collected posts or sketch board entries from Cyworld mini-hompies and
published them OFFline?
Of course, only works that have received individual permission.

It would promote Cyworld... (and such a planned publication would be the first in the industry)
The publisher would make money if it did well, which is good...
Individuals would become famous, and if they're lucky, they could even debut.

If the book design were also created to match Cyworld's basic concept,
I think it would be pretty.

Or, we could select a few people or just one person and publish only their posts.

In my case, my hobby is being an artist and a programmer, and I'm very interested in IT.
So, my hobby is observing current trends.
(I'm trying to become a person with high emotional, rational, and intellectual balance, at least in words... )

I'm also decorating my mini-hompy,
and blogs seem like they will definitely become a culture in the future.
Recent best-selling picture books all originated from the internet,
and seeing writers like Gwiyeoni emerge also shows...

It makes me think that the internet is leading culture.

Of course, it might be difficult for an online company like Cyworld to easily jump into publishing...
But I think there's already plenty of content for publishing.
Many people I know already have a lot of emotional or practical good content on their mini-hompies.

Even if Cyworld isn't the first to implement what I suggested,
I think someone will do it soon. ^^
Considering it came from my mind, you know.
Even at this moment, my writing might be seen on other blog sites.

Oh, and thank you so much for featuring me in the cover story last time.
Now, if I just get 'Today's Member', I'll have achieved a triple crown, including Best Mini-Hompy. ^^
I'll challenge for it in a few months. ^^

The song price going up to 500 won is a bit... but...
Please make a lot of money and provide good service.

I'm also studying and working part-time, pondering how to make money.
Someday, after graduating, if I like Cyworld, I might even apply for a job there. ^^
