**죽기 싫으면 사랑하라! {{IMG:1}} **(스팟!)
얼마나 멋진 협박인가...

**[커플지옥, 솔로천국]**을 외치는 솔로들도 사실은 **사랑하고 싶어서다.**
하지만 우리는 정말 죽음을 각오하고 사랑해본적이 있는 걸까?

만화적 느낌으로 코믹하게 그려가지만, 오늘날의 씁슬한 사랑론을 생각하게 해주는 만화다.

사랑은 변하지 않았다. 사람들이 변했을 뿐이지.
그래서 오늘날의 사람들은 누군가와 더 많이 더 빨리 연결되고 싶어하지만 모두들 외롭다.

" 사랑은 인류를 구원하리라! " {{IMG:2}}
내가 예수를 좋아하는 이유중 하나는 사랑 하나로
세상과 맞장 뜬 수천만년에 한번 나올까 말까할 로멘틱가이었기 때문이다.

**Gorita**
Born: 1974
Debut: 2003

[http://cartoon.media.daum.net/toon/series/flying/general/read?seriesId=153104&cartoonId=1921&type=g](http://cartoon.media.daum.net/toon/series/flying/general/read?seriesId=153104&cartoonId=1921&type=g)

Ah... it's truly the best!!!! Wasn't this the ultimate comic imagination?
A comic absolutely necessary in an age like today.
I feel like I'm going to become a fan of the Onlylove Saint from the GDB Nebula.

**Love if you don't want to die! {{IMG:1}}** (Spot!)
What a wonderful threat...

Even solos who shout **[Couple Hell, Solo Heaven]** actually **want to love.**
But have we ever truly loved, ready to face death?

While it's drawn comically with a cartoonish feel, it's a comic that makes you think about the bitter theories of love today.

Love hasn't changed. Only people have.
That's why people today want to connect with someone more and faster, but everyone is lonely.

"Love will save humanity!" {{IMG:2}}
One of the reasons I like Jesus is that he was a romantic figure who, with love alone,
stood up against the world, a figure who might appear only once in tens of millions of years.
