About a week ago, I think... a photo taken at Sokcho Port.
Perhaps because it was an outing after a long time,
I wanted to take photos, even while my wife nagged me.

The sea breeze at Sokcho Port, where we went for sashimi, was still chilly,
and I don't know if the lady did it on purpose,
but she had covered an incandescent light bulb with fish whose names I didn't know.

Fish Lampshade

Originally, three brothers who lived in the sea were now embracing a light made by humans.

Being like that,
is it warmer than when you were in the sea?

It might just be fish carcasses,
but somehow, I felt both laughter and emotion.

Are laughter and emotion completely incompatible feelings??

Haha. Anyway, that's how it was.

There are days when I just want to grab a camera and go out to take photos by myself.
