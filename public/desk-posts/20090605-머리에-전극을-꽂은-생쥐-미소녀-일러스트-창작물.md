Used only OpenCanvas, which is freeware.
These days, the image of rats is already not good...
Akasa-nim ([http://nblog.akalog.wo.tc/](http://nblog.akalog.wo.tc/)) asked me to draw a **moe girl with electrodes in her head**...
This is the image I created, trying my best to make it not look like horror.
 
I drew this little by little during lunch breaks.
Trying to do it without Photoshop, giving it shading and shadows was harder than I thought.
It's my first OpenCanvas drawing, and it's a really good tool for drawing.
