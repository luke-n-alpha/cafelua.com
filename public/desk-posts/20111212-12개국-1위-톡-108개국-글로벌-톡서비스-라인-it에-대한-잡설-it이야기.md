**1. 무료 SMS에서 시작한 IM(톡서비스)**
왓츠앱으로 시작된 IM의 열풍은 카카오톡이라는 걸출한 앱을 만들어냈습니다. 그리고 카카오톡의 독주가 영원할 것 같았지만, 무료통화를 무기로한 다음의 마이피플이 그룹을 추가한 틱톡이 최근에는 상당한 유저수를 확보했습니다. 현재 사실 1명당 복수개의 메신저를 사용하는 셈이죠. 무료sms를 특징으로 시작했던 IM서비스는 voip, 커뮤니티등의 기능을 갖추며 단순한 무료sms를 넘어 강력한 특징들을 갖춰가고 있습니다.

**2. 12개국 1위, 108개국 런칭. 글로벌 톡서비스. 라인**
그러한 와중에 해외에서 위세를 떨치고 있는 IM이 하나 있는데 nhn japan의 Line입니다. 라인은 nhn이 아닌 nhn japan의 앱입니다. 때문에 대부분의** nhn이 가진 국내라는 한계를 넘어 글로벌앱으로 시작되었으며, 출시된지 얼마되지도 않아 벌써 800만 다운로드를 돌파했다고 합니다.  카카오톡은 3천만으로 아직 격차는 있지만, 그 가속도는 괄목할만한 수준입니다.
**
** 1) 깨끗한 무료통화**
그리고 무료 sms보다도 해외에서는 무료 음성통화로 더 알려져 있다고 합니다. 그래서인지 라인의 아이콘은 전화수화기입니다. 실제로 통화를 해보면 마이피플에 비해 음성통화 품질은 놀랄만큼 좋았습니다.
** 2) 동아리, 카페 친구들과 이야기하기 쉬운 그룹기능**
또한 가장 강력한특징은 그룹의 바이럴 효과였습니다. 그룹을 만들어 사람들을 초대할수 있고, 모든 그룹원들은 또 다른이들을 초대가 가능하며, 새로 그룹에 편입한 친 새로운 사람을 친구추가가 쉽게 가능했습니다. 이는 마치 페이스북의 그룹과도 같은 개념이었는데, 얼마전 황병선교수님이 이야기하신 커뮤니케이션 도구는 커뮤니티가 가장중요하다고 말씀하신 이야기가 떠올랐습니다. 또한 재밌는것은 그룹원들 누구나 그룹의 사진과 그룹명을바꿀수 있다는거였는데요. 이를 통한 소소한 즐거움을 나눌수 있을것 같았습니다. 다만 페이스북과 마찬가지로 히스토리는 그룹원들에게 보이니, 장난도 정도것.. ㅋ 그리고, 그렇게 만들어진 그룹에서는 시도 때도 없이 알림이 울릴 수 있으므로 그룹에서 알림을 끌수 있게 되어있는 점 또한 좋았습니다.

**1. IM (Talk Service) that Started with Free SMS**
The IM craze, sparked by WhatsApp, gave birth to the outstanding app KakaoTalk. And while KakaoTalk's dominance seemed eternal, Daum's MyPeople, armed with free calls, and TikTok, which added group features, have recently secured a significant number of users. Currently, it's common for one person to use multiple messengers. IM services, which started with free SMS as their main feature, are now acquiring powerful features beyond simple free SMS, such as VoIP and community functions.

**2. No. 1 in 12 Countries, Launched in 108 Countries. Global Talk Service. Line**
Amidst this, there is one IM service making waves overseas: NHN Japan's Line. Line is an app from NHN Japan, not NHN. Therefore, it started as a global app, overcoming the domestic limitations of most NHN services, and has already surpassed 8 million downloads shortly after its release. While KakaoTalk has 30 million, the gap is still significant, but Line's acceleration is remarkable.

**1) Clear Free Calls**
And overseas, it's said to be more known for free voice calls than free SMS. Perhaps that's why Line's icon is a phone receiver. When I actually made a call, the voice call quality was surprisingly good compared to MyPeople.

**2) Group Features Easy for Talking with Club and Cafe Friends**
Also, the most powerful feature was the viral effect of groups. You can create a group and invite people, all group members can invite others, and new people who join the group can easily be added as friends. This was a concept similar to Facebook groups, and it reminded me of Professor Hwang Byung-sun's recent statement that community is the most important aspect of communication tools. What was also interesting was that any group member could change the group's photo and name. I felt like this could lead to small joys being shared. However, just like Facebook, the history is visible to group members, so keep pranks in moderation... lol. And, since notifications can ring constantly in such groups, the ability to turn off notifications for a group was also a good feature.

**3) Weather Friend, Japanese Translation, Convenient Chat Friends, Maybe Even SIRI Someday..**
And another interesting feature was chat friends like Weather Friend, Japanese Translation, and Lime.
{{IMG:1}} {{IMG:2}}

If you tell it a region, it tells you the weather, and if you speak in Korean, it speaks in Japanese. Lime is a bot similar to SimSimi. These chat friends felt like Siri or a search engine. If they get smarter someday, couldn't they become Siri?

Other features included fun emoticons, fast and pleasant speed, and QR code friend addition.{{IMG:3}} ** [ My QR for adding Line friends ]**
Of course, it's not without its drawbacks.

**3. Line's Disadvantages and Regrettable Points**
Unlike most IMs, **1) it didn't support pinning chat partners to the home screen**, and perhaps because it's a global app, **2) initial consonant search was not available.**
**3) It was difficult to invite or edit groups directly during group chats**, and unlike Naver Talk which supports large video file transfers, **4) video transfer was not possible at all**. I also thought it would be good if **5) it supported PC or Mac, like Naver Talk.**
**6) Friend Introduction Feature: A feature to introduce friends to other friends would be good...**
**7) When a new friend is added, a red number appears, but it's hard to tell who the new person is. An icon for new friends in the list would be helpful. (That way, I could group them.)**
Wow... now that I've listed them, there are still a ton of things to improve.

**4. Could Line's Community Tools Be Integrated into Other Communities?**
One more suggestion I have is that I'd like to use Line's powerful groups in other web communities. I invited friends from Cyworld and Naver Cafe's small groups and clubs, and I wondered what it would be like if they could be used directly on the Cyworld and Naver Cafe websites. Just like Naver Talk can be used on Naver Me.
**If Line provides an open API and web services utilize it, a wider variety of services would be possible.** If a means to map existing web communities with Line's groups is provided, I believe it could be a sure way to achieve the expansion into communities that Professor Hwang Byung-sun mentioned.

**5. Concluding, Wishing for Global Success and a Personal Note**
Looking at Line, IM is continuously evolving. As the name 'smartphone' implies 'phone,' smartphones as communication tools will not disappear. Mobile carriers will likely try to regulate or express dissatisfaction with service providers, as seen with KakaoTalk. However, this is already the prevailing trend. Networks should not monopolize services, and that is the way to secure global service competitiveness. I hope that domestic IT companies will emerge as key players in the global IT industry.
Finally, the reason I'm publishing this post is that I usually don't talk about internal services (this is the second time recently, after a blog post!), but I also have a personal motive: to increase my friends for Line's ongoing internal event. **If you install Line, please add fstory97 as a friend. However, regardless of my personal motives, I think you'll find it's a truly clean IM. There's an event going on, so if fate smiles upon you, you might even win a Ray car!!!**
