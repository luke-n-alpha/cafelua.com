****Many people pointed out the browser comparison screen, so I've revised it based on the Naver app, which is a 3rd Party app. The Naver app is used by over 10 million people as a browser in Korea. ******** ** Today, I touched the Vega and Galaxy S4 for the first time. What I felt was... Vega is just Vega. Even though I was tired of the Galaxy S4's design, Vega's limitations were clearly felt. **
**1. Galaxy S4 vs Vega Iron**
**1) Almost no difference in grip, same size**
There wasn't much difference in grip. There's a difference of a few millimeters, but the perceived difference is very small, to the point where I felt the sizes were almost identical.
{{IMG:1}}
**[ Iron's cool-looking zero bezel ]**
{{IMG:2}} **[ Horizontal difference is negligible. ]
**{{IMG:3}}
**[ Don't even know if there's a thickness difference, especially since the Galaxy S4 is rounded ]**
**2) Iron is heavier than Galaxy**
When you lift both at the same time, there's a surprising weight difference. The heft of an iPhone with its glass screen was felt in the Vega Iron.
**3) Zero Bezel, Screen Size? Enticed by zero bezel, but usable screen area is smaller.**
When I first saw it, the bezel of the Vega Iron felt pretty. However, upon using it, despite having the same LCD size, the usable screen area felt much smaller and frustrating. This was the difference in feeling brought about by the Galaxy S4 using hardware keys and the Vega Iron occupying a certain area of the screen with software keys.
{{IMG:4}} [ The Galaxy S4 shows more than one and a half lines below Naver's main page. ]
{{IMG:5}} [ **When switching to landscape, due to the right SW area, the width is narrow,**** preventing adaptive web from functioning. Both Naver app and Daum app were tested, but ****the same phenomenon occurred.****]****
****[ The SW keys at the bottom have the biggest impact.** **Of course, they can be hidden. ****However, with the Galaxy S4, the physical key area below is also touchable, making that area feel like a touch input, whereas with the Vega Iron, the bezel area below feels like wasted space.] **
Only the bezel is small; the usable area felt much wider on the Galaxy S4. It didn't have the dazzling vastness I felt when I touched the Optimus G Pro. Is this good enough for a 5-inch screen? After touching the Galaxy S4, my eyes were spoiled...
**4) Slow AF**
The AF was noticeably slower than the Galaxy S4. It felt almost slower than the Optimus G Pro, and for me, with a child, slow AF is a fatal flaw.
**2. Conclusion, Vega Iron is still a budget phone.**
The Iron will likely settle as a budget device. If it's the same price as the Optimus G Pro, I'd have no choice but to pick the G Pro. Although it's compared to the Galaxy S4 in advertisements, it felt like a competitor in a different weight class. Pantech has a long way to go...
