**메타버스와 AI**

** 오픈소스의 약진**

하지만, 여기에서 많은 분들은 AI가 글로벌과 대기업들의 전유물인듯 생각했던 것 같지만, 최근 1년간의 상황을 보니 AI를 끌고가는 건 대기업의 대형 실험도 있지만, 결국은 이를 폭발 시키는건 오픈소스인것 같습니다. ChatGPT자체가 plugin 생태계를 꾸리며 OS가 될 여지도 분명히 있지만, 저는 여기에 조금 부정적입니다. 물론 ChatGPT는 제2의 페이스북이나 네이처럼 거대 플랫폼이 될겁니다. 하지만 ChatGPT가 인터넷을 대체할 수는 없습니다.  미드저니가 나왔을때 사람들은 열광했고, 창작자들은 걱정했습니다. 그러나 Stable diffusion이 나오면서 최소한 개인창작자들이나 개발자들에게도 기회가 발생했으며 엄청나게 빠르게 발전 중에 있습니다.   생성형 이미지 AI도 마찬가지입니다. Stable diffusion 의 플러그인이 krita 오픈소스 그래픽에디터로 들어가면서 놀랄만큼 inpaint등이 편해졌습니다.  ChatGPT도 마찬가지로 대체할 수 있는 작은 모델들이 나오기 시작했습니다. 회사의 민감정보, 혹은 일반적인 서비스가 어려운 특화정보들은 ChatGPT보다는 이러한 모델들이 선호가 될겁니다.​

**메타버스 키워드는 져도 모질라허브는 계속 갑니다.**

메타버스 또한 마찬가지입니다. 빌리버는 모질라허브라는 글로벌 메타버스 오픈소스 프로젝트를 기반으로 클라우드 플랫폼을 만들고 있습니다. 메타버스 3D생태계를 만들겠다는 노력들이 많지만, 이미 스케치팹에서 모질라허브의 가장 인기있는 모델은 4.300회의 다운로드와 391회의 즐겨 찾기 2만의 조회수를 가지고 있습니다. 어떤 버티컬 메타버스의 프로슈머 생태계가 이런 생태계를 가지고 있을까요 ?

이 hubs exporter도 오픈소스로 추가 수정이 가능합니다. 이렇게 되면 장점은 hubs 프로젝트는 표준 3D모델과 아바타들만 플레이하는 오픈소스이고, 제작 부문은 완전히 전문 그래픽/애니메이션 도구인 blender에 맡기고, 호환성만 맞추면 된다는 의미입니다. 이미 웹브라우져의 WebXR과 WebGPU성능은 매우 좋아졌습니다.  모질라허브의 기본 맵도 Spoke가 아닌 블랜더로 제작된 맵을 보여주고 있습니다. ​

**Metaverse and AI**

Many seem to believe the metaverse has failed, seeing Facebook, which changed its name to Meta, now focusing on AI instead of the metaverse. However, my feeling is a bit different. AI is a technology that can accelerate the metaverse. The metaverse's core lies in 3D and UX experiences, where all interactions occur through vision and movement, without keyboards or mice. Therefore, it must be able to recognize images from a camera in real-time and feed back my body's reactions to virtual objects. And AI handles all these technologies. Furthermore, one of the biggest problems with the metaverse was the high cost of content production. Rich experiences, conversely, demand more cost. For the metaverse, which always suffered from a lack of content, generative AI comes as a welcome rain.

**The Rise of Open Source**

However, many here seemed to think that AI was the exclusive domain of global corporations and large enterprises, but looking at the situation over the past year, while large-scale experiments by big companies are driving AI, it seems open source is ultimately what's making it explode. While ChatGPT itself certainly has the potential to become an OS by building a plugin ecosystem, I'm a bit skeptical about this. Of course, ChatGPT will become a giant platform like a second Facebook or Naver. But ChatGPT cannot replace the internet. When Midjourney came out, people were enthusiastic, and creators were worried. However, with the advent of Stable Diffusion, opportunities arose for individual creators and developers, and it's developing incredibly rapidly. The same goes for generative image AI. As Stable Diffusion's plugin integrated into the Krita open-source graphic editor, features like inpainting became surprisingly convenient. Similarly, smaller models capable of replacing ChatGPT have begun to emerge. Sensitive company information, or specialized information difficult for general services, will likely prefer these models over ChatGPT.

**Even if the Metaverse keyword fades, Mozilla Hubs continues.**

The metaverse is also the same. Believer is building a cloud platform based on Mozilla Hubs, a global open-source metaverse project. While there are many efforts to create a metaverse 3D ecosystem, the most popular Mozilla Hubs model on Sketchfab already has 4,300 downloads, 391 favorites, and 20,000 views. Which vertical metaverse's prosumer ecosystem has such an ecosystem?

Looking at YouTube, cases utilizing Mozilla Hubs have also increased significantly over the past year. And another fact I've recently learned and felt is that while Mozilla Hubs' web editor, Spoke, was powerful and good, it had limitations in graphics and production quality issues. After all, Spoke wasn't a professional 3D graphics editor. However, with the emergence of Blender's Hubs exporter, also open-source, the quality has significantly improved. [https://youtu.be/A51eHOkR0IY](https://youtu.be/A51eHOkR0IY)

This Hubs exporter is also open-source and can be further modified. The advantage of this is that the Hubs project is open-source, playing only standard 3D models and avatars, while the creation aspect is entirely entrusted to Blender, a professional graphics/animation tool, requiring only compatibility. WebXR and WebGPU performance in web browsers have already greatly improved. Mozilla Hubs' default map also shows a map created with Blender, not Spoke.

Attending MSF standards meetings and observing recent trends, one thing is clear: the technical standards and environment for the internet's transformation into the metaverse are increasingly moving towards open source. Observing these movements, I feel that the idea of the metaverse and AI being driven solely by big players might be a bit of an overconcern, and I'd like to suggest that metaverse projects attempting to build everything independently should reconsider their direction.
