떨쳐버리려고 ..새벽공원을 아무리 달려도 희미해지지도 잊혀지지도 않아.가을하늘 새벽별은 유난히 맑더라.별을 보고 또 생각 나버렸어.찬 이슬이 흥건한 벤치위에 그리움을 담은 깊은 기침만드러누워서 바라본 하늘에는 별들만이 병든 나를 바라본다.​2003.09.22

그 때는 얼마나 달렸는지 모르겠다. 답답해서 달렸고, 무엇이 그렇게 억울했는지 달렸다.그런데 지나고보니까 잊지 않기 위해서 달린거 아닌가 싶다.마누라가 내게는 집요함이 있다더라.. 인정.금주는 결혼 10주년이 지난 주말이었다.그렇게 힘들게 결혼했는데, 미안한것만 투성이다.​2016.11.06

To shake it off... No matter how much I run through the dawn park, it doesn't fade or disappear. The dawn stars in the autumn sky were exceptionally clear. Seeing the stars, I thought of it again. Only a deep cough filled with longing on a bench soaked with cold dew. Lying down and looking at the sky, only the stars gaze upon my ailing self.
2003.09.22

I don't know how much I ran back then. I ran because I was frustrated, and I ran because I felt so wronged. But looking back, I wonder if I ran not to forget. My wife says I have a persistent streak... I admit it. This past weekend was our 10th wedding anniversary. We got married with such difficulty, but all I feel is sorry.
2016.11.06
