I was diagnosed with heart disease. This tumor, which had been removed through agonizing chemotherapy that felt like death and several surgeries, was growing again when my chest was opened. Just how much more pain do I have to endure to get better? How much more of the medicine called 'pain' do I have to take, and how many more chest-splitting surgeries do I need to cure you? This disease, which is you, is so difficult and sad.

In the medical records, it was written in tiny letters:
- You have a terrible disease that even threatens your life.
- The duration of treatment and complete recovery cannot be guaranteed.
- Moreover, this disease is very different from others.
- Most diseases require hope to heal, but this disease requires you to abandon hope. -

But... if a human abandons hope, they die at that very moment.
2003. 9. 12

A line like something from 'Chunibyo' (middle schooler syndrome)... Back then, I truly felt like I would die from love.
