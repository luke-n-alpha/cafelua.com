**Winter Fairy**

{{IMG:1}}

2002. 06. 05
Admiring the brilliance of midsummer, which even warmed her cold heart, she played music.
Forgetting even that she was a winter fairy...
Unaware that the birdsong around her was fading and the colors of the plants were changing, she praised the summer she saw for the first time.
But at the sight that would soon unfold, she would surely grieve.
She wouldn't even be able to shed tears. Because she is a winter fairy.
Holding a frozen bird to her chest, only icicles would form in her eyes.

by Forest Story
**2015. 04. 09**

Reading this now, I'm reminded of Frozen, which was released last year.

With my analytical personality, direct way of speaking, and lack of tact, I often get misunderstood.
The theme of a being that is cold on the outside but warm on the inside is a topic I've talked about a lot.

However, I actually don't dislike this personality of mine.
Because I believe that by keeping a cool head, one can protect what is warm.

One should do what one is good at. I just think that way.
