소속 애플

**
**[ ▲ 요즘은 내가 대세. 아이돌이 부럽지 않은 스티브 잡스 ]**
**** **
**** 최근 아이폰이 출시된 이후 스티브 잡스에 관심이 크게 쏠리고 있습니다. 그의 리더쉽과 성공의 비결에 관해 침이 마르도록 칭찬하는 글들을 많이도 봅니다. 그 중 얼마전에는 애플의 주가상승으로 마이크로소프트웨어를 추월하였다는 소식과 함께 스티브잡스가 오랜 숙적인 빌게이츠를 이겼다는 기사가 실렸습니다.
**   **[**게이츠 누른 잡스, 그들의 '30년 전쟁'**](http://news.naver.com/main/read.nhn?mode=LSD&mid=sec&sid1=105&oid=008&aid=0002318561)** (머니 투데이 2010년 4월 23알)**
그러나 저는 오히려 이 기사로 빌게이츠에 대해 다시한번 생각해보게 되었습니다. 그리고, 대비되는 스티브 잡스의 인생과 비교해보게 되었습니다. 빌게이츠와 스티브 잡스 두 걸출한 인물 둘 다 능력은 영웅급이겠으나 그중 딱 한명의 삶을 대신 살라고 하면 누구의 삶을 살고 싶은가요? 그 둘 중 어느 누가 더 성공한 삶을 살고 있을까요? 많은이들이 최근 영웅시 되는 분위기에 의해 스티브 잡스를 선택할것입니다. 하지만, 전 주저없이 빌게이츠를 선택할 것입니다. 의아하게 들릴지도 모르겠으나 그 이야기를 해보려고 합니다.

** 2. 대단한 두 인물****
** 두 인물 모두 대단한 사업적 성공을 거뒀습니다. 그들의 성공의 이유를 떠올리라면 스티브 잡스하면 창조적인 아이디어이겠지만, 빌게이츠하면 무엇이 떠오르나요? 남의것을 카피해서 성공했다는 오명? 카피만으로 성공을 할 수 있다면 그렇게 쉬운게 어디있을까요? 솔직히 전 둘 다 어떻게 성공을 거두었는지는 잘 모르겠습니다. 기가막힌 운과 타이밍, 시장을 보고 판단하는 적절성, 그리고 그것을 실행에 옮기는 행동력, 타인을 설득하는 프리젠테이션 능력 이 모든 것 일 수도 있겠죠. 이는 많은 블로그들과 도서들이 이야기를 하고 있고 저보다도 더 잘아시는 분들이 많으리라고 봅니다.

** 1. Did Steve Jobs beat Bill Gates?****
**

{{IMG:1}}

http://people.search.naver.com/search.naver?where=people&sm=sme_clk&query=%EC%8A%A4%ED%8B%B0%EB%B8%8C%EC%9E%A1%EC%8A%A4&os=174292&ie=utf8

[**Steve Jobs**](http://people.search.naver.com/search.naver?where=people&sm=sme_clk&query=%EC%8A%A4%ED%8B%B0%EB%B8%8C%EC%9E%A1%EC%8A%A4&os=174292&ie=utf8) Entrepreneur

Affiliation: Apple

 **
**[ ▲ I'm the trend these days. Steve Jobs, as popular as an idol ]**
**** **
**** Recently, since the launch of the iPhone, there has been a great deal of interest in Steve Jobs. I see many articles praising his leadership and secrets to success until my mouth runs dry. Among them, there was a recent article about Steve Jobs beating his long-time rival Bill Gates, along with the news that Apple's stock price surge had surpassed Microsoft.  
**   **[**Jobs who beat Gates, their '30-year war'**](http://news.naver.com/main/read.nhn?mode=LSD&mid=sec&sid1=105&oid=008&aid=0002318561)** (Money Today, April 23, 2010)**
 However, this article made me think about Bill Gates once again. And I compared his life with the contrasting life of Steve Jobs. Both Bill Gates and Steve Jobs are outstanding figures, both with heroic abilities, but if you had to live just one of their lives, whose life would you choose? Which of the two is living a more successful life? Many people would choose Steve Jobs due to the recent hero-worshiping atmosphere. But I would choose Bill Gates without hesitation. It might sound strange, but I'd like to tell you why.
 
** 2. Two Remarkable Figures****
** Both figures achieved tremendous business success. If you think of the reasons for their success, Steve Jobs brings to mind creative ideas, but what comes to mind when you think of Bill Gates? The stigma of succeeding by copying others? If success could be achieved simply by copying, what could be easier? Honestly, I'm not sure how either of them achieved success. It could be a combination of incredible luck and timing, the appropriateness of market judgment, the ability to execute, and the presentation skills to persuade others. Many blogs and books discuss this, and I believe there are many people who know more about it than I do.
 
**3. Why I think Bill Gates' life is more admirable
** Rather than comparing their ambiguous abilities as above, I chose Bill Gates because of the comparison of their visible results.

**[ ▲ A husband is made by his wife. Woman power, Mrs. Melinda ]**

  Although Steve Jobs married at 38, little seems to be known about his married life. ** **In contrast, Bill Gates' statement, **"I started philanthropy solely because of Melinda,"** seems to mean that he met not just a supportive wife but a new vision and partner. This is very enviable. The fact that he, who could not escape criticism for monopoly during his business career, chose a life of sharing, could also mean that he is someone who listens well to others.
 Conversely, Steve Jobs makes excellent decisions, but he has a strong image of not listening to others. Perhaps because of this, he is constantly at war with many companies. It seems like a very tiring life. While MS monopolized the OS aspect and exerted great influence, the Windows ecosystem had collaborative relationships with many companies. In contrast, Apple's falling out with Adobe and Google, with whom they once worked well, makes his approach seem somewhat precarious.

** 3) The Scope of the World They Change
** Third is the scope of the world they change and contribute to.
 Bill Gates, married to Melinda, is said to be more famous in Africa, far from where IT civilization shines, rather than in places where IT thrives. 
 

Some might consider his philanthropy as merely a luxury for a rich person, like a certain Korean chairman, but Gates studies extensively for his good deeds, showing that it's not just an act, which makes me feel a lot different from Korean rich people. Steve Jobs' changes are limited to IT users, but Bill Gates is crossing that line and actually changing the world.

** 4) The Conclusion is a Happy Life
** If you look at the long arc of life and ask which one would be happier, I would unhesitatingly choose Bill Gates as my model. This is because Steve Jobs, despite his brilliant ideas and abilities, gives the impression that something human is lacking.
 What kind of person would a company want? Perhaps a workaholic like Jobs would be more to a company's liking. But since I'm just a laborer, I envy Gates' role model more.
 
**4. And finally, Self-Reflection
