LG U+의 서비스 불만에 SKT로 이주해온지 얼마 안지났지만, SKT역시 많은 문제점을 보이는 군요. 특히 이번에 T스토어앱의 결제 시스템을 보고 기가막혔습니다. 환불을 요구했지만, 돌아온 답장은 더 황당했구요. 구글 마켓보다 정리가 잘되있는 T스토어라고 생각했지만, 돈을 받는 유료 시스템에 관해서는 이렇게 만들어 놓다니.. 이 이야기를 해보려고 합니다.

또한 전자상거래법 17조를 들었는데
**  전자상거래법 17조 약관 26조 : 단순변심에의해 7일이내에 환불 가능합니다. 물론 SW에 이 법을 적용하는건 무리라는건 이해가 갑니다만(혹시 시간의 경과에 의하여 재판매가 곤란할 정도로 상품등의 가치가 현저히 감소한 경우가 즉시환불도 해당한다고 말하시지는 않겠죠?),

거기다 구글은 15분 이내에 무조건 환불이 가능한 상황이니, 어느쪽이 더 유저를 위한 것인지, 안전한 결제방법인지는 말 안해도 아실거라고 봅니다. 카드결제처럼 복잡한것도 문제지만, 지나치게 단순한것도 문제입니다.

It hasn't been long since I switched to SKT due to dissatisfaction with LG U+'s service, but SKT also seems to have many problems. I was particularly appalled by the payment system of the T Store app this time. I requested a refund, but the reply I received was even more absurd. I thought T Store was better organized than Google Market, but to design a paid system that handles money like this... I'd like to talk about this.

**1. Purchased apps are absolutely non-refundable.**
**(Payment is super easy with one touch, but no refunds)**
****
{{IMG:1}}
It's exactly as the title says. They say refunds are absolutely impossible, whether you ran the app or not. It's not like a clothing store in an underground mall...

They also cited Article 17 of the Electronic Commerce Act.
**Electronic Commerce Act Article 17, Clause 26: Refunds are possible within 7 days due to a simple change of mind. Of course, I understand that applying this law to software is unreasonable (you wouldn't say that cases where the value of goods has significantly decreased due to the passage of time, making resale difficult, also apply to immediate refunds, would you?),
But I don't understand how Article 17 of the Electronic Commerce Act becomes a reason for non-refundability.**

**Actually, there's another reason why I purchased the app and requested a refund. My purpose was to conduct a comparative study on mobile payment systems between different markets. But this T Store app, as soon as you press the purchase button, it's immediately paid for and downloaded.** Apple's App Store asks for a PIN, and Google Market shows a Visa card, but T Store has none of that. Payment is immediate upon button click, and no refunds. And apparently, it's added to your phone bill later.
Furthermore, Google allows unconditional refunds within 15 minutes, so I believe you already know which one is more user-friendly and a safer payment method. While overly complex card payments are an issue, excessively simple ones are also a problem.

{{IMG:2}}
**[Good Google offers unconditional refunds within 15 minutes like this]**

**2. Incorrect customer inquiry response, did I see things?**
So I left an inquiry with customer service about the issue. The reply came a few days later. The fact that it took several days for a money-related issue was already quite absurd... If it were a refund due to an actual error, SKT would hold onto the money during that period and earn interest from it. This is what's called 'breakage revenue.' OK Cashbag actually makes profits this way. Money is a very sensitive issue, so it's usually handled in the shortest possible time. So, not only do I not understand why it took so long, but the content of the reply was even more preposterous.

| Hello! |
| --- |
| I accidentally touched 'purchase' in the T Store, and it was immediately paid for without any confirmation process. ㅡㅡ; I haven't even played the game. Please refund it. **Hello. This is the T Store Operations Team.** First, we sincerely apologize for not being able to provide a quick response. In the cancellation process, it is not possible to cancel a purchase unless there is an issue due to a content development problem or a content error. When purchasing a product, the process is as follows: Select Product > Click Purchase > Select Payment Method > Complete Purchase (Payment). Purchase processing will absolutely not occur unless the customer presses the 'Confirm' button. We ask for your understanding that we could not provide a satisfactory answer to your request. Thank you. |

**I have never seen a 'select payment method' step.** ㅡㅡ; Just in case, I tried to reproduce it. And it was paid for just as before.

**3. Problems with this payment method**
If such a simple payment system is possible, then refunds should be easy. However, refunds are not allowed. A bigger problem was, **is it okay to spend money without a PIN or SMS authentication process? Is this perhaps illegal?**
Also, **what if children use their parents' phones to download a bunch of games from the T Store???** This is a perfectly conceivable situation.

**4. A story that somehow reminds me of the Nate button**
In the past, feature phones had a Nate button. A very large one, right in the middle. If you accidentally pressed the button, exorbitant data charges would incur. Later, as user complaints grew fierce, they finally added a confirmation step when pressing the NATE button. They say the profits earned that way were quite enormous.

**I ask SKT: Is the current purchase and refund method acceptable to users?** Many people criticized Google's market, and I believe T Store provided excellent solutions in many aspects, showing how a carrier-centric market could operate. However, doesn't this purchase and refund system look like it's setting traps for user mistakes and waiting for profits? They must have at least benchmarked Google Market, so I don't understand why they made it this way. **I believe it's right to either set a refund period like Google or add steps to the payment process, such as creating a PIN.**
