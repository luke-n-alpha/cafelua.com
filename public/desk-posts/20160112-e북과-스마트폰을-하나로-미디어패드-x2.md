​최근 저는 e북을 보기에 맛들렸습니다. 그동안 전자책을 보려고 이것 저것 고민을 많이했습니다. 특히 웹툰이나 페이스북, 기사 등을 모바일로 자주 접하는 저로서는 읽기에 최적화된 기기가 꼭 필요했죠. 그 때문에 여러가지 태블릿도 고민해봤습니다. 그러면서 한가지 계속 포기를 못했던것 중 또 하나가 바로 이동성입니다. 그래서 찾아보던 중 발견한 패블릿이 바로 미디어패드 X2입니다. 역시 이번에도 또 중국산 제품을 소개시켜드릴것 같네요. 우선 미디어패드 X2가 생소하다면, 갤럭시W를 떠올리셔도 됩니다. 7인치 패블릿인 갤럭시W와 유사하지만, 성능과 가격면에서 앞서는 것이 바로 미디어패드X2죠.​

3. Plan and Settings

The plan is the 'No-Conditions USIM LTE 31'.

[**Plans - USIM 3G Plans - No-Conditions USIM LTE 31 | Hello Mobile (4)** cjhello.com](http://cjhello.com/mv_Client/rate/rate_01_01_view.asp?curIndex=4&paymentCode=PDB0000027&tabMenu=C)

350 minutes voice, 6GB data, 350 messages, Olleh Wi-Fi, all for 34,100 won including VAT, no contract. After initial receipt, there's a slight inconvenience like changing the language to Korean, installing a Korean keyboard, and setting up KT APN, but I'm extremely satisfied.

After inserting the Hello Mobile USIM, the APN settings are as follows: When you first insert it, calls work, but the internet doesn't. Settings > Wireless & Networks, More > Mobile Networks > Access Point Names > Menu > New APN Name: Anything... Use KT, and leave everything else as is, except for the following:

APN: lte.ktfwing.com
MMSC: mmsc.ktfwing.com:9082
MMS Port: 9093
MCC: 450
MNC: 08

And then, you need to reboot... for the internet (4G) to work.

I set it up with a monthly plan of 34,000 won. If purchased new, it would probably cost about 100,000 won more. Approximately 430,000 won with a monthly plan of 34,000 won. Isn't that attractive? With reduced bezels and increased phablet popularity these days, even 7 inches is no longer an overly burdensome size for calls. On the other hand, it's great for viewing things, so clear and spacious. I don't think I can ever go back to a smaller one ㅜㅜ I've set it up nicely, feel proud of it, and am diligently reading.
