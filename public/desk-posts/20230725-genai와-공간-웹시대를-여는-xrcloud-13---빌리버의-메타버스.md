**1. 메타버스에 가장 큰 영향을 준 엔데믹**

2023년 들어 메타버스의 가장 큰 변화라면 사실 사회의 펜데믹에서 엔데믹의 전환입니다.  이로 인해 매우 많은 것들이 바뀌고 있습니다. 메타버스라는 키워드가 펜데믹의 수혜주였음은 누구도 부정하지 못할겁니다. 저희도 비대면 가상교실 서비스 기술로 펜데믹때 주목을 받았고, VR웹툰 기업에서 메타버스 기업으로 피봇을 했습니다. 그리고 입밖으로는 꺼내지 않겠지만, 엔데믹으로 인해 많은 분들이 메타버스가 끝난 것 같다라는 이야기들을 조심스럽게 하십니다.

**3. 메타버스를 XR로! 공간 웹 기술의 가치**

**4. 공간 웹의 미래**

저희는 이러한 웹 메타버스를 공간웹이라고 부르려고 합니다. 사실 애플 따라한거 맞습니다. 애플이 메타버스라는 용어 대신 공간 컴퓨팅이란 용어를 사용했고, 저희도 이에 맞춰 공간 웹이라는 용어를 사용해보려 합니다. WebXR은 너무 기술적인 냄새가 나긴 하거든요. 모든 웹은 2D를 넘어 3D로 진화 할 것입니다. 물론 3D웹에  2D가 완전히 사라지는 것은 아닙니다. 정보 전달과 생산성도구면에서는 여전히 2D가 효과적입니다. 실제로 애플의 공간 컴퓨팅도 사실 브라우져 공간에 띄워놓은 형태의 시나리오들을 대부분 보여주었고, 현재 WebXR로 거의 즉시 구현 가능한 기술들에 가깝습니다.  그러면 이 시장 규모는 얼마나 될까요 ? 저는 정확히 XR과 웹시장의 합으로 생각합니다. 국내 시장의 경우 조달청 나라장터의 키워드를 분석해봤습니다. 2020년부터 XR이라는 용어가 등장하여 VR,메타버스 등의 용어와 섞였고, 그 합은 계속 증가하고 있습니다. 이에반해 홈페이지, 웹사이트의 키워드의 양은 계속해서 줄어들고 있습니다. 2023년에는 그 차이가 정말 크지 않더군요. ​ 저는 미래의 웹은 모두 공간 컴퓨팅을 고려할 것이며 공간웹 시장으로 펼쳐지리라고 봅니다. 그리고 이러한 시장은 사실상 인터넷 시장이라 봐도 무방하며, 웹은 거의 모든 IT시장을 이야기 합니다. 따라서 인터넷의 성장은 아직도 많이 남아 있으며, 이는 XR과 합쳐져 공간컴퓨팅의 한 분야로 공간 웹은 지속 성장하리라 기대합니다.​

**1. The Endemic: The Biggest Influence on the Metaverse**

The biggest change in the metaverse since 2023 is, in fact, the transition from a pandemic to an endemic society. Many things are changing because of this. No one can deny that the keyword "metaverse" was a beneficiary of the pandemic. We also gained attention during the pandemic with our non-face-to-face virtual classroom service technology and pivoted from a VR webtoon company to a metaverse company. And though I won't say it out loud, many people are cautiously suggesting that the metaverse seems to be over due to the endemic.

**2. Belivvr's Metaverse - The Internet is the Metaverse**

However, Belivvr doesn't think so. We are a company with the philosophy that the internet is the metaverse. From the beginning, we didn't develop a game-type metaverse or use game engines; instead, we developed a B2B metaverse based on WebXR's web standard technology.

**3. From Metaverse to XR! The Value of Spatial Web Technology**

Meta Quest 3 and Apple Vision Pro are still scheduled for release this year. As a result, I predict that the metaverse will see games decline, and the value of XR industrial applications and spatial web technology will begin to be seriously recognized. There are several factors behind this. In fact, web technologies like WebGPU and WebXR hadn't matured sufficiently, and even in metaverse standard groups like MSF, where we were active, they were only just beginning to discuss 3D file format compatibility. The development of XR devices was also slower than expected due to issues like semiconductor supply, and paradoxically, non-face-to-face solutions gained attention during the pandemic, leading to more focus on traditional PC-based non-face-to-face solutions. The abundant investment market then flocked to Web3, overshadowing the metaverse keyword. However, I believe that this decline of the endemic and Web3 is actually bringing the metaverse back to its essential XR technology. And with spatial computing as its goal, Apple has introduced the Vision Pro. And just as computing environments have always been, I believe that the web will rise alongside apps. Recently, the Web 4.0 initiative was unveiled in Europe.

EU specialist Euroactiv reported on the 9th (local time), citing internal documents from the European Commission, that an initiative on Web 4.0 and virtual worlds would be announced on the 11th. According to the report, the European Commission is expected to introduce the concept of Web 4.0, which blends artificial intelligence (AI), the Internet of Things (IoT), blockchain, metaverse, and extended reality (XR), to "get a head start in the next technological transition." This follows Commissioner Thierry Breton's announcement last September that a non-legislative initiative on the metaverse would be unveiled in 2023.

**4. The Future of the Spatial Web**

We intend to call this web metaverse the "spatial web." To be honest, we're following Apple's lead. Apple used the term "spatial computing" instead of "metaverse," and we also want to try using "spatial web." WebXR sounds a bit too technical. All webs will evolve beyond 2D to 3D. Of course, 2D won't completely disappear in the 3D web. 2D remains effective for information delivery and productivity tools. In fact, Apple's spatial computing mostly showed scenarios where browsers were floated in space, which are close to technologies that can be implemented almost immediately with current WebXR. So, how big is this market? I believe it's precisely the sum of the XR and web markets. For the domestic market, I analyzed keywords from the Public Procurement Service's Nara Jangteo. The term "XR" appeared from 2020, mixing with terms like VR and metaverse, and their combined volume has continuously increased. In contrast, the volume of keywords like "homepage" and "website" has steadily decreased. In 2023, the difference wasn't very significant. I believe that future webs will all consider spatial computing and will unfold into a spatial web market. And this market can practically be considered the internet market, as the web encompasses almost all IT markets. Therefore, the growth of the internet still has a long way to go, and combined with XR, the spatial web is expected to continue growing as a field of spatial computing.
