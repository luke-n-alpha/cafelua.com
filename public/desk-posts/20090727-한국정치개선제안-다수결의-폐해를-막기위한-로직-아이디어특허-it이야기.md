미디어법 통과를 보면서 들은 생각이 현재 현대사회의 민주주의란것이 얼마마 많은 헛점을 가지고 있는지 다시 한번 생각해 볼 수 밖에 없었다. 투표는 LCD화면을 클릭하게 되어있지만, 대리투표가 가능할정도로 얼마나 허술한 시스템인가? 겉만 IT를 입혔다고 첨단인 것이 아니다.

** 폐해1 ) 다수결의 폐해**
대부분의 결의는 의결수를 기반으로 하게 되어 있고, 그 기반에는 다수결을 따른다.
하지만, 다수결이란 현재 한국의 정치처럼 정당위주의 정치에서는 절대로 옳은 정책이 아니다. **다수의 폭력은 결국 의석수의 비율을 반영하지 못하고, 다수가 100%가 되고, 차 후에 다수 당이 되고, 정권을 잡기 위해 정권기간내내 차기 대선을 위한 삽질을 보여준다.**
이번 미디어법 결의의 시선은 한나라당이던 민주당이던 난 곱게 보지 않는다. 이명박 대통령이 미디어법은 정치와 관계 없다고 입에 발린 거짓말을 한다 하는데 차라리 그냥 가만 있는 편이 낳지 않을까 ? 정치와 관계 없으면 좀 내어주면 어떤가? 그게 그렇게 중요한가 ? 어째서 그걸 자기들 뜻대로 처리해야만 다음 민생 법안을 논하겠다고 하는가 ? 민생 법안이 인질인가 ?
경제를 살리는데 민생 법안이 더 여파가 클까? 겨우 수백억짜리 회사 몇개 만드는 미디어법의 여파가 클까 ? 국민들의 기만 좀 그만했으면 좋겠다.

** 그러나, 정당이란 것의 태생이 그렇다면, 현재 한국의 민주주의 제도는 문제가 있다는 의미이다. 환경을 고려하지 않는 시스템만큼 나쁜것이 없다. 고객을 고려하지 않은 프로그램은 얼마든지 악용되기에 충분하다.**
버스를 타고가다가 그러한 고민을 했고, 몇가지 로직을 만들어보았다.

**1. 목표**

Watching the media law pass, I couldn't help but reflect once again on how many flaws democracy in modern society currently has. Voting is done by clicking an LCD screen, but how flimsy is the system if proxy voting is possible? Just because it's superficially dressed in IT doesn't make it advanced.

**Flaw 1) The Flaw of Majority Rule**
Most resolutions are based on the number of votes, and that basis follows majority rule.
However, majority rule is absolutely not the right policy in party-centric politics like current Korean politics. **The tyranny of the majority ultimately fails to reflect the proportion of seats, the majority becomes 100%, then becomes the majority party, and spends the entire term in power digging for the next presidential election to seize power.**
I don't look favorably upon the resolution of this media law, whether it was the Grand National Party or the Democratic Party. President Lee Myung-bak says the media law has nothing to do with politics, a blatant lie. Wouldn't it be better if he just kept quiet? If it has nothing to do with politics, why not just give in? Is it that important? Why do they insist on handling it their way before discussing other livelihood bills? Are livelihood bills hostages?
Will livelihood bills have a greater impact on revitalizing the economy? Or will the media law, which creates only a few companies worth a few tens of billions of won, have a greater impact? I wish they would stop deceiving the public.

**However, if that's the nature of political parties, it means there's a problem with Korea's current democratic system. There's nothing worse than a system that doesn't consider its environment. A program that doesn't consider its users is ripe for abuse.**
I pondered this while riding the bus and came up with a few logic concepts.

**1. Goal**
1) A system where the opinions of the minority are not ignored.
2) An open system that can receive real-time feedback and evaluation from the public.
3) A system capable of diverse applications and feedback.

**2. Method**
**1) Creation of a Bill Object** - Each bill has multiple categories (characteristics). For example, the media law has categories such as economy and media, and categories can have a hierarchy like products. Also, these bills will each have a page on the web.
* The bill page will not have comment sections or poll systems that contain abusive elements.

**2) Real-time Opening of Bill Voting Status** - The voting status of each bill is opened in real-time, showing which National Assembly member made which choice on which bill. This can be used as a log of party tendencies and individual National Assembly members' tendencies for each bill.

**3) Abolish the one-person, 1.0 vote system for National Assembly members, allowing for fractional voting.**
**Purpose: Prevent the flaws of majority rule.**
**- Logic 1) When a National Assembly member votes for the minority side, their vote, once expended, will have n times the voting power in subsequent bills.**

Formula for n:
* When the difference between the maximum and minimum number of seats of political parties is m, n is calculated as n=m√m. For example, if a party with 1 seat and a party with 5 seats are considered the maximum parties, then 5√5 means that even if the party with 1 seat chooses the opposing opinion in all 5 votes, it can still secure voting rights, thus preventing the flaws of majority rule.

**- Logic 2) Allow National Assembly members to check the importance of each bill and determine its weight.**
**Purpose: Better guarantee the rights National Assembly members wish to exercise.**
**Provide data for the public to understand the interests of National Assembly members, which can be referenced in future elections.**
(Let them fight tooth and nail over the media law. The public will remember.)

They can cast more votes on bills they consider more important. Since it's likely they'll consider everything important, the importance will follow a graph like the one below quarterly. The total sum per quarter is designed to approximate 0, and if it doesn't match 0, the remaining weight is carried over and reflected in the next quarter.
I haven't thought about the exact algorithm, but the algorithm would be structured with the following concept.
(If a total of 5 bills are to be resolved in one quarter)

**Case 1] All evaluated as important (actual applied weight gradually decreases)**
{{IMG:1}}
**Case 2] All positive values (actual applied weight gradually decreases)**

{{IMG:2}}
**Case 3] Maximum and minimum values reflected alternately (all reflected normally - actually needs to be an even number of times)**
{{IMG:3}}

**4) Create a field for stating the reason for voting during a vote, visible to the public.**
Purpose: To allow the public to see how sincerely National Assembly members voted and if they provided legitimate reasons.

**5) This data is publicly available on the web without any conditions and provided in a processable format such as Open API.**
- Services like finding the National Assembly member most similar to oneself after voting are possible.

**6) For presidential candidates for whom no previous data exists, this system can present hypothetical bills and analyze the results.**

**Effect: This enables more statistical logic in politics and higher efficiency in democracy. Incidentally, it might also have the effect of penalizing less intelligent National Assembly members. National Assembly members and parties will likely exercise voting rights using advanced techniques. Future need for supplementation if abusing elements are found.**

**Personal Opinion:** In a world where companies use diverse analytical methods and react in real-time (RTE) down to fractional seconds, the political system seems to operate with a stubbornly inefficient structure. The current president's corporate management style when he was CEO is completely different from how companies operate now. If so, isn't it time for the National Assembly to also adopt various ideas and systems to address this?
It's right for National Assembly members to do as they wish, because the public elected them. Of course, I oppose violence. But if it's a flaw of an undemocratic system, it should certainly be corrected.
