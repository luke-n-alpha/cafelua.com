**4. 무선 컨탠츠의 증가**
**{{IMG:4}}**

앱스토어같은 어플리케이션 뿐만 아니라 모바일 네이버, 다음같이 이원화된 웹사이트의 증가가 예상되며 모바일 웹디자이너 모바일 웹프로그래머, 모바일 웹기획자의 수요가 증가가 예상됩니다. it인력의 대우가 좋아질지는 아무도 모르지만 말입니다. 여전히 재취업과정은 it가 대다수입니다. 또한 행정, 은행등이 모바일로 이루어지기 위해 모바일 공공프로젝트도 증가할 것으로 예상됩니다.

**5. 모바일 비즈니스 모델 니즈 증가**
**{{IMG:5}}**
[이미지 출처 : [http://news.naver.com/main/read.nhn?sid1=105&oid=073&aid=0001997979](http://news.naver.com/main/read.nhn?sid1=105&oid=073&aid=0001997979)]

사이트가 이원화되며 비용이 증가되는 측면만큼 비즈니스모델에대한 니즈도 커질 것입니다. 모바일 쇼핑, 광고시장의 영역 확대가 예상되며 이에 대한 최대 수혜자는 가장 많은 이용자와 컨탠츠를 기보유하고 있는 국내포털이 될것입니다. 국내 3대 포탈 중 네이버와 다음은 자사의 컨탠츠를 최대 이용할 것이며 네이트의 경우 skt와의 전략적 제휴를 통해 시장을 공략할 것 같으며 로컬라이징에 익숙치 않은 구글은 국내에서는 PC웹시장보다도 더 낮은 모바일웹 점유율을 갖게 될것입니다. 풀브라우징을 지원한다하더라도 이동중 요구하는 플랫폼은 최소한의 차이라도 갖게 마련이며 이는 국내업체들의 작은차이가 구글의 입장에는 큰 벽이 될것입니다.
그러나 구글의 한가지 가능성이 존재한다면 안드로이드마켓의 성공으로 자발적인 국내개발자들에 의한 컨탠츠 로컬라이징입니다. 이 경우에는 풀브라우징과는 좀 차이가 있고 어플리케이션의 개념이 강할것으로 구글은 국내에서는 모바일 웹에서 얻는 수익보다는 안드로이드같은 모바일 플랫폼에서 얻는 수익이 더 클것같습니다.

**6. 꼭 아이폰이어야 했을까? 그리고 우리의 과제**
**** ****
{{IMG:6}}

이러한 환경적 변화는 아이폰이 아니라 어떤 모바일단말이던간에 이정도의 시장에 임팩트를 주었다면 이러한 변화를 몰고왔을 것입니다. 아이폰이 이러한 환경을 만들게된 기술적 동인만을 따지자면 아주 오래전부터 우리곁에 그만한 단말들은 존재했습니다. 그러나 아이폰은 그 기계가 국내가 얼마만한 점유율을 확보하던간에 풀브라우징과 이동통신망이 아닌 다른망과의 컨버젼스 같은 기술에 파워유저를 넘은 일반인들의 이목을 집중시켰습니다. 이러한 기술은 이미 2g시절에 국내에는 확보되어있었으나 여러가지 환경요인으로 음성통신망 수준에 머물러있었던 것이 사실입니다.
국내의 모바일 환경을 불평하는 분들도 많고, 선진화되지 못한 요금제 탓인지 데이터통신의 이용률은 그리 높지못해도 한국의 모바일 소프트웨어 기술, 무선 인프라기술은 세계의 다섯 손가락에 뽑히는 수준입니다. 이러한 장점을 살릴수 있다면 우리가 아이폰을 먼저 사용했던 해외를 부러워 할 필요는 없을 것입니다. 이제 문제는 환경이 아니라 우리의 행동과 전략이 향후 10년의 먹거리를 만들 수 있습니다. 미적거리면 시장은 기다려주지 않습니다.

** 마지막으로 그러나 미래는 아무도 모릅니다. **제가 이 글을 써 공개한 순간 누군가의 전략에 영향을 주었을지도 모르고, 공개된 예언은 이루어질 가능성은 더 낮아진다고 하죠.  무엇보다도 시장은 훨씬 복잡하고 이들을 움직이는 것은 불확정성을 지닌 사람이기 때문입니다. 사람은 어처구니없는 실수와 예상치못한 대단한 능력을 발휘하곤 하죠. 그러나 새로운 미래가 조금은 우리들에게 더 좋은 세상이길 바랍니다.

The iPhone, which has tormented us with countless rumors, seems to finally be making its appearance. The market atmosphere seems highly encouraged, and Apple devotees who believe in Steve Jobs appear to be preparing their ammunition. Perhaps the significance of the iPhone, which was merely a smartphone, was that it served as an opportunity to inform the general public what a smartphone is, and it played a leading role in transforming regular phones into smartphones. And, if these usage patterns truly become widespread, what changes will come to us? I've thought about this a bit. Of course, these are just my personal imaginings, so they might be nothing more than that.
****
****
**1. Increased Need for Realistic Data Plans**

This need has existed before, but it will now materialize, and the market will move. Of course, we'll have to wait and see how realistic KT's plans will be.

**2. Increase in Home Wireless APs**
**{{IMG:2}}**
[Image Source: [http://blog.naver.com/csr1214?Redirect=Log&logNo=20020411093](http://blog.naver.com/csr1214?Redirect=Log&logNo=20020411093)]

Home APs, already spreading with services like LG 070 phones, are expected to increase with the addition of iPhone users who desire Wi-Fi. This holds potential for future development into home gateways, accelerating subscriptions to TPS (Triple Play Service) and positively impacting the spread of IPTV.

**3. Increased Need for 4G**
**{{IMG:3}}
**[Image Source: [http://blog.naver.com/gojump0713?Redirect=Log&logNo=140054162664](http://blog.naver.com/gojump0713?Redirect=Log&logNo=140054162664)]

While 3G assumes data transmission, it is not entirely IP-based and has bandwidth limitations. Wi-Fi coverage has clear limits unless KT opens Netspot and sufficiently expands its coverage. While on the move, 3G usage will increase as much as Wi-Fi increases, eventually becoming the driving force for all-IP-based 4G.

**5. Spread of VoIP Internet Phones**
** **
[Image Source: [http://blog.naver.com/cyberibh?Redirect=Log&logNo=100016200413](http://blog.naver.com/cyberibh?Redirect=Log&logNo=100016200413)]

Apple had a negative stance on Google's VoIP application, but this seems to have been strongly influenced by its business model with AT&T. Even without the iPhone, the situation is already quite possible due to mobile platforms from MS and Google. The iPhone's great significance lies in already demonstrating the potential of VoIP in mobile communication, and eventually, a new business model from a competing operator could change the market landscape. In Korea, QPS is not technically true convergence yet, but the convergence of internet phones like 070 and mobile phones holds significant potential.

**4. Increase in Wireless Content**
**{{IMG:4}}**
[Image Source: [http://blog.naver.com/joowon77?Redirect=Log&logNo=68595954](http://blog.naver.com/joowon77?Redirect=Log&logNo=68595954)]

Not only applications like the App Store but also an increase in dual-platform websites like mobile Naver and Daum is expected, leading to an increased demand for mobile web designers, mobile web programmers, and mobile web planners. No one knows if the treatment of IT personnel will improve, but IT still accounts for the majority of re-employment processes. Furthermore, mobile public projects are expected to increase as administration and banking move to mobile platforms.

**5. Increased Need for Mobile Business Models**
**{{IMG:5}}**
[Image Source: [http://news.naver.com/main/read.nhn?sid1=105&oid=073&aid=0001997979](http://news.naver.com/main/read.nhn?sid1=105&oid=073&aid=0001997979)]

As websites become dual-platform, costs will increase, and so will the need for business models. Mobile shopping and the advertising market are expected to expand, with the biggest beneficiaries being domestic portals that already possess the largest number of users and content. Among Korea's top three portals, Naver and Daum will maximize the use of their own content, while Nate seems likely to target the market through a strategic alliance with SKT. Google, unfamiliar with localization, will likely have an even lower mobile web market share in Korea than in the PC web market. Even with full browsing support, platforms required while on the move will inevitably have at least minimal differences, and these small differences for domestic companies will be a significant barrier for Google.
However, one possibility for Google exists: the success of the Android Market leading to content localization by voluntary domestic developers. In this case, it differs somewhat from full browsing and will be more application-centric. Google's revenue in Korea will likely come more from mobile platforms like Android than from mobile web.

**6. Did it Have to Be the iPhone? And Our Task**
**** ****
{{IMG:6}}

These environmental changes would have occurred with any mobile device that had such an impact on the market, not just the iPhone. If we consider only the technological drivers that created this environment, devices of similar capability have existed among us for a very long time. However, regardless of its market share in Korea, the iPhone drew the attention of the general public, beyond power users, to technologies like full browsing and convergence with networks other than mobile communication networks. Such technologies were already available in Korea during the 2G era, but due to various environmental factors, they largely remained at the level of voice communication networks.
Many complain about Korea's mobile environment, and perhaps due to underdeveloped pricing plans, data communication usage isn't very high. However, Korea's mobile software technology and wireless infrastructure technology are among the top five in the world. If we can leverage these strengths, we won't need to envy overseas countries that adopted the iPhone earlier. The problem now is not the environment, but our actions and strategies, which can create our sustenance for the next 10 years. If we hesitate, the market will not wait.

**Finally, however, no one knows the future.** The moment I wrote and published this article, it might have influenced someone's strategy, and it's said that public prophecies are less likely to come true. More than anything, the market is far more complex, and what drives it are people with inherent uncertainty. People often make absurd mistakes and display unexpected, great abilities. Nevertheless, I hope the new future will be a slightly better world for us.
