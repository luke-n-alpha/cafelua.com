그 직후 같은 직종의 N회사에 입사했습니다.

돌아보면 가장 총력을 기울였던것은 SK컴즈의 입사때였습니다. 미니홈피를 꾸미고, 지인들의 응원메시지도 적었습니다. 뿐만아니라 SK컴즈의 문제점과 개선점 분석및 포트폴리오까지 만들어서 갔죠.

개발자 출신에 저같은거 해온 사람은 저 밖에 없었다고 놀라더군요. 뿐만아니라 베스트미니홈피 유저였기 때문에, 미니홈피 소개에서도 자신이 있었습니다. 수많은 일촌들의 힘과 응원메시지도 빌렸구요. 사내의 신문사 같은데서는 제 포트폴리오가 특이하다고 사진까지 찍어갔습니다.

그러나 정작 불합격 메시지를 받으니 참 씁슬하더군요. 뿐만 아니라 나중에 예쁜 컵까지 보내줬는데, 어째 깨지는걸 보내줬는지 불평까지 싸이월드 CEO분께 적어 보내드렸습니다. " 제발 깨지는거 불합격 메시지로 보내지 마세요." 라구요..

또한, 1차에서 거르는것이 아니라 몇차에 가까운 전형이다 보니 워낙 많은 에너지를 소모하게 했구요. 굉장히 허탈한 느낌이었습니다.

그리고, 나중에 곰곰히 생각해보니. 개발자로서 원하는 사람은 애시당초 전형에 나온 그러한 인재가 아니었다라는것. 그저 외부에 대외적인 이미지 때문에 미니홈피를 꾸미게 한것이 아닌가 싶었습니다. 또한 그걸 실제로 보더라도 회사입장에서 입장 바꿔 생각해놓고 보니, 미니홈피 잘꾸몄다고 사람을 뽑는다고 생각하니 우습더군요.

덕분에 생각에 대한 방향 전환을 하는 계기가 되었습니다. 후에 동종기업의 N회사에 입사지원을 할땐 철저히 그러한 방향으로 준비를 했죠. 기업이 원하는 진짜 사람은 어떤것일까 ? 신입이지만 이직 성격이 있는지라 이전기업에 대한 물음이 제일 조심스러웠습니다. 처음에는 자신감과 이전기업에 대한 약간의 험담도 늘어놓았지만, 나중에는 오히려 그러한 자신이 후회되어서 심사관에게 제가 한말은 모두 잊어달라고 편지를 써서 건내주고 나왔습니다.

그것이 잘 봐준것인지, 합격이 되었더군요. ^^; 그리고 지금은 잘 다니고 있습니다. 현재 다니고 있는 회사도 3차전형 까지 있는지라 만약 SK컴즈처럼 모든 에너지를 소모하지 않기로 처음부터 마음먹었었습니다. 그러니 오히려 편해지더군요.

그리고, 좀 더 객관적으로 회사가 원하는 사람을 생각해볼수 있었습니다.

아무튼 낙방이란 쓴 기억은 여러모로 약이 됩니다. 하나, 기업에서도 좀 더 솔직하고, 상처라 덜되게 해주었으면 합니다.

그 후 미니홈피를 모두 폐쇄하고, 모든 자료를 블로그로 옮겼죠. ^^;

농담으로는 맘상해서이고.. 어느정도는 회사것을 써보기 위해서입니다.

Immediately after that, I joined Company N in the same industry.

Looking back, the time I put in the most effort was when applying to SK Comms. I decorated my mini-homepage, wrote down messages of support from acquaintances. Not only that, but I also created an analysis of SK Comms' problems and areas for improvement, along with a portfolio, and brought it with me.

They were surprised, saying that among developer backgrounds, I was the only one who had done something like that. Furthermore, because I was a 'Best Mini-homepage' user, I was confident in introducing my mini-homepage. I also leveraged the power and support messages of countless 'Ilchon' (friends on Cyworld). Even internal company newspapers took photos of my portfolio, saying it was unique.

However, receiving the rejection message was truly bitter. Not only that, but they later sent me a pretty cup, and I even wrote a complaint to the Cyworld CEO, asking why they sent a broken one. I wrote, "Please, don't send broken items as rejection messages."

Also, since it wasn't just a first-round screening but a multi-stage selection process, it consumed an enormous amount of energy. I felt incredibly empty.

And then, thinking about it carefully later, I realized that the person they truly wanted as a developer was not the kind of talent presented in the selection process from the start. I suspected that decorating the mini-homepage was merely for external image purposes. Furthermore, even if they actually looked at it, putting myself in the company's shoes, it seemed ridiculous to think they would hire someone just because they decorated their mini-homepage well.

Thanks to that, it became an opportunity for me to change my direction of thinking. Later, when applying to Company N, a competitor, I thoroughly prepared in that new direction. "What kind of person does the company truly want?" Although I was a new graduate, it felt like a job change, so questions about my previous company were the most delicate. At first, I spoke with confidence and even a little gossip about my previous company, but later, I regretted it so much that I wrote a letter to the interviewer asking them to forget everything I had said, handed it to them, and left.

Perhaps they looked favorably upon that, as I was accepted. ^^; And now I'm doing well at work. The company I'm currently with also has up to a third round of interviews, so I decided from the beginning not to expend all my energy like I did with SK Comms. That made it feel much more comfortable.

And I was able to think more objectively about the kind of person the company truly wants.

In any case, the bitter memory of failure serves as a valuable lesson in many ways. One thing, I wish companies would be more honest and make rejections less hurtful.

After that, I closed all my mini-homepages and moved all my data to a blog. ^^;

Jokingly, it was because I was upset... and to some extent, it was to try out the company's own platform.
