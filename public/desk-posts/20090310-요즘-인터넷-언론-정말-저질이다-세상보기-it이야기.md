The Kyunghyang Shinmun, which I subscribe to via NewsCast, had a headline that said **"Government, may close Agora..."**
I was surprised and clicked on it. {{IMG:2}}
[http://news.khan.co.kr/kh_news/khan_art_view.html?artid=200903101754211&code=930201](http://news.khan.co.kr/kh_news/khan_art_view.html?artid=200903101754211&code=930201)
 
 
The gist of it was that the Grand National Party's internet bill could become a poison pill clause allowing them to shut down any website they want, and that a Democratic Party lawmaker mentioned "Agora closure" in their statement.
 
While I certainly oppose the Grand National Party's internet law with its poison pill clauses, it's truly frustrating to see a Democratic Party lawmaker openly connect that law directly to Agora's closure, and for Kyunghyang to report it as if the government had made such a statement.
 The Kyunghyang Shinmun's headline is written as if the government said it could close Agora.
 
I thought Kyunghyang was at least better than Chosun, JoongAng, Donga, and Hankyoreh...
(I've seen much worse from other places in the past.)
There's really nothing to read. {{IMG:3}}
 
And on the main cast page, they put up a picture of human skin... I'm scared kids might see it.
Isn't there any decent newspaper to read?
 
I really shouldn't go near the news... but the clickbait and provocative article titles always make me click.
 
OpenCast seems to have better quality these days.
 
**The media is truly low-quality these days. {{IMG:4}}**
 
Did portals ever truly have the right to comment on the quality of media outlets? {{IMG:5}}
