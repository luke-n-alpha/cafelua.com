** IPTV의 확산 그러나 진짜 IPTV는 없다.**
어느새 많은 사람들이 TPS등의 결합 상품을 계기로 IPTV 가입자가 크게 늘고 있습니다. 라이브방송도입, FTTH같은 초고속 통신망과 미디어법등으로 제도적 환경적으로는 어느정도 배경은 깔린것 같습니다. 하지만 저도 LG mytv를 이용중이지만 아직 VOD서비스외에 IPTV의 본연 장점인 쌍방향 서비스로서 이것이 IPTV라 말할수 있는 서비스는 없는 것 같다는 생각이 듭니다.
이러한 원인에는 현재 기술적 한계와 폐쇄성도 존재하는데 향후 IPTV가 어떤 모습으로 발전해야 시장에서 자리를 잡고 새로운 시장을 열게 위해서는 어떠한 모습으로 진화해야 할것인지에 관해 이야기를 해보려고 합니다.

**1. 미디어베이로서의 발전 가능성**
: 본래부터 동영상을 제생하기 위한 IPTV는 태생적으로 미디어베이로서의 역할을 충실히 갖출 요건을 가지고 있습니다. 홈씨어터와 연결되는 놈도 바로 IPTV 이며, 가정에서 가장 좋은 AV환경과 연결됩니다. 현재 나와있는 HDD형 DIVX플레이어같은것은 충분히 대치될 가능성이 높다고 봅니다.
{{IMG:2}}
** 1) 소프트웨어적인 코덱 지원 필요 **
mylgtv의 경우에 pc의 동영상을 직접 플레이가 가능한데 그 포맷이 매우 제한적이고 코덱이 업데이트가 되는지는 알길이 없습니다. 따라서 다양한 코덱지원을 위해서는 소프트웨어적인 코덱 지원이 필수 불가결합니다.
****
** 2) 멀티미디어 유통기기로서의 발전 필요**
예전에 딴짓하면서 TV의 음악방송을 켜두곤 했었습니다. 한데 dmb satio처럼 음악 전용 방송이 아예 사라졌습니다만 아예 음악 전용 sw 지원이 필요하지 않나 싶습니다. 또한 이러한 기능을 제공할때는 아예 TV가 옵션으로 화면보호되었으면 하고 바랍니다. 또한  IPTV의 STB에 달린 usb를 이용해 mp3 플레이어나 아이팟같은 외장장치의 억세스가 용이 해야 PC를 제외하고 유통기기로서의 성능은 극대화 될것입니다. 이미 음원시장에 자리를 잡은 3대 이동 통신사가  iptv사업자기 때문에 음원 공급문제는 그리 어렵지않게 해결이 가능하지 않을까 싶습니다.

** 3) 가상 스토리지 이용을 통한 장치 동기화**
이때 또한 여러 장치간에 분산된 음원이나 사진, 동영상이 문제가 되는데 이전에 제가 포스팅한 N드라이브같은 네트워크 가상 스토리지 이용할 수 있을 것입니다.

LG전자의 경우에는 이쪽 기술은 눈에 띄는게 없어서 자체 개발은 답이 아니라는 생각이 듭니다. 이러한 모바일 플랫폼은 비디오게임기처럼 화려하지는 않지만, 보드게임과 기초적인 3D처리까지도 가능할 것 이며 개발 단가 또한 낮으리라고 예상됩니다.

** The Spread of IPTV, But No True IPTV Exists.**
 In no time, many people are seeing a significant increase in IPTV subscribers, driven by bundled products like TPS. With the introduction of live broadcasting, ultra-high-speed communication networks like FTTH, and media laws, the institutional and environmental groundwork seems to be laid to some extent. However, even though I use LG mytv, I feel that beyond VOD services, there are no services that truly embody the interactive nature, which is the inherent advantage of IPTV, making it worthy of being called "IPTV."
 These issues stem from current technological limitations and a closed ecosystem. I'd like to discuss how IPTV needs to evolve to establish itself in the market and open up new opportunities in the future.

**1. Potential for Development as a Media Bay**
 : IPTV, originally designed for video playback, inherently possesses the requirements to fully function as a media bay. It's IPTV that connects to a home theater, linking to the best AV environment in the home. I believe that current HDD-type DIVX players are highly likely to be replaced by it.
{{IMG:2}}
** 1) Need for Software Codec Support **
  In the case of mylgtv, it can directly play PC videos, but the formats are very limited, and there's no way to know if codecs are updated. Therefore, software codec support is essential for diverse codec compatibility.
**** 
** 2) Need for Development as a Multimedia Distribution Device**
 I used to turn on music broadcasts on TV while doing other things. However, music-only broadcasts, like DMB Satio, have completely disappeared, but I wonder if dedicated music software support is needed. Also, when providing such features, I hope the TV would have an option for a screen saver. Furthermore, to maximize its performance as a distribution device, excluding a PC, it should be easy to access external devices like MP3 players or iPods using the USB port on the IPTV STB. Since the three major mobile carriers, who have already established themselves in the music market, are also IPTV operators, I don't think the issue of music content supply will be too difficult to resolve.

 
** 3) Device Synchronization via Virtual Storage**
  At this point, distributed music, photos, and videos across various devices become an issue, which could be resolved by using network virtual storage like N-drive, which I previously posted about.

**2. Potential for IPTV to Develop as a Gaming Platform
******
**1) Need for Improvement in Remote-Centric Device Interface**
  myLGTV also has a game section, but trying to play with the remote control drove me crazy. IR devices seem to have inherent limitations. Even the 2.4GHz RF keyboard I used before showed fast speeds from over 10m away.

 To support games that require real-time interactivity, the introduction of devices beyond the remote control is urgent. Wireless keyboards, mice, joysticks, Wiimotes, etc., should support immediate responses and be an expandable platform.
  

{{IMG:3}}
 [Xboard Wireless Keyboard]

**2) Introduction of Other Game/Open Platforms**
**  - Collaboration with Video Game Companies:** What if we collaborated with Nintendo, Sony, and MS? It would be easy for promotions, and they are probably already considering selling software over the internet someday. In such a situation, would they refuse if a certain market share in Korea is guaranteed? This could simultaneously solve various problems like 3D acceleration and development platforms, create an outlet for the Korean video game S/W industry, and even build a market for exports.

**  - Utilization of Open Mobile Platforms:** If we compromise a bit on 3D and lower costs, we could use mobile platforms. OS-X probably won't be supported at all ㅡㅡ; Android and Windows Mobile could be the solution. In this case, it naturally extends to the Mobile IPTV platform. Samsung's proprietary OS used in its Jet phone could also be considered, but as the effectiveness of Samsung's embedded OS policy is still uncertain, it doesn't seem like a good option yet. For LG Electronics, there's no noticeable technology in this area, so I think in-house development is not the answer. Such mobile platforms, while not as flashy as video game consoles, would be capable of board games and basic 3D processing, and development costs are also expected to be lower.
 {{IMG:4}}
 
** 3) Convergence Model of Game Consoles and Broadcasting**
 A convergence model of game consoles and broadcasting already exists under the name of e-sports. However, content specialized for such a model is not yet visible, and the IPTV set-top box is highly likely to be the platform with the highest accessibility to broadcasting. Enjoying game broadcasts while playing games. Will children's addiction become even more severe? At least it's fortunate that the IPTV set-top box is easier to regulate than a PC.

**3. Development towards IPTV 2.0**
 A 2.0 platform that allows participation and sharing can also be introduced to IPTV.
** 1)   Full Browser Integration
** This is, above all, a natural and prerequisite task. If a full browser is properly integrated and the Korean web develops, I believe the electronic payment issues currently caused by ActiveX will soon be resolved.
** 2) Enhanced Search Functionality**
 Currently, some IPTVs don't even support search. It takes ages to find a movie you want to watch with a slow remote control.
** 3) Content Platform Suitable for 2.0**
 A broadcasting platform suitable for IPTV, a content platform that allows user participation, could improve the quality of broadcast participation compared to existing SMS methods.
**4) Expansion of IPTV Software Distribution Platform**
 As briefly mentioned above, if open OSes are used, IPTV has a high potential to function as a software distribution platform, much like an app store.

**4. Full Browsing Portal for IPTV
** Even with full browsing support, the interests of IPTV users and PC users may not be the same. IPTV users are likely to be more "light" users. Accordingly, web pages should focus on services with more viewing than input, and simple feedback like comments without using attachments. If such an environment is provided, domestic portals will automatically start creating OZ-like pages, just as they did before.

 As IPTV performance approaches that of a PC, its usage may decrease, but since preferred content and device resolutions can differ, rearrangement is needed. Also, if various devices like the Wii are provided, the focus will be on creating UX tailored to them. For now, touch doesn't seem to be the answer. ㅡㅡ;
