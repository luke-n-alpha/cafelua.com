얼마전 PAG의 11월 정기 세미나에 참석해서 세미나에 관한 내용 공유드립니다. [**http://platformadvisory.kr/**](http://platformadvisory.kr/)**주제: 밴드 글로벌 진출, 어떻게 할 것인가?
부제: 국산 소셜 벤처의 글로벌에서 살아남기
발표: 원만호 실장(스타트업 캠프모바일)** 회사 업무를 마치고 가다보니 첫번째 세션에 이미 몇십분 정도를 날려먹었지만, 총 두가지 세션으로 이루어졌던 해당 발표는 여러가지로 도움이 되는 자리였습니다. 해당 발표의 내용은 먼저 캠프 모바일의 밴드의 글로벌 전략과 싸이월드의 글로벌 실패 사례에 대한 발표였습니다.
먼저 밴드의 경우에는 최근 라인의 무서운 성장세로 인하여 조금은 주목을 덜 받고 있지만, 캠프모바일의 밴드도 꽤나 괜찮은 성적으로 글로벌에서 성장중에 있습니다. 제가 발표를 중간에 들어갔을때는 이미 원만호 수석부장님이 발표를 시작한 뒤여서 중간 부터 밖에 소개를 못 시켜드립니다.

**2. 싸이월드의 글로벌 진출 실패 사례
** 두번째로 발표된 싸이월드의 글로벌 실패 사례에 대한 분석 내용이었는데요. SK Planet 의 이형주 매니저님께서 발표 해 주셨습니다. 실제 싸이월드 글로벌을 수행했던 경험담을 중심으로 했고, 밴드의 글로벌 진출 전략과 대비되는 면이 많아 시사하는 바가 컸다고 봅니다.  **요약하자면 밴드는 멘땅에 헤딩과 속도의 벤처 정신이 중심에 있었다면, 싸이월드의 경우 한국의 성공사례와 원대한 계획이 그 발목을 잡았다고 할 수 있습니다.

: 단순히,모든 실패한 서비스는 결과론적 이야기,   **- 그럼 진짜 이유는 뭐였을까?**

I recently attended PAG's November regular seminar and would like to share the content of the seminar. [**http://platformadvisory.kr/**](http://platformadvisory.kr/)
**Topic: Band's Global Expansion, How to Do It?
Subtitle: Survival of a Korean Social Venture in the Global Market
Presenter: Manho Won, Director (Startup Camp Mobile)**

I arrived after finishing work, so I missed several tens of minutes of the first session, but the presentation, which consisted of two sessions, was helpful in many ways. The content of the presentation covered Camp Mobile's Band's global strategy and Cyworld's global failure case.

First, while Band receives slightly less attention due to Line's formidable recent growth, Camp Mobile's Band is also growing globally with quite good results. Since I joined the presentation midway, after Senior Director Manho Won had already started, I can only introduce it from the middle.

The presentation mainly discussed the case of entering Thailand.
**1. Band's Global Expansion Case**
The methods for global expansion are as follows:
{{IMG:1}}
In summary, it means to observe carefully, act first, and break through. ^^
Jokingly, people said it was the secret to sales, but it seems the way to do business is the same everywhere in the world. These methods are particularly important globally because:

**There is no established foundation, and the market is largely unknown.**

This characteristic aligns with Cyworld's failure case, which was presented later.
Additional detailed content of the presentation:

**1) Local Characteristics Observed in Thailand, Different from Korea**
- Culture: Manufacturing is not well developed, so foreign cultures are well received.
- Content: Japanese manga, Facebook.
- Consumption patterns: 24-hour convenience stores, cash.
- TV: 100 channels.
- Students: Club activities, stronger discipline and organizational culture than in Korea.

**2) And Local Concerns at the Time**
- Information spreads quickly, but its persistence time is low. (Interests rapidly shift from celebrity news yesterday to bakery news today.)
  Wouldn't it be difficult to continuously spread one brand?

**3) Useful Korean and Local Personnel**
3.1) Korean Personnel
    - PR background with language skills: PR agencies were very important (good cost-effectiveness).
    - Global consulting firm background.
3.2) Local Personnel
    - Famous bloggers (social hubs).
    - Local marketing agencies.

**4) Global Work Stages**
- Groundwork & Sensing
- Social Media Operation
- Small Group Viral (finding the group where the service works best)
- Seasonal Online Events (good) and App Ads (not so good these days)
- BTL -> ATL

**5) Band's Global Strategy**
- Deliver core value: Be the best at groups. (We will only talk about groups.)
- How is it different from Line? How is it different from Facebook?
  : Facebook is a newsfeed, Line is a messenger, Band is groups!
  (Groups from the first page)
- The next social platform that connects users in horizontal groups, regardless of which social messenger (Line, Facebook) they use (Facebook, Line invitations possible).

**6) Band's Current Status**
- Bands created: 7 million
- Revenue model: Not yet, but plans to attach mobile, advertising, games, and content.
- Leverage with Line:
  : Receives a lot of help from Line's brand value.
  : Does not receive much direct help as an installation path.

**2. Cyworld's Global Expansion Failure Case**
The second presentation was an analysis of Cyworld's global failure case. Manager Hyungjoo Lee from SK Planet presented it. It focused on his actual experience in executing Cyworld Global and offered many contrasts with Band's global expansion strategy, providing significant implications.
**In summary, while Band was centered on a venture spirit of "hitting the ground running" and speed, Cyworld's success in Korea and its grand plans ultimately hindered it.**

Additional detailed content of the presentation:

**1) How did Global Cyworld turn out?**
- Started in 2005 and ultimately failed.

**2) Why did it fail?**
- Outsiders easily said it was due to localization failure, strategy failure, or market conditions.
  But let's reconsider.
  : Localization failure: Using Google's strategy for localization? Makes no sense.
  : Business failure due to market strongholds? Facebook was also a latecomer in social networking.
  : Simply, all failed services are retrospective stories.
  **- So, what was the real reason?**
  ** : Too grand a plan, took on too much.**
    -> Worried about and built a service structure for hundreds of millions of users when there were no users. Consequently, it was slow.
    -> Should have started with Japan, but only dreamed of a global scale.

**4) Difficulty of a Global Standard Community that Contains Culture**
- 1:1 communities have global standards: chat, phone, email, MIM... => Evolved into utilities.
- There was no global standard community format: cafes, forums, vary greatly by region.
  Why??? - When many people gather, culture emerges, develops differently depending on the environment, and it's difficult to capture common aspects.
- Community is culture...
- Koreans gather under a leader, with a chief and general secretary - if it grows, branches split off. This cultural aspect is significant, but such a structure is not familiar overseas.
