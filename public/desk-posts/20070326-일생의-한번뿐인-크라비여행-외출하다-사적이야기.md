1. 일단 안가본 사람 많다. (남들과 똑같은건 별루..)

My first overseas trip and my honeymoon destination was Krabi, Thailand.
It was a place chosen based on the recommendation of Gaya Travel Agency at a wedding expo.
For Thailand, it was a bit expensive at 1.7 million won per person,
but I had no regrets.
 
The reasons for choosing were:

1.  Not many people have been there. (I don't like doing the same things as others..)
2.  I don't like overly busy places. (My daily life is already tough, so travel shouldn't be tough too.)
3.  Shouldn't it be beautiful?

We were captivated by the photos of the cave restaurant.
 
{{IMG:1}}
      [The photo that captivated us] 

After going, the cave restaurant wasn't a real cave, OTL.
It was actually a recessed area within a cliff
(but it was still awesome).

After getting married on November 4, 2006, and spending a night in Incheon,
we boarded a plane early in the morning from Incheon Airport~
{{IMG:2}}
It was a Thai Airways plane.
It was harder than going to Jeju Island.
The seats, which were almost upright, made my back hurt.

The first thing that greeted us was a double-decker tour bus.
Come to think of it, it looked a bit similar to the Seoul tour bus in Seoul.
As expected, the view was good from up high.
{{IMG:3}}

And the first foreign meal we had was MK Suki..
{{IMG:4}}
No matter how I look at it, isn't this fish cake soup?
It seemed like shabu-shabu with fish cakes instead of meat...
Still, the fish-shaped fish cakes were cute. ^^

Our first night spent at the Hilton Hotel in Bangkok..
As expected, Bangkok nights were wonderful.
The hotel was also great..
{{IMG:5}}
[Night view from the hotel and a glass of wine]

The reason we departed in the morning and arrived at night was not to rush straight to the resort,
but because we wanted to experience an overseas city directly.
Moreover, we were incredibly lucky that day because it was a festival day.
The river was bustling with people floating paper flowers and making wishes.
Below, there were also people picking up the floated paper flowers. (To sell them again, perhaps?)
{{IMG:6}}
[People floating paper flowers]
{{IMG:7}}
[And people picking them up again]

Anyway, we tried it too.
We wished for a good life and (being Christians) prayed as well.

Then we roamed the market streets of Bangkok.
We bought slippers and clothes for a few thousand won.. and ate street food..
Should I say it had a bit of a Namdaemun Market vibe?
The goods sold in the market were incredibly cheap.

We also bought a phone strap from an old lady selling goods on a pedestrian bridge.

When we gave her paper money, she gave us change in coins.
We were surprised.

I can't quite remember how many baht it was, but I definitely had paper money with the same number written on it.
We wondered if there was a currency smaller than baht and if the old lady was trying to trick us, so
we asked for paper money.
Later, we found out that there are indeed coins. ㅜㅜ (Sorry, Grandma~)

While roaming the night streets, fireworks lit up the sky~
{{IMG:8}}
Thinking we had really timed it perfectly, we had fun taking photos..

The next day, we took another domestic flight to Krabi.. (Oh, it's far)

Rode a van~ and then a speedboat~~

We finally arrived ^^

The first thing we were given upon arrival was a refreshing tropical fruit juice..

And that flower, it looked like acacia or wisteria..
It smelled good too.

Then we listened to the staff explain about the resort
and went to the Smurf house.
 
{{IMG:9}}
  [Ta-da~ This is where we stayed]
 
{{IMG:10}}
   [2nd floor bedroom]

{{IMG:11}}

Inside, there was exotic music and the fragrant smell of wood
and a truly beautiful two-story interior.
I thought it might be hot because it's the tropics, but the interior was surprisingly cool.
There were no bugs either.
On the first floor, delicious cookies were constantly refilled.
However, the drinks in the fridge were extra charge!! (We didn't drink them. ㅋㅋ)
 
And just a short walk away, a wonderful sea and pool
{{IMG:12}}
[Beach]
{{IMG:13}}
[Pool]
{{IMG:14}}
Usually, people go to a pool villa for their honeymoon, but since the sea and a large pool were so close there,
we didn't specifically choose a pool villa. In fact, some brides who saw monkeys swimming and urinating in pool villas at night were so horrified they refused to go in.

Upon entering, there was also a faucet where you could wash your feet.
{{IMG:15}}

The coastal cliffs were on par with the admiration I felt when seeing them in Jeju Island..
The water color was truly emerald.

And another fun thing was the exotic food
{{IMG:16}}
But I missed gochujang and kimchi.. (Sunchang~!)
I had packed travel gochujang in a tube, but I regretted forgetting it every time we went to a restaurant. ㅜㅜ
If you're sensitive to greasy food when traveling abroad, it's good to bring some~

I went to get an aroma massage, and they only gave me one embarrassing pair of panties.
{{IMG:17}}
[Aroma massage room: You lie face down on that bed. And those ridiculous mesh panties]

I had to hear the ladies say "don't be shy."
Honestly.. it was an even more embarrassing pair of panties for a man.
When getting the massage, I must have been tired and lay down, and the next thing I remember was being told to wake up ㅡㅡ;
What on earth happened..?

Time quickly passed as we mostly played at the beach.

This time, we went snorkeling.
Wonderful scenery even on the way..
{{IMG:18}}
And we went into the water....

I took a few shots with the underwater camera I got from the travel agency,
but none of them turned out well, so I later got an earful from my wife ㅜㅜ
On top of that, wanting to take more photos, I put my camera into the water using an aqua pack..
but the pack accidentally leaked.. ㅜㅜ and I sent my A80 to camera heaven.
Because of this, I felt so sorry to my wife that I couldn't say anything.
The atmosphere was tense ~~

Everyone, let's not take cameras into the water.
It seems better to buy more underwater cameras instead.

And finally, during a brief stop back in Bangkok, we bought gifts for our parents and friends..
(90% of the money we brought was spent here)
I bought my wife a garnet, her birthstone, a red gem.
Because it was much cheaper than in Korea~..

And in the evening, we saw
a gay show~, Calypso show
{{IMG:19}}
It was surprisingly fun.
We couldn't see the whole thing because we had to make it back for our flight.. but still.. it was a unique experience.
And coming home, I was really tired.

But, it's a place I definitely want to visit again.
I want to save up separately and go back with my wife someday.
{{IMG:20}}
I hope that when I go back, it will still be unpolluted and not crowded,
and that its clean nature will be preserved as it is.
