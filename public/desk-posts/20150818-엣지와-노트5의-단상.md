**Why Were the Galaxy Note 5 and Galaxy S6 Edge+ Divided?**
Samsung ultimately released the Galaxy Note 5 and Galaxy S6 Edge+ separately. Except for the design, these two products are almost identical twins. The Galaxy Note 5 comes with an S Pen, while the Galaxy S6 Edge+ features an Edge display... thegear.co.kr

http://thegear.co.kr/9140

The Edge's design is unique and suitable as an icon for Samsung, which produces displays. However, the problem is that the Edge display is unlikely to establish itself as a killer feature that *requires* purchasing that specific device. If you consider whether the iPhone was able to create differentiation solely with its hardware design, the answer becomes simple. What Samsung needs is a unique software service. Personally, I suspect the reason for the separation of the Edge display and the Note Pen is technical. The integration of the Note Pen, or Wacom pen, generally makes devices thicker or heavier, and depending on the manufacturer, distortion can be severe towards the edges of the screen, or lines can appear shaky. So, wouldn't it be impossible to integrate an electromagnetic pen into a curved screen, or at least difficult to maintain its quality?
