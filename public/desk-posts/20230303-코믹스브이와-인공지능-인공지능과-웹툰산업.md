Sharing internal presentation materials from 2019 regarding the AI development efforts previously undertaken by Believer (formerly ComicsV). At that time, if you talked about AI in webtoons, everyone heard it like it was someone else's problem, but the market changes brought about by Stable Diffusion, ControlNet, and LORA are astonishing. Of course, I don't think this is the definitive answer now (generative AI has presented many challenges), but I'm sharing it because I believe it can offer several implications for the current comic/webtoon market.
