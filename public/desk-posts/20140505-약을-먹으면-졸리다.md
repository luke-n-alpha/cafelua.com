**Taking Medicine Makes Me Sleepy**

{{IMG:1}}

2002. 11. 06
That's right.. Taking medicine made me sleepy.. --;
After taking medicine, I dozed off for an hour at the PC bang.
A customer woke me up, paid, and left.. --;

by Forest Baby
**2014. 05. 05**
At that time, I was working part-time at a PC bang. Back then, StarCraft 1 and Diablo 2 were hugely popular, and if there was a representative image of that era, it would probably be the PC bang. There were quite a few customers who would just run off without paying, so the person who woke up a dozing part-timer, paid, and left must have been a very conscientious person. If there were more people like that in the world, wouldn't it be a much better place than it is now?
