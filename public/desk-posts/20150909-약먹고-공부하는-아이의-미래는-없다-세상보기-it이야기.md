A post I saw on Facebook..

What I want to say in this post is: **"10 years from now, parents won't even be able to take responsibility for their child's life."** If that were possible, I'd recommend starting a business instead. If you could foresee 10 years into the future, you could achieve success that would make Zuckerberg envious. In Korea, professions ending with 'sa' (like doctor, lawyer) are already saturated, and academic competition has to be global, making it endless. So, unless a child is truly passionate about academics and loves studying for its own sake, I wouldn't recommend it much. If it's concentration maintained by medication, I'm sorry, but the smartphone you're holding does a better job. The emergence of software that can maintain endless concentration and stamina as long as batteries are supplied, and can already perform tasks requiring high-level knowledge comparable to university graduates, is predicted within the next 10 years (or even 5 years). You cannot develop 'problem-solving' abilities with medication. Medicine is for when you're sick, not a buff. In the game StarCraft, using a stimpack provides very high efficiency for a short period, but a single zergling attack can kill you. Therefore, stimpacked marines are only meaningful when they move in groups. In other words, if your goal is just to make your child a component of a large group, it will be difficult to survive after the group's collapse. And in a future with high uncertainty, the survival cycle of large groups becomes very short. Who do you think is ruining your child's future?
**The conclusion is that a time is coming when individual resilience will be crucial.**
What kind of future are you preparing your child for right now?
