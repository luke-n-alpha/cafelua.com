** 위의 조건들을 만족하는 것을 찾다보니 결국은 값싼 안드로이드 태블릿들도 탈락하고 남은 것이 아이나비 계열의 안드로이드 기기 밖에 없더군요. OTL. 스마트A와 K9, K11 정도가 그 타겟에 올랐는데, 결정적으로 K11을 선택하게 된 이유는 인스턴트 부트때문이었습니다. 네비게이션은 빠른 부팅이 중요하죠. 안드로이드 네비게이션을 선택하겠다고 마음 먹었을때, 미처 생각하지 못한 결정적인 부분이었습니다. 안드로이드기기는 윈도우 기기 대비 부팅이 느리다라는 거였습니다. 아무튼 그것 때문에 값싼 K9보다 조금 더 돈을 주고 K11을 선택했습니다. 내장 배터리 용량이 작아서인지 광고한만큼 매일 바로바로 켜지지는 않지만, 자체 부팅 속도만 해도 무척 빨라 썩 쓸만했습니다. 매년 업그레이드 비용을 내야하지만, 항공사진 네비게이션은 정말 여태까지 모든 네비게이션 지도 대비 가장 보기 좋더군요.  **(항공사진은 32기가를 쓰는게 좋다고 합니다. 그런데 업그레이드 할때 50기가의 빈공간이 필요합니다. 업그레이드를 대리점에서 돈받고 해주기는 하는데, 충분한 용량의 하드디스크가 있는 컴퓨터를 확보하는게 정신건강상 좋습니다. )**

**2. 아이나비를 구매한 후 넘어온 산들**** 1) 일반 매립용 젠더 사용 불가, 자사 컨트롤박스 사용 의무** 기존에 있던 기기를 떼고 아이나비대리점에 매립하러 갔더니, 일반 네비게이션의 확장 케이블이 아닌 별도의 컨트롤 박스를 사야 한다고 합니다. 매립 부자재를 반품하고 다시 대리점을 갔더니, 대리점에서 보유를 하고 있지 않다는것, 그런데, 그 컨트롤 박스 조차 재고 없음. 그런데 의외로 문제는 간단히 해결됬습니다. 기기의 자동부트를 꺼두었다가, 기기 이상으로 판단하고 수원의 AS점에 갔더니, 그곳에서는 팔더군요. 그래서 구매를 해서 다시 아이나비 대리점으로 고고싱. 그럭저럭 매립했습니다. 일반 매립용 젠더나 케이블이 안된다는 사실을 일단 곧이 곧대로 믿을 수는 없겠지만, 컨트롤 박스에 디스플레이 끄는 버튼이 유용해서 그냥 만족했습니다. 디자인도 나쁘지 않구요.
** 2) 컨트롤 박스에 마이크로 SD카드의 USB젠더 사용시, 연결 불량** 마이크로 sd카드를 usb젠더에 넣고 컨트롤 박스에 꽂아 쓰니, 네비게이션이 동작하는 중 자꾸 외장 메모리와 연결이 끊어집니다. as점 갔는데, 마이크로 sd카드 젠더를 의심하시더군요. 결국, 일반 USB올때까지 대기중입니다. (이걸로 해결되기 바래요 ㅜㅜ)

**Searching for something that met the above conditions, even cheap Android tablets were eliminated, leaving only iNavi-series Android devices. OTL. Smart A, K9, and K11 were among the targets, but the decisive reason I chose the K11 was its instant boot feature.** **Fast booting is crucial for navigation.** **This was a critical aspect I hadn't considered when I decided to go with an Android navigation system.** **Android devices boot slower compared to Windows devices.** **Anyway, because of that, I paid a bit more than the cheaper K9 and chose the K11.** **Perhaps due to the small internal battery capacity, it doesn't turn on instantly every day as advertised, but its own boot speed is very fast, making it quite usable.** **Although I have to pay an upgrade fee every year, the aerial photo navigation map is truly the best-looking compared to all navigation maps I've used so far. **(It's recommended to use 32GB for aerial photos. However, 50GB of free space is needed for upgrades. While dealerships can perform upgrades for a fee, it's better for your peace of mind to have a computer with sufficient hard drive capacity.)****

**2. Hurdles after purchasing iNavi**** 1) Cannot use general embedded adapters, mandatory use of proprietary control box** When I removed my old device and went to an iNavi dealership for embedding, I was told I had to buy a separate control box, not a general navigation extension cable. I returned the embedding accessories and went back to the dealership, only to find they didn't have it in stock, and even the control box itself was out of stock. However, the problem was surprisingly simple to solve. I had turned off the device's auto-boot, then, assuming a device malfunction, I went to the AS center in Suwon, and they sold it there. So I bought it and headed back to the iNavi dealership. I managed to get it embedded. While I couldn't entirely believe that general embedding adapters or cables wouldn't work, I was satisfied because the display off button on the control box was useful. The design isn't bad either.
** 2) Connection issues when using a micro SD card USB adapter with the control box** When I inserted a micro SD card into a USB adapter and plugged it into the control box, the navigation system kept losing connection with the external memory while operating. I went to the AS center, and they suspected the micro SD card adapter. Ultimately, I'm waiting for a regular USB drive. (I hope this solves it ㅜㅜ)
** 3) K11 does not have iNavi Apps (Market).** The K11 does not have iNavi Apps. I found this out after purchasing, and it made me think I should have bought the K9 instead. Thanks to this, to get apps, I have to use other black markets or extract APKs from other devices. ****4. Disadvantages****
1) Need for smooth UX between app environment and navigation transition**
The app environment and navigation transitions are not smooth. Pressing the home key in the upper right corner is definitely a burden while driving. I also thought it might have been better if the notification bar was exposed as is even in iNavi Air.
**2) Keyboard hell. Give us a market!!**
The portrait keyboard is hell.. Why did you make it that way? iNavi Apps, please!!
**3) Voice function is really bad**
For voice search, couldn't you just use Google's when connected to the internet? You can't actually speak the destination for the important destination search using voice search. To give commands by voice, you have to touch it every time, so why use the voice function if you're going to touch it?
**4) Daum Road View integration is not great**
No use for it. It won't work anyway if there's no internet connection. I thought it might be better to just preload Naver Maps or Daum Maps and support search on both.
**5) iNavi seems to be focusing only on high-end embedded models**
This is how I feel about post-purchase support. I thought the K11 was high-end too...
