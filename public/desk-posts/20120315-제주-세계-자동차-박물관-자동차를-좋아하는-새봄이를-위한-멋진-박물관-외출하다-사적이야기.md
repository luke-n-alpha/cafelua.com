얼마전 제주도로 여행을 다녀왔습니다. 3월인데다가 궂은 날씨로 인해 좀 추웠습니다. 더군다나 저희는 이제 16개월의 새봄이가 있었던 관계로 아내에게 이런 날에 휴가를 잡았다고 무척 원망을 들었습니다. ㅜㅜ 미리 일정을 잡은지라 바꿀수도 없고, 며칠전에 날씨를 확인한 후 우천시 갈만한 곳을 최대한 잡아두었습니다. 1박은 제주 중문에서, 2박은 섭지코지 근처에서 하기로 했던 관계로, 우선 첫째, 둘째날은 제주 동부와 중문을 돌기로 했죠. 제주 여행기를 또 별도로 올릴 것도 같습니다만, 우선 오늘은 첫날 여행지 중 한군데 였던, 세계 자동차 박물관을 소개시켜드릴까 합니다.  다녀왔던 곳 중 아이와 가족들의 반응이 가장 좋았던 곳이고, 블로그 이벤트 까지 하고 있더라구요.{{IMG:1}}
**1. 이동과 도착** 소셜 커머스에서 얻은 반값 비행기표를 타고 도착한 제주공항은 비가 오고 있었습니다.일기 예보상 3박 4일 중에 3일이 비가 온다고 했으니 .... 안습. 그러나 다행히 첫날 외에는 비가 안왔습니다. 대신 3월의 제주는 바람이 찼죠. 이러한 날씨에 맞춰 첫날은 실내 이동을 주로 하였습니다. 그래서 잡은 곳 중 한군데가 바로 세계 자동차 박물관입니다. 위치는 제주 동남부 정도로 보시면 되겠습니다.

사실 이곳은 첫날의 두번째 행선지였습니다. 첫번째는 테지움 사파리였고, 두번째로 잡은 곳이 바로 세계 자동차 박물관이었죠. 첫날에 겨우 두군데 였지만, 반값 비행기라는 메리트 때문에 2시넘어서 도착한 제주의 두군데 일정도 빠듯했습니다. 세계 자동차 박물관은 17시까지 입장 가능하고, 18시에 문을 닫거든요. 테디베어 박물관에 비해 테마가 있다고 생각해 결정한 테지움 사파리를 후다닥 들르고 도착한 자동차 박물관 주차장으로, 이동. 도착한 박물관은 비가 여전히 내리고 있고, 건물과 꽤나 떨어져 언덕위에 있었습니다. 앞의 주차장을 지키는 아저씨는 박물관 앞까지는 자동차 진입불가라고 안내를 해주시더군요. 때마침 캐리어가 고장나 우산을 꺼낼수가 없는 사태가 발생했습니다.  (언제나 제 여행은 다이나믹합니다. {{IMG:2}})   덕분에 우산도 없이.. 새봄이를 꽁꽁싸서 언덕길 뛰어 오르기 (헉.헉. {{IMG:3}} )야외에도 멋지구리한 자동차들이 있었지만, 비가오는 관계로 사진한장 못남기고 한달음에 언덕길을 뛰어올라, 세계 자동차 박물관에 도착했습니다. 참고로 주차장 복귀시에는 티켓팅 하시는 분께 말씀드려 혼자 주차장으로 내려가서 자동차를 끌고 올라와 가족들을 픽업했습니다.
**2. 입장** 티켓팅은 제주모바일로 바코드 찍고 입장. (일반 8000원, 모바일 쿠폰등의 쿠폰 이용시 6800원) 그리고, 새봄이 카 선택. 제가 이곳을 선택한 결정적인 이유는 아이가 자동차를 타고 관람할 수 있다는 점이었죠. 어린이 자동차가 저희집에는 없는데.. 어디가서 새봄이는 이런 어린이 자동차만 보면, 안내리겠다고 난리입니다. 자동차의 한쪽 문이 열리는데, 이걸 열면, 가겠다는 건줄 알고, 자동차를 탄채로 이챠! 하며 문을 닫아버립니다. 역시 자기가 제일먼저 이 자동차를 보고 달려들어 올라탔습니다.﻿﻿﻿{{IMG:4}} **[ 이거 내 차! ]**﻿﻿﻿사진은 감기기운이 있어 새봄이 얼굴상태가 안좋아 보이는데, 사실 무척 좋아하고 있는 사진임을 참고 해주시길 바랍니다. 이곳은 이렇게 부모가 아이의 자동차를 밀어주면서 관람이 가능합니다.

I recently traveled to Jeju Island. It was March, and the bad weather made it a bit cold. Moreover, since we now have Saebom, who is 16 months old, my wife complained a lot about me booking a vacation on such a day. ㅜㅜ Since the schedule was set in advance, we couldn't change it, so after checking the weather a few days before, we made sure to plan as many indoor places as possible in case of rain. Since we planned to stay one night in Jungmun, Jeju, and two nights near Seopjikoji, we decided to explore eastern Jeju and Jungmun on the first and second days. I might post a separate travelogue about Jeju, but for today, I'd like to introduce one of our first-day destinations: the World Automobile Museum. It was the place that received the best reactions from the child and family among all the places we visited, and they were even holding a blog event.
{{IMG:1}}

**1. Travel and Arrival**
Jeju Airport, where we arrived on a half-price plane ticket obtained from social commerce, was raining. The weather forecast said it would rain for 3 out of 4 days and 3 nights.... so sad. However, fortunately, it didn't rain except for the first day. Instead, Jeju in March was windy and cold. To match this weather, we mainly moved indoors on the first day. One of the places we chose was the World Automobile Museum. Its location can be considered in the southeastern part of Jeju.

Actually, this was our second destination on the first day. The first was Teseum Safari, and the second place we chose was the World Automobile Museum. Although it was only two places on the first day, due to the advantage of a half-price flight, our schedule for these two places in Jeju, arriving after 2 PM, was tight. The World Automobile Museum allows entry until 5 PM and closes at 6 PM. After quickly visiting Teseum Safari, which we chose because we thought it had more of a theme compared to the Teddy Bear Museum, we moved to the automobile museum parking lot. The museum we arrived at was still raining, and it was located on a hill quite a distance from the building. The man guarding the parking lot in front informed us that cars were not allowed to enter up to the museum entrance. Just then, our carrier broke, and we couldn't get our umbrella out. (My trips are always dynamic. {{IMG:2}}) Thanks to that, without an umbrella... I bundled Saebom up tightly and ran up the hill (pant, pant. {{IMG:3}}) There were cool cars outdoors too, but due to the rain, I couldn't take a single photo and ran up the hill in one breath, arriving at the World Automobile Museum. For reference, when returning to the parking lot, I spoke to the ticketing staff, went down to the parking lot alone, drove the car up, and picked up my family.

**2. Admission**
Ticketing was done by scanning a barcode with Jeju Mobile. (General 8,000 won, 6,800 won when using coupons like mobile coupons) And, choosing Saebom's car. The decisive reason I chose this place was that children could ride in cars while touring. We don't have a children's car at home... but whenever Saebom sees one of these children's cars, she makes a fuss about not getting out. One side door of the car opens, and if you open it, she thinks it means she's going, so she closes the door with an 'Eechya!' while still in the car. As expected, she was the first to see this car, ran to it, and climbed in.
﻿﻿﻿{{IMG:4}} **[ This is my car! ]**﻿﻿﻿
Please note that although Saebom's face looks a bit unwell in the photo due to a cold, she was actually very happy. Here, parents can push their child's car around while touring.

**3. Exhibits**
Upon entering, we were greeted by stylish classic cars. It was nice to experience a different charm compared to a motor show. You can see various old cars from around the world.

They say that even 'yeolyeodeol-gye' (eighteen steps/ranks) were created to ride this car... What a name. Our ancestors' naming sense, reminiscent of Kim Eo-jun. If I keep talking about this, my blog might be designated as harmful to youth.

It's like watching the process of internal combustion engine cars pushing out horse-drawn carriages. Now, if you replace 'horse-drawn carriage' with 'electric car,' it would be the same, wouldn't it? Regulations for electric cars need to be established.

**4. Traffic and Driving Experience**
﻿Towards the end of the exhibits, such experiences were also possible. ﻿{{IMG:13}}

 {{IMG:14}}
 It's possible even in the rain. Saebom loves cars so much that I suggested we let her try, but I only got scolded by my wife... 'It's raining and cold, where are you going to let her do that?' They even said they'd give a driver's license...

**5. Cafe and Souvenir Shop**
As expected, the end of such a course is always the souvenir shop. ﻿{{IMG:15}}

Most of them were car models that could also be seen in Seoul, but after seeing the real cars, the desire to own even a model naturally arises. I guess I'm a child too. However, since it was only the first day of our trip and we had many expenses ahead, I postponed my desire to buy anything for now.

**6. Overall Review**
If you explore thoroughly, it would probably take quite a long time, but the automobile museum, which we toured feeling like we were taking a stroll and taking photos...
