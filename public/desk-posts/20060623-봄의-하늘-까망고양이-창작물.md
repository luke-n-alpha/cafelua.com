문득 하늘을 보니 봄이 와 있었다.
풍덩 빠져 죽고 싶은 파란색.

그제서야 문득 2004년이구나 하는 생각이 들었다.

어느새 시간은 스물중반을 넘어
나만 홀로 남겨두고 훌쩍 여기까지 와있었구나

2004. 02. 15

{{IMG:1}}

Suddenly, looking at the sky, spring had arrived.
A blue I want to plunge into and drown in.

Only then did it suddenly occur to me that it was 2004.

Before I knew it, time had passed my mid-twenties,
leaving me behind, alone, and had come all the way here.

2004. 02. 15
