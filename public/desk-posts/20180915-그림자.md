When evening comes, shadows lengthen.
They lengthen and lengthen,
until night falls and they are buried in darkness and disappear.
But I wonder if it's not that my shadow kept getting longer and longer, until it became night.
Tears become stars..
I see myself, exhausted from the day's routine.
That's why night becomes the time of my shadow.

2004.01.30
