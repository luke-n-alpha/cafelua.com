**1. n스크린이란 ?
** 애플의 tv, 아이폰, 아이패드로 이루어지는 전략을 3스크린 전략이라도 일컬어집니다. 이는 애플이라는 플랫폼과 애플을 통해 구매한 컨텐츠를 사용하는 통합 컨버젼스의 개념이죠.
그에 반해 한국의 기업들은 전통적인 가전제품의 강자로서 n스크린전략을 기획하고 있습니다. 다양한 제품들이 하나의 컨텐츠를 공유할 수 있다는 것이죠. 이중에 가장 기본적인 것이 웹과 앱이 tv로 녹아드는것을 들 수 있습니다.
그런데 여기에 사용자 컨탠츠를 공유하는 기능으로서 등장한것이 클라우드 서비스입니다. 네이버의 n드라이브와 kt의 u클라우드, 그리고 u플러스의 u플러스박스 같은 개념이 바로 이것이죠. 이러한 서비스는 사용자의 파일을 웹의 스토리지에 올려 스마트폰과 pc, 향후에는 tv와 패드등에서 볼수 있는 개념입니다. 그러나 이러한 기능을 위해서는 네트워크가 반드시 필요합니다. 웹에 올려져 있는 컨텐츠이기 때문이죠. 이동통신사의 3g를 통해 플레이하는것은 무제한 요금제가 아닌 이상 부담스럽기 마련입니다. 때문에 현재는 이러한 클라우드 서비스를 이용하는데는 wifi 이용률이 높습니다.

**1. What is n-screen?**
Apple's strategy, consisting of Apple TV, iPhone, and iPad, is referred to as the 3-screen strategy. This is a concept of integrated convergence that uses the Apple platform and content purchased through Apple.
In contrast, Korean companies, as traditional powerhouses in home appliances, are planning an n-screen strategy. This means that various products can share a single piece of content. Among these, the most basic example is the integration of web and app content into TVs.
However, cloud services have emerged as a feature for sharing user content. Concepts like Naver's nDrive, KT's uCloud, and Uplus's Uplus Box are examples of this. These services allow users to upload their files to web storage and view them on smartphones, PCs, and in the future, TVs and tablets. However, these features absolutely require a network connection, as the content is stored on the web. Playing content via a mobile carrier's 3G network can be burdensome unless you have an unlimited data plan. Therefore, currently, Wi-Fi usage is high for utilizing these cloud services.

**2. A taste of n-screen through Wifi100 + Digital Photo Frame**
How far has this n-screen strategy come? Most people are familiar with smartphones, but smartphones are always carried around and are devices that demand focus, so they are actually closer to computers. This time, I'd like to talk about an analog-like device that supports Wi-Fi but isn't as complex as a computer. It's a photo frame.
A Wi-Fi-enabled digital photo frame recently appeared in our house. My wife had been getting a lot of grief for just taking pictures with her digital camera but never printing them or putting them in a frame, so this item is my savior.

**3. Many devices trying to access the network, that's n-screen**
The n-screen strategy can include various devices such as wall clocks, refrigerators, cameras, and navigation systems. You could take a picture with your iPhone, upload it directly to Uplus Box, and then use it as a background for a wall clock or navigation system.
This is n-screen and part of ubiquitous computing. In the home, wireless will have to be responsible for the network, and currently, Wi-Fi is the most suitable technology for this. As more diverse devices connect wirelessly, wide bandwidth and speed must be guaranteed.
This is why protocol technologies like IPv6 and network technologies like Wifi100 are needed.
