Actually, my Galaxy S2 contract ends at the end of May, and I've been continuously interested in the latest smartphones. Since there are various phones at the company, it's great that I can directly try them out with just a little interest. At this point, I've been looking at various pros and cons. So, I'd like to briefly write down my conclusions on the various products I've experienced.

| **Galaxy Note 2** | **Optimus G Pro** | **Vega R3** | **Galaxy S4** | **Vega Iron** | col6 |

**1. If I had to choose from models currently on sale?**
- Optimus G Pro
* The reason I excluded Vega No.6 is that in competition with Note 2 and G Pro, it couldn't get good scores in all aspects except price, and it was too big (even the Note 2 is a burden!).

**2. If I had to choose from models that will be sold in the future?**
**- If money is no object, coolly S4 ㅜㅜ (Why ask?)** / If not, still G Pro? Or Vega Iron?
- If not, I'd probably compare the Iron and G Pro (its performance isn't much higher than the G Pro, but if the price is low enough... Actually, the Iron seems more like an upgraded version of the R3, and the smaller battery than the R3 is a concern.) Looking at the G Pro's screen, the Iron will likely seem small. After trying the G Pro in the sample room and then seeing the Galaxy S2... it felt like moving from a 40-pyeong apartment to a 20-pyeong one. Ultimately, I need to at least try them out before deciding.
*** The reason why the price merit feels greater than performance is that actual performance has largely become standardized at a high level. That's why I didn't compare CPU or RAM. (This is also what a friend who makes smartphones at S Electronics told me yesterday. 'I choose the cheap one. They're all pretty much the same.') If the smartphone subsidy 'ice age' continues like it is now, the price merit will become even greater, won't it?****
