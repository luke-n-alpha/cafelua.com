The weather was so nice, so I grabbed my camera at lunchtime and explored a nearby park.

It's a season I've seen for over thirty years,
but perhaps because I've been busy lately, the flowers looked unbearably more beautiful.

At times like these, I wish I could draw, but I'm content with just photos.

{{IMG:1}}

{{IMG:2}}

But then again, aren't people more beautiful than flowers?

The sight of people out in the park, filled with the scent of spring, made for an even warmer and more leisurely afternoon.
I guess candid photography is my hobby...
Definitely planning an outing this weekend!
