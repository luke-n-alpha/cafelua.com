어제에 이어 역시 ICT산업의 일부인 게임산업에 대해 이야기하겠습니다. 그리고 사실 게임산업은 ICT뿐만 아니라, 소설, 영화, 음악의 대중문화 콘텐츠 산업의 일부기도 합니다. 때문에 이 두가지를 버무려 이야기를 할까 합니다. ** 1. 게임은 장려해야할 콘텐츠 산업이 아니야?**우선 제가 이 포스팅을 작성하게 된 결정적인 이유는 문캠프와 박캠프의 게임 '셧다운제'에 대한 입장 표명 때문입니다. **참고 :경실련 정책선거 도우미**[http://vote.ccej.or.kr/new/test/1211_04popup.php?q_idx=131](http://vote.ccej.or.kr/new/test/1211_04popup.php?q_idx=131) **
여기서, 제가 가장 문제삼고 싶은 부분은 빨간색 줄을 쳤고, 제가 찬성하는 부분은 파란색 줄을 쳤습니다. **2. 박캠프의 의견 검토**  **게임셧다운제의 실효성을 높이기 위해서는 모바일게임 셧다운제도 필요함**   : 실효성이 증명된 적 없습니다. 셧다운제 이후 학업능력이 향상됬다던지에 대한 보고는 없습니다. 오히려 문재인 후보의 답변에서 처럼 주민번호 도용의 부작용이 크게 대두되고 있습니다. ** 게임중독예방을 위해서는 무엇보다 가정의 관심과 지도가 중요하고, 사회는 게임이외에 청소년이 즐길 수 있는 오락․여가 문화 환경 조성에 힘써야할 것임**  핵심은 잘 짚으셨다는 생각이 듭니다.

**3. 박캠프의 결어는 그야말로 경악! ** **게임에 몰두하는 아이들의 열정과 집중력을 창의적 생산성으로 승화시키도록 할 것임** 저를 가장 분노하게 만든 대목입니다.
** -> 게임은 소모적인 마약에 불과 ?**이 문구의 의견은 게임에 몰두하는 아이들의 열정과 집중력은 소모적인 시간낭비라고 정의하고 있습니다. 제 타임라인에 이 부분을 올렸더니 모 선배님께서는 **'**** 아... 그래서 내 인생이 이렇게 Jot 같구만. 겜 끊고 자X나 할걸 그랬네'** 라고 하시더군요. IT분야에서 말하면 다 아는 회사 다니시면서 열심히 일하시는 분입니다. ** 아무리, 실현방안이 끝내주는 것을 내놓으셔도 게임을 소모적인 문화로 정의한 순간, 이미 게임산업에 대한 시각은 마약산업에 불과한겁니다.**** ****참고 :   문화 체육관광부의 2011년 콘텐츠 산업백서**[http://www.mcst.go.kr/web/dataCourt/research/researchView.jsp?pSeq=363](http://www.mcst.go.kr/web/dataCourt/research/researchView.jsp?pSeq=363){{IMG:1}}
그 시끄러운 한류의 음악보다 비중은 두배가 넘고, 연평균 증가율 역시 더 큽니다.이 표에 의하면 **대한민국에는 9조원의 마약이 유통**되고 있군요. 더군다나 글로벌 수출 비중으로 따지면

마약(디아블로)를 사려고 모여있는 대한민국 국민들의 우매한 모습입니다. 경찰 불러서 쫓아내야겠어요. 셧다운제 해야 하니 12시 넘으면, 애들은 다 쫓아보낼까요? {{IMG:3}} 배용준님과 디아블로님과의 차이는 잘생겼다 밖에 차이가 없습니다. *** 게임뇌 이론을 절대선으로 믿는 분이 혹시 박캠프에 계실까 싶어 링크도 첨부합니다.**[http://isao76.egloos.com/2368204](http://isao76.egloos.com/2368204)[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_brain](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_brain)**his theory became popular in Japan while Japanese scientists ignored him,** so the Japanese neuroscientist leader Tadaharu Tsumoto claimed in 2006 that they must criticize even outlandish theories like Mori's[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_controversy](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_controversy)  캠프에서 확증되지 않은 미신 자료를 들고 오시는 분이 계시면 당장 내쫓으셔야 합니다. **코리아 3대 미신에 하나를 더 추가해야겠군요.**** 게임은 결단코, 소모적인 문화활동이 아닙니다.** 참고 : 게임중독 존재해도 규제에 반대하는 이유**[http://fstory97.blog.me/70147843013](http://fstory97.blog.me/70147843013)** **4. 문캠프의 의견 검토**** 상당히 교과서적인 답변입니다. 점수를 깎지도 크게 높게 주기도 어려운 것 같습니다. **** 관련 업계 종사자, 시민사회단체, 학부모 등 다양한 의견수렴후 확대시행 여부를 결정해야 할 사안**임어쩐지 국민의 의견을 들어보고 하겠다라는 안캠프 스타일의 느낌이 나지만, 이 부분은 잘 모르는걸 섣불리 건드는 것 보다는 훨씬 낫다고 봅니다.
오히려 실행계획면의 구체성면에서는 박캠프에 비해 낮은 수준이라는 것은 조금 낮은 점수를 줄 수 있겠네요.
**5. 부모와 아이를 하나로 묶은 교육에 의한 접근** 개인적으로 하나 제안을 드리자면, 부모교육 의무 프로그램을 신설해야 된다고 봅니다. 대한민국 국민이면 예비군 훈련을 받듯이, 부모면 부모다워 질 수 있는 의무 교육프로그램 말입니다. 콘텐츠, 게임 산업 이야기를 하다가 갑자기 이게 무슨 이야기인가 하시겠지만, 이 교육이 필요한 이유는 부모가 콘텐츠 시대에 적응을 하지 못하고 있기 때문에 많은 문제가 발생하고 있기 때문입니다.

Following up on yesterday, I will discuss the game industry, which is also a part of the ICT industry. In fact, the game industry is not only part of ICT but also part of the popular culture content industry, encompassing novels, films, and music. Therefore, I'd like to blend these two aspects into my discussion.

**1. Isn't Gaming a Content Industry That Should Be Encouraged?**
First, the decisive reason I wrote this post is due to the statements from the Moon camp and Park camp regarding the game 'shutdown system'.

**Reference: Citizens' Coalition for Economic Justice Policy Election Helper**[http://vote.ccej.or.kr/new/test/1211_04popup.php?q_idx=131](http://vote.ccej.or.kr/new/test/1211_04popup.php?q_idx=131)

**Q. Should the game shutdown system be expanded to include mobile games to prevent game addiction among teenagers?**

**Park Geun-hye A.** Agree / **To enhance the effectiveness of the game shutdown system, a mobile game shutdown system is also necessary.** In addition to implementing the game shutdown system, **family interest and guidance are paramount for preventing game addiction among teenagers, and society must strive to create recreational and leisure cultural environments that teenagers can enjoy besides games.** The reason teenagers fall into game addiction is that the social climate, which emphasizes only studying, significantly curtails outdoor activities, leading to more time spent at home, where games are chosen as a form of entertainment. Therefore, the Saenuri Party intends to **increase the number of professional counselors in elementary, middle, and high schools nationwide from the current 5% to 100% and promote a policy that provides not only face-to-face counseling between teachers and students but also real-time SNS-based counseling services.** Furthermore, by dispatching 'media education specialists' to elementary, middle, and high schools nationwide to conduct 'Pride Media Education,' which includes media content creation activities and proper SNS usage, **we will help children's passion and concentration in games sublimate into creative productivity.**

**Moon Jae-in A.** Other / The game shutdown system has both positive and negative aspects. Emphasizing only legal enforcement measures could lead to side effects such as the contraction of related industries and the theft of teenagers' resident registration numbers. Therefore, **this is an issue that should be decided after gathering diverse opinions from industry professionals, civil society organizations, parents, and others.**

Here, the parts I want to highlight as problematic are underlined in red, and the parts I agree with are underlined in blue.

**2. Review of Park Camp's Opinion**

**To enhance the effectiveness of the game shutdown system, a mobile game shutdown system is also necessary.** : Its effectiveness has never been proven. There are no reports of improved academic performance since the shutdown system was implemented. On the contrary, side effects such as resident registration number theft are becoming a major issue, as mentioned in candidate Moon Jae-in's response.
**Family interest and guidance are paramount for preventing game addiction among teenagers, and society must strive to create recreational and leisure cultural environments that teenagers can enjoy besides games.** I think the core issue has been well identified.
**Increase the number of professional counselors in elementary, middle, and high schools nationwide from the current 5% to 100% and promote a policy that provides not only face-to-face counseling between teachers and students but also real-time SNS-based counseling services.** The specific implementation plan is good. Was this a reflection of my previous comment on the Facebook timeline to someone from the Park camp, where I suggested an 'education'-based approach? However, I believe 'parents' are more important than 'teachers'.

**3. Park Camp's Conclusion is Absolutely Appalling!**

**We will help children's passion and concentration in games sublimate into creative productivity.** This is the part that angered me the most.

**-> Is gaming merely a wasteful drug?** The opinion in this phrase defines children's passion and concentration in games as a wasteful use of time. When I posted this on my timeline, a senior colleague commented, "**Ah... so that's why my life is so messed up. I should've quit gaming and done something else.**" He works diligently at a well-known IT company. **No matter how excellent the proposed solutions are, the moment gaming is defined as a wasteful culture, the perception of the game industry is reduced to nothing more than a drug industry.**

**4. Review of Moon Camp's Opinion**

**This is a fairly textbook answer. It's hard to deduct points or give a significantly high score.**
**This is an issue that should be decided after gathering diverse opinions from industry professionals, civil society organizations, parents, and others.** It somehow gives off an "Ahn camp" vibe of wanting to hear the public's opinion, but I think this approach is much better than rashly meddling with something one doesn't fully understand.
However, the lower level of specificity in the implementation plan compared to the Park camp might warrant a slightly lower score.

**5. An Education-Based Approach Uniting Parents and Children**
Personally, I would suggest establishing a mandatory parent education program. Just as South Korean citizens undergo reserve forces training, I mean a mandatory education program that helps parents become more parental. You might wonder why I'm suddenly talking about this amidst discussions of the content and game industries, but this education is necessary because many problems arise from parents' inability to adapt to the content era.

**First, from an ICT perspective, I hope it includes content roughly like the following:**
- How to distinguish good games from bad games
*  Examples of various games, currently popular games
- The impact of games and the internet, and their history and direction of development
- How to control a child's game time
- Content copyright education
- Internet and game etiquette education
- Education on the negative and positive effects of the internet
- Personal information protection education
I believe this must be made **mandatory education**. And the curriculum and lecture content should be updated frequently.

And the global content created by those 100,000 people could be seen by hundreds of millions.
Please consider the stance on the content industry.
