**Luke Feat:** "Will there come a time when anyone can create and use simple apps with Vibe Coding?" I can unequivocally state that **"it will never come"** and would bet my entire fortune on it. (As some of you may know, I no longer have a fortune to bet...) In fact, **this assertion was once a catchphrase repeatedly shouted in the field of software education.** However, reality was different. The reason is very simple: **Most users don't even know what service or app they truly want.** And the process of concretizing that 'want' – **this is precisely the core of software education and development.**

**The True Value of Software Education**
After the nationwide software education initiative, did everyone create apps? The answer was **no**. Does that mean software education failed? **Absolutely not.** What we learn through this education is **an understanding of how the software we use daily operates and the process by which it is created.** Furthermore, it provides the ability to communicate smoothly with software development experts and the knack for systematically concretizing ideas even in non-software fields. I still take pride in having contributed to this area during my time at SPRi.

**The True Purpose of Vibe Coding**
I believe the important message Vibe Coding aims to convey lies precisely here.

**"The goal is not to create apps. The true objective is to learn the concept of collaborating with AI agents and to understand the internal workings of AI."**

The clearer this point becomes, the more convinced I am that pair programming with AI (or AI coding) requires a completely different direction and technological development. Since Vibe Coding is so popular, I wonder if I should just go along with the term, but the process **isn't really 'vibey' in practice.**
