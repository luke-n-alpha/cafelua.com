어제인가.. 제 후배랑 자판기에서 커피를 꺼내 마시는 도중
후배가 쿠키를 가져왔다고 해서 하나 꺼내주었습니다.
쿠키는 엄지 손가락하나 크기의 빨간색 비닐포장..

그래서 생각해본 아이디어가
자판기 종이컵에 종이컵 디자인과 딱 어울릴만한 쿠키를 붙여주면 어떨까 하는 상상이었습니다.
커피와 쿠키.. 어울리죠 ?

쿠키도 종류별로 고를수 있으면 좋고...
가격은 물론 조금 더 올라가겠지만.. 쿠키값이야 얼마 안돼니...

현재는 종이컵을 주르륵 밀어넣는 방식이지만
아마 이렇게 되면 종이컵도 색상과 디자인도 차별화 시키고 옆에 쿠키봉지가 붙어있으니
주욱 껴서 넣기는 조금 어려울듯합니다만 생각해보면 안될것도 없지 않을까요 ?
압착식으로 순간적으로 붙여서 떨어뜨려주는 방법도 있고..

처음에는 그저 쿠키와 종이컵이 떨어져 나오는걸 상상했지만
종이컵에 빨간 쿠키 봉지를 붙여보니 크기나 디자인이 너무 맘에 들어서.. ^^

쿠키와 커피도 좀 고급화 시키면 조금 가격을 올리더라도
웰빙이라고 하여 잘 팔릴듯한데.. 어때요 ? ^^;

First Written: 2004. 04. 01
(Filed for patent in Dec 2004: Approval is completely uncertain)

Yesterday, I think... While I was getting coffee from a vending machine with my junior colleague,
my colleague said they brought cookies, so I gave them one.
The cookie was in a red plastic wrapper, about the size of a thumb.

So the idea I came up with was
the thought of attaching a cookie that perfectly matches the paper cup's design to the vending machine's paper cup.
Coffee and cookies... they go well together, don't they?

It would be great if you could choose cookies by type...
Of course, the price would go up a bit... but the cost of a cookie isn't much anyway...

Currently, paper cups are dispensed by being stacked and pushed in,
but if this were implemented, the paper cups would also have differentiated colors and designs, and with a cookie packet attached to the side,
it might be a bit difficult to stack and insert them. But if you think about it, couldn't it be possible?
There could also be a method of instantly attaching and detaching them using a compression mechanism.

At first, I just imagined the cookie and paper cup coming out separately,
but when I attached a red cookie packet to the paper cup, I really liked the size and design... ^^

If we also make the cookies and coffee a bit more premium, even if the price goes up a little,
they might sell well as a "well-being" product... What do you think? ^^;
