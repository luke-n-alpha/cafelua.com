Sitting at home after a long time,
I didn't know what to draw...
But with the thought of wanting to draw...
I tried my hand at this and that.

As I drew...
The thought of wanting to leave suddenly came to me.

The sky... the sea...
More than the usual office worker's wish to go on a trip,
I envy their freedom.
