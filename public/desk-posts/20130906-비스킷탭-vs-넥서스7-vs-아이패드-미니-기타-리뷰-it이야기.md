**1) Performance**

These are Antutu 4.0 scores. The Biscuit Tab is overwhelmingly superior. The Biscuit Tab recorded around 19,700 points, higher than the Galaxy S3, while the Nexus 7 1st generation recorded 13,700 points, between the Galaxy S2 and HTC OneX.

**2) Screen**
{{IMG:3}}
Due to the browser screen and software screen bar, the same screen shows a smaller area.
{{IMG:4}}
Naver app, subtly different. The Biscuit Tab operates with an adaptive UI. It seems to be right on the border of ppi. Note that since the Nexus 7 has not received an OS upgrade, I don't know how it will change after an upgrade. Another characteristic of the IPS-equipped Biscuit Tab is its strong white expression.

**3) Thickness**
{{IMG:5}}
In terms of thickness, it's subtly thinner than the Nexus 7. The weight feels similar by hand, but the Biscuit Tab's metallic back panel feels more premium compared to the Nexus 7's plastic back panel.

**4) Other**
What makes the Nexus 7 better than the Biscuit Tab is the inclusion of GPS and an ambient light sensor.

**2. Biscuit Tab (190,000 won) vs. iPad Mini (350,000 won)**

**2) Screen**
In fact, the 8-inch iPad is overwhelmingly superior.

Even with the same IPS, the iPad Mini has better white expression. (As I always say, after using it for a while, they're all pretty much the same.)

**3) Thickness**
**iPad Mini Wins!**

**4) Other**
The biggest strength of the iPad is its tablet-specific apps. Android tablets still don't have many applicable apps. On the other hand, many domestic services don't properly support the iPad, which is another inconvenient reality! We'll talk about this again when we discuss apps.

**3. Group Comparison**
{{IMG:8}}
Nexus 7 1st Gen / Biscuit Tab / Kindle Fire 1st Gen / iPad Mini
The characteristics are clearly evident. The Nexus 7 with its widgets spread out, the Biscuit and Amazon divided into books and apps, and the iPad with its apps spread out. The goals of each device are clearly revealed on the first screen. I considered comparing it with the Kindle Fire 1st generation, but its hardware is inferior to the Nexus 7, so I didn't.

**\* Summary: While the Biscuit Tab is somewhat inferior to the iPad Mini, it is clearly a much better device than the Nexus 7 1st generation. No matter how much "Nexus pride" there is, it would probably feel a bit unfair to be compared to the Nexus 7.**
