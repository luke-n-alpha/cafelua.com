There will be days when you cry so bitterly in life, but there will also be days when you rejoice like your dad. 

 
The baby born in the next delivery room at a similar time, even after letting out its first cry,
stopped crying only after reaching the nursery.

On a happy day like today, perhaps because I felt the same way as a new father, I almost cried too.
Dad is grateful for you, who cried healthily, and for God, who allowed you to be.
