**I Don't Like Drawing Men**

{{IMG:1}}

2002. 03. 14
I have to draw my character.. but I don't want to. --; I really don't like drawing men..
by SoopSokAegi
**2014.05.05**
This is a story many people who enjoy doodling or drawing comics as a hobby would relate to. They mostly like drawing characters of the opposite sex, which is probably a feeling akin to longing for the opposite sex. If someone calls me an otaku who likes loli characters, I can't really make any excuses, but **I am now married to 3D.** (And she's a beauty recognized by others.) Could there be any more definitive proof than this?

Still, I still like drawings of beautiful girls that purify my heart.

Guess this is my otaku coming out...
