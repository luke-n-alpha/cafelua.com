Damn it...
2002.07.262015.05.11
I don't remember why I drew this picture.
Therefore, I couldn't find a suitable title.
(There are no titles in Cyworld's galleries.)
However, the sense of frustration felt in the picture
seems to remain the same.

The feeling that something is terribly wrong
And, a sense of alienation

Even now, sometimes I feel like drawing pictures like this.
