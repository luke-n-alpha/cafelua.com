Listening to the broadcast, it seems there are a few misunderstandings, so I'd like to clarify.

**1. Irregular play will cause the system to collapse without resistance.**
AlphaGo recalculates with every single move. AlphaGo doesn't have a grand plan that it follows. Therefore, irregular play simply means that a probabilistically low input value is entered. It doesn't cause problems because the data is missing. And among the 160,000 data points, there is amateur data, and artificial intelligence often has noise intentionally introduced. This is to make it react to irregular play or unexpected data. When reinforced in this way, it shows lower results for data that only plays by the book, but to improve overall performance, it's better to achieve 98% performance in the best situation and 60% in exceptional situations, rather than 100% in the best situation and 10% in exceptional situations. It's safe to say that it never becomes 0. If you thought AlphaGo's strategy (?) would collapse due to irregular play, it seems you misunderstood AlphaGo from the beginning. AlphaGo reads the board with every move and calculates with every move to derive the best result.

**2. Difficult problems, shaking it will make it ponder more.**
That's not true. Whether the probability is low or high, the search speed is similar. Therefore, no matter what you do, the speed is mostly the same. If it takes a short time to derive the optimal result, it's truly the best move, and within the time limit, it will derive only the best value from the data searched within that time limit.

**3. There are no techniques that only humans can do.**
There are no such things. Artificial intelligence is not function-based programming. It models human habits and skills. Therefore, if a human did it, AI would do it. If humans don't do it, AI tends not to do it either. Also, self-learning is an algorithm called artificial intelligence, which originally refers to programs that learn. This was already done decades ago. It was just a matter of performance and data.

It seems there's a misunderstanding that AlphaGo is a low-level AI based on rules (heuristics). AlphaGo is the product of collective intelligence and big data analysis of human Go game records. Lee Sedol is currently fighting against the results of 160,000 game records of big data. That's all there is to it.
