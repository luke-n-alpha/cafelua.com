This animation, which concluded with 26 episodes, is a coming-of-age drama about Tohru, who was always ostracized by playing the role of an onigiri in the Fruits Basket game, and people cursed by the Chinese zodiac who transform into animals when embraced by the opposite sex. (My interpretation is weird~) --;

At first glance, it might seem like a love story with one man and one woman, or even a love triangle, but it's actually a story about human relationships. Hurting each other, embracing each other..

And relying on each other... And honestly acknowledging oneself and taking a step forward...
Watching this animation, I somehow find myself reflecting on myself and the people around me. And it gives me strength. ^^ Let's always move forward brightly and boldly.
