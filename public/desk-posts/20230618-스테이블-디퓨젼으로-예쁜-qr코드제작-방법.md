Hello. This is Luke from Believer. I'm currently producing AI webtoons with Stable Diffusion, but I'm also thinking of expanding beyond webtoons to various other uses. One of them is the creation of artistic QR codes. This was made by referencing the YouTube video below.

To briefly summarize, it's implemented by applying Stable Diffusion's i2i and two ControlNets. The final result I made first is as follows:

{{IMG:1}}

QR code to promote LOOOL.IO

Pretty, isn't it? Berry, the character created by Believer, always looks beautiful. Actually, for the original image, you prepare the QR code to be composited and the original image as shown below. Even if the QR code is artistically composited onto the face, it would look strange if there were blotches, so adjust the size carefully. (The QR code was made on Naver. I used to plan the final revamp of Naver QR codes... I'm really proud that I made it and continue to use it so well.)

They are **control_v1p_sd15_brightness** and **control_v11f1e_sd15_tile**. Both are applicable to SD1.5 models. One adjusts brightness, and the other, judging by its theme, seems to adjust 3D objects based on it.
First, put Berry's image into i2i, and then put the QR code image into ControlNet 0 and ControlNet 1. Of course, the output resolution should all be the same, right? And the values I set are as follows:
- **Controlnet0**
    - Preprocessor : inpaint_global_harmonius
    - processor : control_v1p_sd15_brightness
    - Control Weight : 0.35
    - Starting ControlStep : 0
    - Ending Control Step : 0.6
- **Controlnet1**
    - preprocessor : inpaint_global_harmonius
    - processor : control_v11f1e_sd15_tile
    - Control Weight : 0.8
    - Starting ControlStep : 0.3
    - Ending Control Step : 0.75

Then, if you run it diligently, several QR codes will be generated. Among them, some don't work because they're too heavily altered, and if the QR code area is small, recognition might not work well. Below are some failed or candidate images.

** * Also, if the brightness difference changes during printing, QR codes may not be recognized well. In our case, we created a QR code, and it was recognized well on a scroll, but failed on a business card. ㅜㅜ Please test it before printing. **** - Addendum. Please do not print QR codes in reverse (white on black). Most QR code readers cannot scan them. (Theoretically, it's not impossible, but developers are lazy.)**** ** As a former Naver QR code planner, I think it would be good if Naver supported this... but the recognition rate is case-by-case and quite difficult. If they could improve it by fine-tuning QR codes and measuring predictive recognition rates... (It's not my service anymore, so I don't know. We should just focus on our own work.)**
