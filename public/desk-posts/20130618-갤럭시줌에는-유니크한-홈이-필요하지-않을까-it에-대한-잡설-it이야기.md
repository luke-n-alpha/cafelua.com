**1. The Need for Galaxy Zoom**
Samsung has introduced an unconventional model called the Galaxy Zoom.
{{IMG:1}}**[ Photo Source : [http://hi-tech.mail.ru](http://hi-tech.mail.ru/) ]**

It's a device that somewhat appeals to me, and perhaps in two years, I might keep an eye on the Galaxy Zoom series. My need for taking photos of my child is very high, and because I frequently use social media like Facebook and KakaoStory, there's a high demand for high-performance smartphone cameras. The reason I bought the Galaxy S3 LTE was also because I rated its camera performance higher than the G Pro or Vega Iron. From that perspective, while I won't buy the Galaxy Zoom right now, it's true that it's a model that appeals to me.

**Camera Specifications**
16-megapixel, 10x optical zoom, (OIS) Optical Image Stabilization, 24-240mm, F3.1 - F6.3, ISO max 3200, BSI CMOS
If it had a quad-core, 3000-series battery, HD resolution, and about 2GB RAM, it would have been decent enough to use as a main device. It seems the performance is a half-step lower, perhaps to position it as a sub-device. Will mobile carriers try to block the call function again? It might end up with a call restriction in addition to the hardware restriction. While sales volume may not be mainstream, I hope it at least has call functionality to expand user choice.
**3. Most Disappointing Software, Galaxy Zoom Needs Its Own Home**
The Galaxy Zoom's first screen uses Samsung's TouchWiz as is.
{{IMG:3}}
**[ Looks like a regular Galaxy when seen this way ]**
Of course, TouchWiz Home, being verified and unbiased, is clearly a well-balanced and good launcher. However, I wonder if the basic TouchWiz UX is not suitable for leveraging the inherent functions of the camera and social network.
The answer to this can rather be found in Facebook Home.
{{IMG:4}}
A spacious, photo-centric screen, and friends' social feeds...
If Samsung were to create a separate Home for the Zoom, shouldn't it look like this? For the domestic market, it could even allow users to choose a theme from Facebook/KakaoStory/Cyworld.
Doesn't a device with unique hardware need unique and different software? I hope Samsung considers this.
