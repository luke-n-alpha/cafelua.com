Yeah.. something about this doesn't feel right.. it feels wrong.. I'm living intensely, without a moment to spare, but it seems all I've gained is an exhausted heart.. Sometimes I feel sorrowful.. Even though there's nothing to be sorrowful about.. I just feel that way. I always fought so well... With a sword in my hand, all I gained were more wounds in my heart.
