**Moon Fishing**
{{IMG:1}}
2002. 05. 03
I guess I should have brought a ladder.

**2015. 03.24**
I didn't draw it with any particular thought in mind. In fact, pictures of placing a ladder on the moon, or trying to catch it with a butterfly net, were very common clichés in fairy tales. However, now it feels dry, as if this kind of leisure, scope of imagination, and range of expression have narrowed. By the way, when was the last time I made up my mind to go stargazing or moon-gazing...? It's always in the sky, but I'm only looking at the ground.
