모바일의 강자 카카오에 대한 주목도는 과거에 비해 많이 시들해진 모양입니다.

정확히 말하면 스마트폰에 대한 신규 기기와 새로운 앱에 대한 주목도 자체가 낮아지지 않았나 하는 생각이 듭니다.

그런 와중에 오래간만에 카카오 뮤직은 현재 2013년 10월 5일 기준하여 구글플레이 1위를 기록하고 있습니다.  **{{IMG:1}}****[ 카카오톡을 넘은 카카오 뮤직? ]**  과연 이 앱이 성공할 수 있을까?

혹은 어떤 의미를 가질까 하는 이야기를 좀 해볼까 합니다. **1.

디지털 음악을 듣는 네가지 방법**** 1) 소유의 다운로드**  디지털 음악을 듣는 방법을 나눠보면 세가지가 있는듯 합니다.

그 중 한가지는 itunes에 보여준 다운로드 구매형 모델입니다.

1곡을 사서 내 단말에 저장해서 듣는 방법입니다.

이는 음반을 구매하는 전통적인 모델에 가장 흡사합니다.

그러나 개인적인 견해를 드리자면, mp3이전의 음반 구매시장은 음원을 구매하는 것이 아니라, cd나 테이프는 만질수 있는 물리적인 소장의 개념이었습니다.

음악 뿐만 아니라, 타이틀의 질감, 색상등도 사실 구매의 요소였던 셈이죠.

그러나, 디지털에서 이 가치는 제로에 가까워졌고, 이 모델은 힘을 잃게 됩니다.** **** 2) 요즘 대세 정액 요금의 스트리밍** 음악을 듣는 최근의 가장 대중적인 방법은 스트리밍입니다.

더군다나 이 방식은 월정액으로 매우 싼 가격으로 접근이 용이합니다.

월 3,4천원이면 거의 모든 음악을 언제라도 들을 수 있습니다.

그러나, 이 방식에 대해 음원 제작자들은 많은 불만을 갖는데 이유는 음원을 한번 들을때 제작자에게 지불되는 가격이 매우 낮다라는데 있습니다. ** 3) 인터넷 방송** 세번째는 인터넷 방송입니다.

이는 특정 사용자를 중심으로 같은 시간에 큐레이션 된 곡을 듣습니다.

곡을 듣는 사용자는 사실 돈을 지불하지 않습니다.

방송을 하는 사용자만이 다운로드형으로 mp3를 잔뜩 받아두고 어플리케이션으로 브로드캐스트를 합니다.

사실상 이 방식은 이용자에게는 무료에 가까운 모델입니다.

채팅창을 통해서 청취자는 신청곡을 쓰고, 채팅창이나 음성을 통해 BJ는 청취자와 커뮤니케이션을 하는 타입입니다. ** 4) BGM** 카카오 뮤직은 위 세가지 모두 아닙니다.

정확히 말하면, 과거에 한참 붐이 있었던 미니홈피나 블로그의 BGM방식입니다.

카카오 뮤직은 음악을 구매한 사용자가 타임라인에 게시하고, 이를 소셜 친구들과 함께 듣습니다.

미니홈피와 블로그가 완전 개방형이었던것과 달리, 카카오의 친구들은 대체로 200명 이내라는 점이 다르죠.

물론  미니홈피의 일촌친구들은 역시 200명 내외였습니다.

그러나 url로 접근이 가능한 미니홈피는 bgm을 검색해주는 변칙 어플리케이션이 나오면서, 원하는 곡을 찾아서 미니홈피만을 뛰어다니며 듣는 어플리케이션까지 등장했었습니다. **2.

카카오 뮤직은 왜 BGM모델을 들고 왔을까?** 위에서 말씀드렸듯이 카카오 뮤직은 최근 대세인 정액 스트리밍이 아닌 과거의 BGM모델을 들고 왔을까요?

그 이유는 바로 카카오의 베이스는 소셜을 근간으로 하려고 하기 때문입니다.

정액 스트리밍이 강세인 이유 중 하나는 매체의 효과가 강력한 TOP100위주의 음원 소비가 강하기 때문입니다.

내 친구가 어떤 음악을 듣는지보다, 요즘 어떤 음악이 더 뜨는지가 더 궁금한게 사실 대한민국의 음원 트랜드입니다.

어제 슈퍼스타K가 방송했다면, 오늘 나는 반드시 그 음악을 들어야 합니다.

오늘 학교가서 왕따 될 수는 없거든요.

그러나, 소셜의 입장에서 보면 이러한 획일화된 음원 트랜드는 도움이 되지 않습니다.

중요한것은 내 친구가 어떤 음악 취향을 가졌는지와 내가 새로 발견한 진주같은 곡을 공유하는 것이죠.

과연 이러한 카카오의 전략은 성공할 수 있을까요? **3.

카카오 뮤직의 빛과 그림자** 사실 가볍게 그렇다, 아니다를 전 말하지는 못하겠습니다.

사실 사용자는 그 두가지 요소를 모두 가지고 있습니다.

누구나 개성이 있지만, 유행에는 또 뒤쳐지기 싫어하거든요.

따라서 카카오뮤직은 전혀 다른방향에서 접근했기 때문에, 제가 말하는 빛과 그림자는 우선은 기존 스트리밍 시장 대비의 약점과 강점 정도지만,  기존의 스트리밍 음원 시장을 망가뜨리며 발전하기보다는 어쩌면 상호 보완적인 관계로, 혹은 새로운 시장을 창출할지도 모릅니다.

Kakao, a powerhouse in mobile, seems to have seen its prominence wane compared to the past. More precisely, I think the overall attention on new smartphone devices and new apps has decreased. Amidst this, Kakao Music has, for the first time in a long while, topped the Google Play charts as of October 5, 2013.

{{IMG:1}}**[ Kakao Music surpassing KakaoTalk? ]**

I'd like to discuss whether this app can succeed, or what significance it might hold.

**1. Four Ways to Listen to Digital Music**

**1) Download for Ownership**
There seem to be three ways to listen to digital music. One of them is the download purchase model demonstrated by iTunes. It's a method where you buy one song, save it to your device, and listen to it. This is most similar to the traditional model of buying an album. However, in my personal opinion, the album purchase market before MP3s wasn't about buying sound sources; CDs and tapes were about physical possession that you could touch. Beyond just the music, the texture and color of the title were also factors in the purchase. However, in the digital realm, this value has approached zero, and this model has lost its power.

**2) Subscription Streaming, the Current Trend**
The most popular recent method for listening to music is streaming. Moreover, this method is easily accessible at a very low monthly subscription price. For 3,000-4,000 won a month, you can listen to almost all music anytime. However, music producers have many complaints about this method because the price paid to the producer each time a song is listened to is very low.

**3) Internet Broadcasting**
The third is internet broadcasting. Here, users listen to curated songs at the same time, centered around specific users. Users who listen to the songs actually don't pay money. Only the broadcasting user downloads a lot of MP3s and broadcasts them via an application. In essence, this method is close to a free model for users. Listeners request songs through the chat window, and BJs (broadcasting jockeys) communicate with listeners via chat or voice.

**4) BGM**
Kakao Music is none of the above three. More precisely, it's the BGM (background music) method from mini-hompy or blogs, which were very popular in the past. Kakao Music allows users who have purchased music to post it on their timeline, and social friends listen to it together. Unlike mini-hompy and blogs, which were completely open, Kakao friends are generally within 200 people, which is a difference. Of course, mini-hompy's 'ilchon' (close friends) were also around 200 people. However, for mini-hompy, which was accessible via URL, unconventional applications emerged that searched for BGM, and even apps that allowed users to jump between mini-hompies to listen to desired songs appeared.

**2. Why Did Kakao Music Adopt the BGM Model?**
As mentioned above, why did Kakao Music adopt the old BGM model instead of the current trend of subscription streaming? The reason is that Kakao's foundation is based on social interaction. One reason for the strength of subscription streaming is the strong consumption of music centered around the TOP 100, driven by powerful media influence. In fact, the music trend in South Korea is more about what music is popular these days than what music my friends are listening to. If Superstar K aired yesterday, I absolutely must listen to that music today. Because... I can't be an outcast at school today. However, from a social perspective, such a standardized music trend is not helpful. What's important is knowing what musical tastes my friends have and sharing a gem of a song I've newly discovered. Can Kakao's strategy truly succeed?

**3. The Light and Shadow of Kakao Music**
Frankly, I can't lightly say yes or no. In fact, users possess both of these elements. Everyone has individuality, but they also don't want to fall behind trends. Therefore, because Kakao Music approached this from a completely different direction, the light and shadow I refer to are primarily its weaknesses and strengths compared to the existing streaming market. Rather than developing by disrupting the existing streaming music market, it might instead form a complementary relationship or even create a new market. I would appreciate it if you keep this in mind as you read.

**3.1. The Light of Kakao Music**

**1) The Past Success of Mini-Hompy Can Predict the Potential Success of Kakao Music.**
The service most affected by the success of KakaoStory was mini-hompy. The fundamental reason is that it stimulated users' slight vanity, and the object they wanted to share on social media—photos—was clearly the same as mini-hompy. For this reason, mini-hompy is often considered the precursor to social networks. And the most successful models on mini-hompy were photos, skins, and BGM. Among them, skins were first successfully sold as skins and icons on KakaoTalk, and secondly, photos found success with KakaoStory. Therefore, the remaining BGM is also an element best suited for sharing on social media. "I listen to this kind of music, my musical taste is unique like this, I felt this way listening to this music, what do you think?" These are highly social elements.

**2) Evolution into Social Internet Broadcasting and a Marketing Tool**
Furthermore, this might not simply remain in the BGM market. Even without a chat room, mobile devices are always online, providing seamless service without needing to be constantly connected. Therefore, it soon implies broadcasting elements. Moreover, the core of internet broadcasting is a fandom culture centered around individuals. There's a possibility that 'ilchon-selling' (friend-selling) could become popular, where a pretty BJ comes out, shares emotions and music, or says 'Welcome to be my ilchon! Lots of BGM! Taking song requests!' And there could be ordinary people or celebrities who want to become famous using this fandom. Even entertainment agencies could use it to promote music. Celebrities' social accounts are managed by their agencies. Just as they compel users to friend them on Facebook or follow them on Twitter, agencies can push new songs to users through Kakao's friend-making feature. And through this, they could use methods to generate added value.

**3.2. The Shadow of Kakao Music**

**1) The Paid Hurdle: Wanting to Listen to the TOP 100, but Unable to Listen to the Music You Actually Want**
This is likely one of the biggest problems: first, there's a hurdle where you can't listen to the music you actually want. Of course, you can buy it. However, with the shortened lifecycle of music these days, spending 600 won per song can be a significant burden. Moreover, the music market is primarily for teenagers, and teenagers don't have money. As proof of this, while Kakao Music is #1, if you look closely, most users have only uploaded or purchased the single free song currently being distributed.

{{IMG:2}} **[ 47 Friends, but only 1 Song Each... ]**

This means everyone is very hesitant about making additional purchases. Furthermore, existing paid subscribers of other streaming services would be even more conflicted.

**2) Easy Counterattack Potential from Existing Paid Streaming Models**
Another major problem is that existing streaming service companies can also offer social music services at any time. This is because they already have many paid users, and there's no reason why they couldn't utilize social graphs from Facebook or even KakaoTalk. If they simply add a social graph to their 3,000-4,000 won paid model, it's highly likely to become an even stronger fortress.

**3) Cannibalization with Kakao Story**
BGM and photo services were a single service on mini-hompy. In fact, it's the same desire, just with different means of expressing one's status. In other words, when you want to ramble about your feelings, you'll have to ponder whether to do it on KakaoStory or Kakao Music. I use several SNS, and even sending the same text content is tough; sharing one with music and another with photos... it won't be easy.

**4. Kakao's Second Vertical Paid Content Social Network, Will It Succeed?**
In fact, Kakao's vertical paid content social network is not its first. KakaoPage was already such a model. Compared to books or videos, music might be a better fit for a social network due to its more emotional nature. However, no matter what, if a paid model is introduced, its propagation speed drops by dozens of times. Considering that the most powerful weapon of social networks was their viral propagation speed, we should reconsider whether a paid model truly fits social networks. Perhaps for the same reason, KakaoPage also faced difficulties. However, for Kakao, which needs to diversify its business models to compensate for slowed growth until its global expansion succeeds, this paid model seems to be a necessary step.

I believe it's not yet time to hastily conclude whether it will succeed or not. In fact, beyond this, telecommunication companies, which control the very distribution channels of the music market, could also pose a significant potential threat to Kakao. Just as they wrestled with Naver in the past, blocking the music supply itself is possible.
