2003년 7월 31일 싸이월드로부터 연락이 왔습니다.
내 갤러리에 관한 것을 커버스토리에 싣고 싶다고.
어쩐지 기뻤습니다. 세상 사람들이 내 그림을 봐준다는것에.

하지만, 왠지 씁슬했던것은...
인터뷰에서는 밝게 적어냈지만, 실은 전혀 밝지 않은 내용이었다는것.

그때는 정말 많이 힘들었습니다.

답답한 마음을 이길길이 없어서
그림을 끄적거리는데 풀어내고 있었습니다.

그림에는 온통 우울한 이야기 뿐이었으며,
정말로 토해내지 않으면 견딜수 없을듯이 그렸습니다.

잘그린다.. 라고 말해준것들은..
그리고 싸이월드측에서 아무렇지 않게 뽑아내준 그림들은
대부분 그녀에게 그려주었던 그림이거나 그녀를 생각하면서 그린 그림이었습니다.

저는 제 그림이 사람들에게 스크랩되는것을 즐겼습니다.
인터넷을 돌고돌아서 그녀가 봐주기를..
그녀가 보고 조금이라도 내 생각을 해주기를..

결국은, 현재 나의 아내가 되기 위한 준비를 하고 있지만. ^^
그리고 그녀는 분명 그때 제 그림을 보았고,
싸이월드의 커버스토리에서 저를 보고 깜짝 놀랐다고 했습니다.

사람들이 주목해주자 왠지 우쭐한 기분도 들었고 복잡한 기분이었습니다.
왜 이런 나를 그녀가 떠났을까 하는 생각도 들고 말이죠.
그리고, 그렇게 그림을 그리면서 많은 생각을 했고
많이 성장했던듯 합니다.

아마도, 많은 사람들이 그런 제 약한 모습이 공감을 얻었던 것이 아닐까요 ?
그래서 많은 힘든 사람들이 제 그림을 보고 위안을 얻었던듯 합니다.
본질은 제자신을 위한 그림에 불과했지만 말입니다.

다행스럽게도 지금은 그녀를 생각했던 제 기록이 되었습니다. ^^

Q. 미니홈피 갤러리에 [나의 잡념들]이라는 컨셉으로 그림을 그리시게된 계기는요?

별다른 계기란건 없어요. 그저 원래 그림을 그리는걸 좋아했거든요. 항상 샤프같은거라도 들고 무언가 끄적거려요. 원래 엉뚱한 생각도 좋아하고.. **생각을 표현한다는 것은 즐거운 것 같아요.**
그리고 자꾸 표현해 버릇하다보면 조금씩 그림이 나아지는것도 느껴지고 반응을 보이는 사람들도 있구요.
싸이월드에서 하나씩 제 자신의 생각들을 그림으로 표현하던것을 여기저기 클럽이나 미니홈피에 게시했었는데 그것들을 하나씩 모으다보니 이렇게 되버렸어요. 시간이 걸려서 그렇지 결국 게시물을 작성하는 것과 똑같아요.
제 생각을 게시물로 작성하듯 그냥 그리는거죠.

Q. [나의 잡념들]에 정말 부지런히 뛰어난 그림을 그려주고 계신데요~힘들지 않으세요?

뛰어나다고 평가하시니 몸둘 바를 모르겠네요.
싸이월드에 워낙 막강한 실력자들이 많은지라 제가 잘 그렸다는 소리를 들으면 실례일듯..^^;
**그냥 그림은 제 마음의 창구같다고나 할까요?** 제가 그림 그리는 횟수가 잦아진다는 것은 사실 마음 속에 무언가 쌓여있거나 머릿속이 복잡한 경우가 많죠. 거의 스트레스 해소용이기도 하고 잘 그리고 나면 뿌듯해요.
가끔 달리는 리플을 보는 기분도 각별하구요. (-o-)v
자기만족에 그림을 그린다고 하지만 결국은 그 누군가 봐줬으면 하는 마음에서 그리는것 만큼은 사실이니까요.
그리고...사실 노는 것 중 하나니까 힘들지는 않아요. 힘들면 벌써 그만뒀죠. 하하~

Q. 그림을 참 잘 그리시는데요~ 혹시 그림공부라도..

그림공부는 특별히 한 적 없어요. 만화를 좋아해서 많이 따라 그렸구요.
동아리를 통해 컴퓨터 그래픽을 하긴 하지만 그림공부를 특별히 가르쳐주는 곳은 아니거든요. 그냥 계속 그리다보니 늘었어요. ^---^; 하지만 여전히 엉망인게 많아요.

예를 들어 인체비례라던가 사실적인 표현에는 기초가 너무 부족해요. 손도 못그려서 맨날 뭉게버리는걸요.
기초가 없어서 동아리 선배들한테 매번 그림을 그리면 한소리씩 들어요.
"왼쪽 손목이 부러졌어" "넌 엉덩이가 거기 붙어있냐?" "맨날 손은 주머니에 넣거나 뒤로 숨기냐?" 이런 식으로 구박을.. ^^; 이렇게까지 말이 나왔으니 이번 기회에 조금이라도 공부해야 할 듯..(아, 또 말로만.. ^^)

On July 31, 2003, I received a call from Cyworld.
They wanted to feature something about my gallery in their cover story.
I was somehow happy. At the thought of people in the world seeing my drawings.
 
But, what made me feel a bit bitter was...
Although I wrote brightly in the interview, the content was actually not bright at all.
 
I was going through a really tough time back then.
 
There was no way to overcome my stifling feelings, so
I was pouring them out by doodling.
 
The drawings were filled with only depressing stories,
and I drew as if I couldn't bear it unless I truly expressed them.
 
The things people said were "well-drawn"...
And the drawings that Cyworld casually picked out
were mostly drawings I had drawn for her or while thinking of her.
 
I enjoyed my drawings being scrapbooked by people.
Hoping she would see them, circulating on the internet...
Hoping she would see them and think of me, even a little...
 
In the end, she is now preparing to become my wife. ^^
And she definitely saw my drawings back then,
and said she was surprised to see me in Cyworld's cover story.
 
As people paid attention, I felt a bit proud and had complex feelings.
I also wondered why she had left someone like me.
And, while drawing like that, I thought a lot
and seemed to have grown a great deal.
 
Perhaps, many people resonated with that vulnerable side of me?
That's why many people going through tough times seemed to find comfort in my drawings.
Even though, in essence, they were just drawings for myself.
 
Fortunately, they have now become my record of thinking about her. ^^

Q. Please tell us what you are currently doing and give a brief self-introduction.

{{IMG:2}} I'm nervous to introduce myself in a cover story that many people will see.
What should I write to look cool? ^^;
I am a cool, healthy 20-something male, both physically and mentally, from South Korea!
(Some people who know me personally might insist I'm not mentally healthy, but I'll insist I am for now.)
What I'm currently doing is playing. I'm a playboy.
(That doesn't mean I play well. ^^;)
To be precise, I'm a student on leave. They also call me unemployed or a "haengja" (a term for someone idle).
At school, I'm studying that common thing called programming.

Until recently, I was attending a computer academy or something, but these days I'm really just playing.
Whether I'm playing, studying, or working, most of the time I'm glued to a computer, unable to get off my butt. --;
Since I've clearly stated I'm playing, I guess I really shouldn't play anymore... Writing "playing" makes me feel quite embarrassed *^^*
(Starting next month, I definitely plan to go to an English academy from early morning!!)

Q. What was the motivation behind you drawing under the concept of [My Random Thoughts] in your mini-homepage gallery?

There wasn't any particular motivation. I just always liked drawing. I always doodle something with a mechanical pencil or something similar. I also like quirky thoughts... **I think expressing thoughts is enjoyable.**
And as I keep practicing expressing myself, I feel my drawings getting a little better, and there are people who react to them.
I used to express my thoughts through drawings one by one on Cyworld and posted them in various clubs or mini-homepages. As I collected them, it turned into this. It takes time, but it's essentially the same as writing a post.
I just draw, as if I'm writing my thoughts as a post.

Q. You're diligently drawing such excellent pictures for [My Random Thoughts]~ Isn't it difficult?

I don't know what to say when you call them excellent.
There are so many incredibly talented people on Cyworld that it feels rude to say I draw well... ^^;
**Perhaps drawing is like an outlet for my heart?** The more frequently I draw, the more likely it is that something is building up in my heart or my mind is complex. It's mostly for stress relief, and I feel proud after drawing something well.
And seeing the comments sometimes is a special feeling. (-o-)v
Although I say I draw for self-satisfaction, it's true that I draw with the hope that someone will see them.
And... actually, it's one of the things I do for fun, so it's not difficult. If it were difficult, I would have already quit. Haha~

Q. You draw really well~ Have you studied art, perhaps?

I haven't specifically studied art. I liked comics, so I copied them a lot.
I do computer graphics through a club, but it's not a place that specifically teaches drawing. I just kept drawing, and I improved. ^---^; But there are still many things that are a mess.

For example, I lack a lot of basic knowledge in human anatomy and realistic expression. I can't even draw hands, so I always mess them up.
Because I lack fundamentals, my club seniors always have something to say whenever I draw.
They'd tease me like, "Your left wrist is broken," "Is your butt really attached there?" "Do you always put your hands in your pockets or hide them behind your back?" ^^; Since it's come to this, I guess I should study a bit this time... (Ah, just talk again... ^^)

Q. Among the drawings in [My Random Thoughts], are there any that other members or you particularly like?

**Drawing 1)** I think I started dedicating myself to the sketch board with this drawing. I remember it was when Cyworld first held a sketch board event. It took me 4 hours to draw this back then, but I guess I improved by drawing more, as it's a bit faster now. Thanks to it, I received a book gift certificate. These days, they give out "dotori" (Cyworld's virtual currency).

**Drawing 2)** I tend to draw a lot on rainy days. I somehow become easily emotional... This is a drawing I did while listening to music. I even added a cool caption: **"Wherever you are, I hope I can be like an umbrella for you."**... ^^; But I still haven't become such a person. And there's no one to shelter... --;

**Drawing 3)** This is a drawing I did during the World Cup. I drew it based on the idea that someone under a large Taegeukgi (Korean flag) wouldn't be able to see the goal. People's reactions were good... But it turns out they fold the Taegeukgi during the game. So someone told me that there's no way you'd miss a goal because it's covered by the flag ㅠㅠ

Q. Where are you currently active on Cyworld?

There's a private school club called 'Meolmi' (Motion Sickness) that first got me involved with Cyworld.
And there's 'Close', which I know as quite a large club on Cyworld, where people gather information and thoughts on idea products, and 'My Inner Sketchbook' created by my cousin sister. Besides those, I sometimes visit 'People Who Seek Images' created by my "1-chon" (Cyworld friend) Sookyung. I'm a ghost member of 'Creative Independence'. I don't have the skills, so I just admire the drawings there.
Lastly, there's 'Happiness Research Society for a Good Life', which I recently opened and am operating.
