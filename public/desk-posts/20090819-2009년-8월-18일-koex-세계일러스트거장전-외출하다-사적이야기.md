I didn't go anywhere special during my vacation, but I went to an exhibition with my wife after a long time.
When I actually got there, there were a lot of children. Was it because it was an exhibition by a children's book author?
The content of the exhibition was also a bit geared towards children. However, the quality of the paintings was too good to be considered only for children.
{{IMG:1}}

I should have brought a compact camera that could take selfies and wide-angle shots, but I only brought my cafe lens, which is a prime lens, and had a lot of trouble taking pictures.

A pretty mouse shaped like a heart ^^;
{{IMG:2}}


It was a painting by a Korean illustrator, and I really liked the blue wave expression and the simple lines of the child's face.
{{IMG:3}}

A gorilla that everyone has probably seen at least once.
{{IMG:4}}{{IMG:5}}

Korea's Cloud Bread. An animation was also being screened.
{{IMG:6}}

The second exhibition hall was mainly a children's experience center, so there wasn't much to see...
But the experience equipment using a triangular mirror was fun.
{{IMG:7}}
{{IMG:8}}

After seeing it, I suddenly felt like drawing again!
After the viewing, we went down to the basement and looked around character stationery stores, including Aniland.
We parked the car in Tancheon parking lot and took the bus back and forth, which made my wife a bit tired, but it was a pretty enjoyable day.
