난 Xp를 쓴지 한 2년됬나 ? 그전엔 계속 2000사용했다. 2000을 포기하게 된이유는 서비스팩4부터.. 계속된 패치란 무엇을 의미하는가 ? 코드의 엔트포리. 느려지는건 당연하다 ㅡㅡ;

Xp 서비스팩2 나올때부터. 이제 슬슬 XP도 수명이 끝났다라는걸 느꼈다. 어차피, 자꾸 땜빵 코드 만드느니, 새로 만드는게 낳겠지.

더군다나, 비스타가 30만원이라고 하던데, DSP판은 10만원이더라. 불법 XP써서 어차피 온라인으로 업데이트 못받을바에는 정품 하나 구입하고 싶다. 갈수록 업데이트 못하면, 몇달마다 한번씩 새로운 wow판 구해서 깔라니 짜증난다.

회사서야 정품이라 그런 걱정은 없지만,

어쩌면 정품을 쓰고 싶어지는 욕구는 코드의 붕괴는 더욱 가속화되었고, 웜이 극성이어서 그러한 약점이 더 부각되기 때문인것 같다.

지금이야 다들 XP가 낫다라고 하지만, 본인도 불과 2년전까지도 2000이 낫다.라고 말했고, 사실 지금도 2000의 후속인 2003이 안정성면에서는 훨씬 나은것 같다.

하지만 어쩌면 그것도 안정성보다는 나중에 나왔기 때문에 아직 패치가 적게 적용되서는 아닐까 ?

<!-- en -->
I've been using XP for about 2 years, I think? Before that, I continuously used 2000. The reason I gave up on 2000 was starting from Service Pack 4... What do continuous patches mean? Code bloat. It's natural for it to slow down, obviously ㅡㅡ;

Since XP Service Pack 2 came out, I started to feel that XP's lifespan was slowly coming to an end. Anyway, rather than constantly creating patch-up code, it would be better to create something new.

Moreover, I heard Vista costs 300,000 won, but the DSP version was 100,000 won. Since I can't get online updates anyway by using illegal XP, I want to buy a genuine copy. If I can't update it more and more, it's annoying to have to get a new 'wow' version and install it every few months.

At work, since it's genuine, there's no such worry,

Perhaps the desire to use genuine software is because the collapse of the code has accelerated even further, and worms are rampant, highlighting those weaknesses even more.

Now, everyone says XP is better, but I myself said 2000 was better until just two years ago, and in fact, even now, 2003, the successor to 2000, seems much better in terms of stability.

But perhaps that's not due to stability, but rather because it came out later and therefore has fewer patches applied so far?
