**1. 대한민국의 정치 발전의 가능성을 보여준 국민의당**선거 끝났으니 밝혀도 되겠지만, 안철수씨에 대한 믿음을 져버린지 오래라서 사실 난 국민의당 지지자는 아니다. 하지만, 제 3당으로 올라온 국민의당의 모습은 매우 흡족하다. 무엇보다도 국민의당이 3당으로 올라오면서, 1당과 2당 모두에게 경고의 표시가 되었다. 다른 당을 지지하지만, 보수 vs 진보의 두개의 세력으로만 나누어진 대한민국에는 늘 불만이었다. 좀 더 다양한 색깔의 사람들이 다양한 목소리를 낼 수 있는 세상이 되었으면 하고 바랬기 때문이다. 그리고, 그 것이 가능하다는 것을 이번 선거에서 보여주었다. 내가 지지하지 않는 1당에 대한 심판보다도 더 기분 좋은일이었다. 이제 다양한 목소리가 더 좋다는 것만 증명만 잘했으면 좋겠다.{{IMG:1}}

**1. The People's Party, which showed the potential for political development in South Korea**
Now that the election is over, I can reveal this, but I lost faith in Ahn Cheol-soo a long time ago, so I'm not actually a supporter of the People's Party. However, I am very pleased with the People's Party's rise as the third party. More than anything, the People's Party's rise to third place served as a warning to both the first and second parties. Although I support another party, I was always dissatisfied with South Korea being divided into just two forces: conservative vs. progressive. This is because I wished for a world where people of more diverse colors could raise diverse voices. And this election showed that it is possible. It was even more gratifying than the judgment against the first party, whom I do not support. Now, I just hope they can successfully prove that diverse voices are better.{{IMG:1}}

**2. The Shameful Christian Liberty Party**
It's shameful. To get involved in politics under the name of Christianity... Moreover, their election pledges were nothing but discriminatory remarks. The abolition of adultery laws, discrimination against Islam, and the prohibition of homosexuality have no connection whatsoever. Of course, a Christian fundamentalist would know that the basis of their argument is simply "the literal words of the Bible." But if that's the logic, then the Earth shouldn't be round, and many scientific disciplines built upon evolutionary theory shouldn't be taught. Even ethics textbooks would have to erase everything except Christian content. In other words, it's a call to return to the Middle Ages. The sight of them publicly asking for one seat and 3% of supporters on the internet was too shameful to describe.
