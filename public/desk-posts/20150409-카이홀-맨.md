**Kai Hol Man**

{{IMG:1}}

2002. 06. 05
Kai Hol Man, with his big head, short legs, no eyes,
and no nose, is sad.
Everyone laughs and enjoys his appearance,
but he cannot even experience true love.

He is just a fun friend forever.
Even when he was flustered because his big head couldn't fit through the door,
everyone enjoyed it.

Even when he was crying,
the children enjoyed it, saying he was sweating.

Disability is not a laughing matter.
Not even a disability of the heart...

by Forest Story. 2015. 04. 09

I wonder if you know Kai Hol Man,
the marketing character for teenagers by LG Telecom,
which is now called Uplus.

Children in a boisterous atmosphere,
pulling Hol Man, who is stuck in the doorway
when entering the classroom.

Somehow, watching that commercial,
I felt uncomfortable.

I wonder if the Naver webtoon called "Ordinary Class 8"
also had a similar theme recently.

Anyway... I, who had never experienced dating like everyone else by my mid-twenties,
think I started to believe that something inside me was broken around this time.
You all know the "comfortable older brother" type, right?
I think I started to dislike being that kind of character around this time.
