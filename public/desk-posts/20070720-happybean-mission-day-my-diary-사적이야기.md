Happybean Mission Day
 
Mission & Mission, let's float a Happybean balloon, symbolizing sharing, at our desks~!
 
As you come to work, take a balloon available on each floor to your desk~
Any NHN employee can take a balloon!
 
Wishing happiness to our wonderful colleagues who think of others as much as the hot summer, please spread the keyword of sharing, 'bean'.
 
 
{{IMG:1}}
 
When I came to work in the morning, there were balloons lined up at the entrance...
After taking one back to my desk,
Playfulness kicked in with my teammates~!
 
We immediately made a balloon craft with paper cups and balloons.
With the chocolate Happybean we made previously,
And Paperman, which we've been enjoying with teammates every lunch break, boarded!
 
We adjusted it with clips to balance the weight,
But when we made it float, the craft flew away in the air conditioner breeze.
In the end, we tied it to the desk. ㅋㅋ
 
After making it, we snapped a commemorative photo ^^;
