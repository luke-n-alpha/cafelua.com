I know that with the current economic downturn, finding a job is tough, and it's hard to create differentiation with ever-increasing demands for qualifications. In light of this, I'd like to introduce an event that's great for adding a line to your resume with a short period of effort. The application deadline is September 6th, so there isn't much time; just apply first and see!

Back when there was a lot of support for startups and abundant liquidity, I found the exaggerated advertisements claiming you could get into companies like Naver, Kakao, Line, Coupang, and Woowa Brothers just by studying coding a bit uncomfortable. After the pandemic, that atmosphere seems to have largely reversed. However, I still believe that software holds significant value in helping solve various problems. Of course, many approach it primarily for employment, but the best way to achieve both is through a hackathon. These days, everyone has 'planted grass' on GitHub, so it's no longer a differentiator. In fact, if a senior developer asks, your skill level is quickly revealed. Anyway, a hackathon is the best event for short-term immersion, especially since they provide food and support. I've always hoped for more hackathons among developers, but usually, there's too much work, so unless it's during a re-employment period, there's no time to participate. Korea is just too demanding. ㅜㅜ

Anyway, what I'm introducing is the Gyeonggi Metaverse Hackathon, a somewhat specialized hackathon with the title 'Metaverse'. First of all, they provide food regardless of awards, and the schedule is as follows:

{{IMG:1}}

1) Participant Recruitment / Application: August 7 (Mon) ~ **September 6 (Wed) 23:59 (Not much time left!)**
2) Preliminary Round: September 13 (Wed) ~ September 15 (Fri) / Gyeonggi Center for Creative Economy & Innovation International Conference Hall
3) Final Round: October 18 (Wed) ~ October 20 (Fri) / Gyeonggi Economic Science Promotion Agency Multipurpose Hall
4) Awards Ceremony: October 20 (Fri) / Gyeonggi Economic Science Promotion Agency Multipurpose Hall

A hackathon involves developing and presenting in a short period to compete, and frankly, no one enters this period without any thought. Please come prepared with some idea of an item and technology beforehand. There's a section to fill out the **application form and development plan**, but it's not a project competition **(they say the competition isn't high)**, and since there's time until the preliminaries and finals anyway, it's probably fine to write something simple first and then modify it later. (That's what a hackathon is for!)

Since the theme is limited to the metaverse, there are a total of 4 tracks, and the prize money and awards differ slightly by track.

{{IMG:2}}

{{IMG:3}}

Among them, tracks 2 and 3 have specified tools. Track 2, being run by The Sandbox Korea, requires the use of The Sandbox tools. Its difficulty seems to be among the easiest.

Track 3 involves developing experiential content for equipment component replacement using the Unity engine and Oculus Quest... It feels a bit like a project or service order, but since many similar projects have emerged, it seems worthwhile for development teams or developers with relevant experience.

Tracks 1 and 4 are open themes with no tool restrictions.
Track 1. XR Content for Public Problem Solving. Quite a few public metaverses have actually been created. While several government metaverses have been built, it's also true that they face many challenges regarding their practical utility. I suspect this is because the metaverse is merely a new tool that can be utilized through collaboration and communication, but it's often treated too much like an all-purpose magic wand. Since it's a hackathon, there's no need to develop on a large scale; simply demonstrating its potential should be sufficient.

Track 4. Department Store Storytelling XR Content. Storytelling XR content will be a challenging task where story and content are even more important than the XR itself. While XR enhances immersion, it doesn't change the fundamental nature of the content. And the most powerful way to deliver content is through story. That's why web novels, webtoons, etc., are gaining popularity. In fact, we are currently planning and developing an ecological environment metaverse for Suncheon City with the support of the Jeonnam Information Culture Industry Promotion Agency. We are structuring it according to the teachers' feedback to plan and arrange exhibition spaces based on stories, and many exhibition spaces are designed with stories following the visitor flow.

Finally, as a company that supports WebXR-based metaverse platform development, our tools cannot be used for tracks 2 and 3 due to their usage restrictions. However, for tracks 1 and 4, you can try using our platform or Mozilla Hubs, the open-source foundation of our platform. The web editor Spoke, provided by Mozilla Hubs, is at a level where even elementary school students can learn and experience it. In fact, my daughter created a Dokdo Museum metaverse using it and won a science award.
