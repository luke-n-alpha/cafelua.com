Hello. I am Luke, CEO of Believer, a company that develops businesses centered on the metaverse and AI webtoons. Today's post will share some rather somber news along with Believer's future direction.

**1. Believer's Team and Business Downsizing, and Mozilla Hubs Service Termination**

Although Believer had established a certain brand presence in the web metaverse in Korea, business progress was challenging due to declining interest in the metaverse. There was ongoing outsourced development until now this year, but the cost of current personnel was becoming unmanageable, making new projects or additional outsourcing desperately needed.

As some of you may know, Mozilla Foundation's Hubs project suddenly announced its termination in February this year and declared its end on May 31st.

{{IMG:1}}

Mozilla Hubs Termination News

For us, who were branding ourselves as an open-source project supported by the Mozilla Foundation, this was additionally very bad news.

{{IMG:2}}

Notices and Posts on Mozilla Hubs Assets and Migration Methods

With the termination of the Hubs project, we saw an opportunity in the market that had created 110,000 avatars and 210,000 scenes. We made a final presentation for an open-source commercialization project conducted by the National IT Industry Promotion Agency, even reaching the evaluation stage, but we ultimately received news of our final rejection. ㅜㅜ

And eventually, based on that rejection, I had no choice but to announce the team's disbandment. Anticipating the worst to some extent, I had been receiving code handover since May 9th, and after completing major feature development, including outsourcing, on May 17th, the remaining personnel were laid off. I extend my gratitude and apologies to the team members who provided development support until their last day of work. Going forward, I will proceed as a one-person development company, focusing on technical support and feature improvements for existing customers rather than aggressive customer expansion, and will look for new opportunities. Although it is a truly regrettable situation, my goal is to do my best not to inconvenience existing customers, and I will explain the current measures and future plans.

**2. Data Migration of All Scene Data Uploaded to XRCLOUD Completed**

The main measure for this is the transfer of data that could be affected by the termination of Mozilla Hubs. Currently, there are quite a few instances where Mozilla Hubs editor Spoke project files have been uploaded to XRCLOUD. The multimedia data in Spoke files of the Mozilla Hubs editor uses relative paths, so we checked numerous media data uploaded to Mozilla Hubs' Reticulum server. If Mozilla Hubs is completely terminated, these 3D files, image files, and all resources will result in dead links. As of May 17th, all data has been completely migrated to our server (Naver Cloud).

*** If anyone's data has not been migrated, please contact us. The files that were on uploads-prod.reticulum.io will now be on api.xrcloud.app.**

**3. Added Data Migration Function When Deploying to XRCLOUD's Web Editor (Spoke)**

We have also developed an evacuation function for those who are concerned about the termination of the existing Mozilla Hubs. The Mozilla Hubs team already provides information on system migration through various methods, but we prepared this function because it might be difficult for non-developers or those who only use the Spoke editor.

Currently, if you import a legacy Spoke file into XRCLOUD, all media files within the Spoke file are uploaded to Naver Cloud, and their links are changed. Therefore, if you have a space uploaded to Mozilla Hubs, you can export the Spoke file and then register it as a scene in XRCLOUD for migration. Since publishing downloads and uploads all data, there may be a delay in publishing.

*** I was wondering whether to disclose this news, fearing too many migrations might occur, but I'm disclosing it anyway. I think such an event could even be a stroke of luck, so I'm sharing it.**

**4. Server Operation Plan Commitment**

You might be concerned about losing your uploaded files due to our team's disbandment. Since we have customers, we must maintain the infrastructure until at least 2025. Therefore, we promise to maintain it. If an unexpected termination is likely to occur, we will provide prior notice.

**5. Future Development Plans: Integration of Web Metaverse and AI Technology**

As mentioned earlier, I have received the handover from the developers, and our team members have organized everything well for easy maintenance. As CEO, I am also a computer science major and a developer with experience in web services and AI model development at Korea's largest internet company. I plan to continuously operate and improve the system in a way that is feasible for a one-person development scope. I am confident that the web will evolve into a 3D environment and will still play a crucial role in XR environments, so I don't think I will give up easily. I expect to be able to cover my living expenses and server costs, including for current customers, even by myself.

Fortunately, with the recent announcement of ChatGPT-4o, the productivity of a one-person developer has significantly increased, so I believe growth and improvement are fully possible once it settles.

I plan to continue developing in a scaled-down direction from what was planned when the team was maintained. Primarily, I will focus on AI NPC development, which currently has the highest utility and is of great interest to me. The goal is to enable a single personal desktop to run AI NPCs and all Mozilla Hubs servers, allowing for the construction of one's own metaverse and AI services even in an intranet environment without the cloud. Most of the related technologies are already available as open-source, so it doesn't require special R&D; it's a matter of time. The most challenging part is for me, as an individual, to fully master the existing XRCLOUD source code, and if I estimate it will take two months, I should be able to show it by the second half of this year at the latest. You'll be able to easily meet webtoons, animations, or idols using the web metaverse, right? With the WebRTC module, conversations with AI characters and multiple people will be possible.
