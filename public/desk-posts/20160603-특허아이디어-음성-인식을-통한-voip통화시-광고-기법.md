The initial idea was just the following.

**1. Advertising method during VoIP calls via voice recognition **
A method of converting voice information into text context during a VoIP call and displaying advertisements relevant to it.

- Client app, packet tunnel, voice recognition unit, context interpretation unit, target ad selection unit
