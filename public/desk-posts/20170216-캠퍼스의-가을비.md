November 11, 2003. 2nd semester of junior year. Autumn rain fell on campus. As if foretelling the end of the year, a madly lonely, bright yellow rain fell, using the gray campus as a canvas. Finally, the trees revealed their cold bare bodies. I only remember this year as one with an awful lot of rain.

2003-11-11

That year was unusually cold, perhaps because of my state of mind. I constantly had a cough, and my body always seemed to ache somewhere.
