After contemplating between 'post' and 'blog,' I've decided to discontinue 'posts.'
The reasons are that 'posts,' due to their nature, are easily accessible via search, make personal space management difficult, and have a strong publishing character.

Due to the recent Megal issue, I've decided to manage my content more strictly and organize my internet life.
As part of this, I will revise my blog operating principles, discontinue 'posts' which were dual-operated, and minimize social network activity.

**1. Revision of Blog Operating Principles**
I have made revisions to operate it as a space for more personal development.

**2. Discontinuation of Posts**
'Posts' feel less like a personal space compared to blogs, and it's difficult to set detailed operating principles, so I am discontinuing them.
Although they appeared on the main page several times, my network activity is not aimed at online PV (page views), so I've decided to boldly give them up.

**3. Minimization of Social Network Activity**
- I used to write insights in a monologue format and comment on world issues, but I will try not to make light comments on world issues, regardless of their importance. There are few matters that should be discussed so lightly, and I believe that expressing anger (critical comments) or liking something contributes little. I will discuss matters I truly consider important on my blog.
I will maintain article sharing in a monologue format + IT insights.
I will also maintain social networks for personal connections. (I didn't use them much for that purpose anyway.)
