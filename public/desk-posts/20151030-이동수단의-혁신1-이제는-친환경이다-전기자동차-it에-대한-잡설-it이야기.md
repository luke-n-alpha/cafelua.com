어제 플랫폼전문가그룹의 모임을 오래간만에 나갔다. 이번 주제는 무인자동차에 대한 미래에 대한 논의가 이루어졌는데, 전기 자동차, 퍼스널 모빌리티, 자율자동차가 3가지의 개념이 가져올 변화에 대한 논의가 전체적으로 이루어졌다. 현재 이 3가지 개념이 뒤섞여서 이야기가 되고 있는데 결과적으로는 우리의 생활은 지금과 완전히 다른형태로 바뀔 가능성이 높다. 스마트폰에 이은 엄청난 변화가 기대되는것이 사실이다. 이에 대한 포스팅을 시리즈로 몇개를 해볼까 한다. 그 첫번째로 우리의 눈앞에 온 전기자동차 이야기를 먼저 해보겠다.
**1. 이미 빠른 속도로 확산되고 있는 전기자동차** 테슬라가 전기자동차로 열풍을 일으키고 있다.

**2. 전기자동차는 절대적으로 친환경이 맞다. ** 전기의 생산 자체가 친환경이 아니므로 전기자동차가 친환경차가 아니라는 주장도 있으나, 인프라의 친환경성을 비교한다면 유류형 자동차와 비교할수 없다. 내연기관자동차를 구동하기 위한 다양한 인프라는 엔진에 들어가기 전 부터 상당히 환경을 망가 뜨릴뿐더러 비용도 높다. 전기의 장점은 전기로 바뀌는 순간 이동, 보관, 활용이 혁신적으로 쉬워진다는 점이다. 거기다 비교적 약점으로 지적되던 보관, 곧 배터리의 성능 내연기관의 효율과 비교할수 없을정도로 빠르게 증가하고 있다. 그 중 대표적인 기업이 LG화학이다. 또한 전기를 가장 많이 쓰는 기업 중 하나인 IT기업들이 전기를 자체적으로 생산 소화 가능한 친환경 IDC센터를 짓는것이 가능하다고 말하고 있고, 테슬라를 비롯한 해외에서는 정말로 태양광 중심의 충전인프라를 만들고 있다.

Yesterday, I attended a meeting of the Platform Expert Group after a long time. The topic for discussion was the future of autonomous vehicles, specifically the changes that electric vehicles, personal mobility, and self-driving cars – these three concepts – will bring. Currently, these three concepts are intertwined in discussions, and as a result, there's a high probability that our lives will change into a completely different form from what they are now. It's true that tremendous changes, following those brought by smartphones, are anticipated. I'm thinking of doing a series of posts on this topic. For the first installment, I'll start with the story of electric vehicles, which are already upon us.

**1. Electric Vehicles Are Already Spreading Rapidly** Tesla is creating a sensation with its electric vehicles.
Chevrolet is said to be launching the 2nd generation Volt in Korea by 2016. The 2nd generation Volt, a PHEV expected to be priced in the 30 million won range, has an all-electric range of about 80km and a total range of over 600km when using fuel. **2. Electric Vehicles Are Absolutely Eco-Friendly.** While there are arguments that electric vehicles are not eco-friendly because electricity production itself isn't entirely green, if you compare the eco-friendliness of the overall infrastructure, they are incomparable to fossil fuel-powered cars. The various infrastructures required to power internal combustion engine vehicles significantly damage the environment and incur high costs even before the fuel reaches the engine. The advantage of electricity is that once converted, its movement, storage, and utilization become innovatively easier. Furthermore, storage, which was previously considered a relative weakness – meaning battery performance – is increasing at an incomparable rate compared to the efficiency of internal combustion engines. LG Chem is a prime example of a company in this field. Moreover, IT companies, which are among the largest consumers of electricity, are stating that it's possible to build eco-friendly IDC centers capable of self-producing and consuming electricity, and overseas, including Tesla, are genuinely creating solar-powered charging infrastructures.

**3. The Electric Vehicle Industry is Not a Car Innovation, But a Car Infrastructure Innovation.** In Korea, the discussion around electric vehicles tends to be confined solely within the automotive industry. There seems to be a strong tendency to think of it merely as the engine being replaced by a motor. However, let's think a bit further. The automotive industry was a prime example of a halo industry for the Korean economy. Creating a single Hyundai car had an enormous halo economic effect, generating numerous partner companies, most of which were related to internal combustion engine parts. Electric vehicles drastically reduce the number of parts. Consequently, many companies will go bankrupt. Even companies that are 'safe' due to regulations will likely only be those related to safety.
