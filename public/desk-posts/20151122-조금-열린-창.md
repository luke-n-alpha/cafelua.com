Even on such dark days, I hope it's open just a little bit to the world. My heart, your heart... Looking at the cloudy outside from a slightly open window in a dark room... 2003.07.26

**There were many days when I was cooped up in my room. My room wasn't originally one where light came in well through the window, but on cloudy days, it was like night almost all day long. And so, I kept trying to sleep, and sometimes I peeked out the slightly open window. ****Yes, back then, I was exactly a wreck..**** **2015.11.22**
