My child is starting school next year, and with the skyrocketing jeonse prices, we recently moved. After moving and unpacking, I'm getting into a new mindset. Then my wife said, "When was the last time you exercised?" Even while I was looking into healthcare and such, my belly was still accumulating 'debt'. I told myself, "Even with this belly, I've exercised before, so it's fine." But since it had been a long time since I exercised, I was actually a little worried. Of course, my weight had also increased a bit (?). Then I got the chance to be a tester for a smart scale. It's called the Yunmai Smart Scale. The app for Xiaomi's Mi Scale had some issues, as I had to find and use a separate Chinese app, which sometimes disconnected. This smart scale is being sold by Komatrade, the official distributor for Xiaomi. I've been using it for about a week, and it feels quite good. I'm sharing the details.

**1. The Yunmai Smart Scale is as light as your body weight**

Yunmai Box: Simple packaging to save on cost as much as on weight.

It's cheap. The price is **currently 26,500 won.** Welcome, **Korean!** I hope my weight becomes this light too...

Much more luxurious than its price... This is the power of China these days ㅜㅜ It looks like this. Somehow, it feels a bit more distracting than the Xiaomi scale (Mi Scale), especially those 'yeonji-gonji' dots... However, there's a reason for those 'yeonji-gonji' dots.

**2. Comparison with Xiaomi Scale**

Simply put, it's smaller, lighter, and thinner. The color is slightly beige. While the Mi Scale is known for its extreme minimalism, this one is also quite sleek.

3. App Installation and Bluetooth Connection

Go to Google Play and search for "YUNMAI".

[**YUNMAI - Android Apps on Google Play** play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yunmai.scaleen)

No need to worry if the app description is in English. Once installed, it's in Korean. After installing the app, you need to register. You only need one registration per family, not one for each member. You can add family members to one account. In other words, my wife can see my weight changes.

{{IMG:1}}

After logging into your account, set your height, age, and target weight, then step on the scale with Bluetooth turned on. **You don't need to manually pair the scale in the Bluetooth menu.**

**4. Body Fat, Skeletal Muscle Mass, Basal Metabolic Rate, and even Body Age**

The reason for those 'yeonji-gonji' dots is where you place your two feet. As soon as you step on it and your weight appears, a white bar progresses, measuring body fat, BMI, and body age. You can check the results in the app.

Most values are R-rated

A bit shocking... It shows scores, and nothing is right except for skeletal muscle mass. Body age comes out as "큰편" (big side) rather than "많은편" (many side), which seems like a translation error. I prided myself on looking a bit younger than my age, so I didn't expect it to be this bad... I have no excuses left for my wife.

**5. Adding People**

To add a person, an add menu appears when someone with a significantly different weight steps on the scale. Alternatively, after adding a user, activate them and step on the scale for automatic setup. However, children cannot be added as it requires a minimum height of 120cm. I assume this is because children grow rapidly, making accurate measurements difficult. Therefore, when my son and daughter step on it, their data isn't separately recorded, but measurements are still possible.

**6. Changed Lifestyle**

After being a bit shocked by the results, I increased my morning stretches and walking distance. I also started being a little more careful about what I eat... And after that...

Hope is in sight.
