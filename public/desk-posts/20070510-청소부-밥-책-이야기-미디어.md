**평점**

좀 흔해보이는 이야기 같았다.
마시마로이야기랑 거의 구조도 같고..

결국은 여유를 가져라. 란것 정도...
똑같은 이야기를 다르게 썼단 생각밖에는...

모모이야기같은 좀 편안한 이야기를 기대했건만,
결국은 바른생활 국어책에 나올법한 자기관리 소설이었다.

찔리는것이 있다면, 기독교인으로서 기도하고 있지 못하다는것.

#### [Cleaner Bob](http://book.naver.com/bookdb/book_detail.php?bid=2538210)

Written by Todd Hopkins, Ray Hilbert | Translated by Shin Yoon-kyung
Wisdom House
**Rating**

I saw it was a bestseller, but...
It seemed like a rather common story.
The structure was almost the same as the Mashimaro story...

Ultimately, it's just about taking it easy.
It just felt like the same story written differently...

I had hoped for a more comfortable story, like Momo's story,
but in the end, it was a self-management novel that could appear in a "proper living" Korean textbook.

If there's anything that pricks my conscience, it's that as a Christian, I haven't been praying.
