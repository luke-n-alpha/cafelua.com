I happened to hear this music when I visited a friend's blog. It's an anime that truly had me obsessed for a while.
I wasn't particularly fond of shojo manga... but its solid story and scenes really captivated me.
Especially the spine-chilling story when it suddenly threw in the love story with Tamahome.
The music in the Tooniverse version was also as great as the original.
However, the Genbu story in the second part of the manga was a bust. I remember giving up after the second volume...
