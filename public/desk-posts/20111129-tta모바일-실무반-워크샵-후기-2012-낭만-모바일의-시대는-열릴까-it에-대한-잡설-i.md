2011년 11월 24일 목요일 다음 커뮤니케이션즈 한남 사옥에서 진행하는 TTA모바일 실무반 워크샵(주제 : 2012, 낭만(浪漫) 모바일의 시대는 열릴까 ?) 에 다녀왔습니다.

[http://onoffmix.com/event/4373](http://onoffmix.com/event/4373)
동향파악을 목적으로 한 업무 차 간 행사였지만, 행사 분위기를 전할까 하여 포스팅을 남깁니다.
먼저 주제는 2012년 낭만 모바일 시대는 열릴까 ? 라는 주제였습니다. 업무를 하다가 점심시간에 부랴부랴 나온 관계로 점심도 못먹고 한남 동 도착.주변에 먹을게 없는 nhn의 그린팩토리 보다도 더 한 공장지대 주변의 다음 한남동 사옥.. 한참을 걸어, 던킨 도넛에서 도넛 하나 때우고, 들어 선 행사장은 생각 이상으로 썰렁했습니다. 정원은 200명에 160명 정도 참석 예정이었던것 같은데....

**1. LG U+ 주소록기반의 SNS로의 접근
** LG U+의 발표를 하는 도중에 살짝 들어가서 앉은 발표 내용은 모바일 시대에 대한 장미빛 전망들을 쏟아 놓으시더군요. 이통사에서 저런 이야기를 하는게 조금, 껄쩍스럽기는 했고, 거기다가 국내에는 SNS가 없었다니.. 저와 약간 관계가 있는 미투데이는 언급 안하셔도 최소한, 다음 사옥에서 하시는 발표에서 요즘까지 무시하시다니... LG U+의 발표에서 탈 Telco이런건, 계속 1년이 넘게 들어봤지만, 이통사가 해온 걸 보면 믿기 어렵고, 솔깃하고 관심이 갔던것은 주소록 기반의 SNS의 언급이었습니다.
SNS의 키가 인적 네트워크라는 점에서 주소록은 매우 중요한 피쳐죠. 거기다가 핸드폰의 기변 때, 대리점에서 주소록 dump를 해주고 있다는 점에서 포털보다는 확실히 유리한 입장입니다.
더군다나, LBS관련된 정확한 위치 정보 또한 이통사가 유리한 입장이죠.  LG U+의 와글이 어떠한 방향인지는 대강 감이 왔습니다. 다만 이용자가 facebook을 대체할만한 그 무언가를 줄 수 있는지는 미지수.

**2. 다음이 게임을 한다고 ? 그것도 DeNA랑 ?**
다음 커뮤니케이션즈에서 게임에 대한 이야기를 해주셨습니다. 사실 제가 이 행사에 가장 궁금했던 부분이구요. 이번 행사는 예고편만 때리고, 다음 본 편은 11월 30일에... 라는 것이 주 내용인 듯 하지만, 요약을 하자면.
**  - 클라이언트 (PC형 케쥬얼.MMO등)는 기존에 잘 되고 있는 미디어 다음 같은 서비스에 레버리지 할 수 있으며 Risk를 지지않는 형태 예정 **
**   - 겜블링(고포류)   피망과의 계약이 곧 끝나며, 좋은 입장에 있음
- 모바일게임(소셜게임 등)  : 모바일 게임 플랫폼 사업 진출
**      ** : 써드파티 모집중 이며 SDK를 배포 (11/30일 발표 예정)**
**       : 상반기는 안드로이드에 집중하며, 과금은 모두 부분 유료화
**       - inAPP purchase(내부결제)를 포함한 모바일 게임 제작 SDK를 포함하여 구글마켓등 타 마켓에도 배포 가능하며, 부분유료화 수익은 inAPP purchase형태로 먹는 형태일 가능성 있어 보입니다. 주요 정책은 게임을 자체 개발하기 보다는 써드파티를 모집하여 관리하고 밀어주겠다는 입장이고, 컨텐츠 확보를 위해 DeNA와 제휴를 했다는 내용이었습니다.

** 가장 의미 있어 보이는 DeNA와의 사업제휴 였습니다. 안드로이드 기반의 게임 포털을 만들 가능성을 보여주며, 일본의 DeNA의 모바게 같은 형태가 되지 않을까 하는 저만의 추측을 해볼 수 있는 부분이었습니다.
**과금에 관한 SDK를 라이브러리 형태로 배포해서 개발자들이 안드로이드 마켓이든, 혹은 다음에서 만들지도 모르는 마켓에서 배포를 하고 수익을 얻는 형태가 아닐까 싶습니다.
이미 네이버에서도 앱들을 직접배포로 뿌리고 있기 때문에 충분히 가능한 시나리오라고 봅니다.
무엇보다도 파이널판타지와 We Rule이 DeNA거란 점에서 DeNA의 컨텐츠 파워는 충분하겠죠. 아이폰용으로 파이널판타지3도 이미 공개 됬더군요.  아무튼 11/30일이 되면 제 예측이 맞는지 확인이 가능하겠죠.

**3. 퓨쳐워커 교수님의 강의? 보이스시대를 넘어 텍스트, 그리고 다음은 포토,
** - 퓨쳐워커란 필명으로 유명하시던 LG전자 소속의 블로거님은 어느새 청강문화대학교의 교수님이..   특히 커뮤니케이션 서비스는 그 자체만으로는 lock in 효과가 없으며 커뮤니티로 고객을 붙잡아야 한다는 말이 꽂히더군요. 이는 많은 커뮤니케이션 서비스들이 나아가야할 시사점을 가장 잘 보여주었다고 생각합니다. 다른말로 하면, 카카오톡이 대세인 시장도 다른 공략할 점이 있단 의미겠죠 ?
또한 모바일 포토 서비스에 관한 이야기도 해주셨는데, 포토 서비스역시 커뮤니티가 키라는 점 또한 동감했습니다.네이버 포토 앨범이 부족한 점도 전 이거라는 생각. 구글플러스의 Instante 업로드가 사진 클라우드와 사진 커뮤니티를 겸하고 있다는 점도 이 같은 주장을 뒷받침 한다고 생각합니다.

개인적으로 모바일에 아직 웹 OS는 부담스럽지 않나 싶습니다만, LTE환경 정도 되고 쿼드 코어 쯤 되면 괜찮지 않을까 ? 싶네요. 국내 자체 웹OS하는 것 보다는 차라리 모질라 재단에 투자하는게 나을거 라는 윤석찬님 페이스북 의견에는 동감

**7. 낭만 모바일, 아니 낭만 IT 올것인가 ?******낭만이 주제였지만, 스마트폰이 2천만 시대에 들면서 부터, 유료앱 시장은 과열되고 있고, 라이트 유저들의 유입이 커짐에 따라 규모보다는 내실이 낮아져 조금은 식어버린 모바일의 열기가 아닌가 싶었습니다. 어떤이들은 모바일이 포화라는 이야기를 합니다.  그러나 어떠한 신기술이 나왔을 때는 과도한 거품이후에 진정한 성장 곡선이 이루어진다고 합니다. 신기술의 성장에 어느정도의 거품이 필요한 것은 분명한 사실이지만, 실제 그 가치가 발휘되는 날은 그 거품이 거둬지고 나서가 아닐까 합니다.

Thursday, November 24, 2011, I attended the TTA Mobile Practical Workshop (Topic: 2012, Will the Era of Romantic Mobile Dawn?) held at Daum Communications' Hannam office.

[http://onoffmix.com/event/4373](http://onoffmix.com/event/4373)
Although it was a business trip aimed at understanding industry trends, I'm posting to share the atmosphere of the event.
First, the topic was "2012, Will the Era of Romantic Mobile Dawn?". I rushed out during lunch from work, so I arrived in Hannam-dong without eating. Daum's Hannam-dong office is in an industrial area, even more so than NHN's Green Factory, which has nothing to eat nearby. After walking for a long time, I grabbed a donut at Dunkin' Donuts and entered the event hall, which was more deserted than I expected. The capacity was 200, and about 160 people were expected to attend...

**1. LG U+'s Approach to SNS Based on Address Book**
I slipped in and sat down during LG U+'s presentation, and they were pouring out rosy predictions about the mobile era. It felt a bit awkward for a telecom company to say such things, and then to claim there was no SNS in Korea... Even if they didn't mention Me2day, which I have some connection to, to disregard it completely in a presentation held at Daum's office... LG U+'s talk about "de-Telco" is something I've heard for over a year, but it's hard to believe given what telcos have done. What caught my attention and sounded promising was the mention of address book-based SNS.
The address book is a crucial feature, given that the key to SNS is human networks. Furthermore, telcos are definitely in a more advantageous position than portals because dealerships dump address books when users change phones.
Moreover, telcos also have an advantage in accurate location information related to LBS. I generally understood the direction of LG U+'s Waggle. However, whether it can offer something that can replace Facebook for users remains to be seen.

**2. Daum is Doing Games? And with DeNA?**
Daum Communications talked about games. This was actually the part I was most curious about at this event. It seems this event was just a preview, and the main part will be on November 30th... but to summarize:
** - Client (PC casual, MMO, etc.) will leverage existing services like Media Daum, planned to be low-risk.**
** - Gambling (Go-Stop) contract with Pmang is ending soon, putting them in a good position.**
** - Mobile games (social games): Entry into the mobile game platform business.**
** : Recruiting third parties and distributing SDK (scheduled for announcement on 11/30).**
** : Focus on Android in the first half, all monetization via freemium.**
** - It seems likely that they will distribute a mobile game development SDK, including in-app purchases, to other markets like Google Market, and monetize through in-app purchases. The main policy is to recruit, manage, and support third parties rather than developing games in-house, and they announced a partnership with DeNA to secure content.**
**The business partnership with DeNA seemed the most significant. It showed the possibility of creating an Android-based game portal, leading me to speculate that it might become something like DeNA's Mobage in Japan.**
I suspect they will distribute an SDK for monetization as a library, allowing developers to distribute games on the Android Market or a potential Daum-created market and earn revenue.
Since Naver is already directly distributing apps, I think this is a perfectly plausible scenario.
Above all, DeNA's content power is sufficient, given that Final Fantasy and We Rule are DeNA's. Final Fantasy III for iPhone has already been released. Anyway, I'll be able to confirm if my predictions are correct on November 30th.

**3. Professor Futurewalker's Lecture? Beyond Voice to Text, and Then Photo.**
- The famous blogger from LG Electronics, known by the pen name Futurewalker, is now a professor at Cheonggang Culture University. What stuck with me was his point that communication services alone don't create a lock-in effect; customers must be retained through community. I believe this best illustrates the implications for many communication services. In other words, it means there are other points of attack even in a market dominated by KakaoTalk, right?
He also talked about mobile photo services, and I agreed that community is key for photo services as well. I think this is also what Naver Photo Album lacks. Google Plus's Instant Upload, which combines photo cloud and photo community, also supports this argument.

**4. Yoon Seok-chan's Introduction to the Mozilla Foundation**
Firefox's dominance seems to have been significantly curtailed by Chrome's success. In this situation, Yoon Seok-chan introduced Mozilla Foundation's strategies and new technologies.
** - First, an HTML5-based browser app store:**
Chrome does it, so Firefox seems to be doing it too. Can developers sell on both?
** - Mobile Web OS:**
A Chrome OS-like form with Firefox on top of an Android kernel.
Personally, I think a Web OS is still too burdensome for mobile, but it might be okay with an LTE environment and a quad-core. I agree with Yoon Seok-chan's Facebook opinion that it would be better to invest in the Mozilla Foundation than to pursue a domestic Web OS.
** - Game demonstration using WebSockets, not Ajax:**
Web developers have more and more to learn ㅡㅡ;
** - WebRTC:**
A technology for P2P communication between browsers without a server, showing the possibility of P2P web apps not based on a server. Google and Mozilla are developing it together, expected to be completed next year. It might become a standard feature for web apps on Web OS.

**5. Other Short Sessions**
There were several other short sessions, but the most memorable one was:
2012, Outlook for Romantic Ventures/Developers - **CEO Noh Sang-beom's "존나 버틴다 (f***ing endure)" spirit!!**
The most relatable part of the presentation by the CEO, who has no less than three business cards, was his talk about "defining the ambiguous things about ventures."

Advice for those who want to start a venture company:
**1) Are you from KAIST or Seoul National University?**

**2) Should I do software outsourcing (SI)?**
- Yes, you should.
Unless your family assets are over 5 billion won, you don't have to.
**3) People are more important than ideas.**
And, **the next slide: "존버 (f***ing endure)" spirit!!**
Kim Taek-jin, one of the six successful IT figures mentioned, told his juniors not to dream of a jackpot, which might sound a bit harsh, but perhaps the reality of romance is closer to what CEO Noh Sang-beom said.
**It's about enduring through dark and barren times, believing in people, and eventually seeing the light.**
Everyone dreams of a jackpot, but perhaps the romance in the actual IT industry is not about hitting a jackpot, but about hitting a medium success and doing what you want, by enduring.

**6. The After-Party Was More Fulfilling Than the Workshop**
At the after-party, where I joined with just a humble power blogger business card, I heard so many great stories from IT seniors. Perhaps the biggest gain from this workshop was getting their business cards and introducing myself once.

**7. Romantic Mobile, or Romantic IT, Will It Come?**
Although romance was the theme, with the smartphone era reaching 20 million users, the paid app market has become overheated, and as light users enter, the substance has decreased rather than the scale, making the mobile enthusiasm feel a bit cooled. Some say mobile is saturated. However, when a new technology emerges, true growth often follows an excessive bubble. While a certain amount of bubble is necessary for new technology growth, the day its true value is realized is perhaps after that bubble has burst.
I, too, am someone who entered the IT industry perhaps dreaming of that romance.
Will romantic mobile come in 2012? No, let me rephrase the question: Will romantic IT come?
If I were to talk about romance, I would talk about Steve Jobs and Wozniak building Apple computers in a garage, rather than the App Store that Steve Jobs created.
**The dreams and actions of young people who believe they can change the world—that is romance.**
And an environment that allows us to dream that romance is precisely the kind of environment where we can dream of romantic IT. In the reality of Korean IT, where people are constantly bogged down by overtime and developers cannot work past their 40s, the pool for such romance seems too small. Somehow, I feel that my student days, when I could tackle anything even if my skills were lacking, were truly good. Was that the extent of romance?
I hope that my juniors, even if just a little, can dream of a better romance. So far, I feel that we've been moving further and further away from romance since the dot-com bubble. This is probably due to life becoming more competitive.
