물놀이로 계획되어있던것이 어쩐일인지 오후 1시로 바뀌었습니다.

나머지 조원들은 11시 40분에 먼저 모여서 식사를 했지만
저는 회사에서 반차를 사용했기 때문에 1시에 맞춰서 도착했습니다.
지난 모임때는 조를 배정하고 저녁을 먹고 가지 않았기 때문에 저는 낯이 익지 않더군요.

It seems like it was a preliminary training-like gathering before July 3rd.
What was planned as a water activity somehow changed to 1 PM.
 
The rest of the team members gathered first at 11:40 AM to eat, but
I arrived right at 1 PM because I had used a half-day leave from work.
Since we were assigned teams and didn't have dinner together at the last meeting, I didn't recognize many faces.
(Regardless of that reason, I'm actually terrible at getting to know people.)
 
There were a total of two events.
The first was the nightmare dominoes, and the second was a game.
 
Why was it a nightmare?
Our team was the only one that couldn't complete it ㅜㅜ
Due to the absence of the person from the designer part,
my shoddy (developer) design was adopted...
When I saw the other teams, they did really well.
 
On top of that, I was actually the one who knocked down the most pieces...
I knew I had shaky hands, so I shouldn't have gone near it...
How should I apologize to my team members ㅜㅜ
 
Moreover, seeing other teams completing theirs made me really anxious and upset.
In the end, we only managed to complete the outline...
