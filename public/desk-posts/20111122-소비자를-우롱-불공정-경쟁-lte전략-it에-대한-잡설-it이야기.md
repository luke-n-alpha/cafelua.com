LTE는 그저 좀 비싸고 빠른줄만 알았습니다. 그런데 좀 더 자세히 알아보니 이건 정말 좀 아니다 싶어서 포스팅을 써야겠다는 생각이 들어서 이번 포스팅을 작성합니다.

**1. 서비스도 안되는 지방에 왜 LTE?**
아시다시피 지방에서는 LTE가 안됩니다. 그럼에도 불구하고 LTE단말을 팔고있습니다. 당연한일이죠. 3g도 되는 듀얼단말이니까요. 그런데 문제는 3g요금제는 가입이 안됩니다. 장난하나요? 쓰지도 못하는 서비스로 비싼 요금제 쓰라니. 유선망에서는 SLA협약이라 하여 계약한 서비스 품질을내지 못하면 이에 대한 책임을 ISP업체가 져야합니다. SLA는 커녕 애시당초 이행못할 서비스를 팔면서 서비스에 대한 돈을 내라니요?

**2. 3g망을 쓰는 유저들을 위한 투자는 하는가?**
어처구니 없는 이야기를 들었습니다. LTE폰은 음성통화는 3g망을 쓴다구요? LTE요금받아서 이통사는 그 중 얼마만큼을 3g품질유지에 쏟아붓고 있을까요? 이런 방식이라면 LTE요금제로 걷어들인 돈 중 상당수, (4g요금 증가분을 제외한 3g요금만큼)을 3g에 재투자해야 하는것 아닐까요? 기존 3g이용자들에 대한 기만이란 생각도 드네요.

**3. wipi로의 회귀, 동영상 서비스는 이통사것만, 망중립성위반**
빠른 동영상 플레이를 광고했던 LTE는 요금제로 인해 사실상 자사의 동영상 서비스에 한해 별도의 요금제를 만들겠다고 합니다.

어디서 많이본 상황입니다. 과거 wipi nate시절에 일부 프로모션앱만 데이터 과금을무료로 밀어주는 일을 했고, JUNE이라 하여 동영상을 편당 과금했던 적이 있죠. 당시 앱가격이 3000원이면 데이터과금이 5000원씩 나오던시절이었습니다. 서비스는 네트워크 비용때문에, 이통사에 종속적이었습니다. 이게 2g시대였냐구요? 아닙니다. 당시도 3g시절이었죠. 당연히 한국의 무선인터넷 시장은 이통사에 짓눌려 숨도 쉬지못했습니다. 이건 인프라를 이용한 불공정경쟁이며 인프라와 서비스간에 의존성이 없어야한다는 망중립성 위반입니다.

**4. LTE폰 밖에 안팔면서, 인기를 논하는 언론플레이**
비싸다. 아직은 아니다라는 평가가 넘치고 있지만, 연일 언론에서는 LTE의 인기를 논하고 있습니다. 아이폰4s의 결과는 초라하기만 합니다. 근데 문제는. 최신 스마트폰은 LTE단말밖에 없고 3g요금 가입이 불가능하다는 겁니다.

I just thought LTE was a bit expensive and fast. But after looking into it more closely, I realized this is really not right, so I felt compelled to write a post, and that's why I'm writing this one.

**1. Why LTE in rural areas where the service doesn't even work?**
As you know, LTE doesn't work in rural areas. Despite this, LTE devices are being sold. That's natural, of course, since they are dual devices that also support 3G. But the problem is that you can't subscribe to 3G plans. Are they kidding? They're telling us to pay for an expensive plan for a service we can't even use. In wired networks, there's an SLA agreement, meaning if the contracted service quality isn't met, the ISP is responsible. Forget SLA; they're selling a service that can't be delivered from the outset and then demanding payment for it?

**2. Are they investing in users who use the 3G network?**
I heard something absurd. LTE phones use the 3G network for voice calls? How much of the LTE fees collected are mobile carriers pouring into maintaining 3G quality? If this is the case, shouldn't a significant portion of the money collected from LTE plans (the amount equivalent to 3G fees, excluding the 4G fee increase) be reinvested in 3G? It feels like a deception to existing 3G users.

**3. Regression to WIPI, video services only from carriers, net neutrality violation**
LTE, which advertised fast video playback, is now effectively saying it will create separate plans for its own video services due to its pricing structure.

This situation looks very familiar. In the past, during the WIPI Nate era, some promotional apps had their data charges waived, and there was a service called JUNE that charged per video. At that time, if an app cost 3,000 won, data charges would come out to 5,000 won. Services were dependent on carriers due to network costs. Was this the 2G era? No, it was the 3G era even then. Naturally, Korea's wireless internet market was suffocated by carriers. This is unfair competition using infrastructure and a violation of net neutrality, which states there should be no dependency between infrastructure and services.

**4. Selling only LTE phones, then using media play to discuss their popularity**
Despite an abundance of evaluations saying it's expensive and not yet ready, the media constantly discusses the popularity of LTE. The results for the iPhone 4S are dismal. But the problem is that the latest smartphones are only LTE devices, and it's impossible to subscribe to 3G plans.

In a situation with no choice like this, can we really discuss the popularity of LTE? HD resolution is a very significant advantage, even more noticeable than LTE. I wonder if the media properly verified whether user needs were for HD or LTE before writing such articles.

**5. Annoying mobile carriers. Should we boycott them?**
Honestly, mobile carriers' strategies seem increasingly petty these days, and as a result, they are eroding the growth potential of the Korean wireless internet market. KT is no different, but it seems the only device they are selling with some sense is the WiBro Evo 4G. If I were to buy a device now, although it's a bit disappointing that it's not HD resolution, I would have bought the WiBro Evo 4G, which offers nationwide coverage, good speed, ample data, and even unlimited 3G.
If things continue like this, 3G phones might even start to command a premium. There are already attempts on Joonggonara (a used goods marketplace) to sell imported Galaxy Note phones with a "3G premium." What do consumers truly want?
