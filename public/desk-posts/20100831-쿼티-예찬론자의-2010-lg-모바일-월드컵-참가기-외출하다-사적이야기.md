언제였던가 한 두주전 즈음에 뜬금없는 전화가 왔습니다. 모바일 월드컵에 참가해보지 않겠느냐는 제의. 제가 한거라고는 안드로이드펍에 올라온 게시물에 lg에서 만든 타자게임을 다운 받아 즐긴것 밖에 없었습니다. 과거 mits 6200과 x1 지금의 옵티머스큐까지 쿼티 예찬론자였기 때문에 남들보다는 조금 빨리친다고 자신하고 있었습니다. 스마트폰으로 포스팅도 하고 소설도 쓰는 사람이 얼마나 되겠느냐 하는 생각도 있었죠.

{{IMG:1}}

http://kr.lgmobileworldcup.com/kr/index_mwc.jsp

**[ **[**2010**](http://kr.lgmobileworldcup.com/)** LG 모바일 월드컵, 우승 상금이 무려 2천만원 ]**

그리고 인터넷을 찾아보니 2000만원이라는 거금이 걸린 경기, 거기다가 한번도 못가본 미쿡! 뉴욕도 보내준다니! 하다못해 8등안에라도 들면 50만원 준다더군요. 게임내에서는 순위권안에 있다는 생각에 해볼만하다는 생각이 들었습니다.
몇번의 잘못된 문자와 어디서 행사가 하는지도 안알려주고 행사의 미숙함이 좀 보이더군요. 그런데 충격받은건 며칠전에 알게된 경기방식, 피쳐폰으로도 경기를 해야한다는것, 피쳐폰안쓴지 몇년째인지 기억도 안나네요. Lb 1600인가가 마지막이었는데.. 그 이후로 거쳐간 폰만 mits 6200, mits4500, x1 전부 터치로도 키패드를 쓰지않았습니다.
그래도 어떻게 해볼까해서 아내의 롤리팝2로 연습했지만 늘지않더군요. 결국 임신한 아내를 대동하고 29일날 쏟아지는 비를 뚫고 용산까지 차를 몰고갔습니다.

엄청나게 비싸진 ipark주차비. 그래서 예전 컴퓨터 부품사러 용산을 뻔질나게 드나들던 기억을 더듬어 경향신문 뒷쪽으로 차를데고 걸어갔습니다. 도착하니... 왠 아이들이 줄을 서 있더군요. 불안감이 엄습했습니다. 그때부터 이런 생각이 계속해서 머릿속을 떠나지 않았습니다. **" 이 나이 먹고 내가 왜 여기에 있을까 ? "**
경기용 티셔츠 받아 입고, 아내는 임신을 핑계대고 경기장안으로 함께 들어왔습니다. 임신안했으면 밖에서 3시간을 기다릴뻔했습니다. 무슨 진행이 여전히 이모양인가 하는 생각이들더군요.
그리고 연습할 충분한? 시간을 준다더니.. 그건 본선경기만이더군요. 짬나는데로 아내의 폰과 제폰으로 연습하고 있었더니 카메라를 들이댔습니다. Kbs라더군요. 헉.. 전 기자가 원하지 않는 인터뷰만 쏟아냈습니다.

기자 : " 어째서 이렇게 빨리 치게됬죠? 문자를 하루에 몇통이나 보내요? "
**나 : " 전 문자 많이 안쓰는데요.(거의 메신저 쓰는데..) 글써서 좀 빨라졌어요. "
**기자 : " 어떻게 오시게됬어요? "
**나 : " 연락받고 왔어요. (오라니까 왔는데..) "**
기자 : " 각오 한마디 해주시죠, "
**나 : " 쪽팔리지 않을정도의 성적만 나왔으면 좋겠습니다. (아.. 이거 방송용 용어가 아니구나.) "
**
한번씩 만져보고 결국 경기에 투입! 피쳐폰은 역시 늘 꼴등, 쿼티는 순위권이었지만 그나마 노트북화면을 보고 치는건 익숙하지 않더군요. 더군다나 떨려서.. 실력발휘가 힘들었습니다.

{{IMG:2}}
** [ 저기 앞에서 나가서 칠려니 완전 떨리더군요. ㄷㄷㄷ ]**
****
점수가 낮아서 떨어졌구나 싶었습니다. 한데 그냥 대강 줄 선 순서대로 옆사람과 대진하여 32강, 16강을 결정했습니다. 그러나 저는 매우 운이좋아 8강까지 진출! 실력 순서대로 했으면 16강도 못올랐을텐데 말이죠.
아무튼 내가 8강에 오르다니! 결국 목표한 50만원을 취득하나 싶었습니다. 아내도 제게 운빨 하나만은 최고라는 칭찬을 했습니다. 우리 아내 뱃속의 하선이 유모차값은 벌었으니 기쁘죠.
아까 인터뷰한 기자도 급방긋이 되어 제게 다시 인터뷰왔습니다. 연습때 취재했던 사람이 본선까지 올랐으니 그림이 나오지 않습니까? 다시 인터뷰..

기자 : " 본선에 오르니 어떠세요? "
** 나 : " 생각치도 모르게 올라서 어리둥절합니다. "
** 기자 : " 본선의 각오한마디 해주시죠. "
** 나 : " (그래 방송용 멘트한번하자.) 열심히 최선을 다하겠습니다. "
**
근데 그때 갑자기 주체측에서 다시 부르더니 스타팀 배틀의 우승자 한명과 점수순으로 정렬해서 9명중 최저 점수자 한명 탈락인데 그게 저라고 하더군요.
그래서 전 물었습니다.
" 가도되나요? "
가도 좋다는 말을 들었죠. 그리고 아까 인터뷰하던 기자분의 어이없다는 표정이 보이더군요. ㅋ
그래서 그자리에서 바로 자리를 떴습니다. 일단 배가고팠거든요. 그렇게 제 모바일월드컵 참가기는 끝입니다.
아내를 고생시키고 얻은 전리품은 팔지도 못하는 것들.. 밥값, 기름값 하면 이만 저만 손해가 아니었네요.

전반적으로 쿼티가 1.5배가까이 빠르고 처음만져본 애들도 대부분 100타를 넘겼습니다. 그런데 잘치는 애들은 피쳐폰으로도 200타가 나오더군요. ㄷㄷㄷ
그러나 역시 쿼티는 진리입니다. 쿼티예찬하고는 그다지 상관없는 참가기였네요. 쿼티만세 !

Sometime, about a couple of weeks ago, I received an unexpected call. It was an offer to participate in the Mobile World Cup. All I had done was download and enjoy a typing game made by LG, which was posted on Android Pub. As a QWERTY enthusiast, from the old mits 6200 and X1 to the current Optimus Q, I was confident that I typed a bit faster than others. I also thought, "How many people post and write novels on their smartphones?"
 
{{IMG:1}}

http://kr.lgmobileworldcup.com/kr/index_mwc.jsp

**[ [2010](http://kr.lgmobileworldcup.com/) LG Mobile World Cup, the prize money is a whopping 20 million won ]**
 
 And when I looked it up online, it was a competition with a huge prize of 20 million won, plus they'd send me to the US, New York, a place I'd never been! They even said if I placed within the top 8, I'd get 500,000 won. Thinking I was within the ranks in the game, I felt it was worth a shot.
 There were several incorrect text messages and they didn't even tell me where the event was being held, showing some amateurishness in the event's organization. But what shocked me was the competition format I learned a few days ago: I had to compete with a feature phone. I can't even remember how many years it's been since I last used a feature phone. The LB 1600 was probably my last one... After that, all the phones I used, mits 6200, mits 4500, X1, I never used the keypad even with touch.
 Still, I tried to practice with my wife's Lollipop 2, but I didn't improve. In the end, I took my pregnant wife and drove all the way to Yongsan on the 29th, through pouring rain.
 

  

 The incredibly expensive iPark parking fee. So, recalling my memories of frequently visiting Yongsan to buy computer parts, I parked behind the Kyunghyang Shinmun building and walked. When I arrived... there were kids lined up. A sense of unease washed over me. From then on, this thought kept running through my mind: **"Why am I here at this age?"**
 I received and put on the competition T-shirt, and my wife used her pregnancy as an excuse to come into the venue with me. If she hadn't been pregnant, she would have had to wait outside for 3 hours. I thought, 'Why is the event still so disorganized?'
And they said they'd give us enough? time to practice... but that was only for the main competition. As I was practicing with my wife's phone and my own whenever I had a moment, a camera was pointed at me. It was KBS, they said. Ugh... I just blurted out interviews that the reporter probably didn't want.

Reporter: "How did you get so fast at typing? How many text messages do you send a day?"
**Me: "I don't text much. (I mostly use messengers...) I got faster by writing."**
**Reporter: "How did you come to be here?"**
**Me: "I got a call and came. (They told me to come, so I came...)"**
Reporter: "Please give us a word on your determination."
**Me: "I just hope to get a score that isn't embarrassing. (Ah... this isn't broadcast-appropriate language.)"**
 After touching them once, I was finally put into the competition! The feature phone was always last, as expected, and QWERTY was within the ranks, but typing while looking at a laptop screen wasn't something I was used to.
 Moreover, I was nervous... it was hard to perform at my best.
 
{{IMG:2}}
**[ I was so nervous having to go out and type in front of everyone. lol ]**
**** 
I thought I had been eliminated due to a low score. But they just roughly paired people up according to their queue order to decide the top 32 and top 16. However, I was very lucky and advanced to the quarterfinals! If it had been based on skill, I wouldn't have even made it to the top 16.
Anyway, I made it to the quarterfinals! I thought I might actually get the 500,000 won I aimed for. My wife also praised me, saying my luck was the best. I was happy because I earned enough for a stroller for Haseon, who's in my wife's belly.
The reporter who interviewed me earlier suddenly brightened up and came to interview me again. Doesn't it make a good story when someone they covered during practice makes it to the main competition? Another interview...

 
 Reporter: "How do you feel about making it to the main competition?"
** Me: "I'm bewildered because I advanced unexpectedly."**
 Reporter: "Please give us a word on your determination for the main competition."
** Me: "(Okay, let's give a broadcast-friendly comment.) I will do my best."**
But then, suddenly, the organizers called me again and said that one winner from the Star Team Battle and one person with the lowest score out of 9, sorted by score, would be eliminated, and that person was me.
So I asked,
 "Can I go now?"
I was told I could go. And I saw the dumbfounded expression on the reporter's face who had interviewed me earlier. lol
So I left the venue right away. I was hungry, for one. And that's how my Mobile World Cup participation story ends.
The spoils I got after putting my wife through trouble were things I couldn't even sell... Considering the cost of food and gas, it was quite a loss.
 
{{IMG:3}}
**[ Spoils obtained through hardship: I already have too many bags like this from the company... ]
**

Overall, QWERTY was almost 1.5 times faster, and most kids who tried it for the first time exceeded 100 characters per minute. However, skilled kids could even type over 200 characters per minute on a feature phone. Wow.
But still, QWERTY is the truth. This participation story wasn't really about praising QWERTY. Long live QWERTY!
(Sometimes, my instant typing speed also exceeds 300 characters per minute.)
