**<The 1st Hack Korea>** It is not easy for IT industry professionals to voice their opinions on social change. Perhaps they have already become accustomed to being in a perpetual 'subordinate' position. However, now some IT professionals are gathering to try and change the hopeless reality of Korea, often called 'Hell Joseon,' with their ideas and technology. The '1st HACK KOREA' hackathon, where they will gather for 2 nights and 3 days starting December 18th at an alternative school called <Kkumirum School> in Yeongdeungpo, will be the beginning of that small movement.

**Event Purpose**
Today's Republic of Korea has become a state of 'Hell Joseon' due to a complex array of numerous problems, including wealth disparity, youth unemployment, labor issues, various absurdities, nationalization of history textbooks, threats to freedom of thought and expression, biased media, collapse of public education, politics at a vegetative state, poor diplomacy, and various accidents like the Sewol ferry sinking. Therefore, under the banner that software changes the world, experts are gathering to find cool ways to save 'Hell Joseon' with IT technology.

**Event Goal**
The goal is for teams to create software, services, games, etc., over 2 nights and 3 days, that directly solve one of the problems arising in 'Hell Joseon,' or collect, analyze, and visualize data and opinions on specific problems and solutions, or assist in decision-making to enhance the nation's dignity.
