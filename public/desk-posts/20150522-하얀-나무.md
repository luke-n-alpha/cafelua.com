**White Tree**

{{IMG:1}}

After a very long journey, all I met was a stubborn and lonely white tree, just like me.

2003. 01. 16
2015. 05. 22

I was feeling that something was wrong around that time. And I think I vaguely knew that the cause was within me.

To love, self-love must be broken. At the time, I was stubborn to make excuses for not loving.
