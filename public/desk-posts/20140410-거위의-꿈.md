**The Goose's Dream**

{{IMG:2}}

2002. 01. 29
In my dream... I fought a goose while carrying a bicycle (not riding it)... But... can geese fly? --; Anyway, the goose was strong. What is this dream? Someone please interpret it for me~
by Forest Child
**2014. 04. 10**
Back then, I had many strange but interesting dreams. It was partly because I liked comics, anime, and games, but anyway, dreams were fun back then.
Whether it was seeing magnificent sights in my dreams or experiencing heart-pounding events, it seems like it was a time when I had many dreams.
That's not to say I don't dream now. I'm still dreaming. Because if I don't dream, my life would be dead. Right now, two of my dreams are sleeping with my wife.
