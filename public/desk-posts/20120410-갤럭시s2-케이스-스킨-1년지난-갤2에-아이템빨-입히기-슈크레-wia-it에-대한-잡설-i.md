** 1. 아이템1, 블링블링 WIA 스킨** 먼저 WIA 스킨입니다.

사실 전 스킨을 씌워본적이 없습니다. 처음 시도 하는거였습니다. WIA스킨이 뭔지도 몰랐구요. 실드라고는 하는데 실드보다는 DIY 스킨에 가까운것 같습니다. 조금 썰렁해보이는 갤럭시 S2에 포인트를... 어떤 색깔로 보내달라고 얘기도 안했는데, 분홍색으로 보내주셨어요. 전 남자인데 !!! 다행히 아내용으로 받은거라. 다행..

It's already been almost a year since I started using the Galaxy S2 Neo Diary case. The contract isn't even over yet, but it's already turning into a rag. I used it well because it had the advantage of being a diary type where I could insert cards, but its weak durability seems to have been a flaw. In particular, the rubber covering the wire support that held the phone started to tear and then completely fell off, and the anti-slip pad attached to the back gradually peeled away, eventually coming off completely because Saebom (my child) played with it, tearing it off...
{{IMG:1}}
**Neo, turned into a rag...**
In the end, I told my wife to buy a cheap new case that she could use for another year. Just then, a case review offer came in. And almost simultaneously, a skin review offer... It was exactly what I needed, so I gladly accepted.
**1. Item 1, Bling-Bling WIA Skin** First up is the WIA skin.

Honestly, I've never put a skin on a phone before. This was my first attempt. I didn't even know what a WIA skin was. They call it a shield, but it seems more like a DIY skin. It's meant to add a touch to the somewhat plain-looking Galaxy S2... I didn't even specify a color, but they sent me pink. I'm a man!!! Fortunately, it was for my wife. Phew...
{{IMG:2}}

The skin is as shown above. It seems there are four colors, and this one is light pink... It looks luxurious with a leather-like texture. You carefully wipe the phone with the alcohol swab included inside, then apply it.
{{IMG:3}}Once applied like this, the phone takes on a feminine look.
The thing to be careful about when applying it is that while you can roughly apply it elsewhere, you must be careful not to cover the front camera. As someone far from meticulous work, I was trembling (ㄷㄷㄷ).
{{IMG:4}}Back side...
{{IMG:5}}
I didn't apply the remaining parts, the front button and camera cover. I lacked confidence due to being far from meticulous work, and also because the black parts looked prettier as they were. There are also leftover Android bots, hearts, and stars; you can apply them wherever you like.
{{IMG:6}}
Here's how it looks after being applied and turned on. It now looks like a 'feminine phone' verification shot.
