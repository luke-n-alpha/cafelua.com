출근길 태풍이 지나가고 나니...
언제 그랬냐는 듯이 날씨가 짠해졌다..
덥지는 않지만, 강한 햇살과 맑은 푸른 하늘.
아 눈부셔라.. {{IMG:2}}

하늘을 향해 사진 {{IMG:3}} 이라도 한방 찍고 싶었으나,
카메라는 두고 온 날.

그런데 결국 점심에는 또 비. {{IMG:4}}

어느새 아침의 화창한 시간은 가고,
일좀 하다가 정신차리고 보니 {{IMG:5}} 야근시간도 다 지나가는 시간..
왜이리 피곤한지. {{IMG:6}}
화요일인데 벌써 목요일같다..

답답한 마음에 블로그에 투덜투덜. {{IMG:7}}
(아이콘 붙이기 생각보다 재밌네...)

요즘은 간단한 일도 너무 힘들게 힘들게 진행되는것 같다.
몸은 천근만근....

아~~~~~~~~ 즐겁게 좀 살자.

{{IMG:1}}
After the typhoon passed on my way to work...
The weather became clear as if nothing had happened..
Not hot, but strong sunshine and a clear blue sky.
Oh, it's dazzling.. {{IMG:2}}

I wanted to snap a picture {{IMG:3}} towards the sky,
but I had left my camera at home today.

But then, it rained again at lunchtime. {{IMG:4}}

Before I knew it, the sunny morning hours were gone,
and after working for a bit, I came to my senses {{IMG:5}} to find that even overtime hours were almost over..
Why am I so tired? {{IMG:6}}
It's only Tuesday, but it feels like Thursday already..

Grumbling on the blog out of frustration. {{IMG:7}}
(Adding icons is more fun than I thought...)

Lately, even simple tasks seem to be progressing with great difficulty.
My body feels incredibly heavy....

Ah~~~~~~~~ Let's live a bit more joyfully.
