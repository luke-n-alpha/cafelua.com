또 한참을 그렇게 걸었다..

얼마전 사무실 복도 창에서 사장님이 내보낸 누렁이를 보았다.
구의동에서 배가 고파 찾아들어온 누렁이는 사무실에서 거둬들였던 작은 고양이였다.
처음에는 무척이나 사납고.. 기생충도 있었고.. 많이 아프기도 했었다.
몇달안에 훌쩍 커버리고 많이 얌전해진 누렁이는 사정상 홍대에서 내버려졌다.
그리고 녀석의 눈과 마주쳤다. 불러도 오지 않았다..

그래. 녀석의 태생은 도둑고양이.
원래 있던 자리로 돌아갔을뿐.
그러나 ... 녀석의 눈에서 읽은것은..

사나워도 서툴러도 혈통좋은 애완용 강아지가 아니고 혼자서도 익숙한 들고양이일지라도..
아픔과 마음정도는 있겠지.

그래도 녀석은 잘 살고 있었다..
대견했다..

잘 살아라. 누렁아..
바보같이 굴지말지 말고... 그렇게 불러도 오지말아라.

And so I walked for a long time again..

A while ago, I saw Nulung, whom the boss had sent away, from the office hallway window.
Nulung, who had come to us from Gye-dong because he was hungry, was a small cat that the office had taken in.
At first, he was very fierce.. had parasites.. and was often sick.
Nulung, who had grown up quickly and become much calmer within a few months, was abandoned in Hongdae due to circumstances.
And I met his eyes. He didn't come even when I called him..

Right. His origin was a stray cat.
He just returned to where he belonged.
But... what I read in his eyes was..

Even if he was fierce and clumsy, not a purebred pet dog, but a wild cat accustomed to being alone..
He must have felt pain and had a heart.

Still, he was living well..
I was proud of him..

Live well, Nulung..
Don't act foolish... and don't come even if I call you like that.
