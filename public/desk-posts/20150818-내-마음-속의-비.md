**Rain in My Heart**

{{IMG:1}}

Rain falls in my heart..
2013. 04. 25
2015. 08. 18

I argued with my wife yesterday.
After an argument, it's still
the same; I feel bad.

Back then, it was so hard and despairing,

Living together
seems difficult in another way.
I know well that I'm not the only one struggling,
and I still seem clumsy.
