닫힌웹. 제가 이야기하려는 것은 네이버나 다음 같은 포털 이야기가 아닙니다. 물론 포털끼리도 서로 닫고 있는 영역들이 있습니다. 이들끼리는 서로의 이해관계에 의해 열고 닫은 부분이 있다고 하지만, 그보다는** 통합 검색 서비스 를 제공하지 않고 있는 사이트들에 대한 검색 설정 이야기를 해보려고 합니다.**

**DC inside의 검색 설정 ([http://www.dcinside.com/](http://www.dcinside.com/))**

**SLR클럽 검색 설정 ([http://slrclub.com](http://slrclub.com/))**

* 전체 검색 불가

**파코즈의 검색 설정(****[http://www.parkoz.com/](http://www.parkoz.com/))**

* 전체 검색 불가

**루리웹 검색 설정([http://ruliweb.daum.net/](http://ruliweb.daum.net/))**

* 각 게시판명은 모르겠습니다. 아무튼 일부 검색 불가.

**2. 학술정보나 통계자료 좀 검색되게 해주세요.**

The Closed Web. What I want to talk about isn't portals like Naver or Daum. Of course, there are areas where portals close off from each other. While it's said that they open and close parts based on their mutual interests, I want to talk about **the search settings of sites that do not provide integrated search services.**

**1. Please make communities searchable.**
You might wonder what I'm talking about, but many community sites don't allow search. Some of the core forums of DC Inside, often called the "final boss" of communities, are not open for search.

**DC inside Search Settings ([http://www.dcinside.com/](http://www.dcinside.com/))**
*   It seems Kogal and Starcraft Gallery were not searchable. What is plastic_c?

**SLR Club Search Settings ([http://slrclub.com](http://slrclub.com/))**
*   Entire site not searchable.

**Parkoz Search Settings (****[http://www.parkoz.com/](http://www.parkoz.com/))**
*   Entire site not searchable.

**Ruliweb Search Settings ([http://ruliweb.daum.net/](http://ruliweb.daum.net/))**
*   I don't know the names of each board. Anyway, some are not searchable.

Besides these, Parkoz, SLR Club, Ruliweb – none of them are open. What's interesting is that these are among the places where the closed nature of portals is most frequently discussed. Ultimately, I wonder if they are considering a model like Ruliweb, which partners with Daum to generate revenue by selling content. Just as portals have their circumstances, communities likely have theirs too.

**2. Please make academic information and statistics searchable.**
At least communities are sites with a somewhat commercial nature, so that's understandable. But what about sites with valuable academic information or statistics?

**itFind Search Settings**
*   Entire site not searchable.

**4. Please make public institutions searchable.**
What's even more absurd is that public information, created with taxpayers' money, is often not searchable. Even data from the Korea Communications Commission is set to be unsearchable.

**Korea Communications Commission Search Settings (****[http://www.kcc.go.kr](http://www.kcc.go.kr/))**
*   Entire site not searchable.

**National Assembly of Korea ([http://www.assembly.go.kr](http://www.assembly.go.kr/))**
*   Entire site not searchable.

Can we even call this "public"? Even the National Assembly of Korea is not searchable. What kind of laws are our esteemed members of parliament making that they make them so hard to find?

**5. Closed Korea. What to search for?**
If there are many sites like these, the equation "search quality = cost" inevitably holds true. A large amount of searchable data in Korea is tightly closed off like this. For portals to focus on external search, user satisfaction with external search must increase. And for that to happen, high-quality information must be accessible externally. However, the reality is quite the opposite. Even a global top company like Facebook has declared itself unsearchable. Is it because we're moving from search to SNS? Everyone says that if search within SNS becomes possible, it would be very powerful. Indeed, Twitter search is called "real-time search" and reflects powerful trends. It's clear that the value of search has not disappeared in the SNS era. Eastsoft also seems to be releasing a new search engine; under these circumstances, how will Eastsoft tackle this? Is there really a solution?
From a user's perspective, even if I don't know much about other things, I wish the search restrictions for specialized academic and public information would be lifted.
