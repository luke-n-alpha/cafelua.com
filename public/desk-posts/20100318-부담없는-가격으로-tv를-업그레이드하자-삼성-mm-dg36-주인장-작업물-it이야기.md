후에 장모님께  XD-63을 실제 구매해서 보내드렸었는데, 거의 두배의 가격인 DG36을 구매한데는 큰 후회는 없습니다.
(그러나 카세트가 주 목적이라면 다른 선택은 할 수 없겠죠.)
사실 가격이 두배라 두 제품을 비교하기는 좀 거시기 하지만, DG-36에 만족한 점을 말씀드리겠습니다.

**1. 만족한 점**
**1) DG-36은 다양한 입출력 포트를 제공합니다.**
- HDMI지원, XD-63을 포기한 직접적인 원인중 하나입니다. 저희 집은 이미 컴포넌트 단자는 IPTV가 차지하고 있기 때문에 TV에 연결을 위해서는 HDMI단자가 꼭 필요했죠. 그리고 연결해본 결과 아주 잘 나옵니다.

**2) 낮은 가격의 홈씨어터**
- 사실 홈씨어터로 이용할 목적으로 집에 가지고 있는 5.1채널의 브리츠 스피커를 이용할 생각을 해보았습니다. 한데 이를 위해서는 리시버를 구매해야하는데 이것의 가격이 배보다 배꼽이 더크더군요. DG-36의 총가격보다 리시버의 가격이 더 비쌉니다. 일반 홈씨어터를 구매하면 가격은 더 뛰죠.

**3) 작고 깔끔한 디자인, 적은 공간 차지**
- 홈씨어터 구축해놓고 쳐박아놓는 이유는 사실 자리의 문제가 많습니다. 특히 리어 스피커의 경우 배치도 곤란한 경우가 많고, 무선인 경우면, 리어의 전원을 따로 써야 하니 곤란하죠. 실제로 활용율이 많이 떨어지는 경우를 보았습니다. 또한 전용 홈씨어터의 경우 오디오와 달리 TV나 DVD를 볼때 외에는 활용도가 좀 떨어집니다. TV를 꺼둔채로 라디오를 듣는다던가 하는 면에서 말이죠.
검은색이라 무난합니다. 위에 볼륨을 표시하는 붉은 원도 전원이 들어오면 잘어울리구요. 다만 잘때나 꺼두었을때는 번쩍거리는 LED 글씨 좀 안보였으면 하는데 계속 스크롤됩니다. 그래서 멀티탭으로 외부전원을 내려놓습니다. 전원을 끄면 완벽히 검은 박스입니다.

**4) 켈프 스피커와 우퍼의 음질**
생각 이상으로 좋습니다. 280W인지라 크게 틀어놓으면 오히려 곤란하죠. 1000W가 넘는거 사실 집에 방음시설이라도 할 요량이 아니면 아무 필요가 없습니다. XD-63과 비교할때도 훨씬 차분한 느낌이드는 소리입니다. XD-63도 막귀인 저에게는 나쁘지 않았지만, 디자인도 그렇지만 좀 더 가볍다는 느낌이 납니다.

LG Electronics XD-63

A while ago, my wife got pregnant, so she's been spending more time at home, which led me to buy a mini-component system. I usually get pretty excited when buying electronics, so I did a lot of research before making the purchase. However, unlike cell phones or computers, it was hard to find user reviews for mini-component systems or other electronic devices online. So, I'm posting this to help others who might be looking to buy one.
I want to make it clear that I was not asked by anyone to write this review. ^^;

The other product I compared until the very end was

The reason was that my wife wanted to listen to cassette tapes.
Later, I actually bought the XD-63 and sent it to my mother-in-law, and I have no major regrets about purchasing the DG36, which is almost twice the price.
(However, if cassettes are the main purpose, there's no other choice, right?)
Actually, comparing two products with double the price difference is a bit awkward, but I'll tell you what I liked about the DG-36.

**1. Satisfying Points**
**1) The DG-36 offers a variety of input/output ports.**
- HDMI support, one of the direct reasons I gave up on the XD-63. Our TV's component port was already occupied by IPTV, so an HDMI port was essential for connecting to the TV. And after connecting it, it works perfectly.

**2) Low-cost home theater**
- I actually considered using my 5.1 channel Britz speakers at home for a home theater setup. However, this would require purchasing a receiver, and its price was more than the main unit itself. The receiver's price was higher than the total price of the DG-36. If you buy a regular home theater system, the price goes up even more.

**3) Compact and clean design, takes up little space**
- The reason home theater systems are often tucked away is largely due to space issues. Especially with rear speakers, placement can be difficult, and if they're wireless, you need a separate power source for the rears, which is inconvenient. I've seen many cases where their utilization rate is very low. Also, dedicated home theater systems, unlike audio systems, are less useful when not watching TV or DVDs. For example, listening to the radio with the TV off.
It's black, so it's unobtrusive. The red circle indicating the volume on top looks good when the power is on. However, I wish the flashing LED text wouldn't show when I'm sleeping or when it's off, but it keeps scrolling. So, I turn off the external power with a multi-tap. When the power is off, it's a perfectly black box.

**4) Sound quality of the Kelp speakers and woofer**
It's better than I expected. Since it's 280W, playing it too loudly can actually be problematic. There's no need for over 1000W unless you plan on soundproofing your house. Compared to the XD-63, the sound feels much calmer. While the XD-63 wasn't bad for my untrained ears, it felt a bit lighter, both in design and sound.

**2. Good features supported by most mini-components these days**
**1) DVD, XVID Playback**
- Most can be used as a home theater alternative. However, it's hard to find one with a woofer these days besides this one. Some say that a woofer can be distracting when listening to regular music, but I'm satisfied with the DG36, so I don't really notice it.

**2) USB MP3 support**
- You can connect a USB drive to play MP3s or watch videos directly from an external hard drive.

**3) CD Ripping**
- Rips CDs directly to MP3 files on a USB drive. (Honestly, I haven't used this feature yet.)

**3. Slightly Disappointing Points**
1) As I mentioned earlier, I wish the LED scroll wouldn't show when the power is off.
2) Slightly slow response (It's a bit slow when the CD comes out. Probably because it's not mechanical.)
3) LED display does not support Korean filenames (Korean characters in MP3 files seem to be broken... It's a Samsung product, why is it like this? Especially for one of the highest-priced lines.)
**- As an alternative, if you connect it to the TV, you can browse the playlist and folders. Korean characters display normally there.**

{{IMG:5}}

**4. My Final Installation Method (For those using IPTV-TV)**
**1) IPTV --- (Video/Audio Component Cable) ---- TV**
**2) TV ----- (HDMI) --------------------- DG36**
- Used when watching DVDs. Only the video signal is sent from DG36 to the TV.
(The audio didn't go to the TV. If this one line could also serve as the AUX role below, it would have been perfect.)
**3) TV ----- (Audio Component Output) --------- DG36**
- Used when watching TV. (Audio signal goes from IPTV -> TV -> DG36), and you listen in AUX mode.
**: At this time, the TV's volume should be set to 0. If the TV volume and DG36 volume are mixed, it creates disharmony and makes it difficult to understand speech. Listening only to the DG36's sound is much clearer and has better sound quality. The power of the sound when watching Chuno is amazing~! It can't even compare to high-end TVs.**

* For 250,000 won, the sound quality becomes much better than any high-end TV. It's incomparable to basic TV speakers. The fact that it doesn't take up much space was the best part. Besides that, the Tuner and CD functions are standard for a mini-component. I wanted to create this post to recommend my method to those who find a home theater system burdensome.
If this price is still too much, or if you have a fondness for cassettes, the XD63 is not a bad option. I don't think there's a need to spend a lot of money on a home theater. The biggest flaw of the XD63 is the lack of connection ports.
