아무튼, 다큐멘터리를 좋아하는 저지만,

그날 점심은 공짜로 먹었구
저녁도 준다고 했는데.. 저는 일이 있다는 핑계로 저녁은 안먹고 빠져나왔습니다.

그럼 다음은 22일날 있을 모임에 대한 후기를 올리도록 하죠.

On June 14, 2006, at 9 AM, I received an email notifying me of a physical examination and orientation.
The meeting place was in front of the SK C&C fountain.

Anyway, I hadn't even fully grasped that I had passed, and after a long three-month selection process, everything seemed to proceed very quickly.

There were many people in front of the fountain when I arrived early in the morning. I learned that day that a total of about 70 people had joined as the 3rd public recruitment class. (I was surprised. It was a lot.) I was glad to see some familiar faces from the interviews. Back then, they were competitors, but now they were colleagues.

In the morning, we had a physical examination. It was less of a physical examination and more like a routine 'health check-up' conducted by the company.

In the afternoon, there was an orientation... The emcee from NHN's education division, working in 'Team & Motivation,' was very entertaining... It made me think that people who work in such departments are truly different. As a developer, I could never imagine leading the atmosphere like that.

They talked a bit about NHN's introduction, the qualities new hires should possess, and corporate culture. The most boring part was... 'My Life, My Path,' or something similar, about a famous designer... Anyway, even though I like documentaries, such obvious storytelling was a bit tedious.

They said not falling asleep was part of the training and a quality new hires should have... Moreover, the World Cup match against Togo was the day before, so I was really sleepy.

After that, they introduced the schedule: another meeting on the 22nd, followed by a two-week residential training starting July 3rd. After Constitution Day, there would be another week of MS Office training. They said they'd help us get certifications. ^^ I wonder if it's for MOUS...

While working at my previous company, I often struggled because I didn't know many MS Office functions.

To those reading this, please, I beg you, don't ask developers about MS Office. Developers may seem like computer experts, but most of them know less about MS Office functions than office workers. Many developers intensely dislike document work, and in fact, coding ability has nothing to do with office tasks.

And then homework was assigned ㅜㅜ
1.  Read 'Game Company Story' and write a handwritten book report on manuscript paper.
2.  Read 'Google Story,' extract 50 keywords, and create 10 sentences.
3.  Learn to play 'You Are the Golf King,' 'Omok,' and 'Gunster' (they said there would be a competition during training).
4.  Create a blog (self-introduction via blog planned during training).

Lunch that day was free, and they said they'd provide dinner, but I made an excuse about having work and left without eating dinner.

Well then, I'll post a review of the meeting on the 22nd next.
