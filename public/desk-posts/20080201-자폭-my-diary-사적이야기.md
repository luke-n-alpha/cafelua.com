When I feel somehow suppressed by external stress,
there are times when I want to show off for no reason.
 
When I see others doing well or getting ahead of me, I always want to say something.
I make pathetic excuses about having lived diligently in my own way,
talk about wanting to be evaluated better, or being superior to others, and point out others' mistakes, but
 
Looking back, I realize it wasn't wise,
and I feel it's self-sabotage.
 
{{IMG:1}}
 
In the end, I lose people's hearts... and all they'll think is, "You're so great, aren't you?"
 
The older I get, I talk about how people are as important as knowledge or skill,
but in reality, I end up acting like a child.
 
Is it an expression of an inferiority complex?
After saying it, it won't even make me feel good anyway...
 
There should be a virtuous cycle where if you enjoy what you do, someone will eventually recognize it,
but sometimes I fret like a child.
 
After all, it's said that truly capable people stand out without needing to boast.....
Ah.... this truly is self-sabotage that won't even break even.
 
 
{{IMG:2}}
Anyway, in reflection mode.
Let's not be a nuisance to those around us. ㅜㅜ
