링플레이는 피쳐폰에서 부터 꾸준히 존재해왔던 전화 수신 화면에 대한 꾸미기 기능에 초점을 둔 앱입니다. 그러나 과거의 피쳐폰과 똑같다고 하면 제가 리뷰할 필요는 없겠죠. 분명히 큰 차이점이 있습니다.

**1) 나의 수신화면이 아닌 내가 전화를 건 타인의 수신화면을 설정하는 기능** 피쳐폰은 단지 하나의 전화수신화면을 소액결제로 다운받아 내 폰에 셋팅하는 개념이었다고 하면, 이는 나의 전화수신화면을 설정하는 기능입니다. 이는 사실 페이스북을 생각하면 간단합니다. 페이스북에 내 사진을 셋팅하고 페이스북의 주소록을 통해 누군가에게 전화를 건다면, 그 사람에게는 내 페이스북의 사진과 함께 전화벨이 울리겠죠. 기존의 피쳐폰이나 주소록들은 자신의 주소록에 일일히 사람들의 사진들을 셋팅했어야 했습니다. 이러한 고민을 간단히 해결할 수 있는 개념입니다. 결국 이러한 기능은 자신의 아이덴티티를 자신이 결정할 수 있게 해줍니다.
**2) 강력한 꾸미기 기능 **이렇게만 이야기하면 사실 어찌보면 그냥 폰 주소록에 페이스북 결합한 개념 ? 정도로만 생각하실지 모르겠습니다. 넵. 사실이 그렇습니다. 하지만 여기서 하나 더 한국 사용자(특히 여성과 학생)들이 좋아하는 강력한 꾸미기 기능을 제공합니다.{{IMG:1}}{{IMG:2}}{{IMG:3}}

**3) 발신자의 링플레이를 내가 변경 가능** 타인의 링플레이를 내 폰에서는 변경할 수 있습니다. 맘에 안드는 친구가 멋진 링플레이를 쓰고 있다면, 돼지로 바꾸셔도 됩니다. 물론, 대신 그 사용자는 제가 돼지로 설정한 것을 볼수 있는 기능이 존재합니다. 이를 모르고 바꾸셨다가는 낭패를 당할 수 있죠. 이러한 기능은 내가 설정한 아이덴티티를 기본으로 하여 타인이 나를 어떤 아이덴티티로 보고 있는지를 알 수 있고, 이를 통해 서로 즐길 수 있는 개념을 제시하고 있습니다. **3. 수신화면 커뮤니티, 톡에 결합한다면 ?**자신의 핸드폰의 수신배경화면을 벗어나 타인과 동기화하고 이를 함께 즐긴다는 점에서 분명 이 서비스의 성격은 커뮤니티 서비스에 가깝습니다. 따라서 어쩌면, 현재 유행하고 관심 받고 있는 톡서비스들이 참고할만한 내용이기도 한 것 같습니다. 톡 역시 전화번호를 중심으로 이루어진다는 것이 동일하기 때문이죠. **4. 아쉬운것은 링플레이의 부분 유료 모델 잘 될까 ?**링플레이의 비즈니스모델은 링의 부분 유료모델입니다. 한데, 유저의 유료에 대한 니즈가 얼마나높을지 잘 모르겠습니다. 분명 학생들 사이에서는 어느정도 인기를 끌만한 아기자기한 요소들을 많이 가지고 있습니다. 그러나 스마트폰에서는 이미지파일을 직접 로딩해서 설정할 수 있기 때문에, 굳이 작가들의 이미지를 돈을 내고 구매를 할까 ? 하는 점입니다.

****1. Ringtone Business Model Fades with Feature Phone Decline**
The most successful business model on feature phones was the ringtone business model, with Danal being a prime example. However, with the shift to smartphones, users could directly set MP3 files as ringtones, significantly reducing this need.

**2. Android's Powerful Customization Features, Unlike iPhone**
The ability to change ringtones is part of phone customization. Especially unlike iPhones, Android offers users many customization options. In fact, smartphones allow for even more customization than feature phones. Using launchers, it's possible to transform a smartphone into something completely different. However, the overly detailed settings available in launchers are often not appealing to general users.

**3. Ringplay: Enjoying the Call Reception Screen**
Amidst this, I'd like to introduce Ringplay, a smartphone customization app that will immediately resonate with existing feature phone users.

Ringplay is an app that focuses on the call reception screen customization feature, which has consistently existed since feature phones. However, if it were exactly the same as old feature phones, there would be no need for me to review it. There are clearly significant differences.

**1) A feature to set the recipient's call screen, not my own**
If feature phones were about downloading a single call reception screen via micro-payment and setting it on my phone, this is about setting my own call reception screen. This concept is simple if you think about Facebook. If I set my photo on Facebook and call someone through Facebook's address book, that person will hear my phone ring with my Facebook photo. Traditional feature phones or address books required users to manually set photos for each person in their address book. This concept easily solves that problem. Ultimately, this feature allows individuals to determine their own identity.
**2) Powerful Customization Features**
If I only talk about it this way, you might just think of it as a phone address book combined with Facebook. Yes, that's true. But here's one more thing: it offers powerful customization features that Korean users (especially women and students) love.{{IMG:1}}{{IMG:2}}{{IMG:3}}
**[ A ring (incoming call background) created with a photo of my daughter ]**
You can set stickers, frames, and even artists' illustrations instead of your own photos. And in fact, the business model is structured around selling these artists' reception screens.
**3) I can change the caller's Ringplay**
You can change another person's Ringplay on your phone. If a friend you don't like is using a cool Ringplay, you can change it to a pig. Of course, the user will then be able to see that I've set them as a pig. If you change it without knowing this, you might end up in an awkward situation. This feature allows you to know how others perceive your identity, based on the identity you've set for yourself, and presents a concept where people can enjoy this interaction together.

**3. Call Screen Community, What if it Combines with Talk?**
Moving beyond just customizing one's own phone's incoming call background to synchronizing it with others and enjoying it together, the nature of this service is clearly closer to a community service. Therefore, it might be something that current popular and trending talk services could refer to. This is because talk services also revolve around phone numbers.

**4. The Regrettable Question: Will Ringplay's Partial Paid Model Succeed?**
Ringplay's business model is a partial paid model for rings. However, I'm not sure how high users' demand for paid content will be. It certainly has many charming elements that could appeal to students. But since smartphones allow users to directly load and set image files, the question is whether people would pay to purchase artists' images.
{{IMG:4}}{{IMG:5}}
**[ It consumes about 300p of credit per year. The moment I saw it, Cyworld came to mind. ]**
Compared to KakaoTalk, which is exploring other business models through gift-con partnerships and e-commerce, I wonder if Ringplay's business model is too weak, given the significant burden that both sender and receiver must install the same app. I think more creative BM discovery is needed.
Alternatively, it might work well if mobile carriers preloaded it. It could even be an advantage for users transitioning from feature phones. Also, since it competes with its charm, it might be worth targeting women and students. (It might even work in Japan.)

**5. Carrier Independence, Following Free SMS, Now Free Video Caller ID.**
Ringplay's greatest significance might be that the ringtone and phone background market, where mobile carriers once wielded all their power, has now become a case where any third-party app can enter as an independent service. Especially on Android devices, SMS and Call also operate as apps, making such service models possible. It's a system where infrastructure and service can be perfectly separated. Apps like KakaoTalk are sometimes referred to as free SMS apps. This is because smartphones allow for Push notifications in addition to SMS. (Though technically, it's not perfect Push yet.)
If KakaoTalk made SMS free between smartphone users, Ringplay has made video caller ID and caller ID services free. Isn't it wonderful that services previously known as mobile carrier services are now emerging as individual services, leading to diverse business models in today's smartphone market? I look forward to seeing what other services will emerge in the future.
