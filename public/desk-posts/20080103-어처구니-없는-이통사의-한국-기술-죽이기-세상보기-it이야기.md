7) Wifi는 되야지 않겠는가 ?

그것이 Wifi는 이통사의 수익을 해치는 모델로 간주하기 때문이라는군요.

그걸 막고 있다는 생각밖에 들지 않는군요.
Wifi를 사실 스마트폰 뿐만아니라, 기존 핸드폰에 접합하는것도 사실은 무리는 아니죠.

그 이유가 무엇일까요 ?

Feeling bored for the first time in a while, I decided to do some window shopping for a phone, even if I couldn't buy one.
 
Conditions:
 1) Must play videos
 2) Easy contact management
 3) Capable of long text input like documents
 4) Must have terrestrial DMB
 5) Must be a phone
 6) Option: Navigation expansion
 
Considering these conditions, it turns out only a smartphone fits ㅡㅡ;
I tried carrying a laptop to do document work, but concluded that even a 12-inch one was too much to pull out and use whenever I wanted.
So, the cheapest way to buy one is to choose a model integrated with a phone.
Therefore, I'm back to smartphones.
 
If the U160 had long text input, I probably would have chosen it...
So, as I was trying to choose a smartphone...
 
A new necessity emerged:
 7) Shouldn't it have Wifi?
After all, if it's a smartphone, my environment at home or work has wireless LAN...
 
So I looked it up ㅡㅡ;
Among recent phones that support Wi-Fi, the only one is Samsung's SCH-m620/m6200 BlackJack.
Models like Bluebird's BM500, which were barely released through group purchases thanks to netizens, couldn't even be launched.
(That's why it's not even in Naver's product database. Since it was released through group purchases, it circulates as skins or databases. Used ones have become rare.)
LG's recent C1 also removed Wi-Fi.
 
Apparently, Wi-Fi is considered a model that harms the profits of mobile carriers.
 
What on earth are they doing? ㅡㅡ;
Consumers want it, the technology exists, and if it spreads, the wireless internet market would obviously explode...
It just feels like they're blocking it.
In fact, integrating Wi-Fi isn't difficult not just for smartphones, but for existing mobile phones too.
 
These days, wireless internet APs are everywhere.
Naver has a wireless team, but I understand their profits are almost zero.
The same goes for other portals.
 
What could be the reason?
 
In Japan, wireless internet users have already surpassed wired internet users.
Isn't Korea's wireless infrastructure already excellent?
Even without using 3G, we can already enjoy a sufficiently limited wireless environment using APs.
The original purpose of 3G was "mobile" wireless communication.
It's not "fixed" wireless communication.
 
Yet, blocking Wi-Fi, which serves as "fixed" wireless communication, is truly absurd.
 
Korea's wireless internet speed is already the best in the world.
I acknowledge that.
 
However, it's already difficult to say that we have a high level of content and users globally.
How long must Korea's excellent technologies and competitive products disappear without even reaching the market, all to protect the narrow-minded interests of carriers fighting over their share in this small country?
 
The BlackJack probably came out because it was a global hit product for Samsung.
There are also rumors that the BlackJack 2 will remove Wi-Fi.
Some say it's due to interference from the built-in GPS...
Others say it's another conspiracy by the carriers...
 
Google's Android. Well, I'm skeptical too.
Korea might be excluded by the carriers.
 
Even when developing phones, I was annoyed by the ridiculous spec restrictions demanded by those carriers...
I quit phone development and had almost forgotten about it, but now it's making me angry again.
 
Next time I change my phone, I'll have to find a used BM500 or BlackJack ㅡㅡ;
