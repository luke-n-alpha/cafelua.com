[Image source: [Jung Joo-yong Columnist](https://www.facebook.com/photo.php?fbid=997307190299777&set=pcb.997307686966394&type=1&theater)]There have been vague sci-fi predictions that machines would surpass humans, but within 3-5 years. In other words, "it's currently under development!" Has there ever been an era where we could say that so unreservedly? The demographic cliff in 2019, when Korea's working-age population first began to decline, and 2020, when the emergence of human-like machines begins... Could this be an era of extreme change? Perhaps smartphones were just the prelude.
