**Empty Your Pockets**

{{IMG:1}}

When circumstances change, my journey will get a little better. I don't care about the saying that abundance makes one lazy. There's no such thing as a law of dreams anywhere.

Let's empty our pockets and set off on a journey, even if there's no destination. Let's quickly empty our pockets and set off on a journey, when our minds are confused.

I lay down in the grass and gazed at the stars in the sky. I spotted a shooting star, but had no wish to make. To love others, to live, to die...

Let's empty our pockets and set off on a journey, even if there's no destination. Let's quickly empty our pockets and set off on a journey, when our minds are confused.

We, who break this ridiculous fate and set off on a journey towards tomorrow, let's spread our wings high, leaving the iron streets behind.

Escaflowne **ポケットを空にして - 坂本眞綾** :: Sakamoto Maaya ::

**2003. 02. 17**
2015.07.08

It got complicated, and I wanted to leave feeling light. I always dreamed of traveling. New encounters, new challenges.
