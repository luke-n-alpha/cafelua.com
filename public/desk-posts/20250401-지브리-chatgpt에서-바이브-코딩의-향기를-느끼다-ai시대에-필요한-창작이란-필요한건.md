Today, while doing several tests, I've come to realize something about ChatGPT-4o's image generation method. If "Vibe Coding" is a coding style where you give clear instructions to the AI and then forget about it until it works, then ChatGPT-4o, famous for spitting out Ghibli-style drawings, seems to operate in a similar fashion. There are many different models and methods for creating images and videos recently. It feels like the LLM listens to the user's story, much like it performs a Chain of Thought (Cto), and converts it into prompts or various AI parameters. Then, it creates an optimal combination of model paths that align with the user's intent, and an AI agent continuously loops, evaluating whether it matches the user's intent until it deems it correct. This structure feels similar to Vibe Coding, where an AI coding tool receives a combination of user prompts and system prompts, plans necessary actions according to the Chain of Thought, performs automated results, and loops to retry upon failure. This method doesn't produce results in one go and consumes a lot of resources. Furthermore, it's not as "vibey" as one might think, as it's constrained by the characteristics of existing AI models. If context size is the biggest obstacle in coding tools, then problems like models tending to overfit to specific art styles or forgetting previously given instructions (similar to LLMs) will arise. Also, once it goes down a wrong path, it's hard to escape. This seems to be an attempt to reflect the shift in image and video generation from simply producing a single image to building workflows, which has spread since ComfyUI became popular two years ago. In other words, the knowledge and know-how of those who previously built AI-based image and video production processes can still be valid. This is similar to how SW engineering knowledge and AI knowledge are meaningful when developing AI coding. Just as one codes by systematically understanding the limitations and characteristics of AI through SW engineering and AI knowledge, professional productions will exert powerful influence when creating content by understanding the limitations and characteristics of AI within a systematically established image or video production process. This was the same reason I sought out specialists when working on AI webtoons, and it proved effective.

Don't be wary of amateurs generating AI art, but rather professionals who use AI tools.
