I didn't intend to draw a boxing girl from the start.
I was trying to draw a shy girl (how is this shy?! {{IMG:2}})
But I messed up the initial composition, and no matter how I looked at it, her right hand was a fist.
Plus, her facial expression... {{IMG:3}}

So, I ended up changing it to a fist. {{IMG:4}}

Artrage just isn't it; Painter seems to be the best.
It's been ages since I last picked up my tablet.
