Even though it was neither good news nor joyful news at all, seeing comments like '[Thanks for the good news.]' on that post made me decide to take drastic measures. Simple greeting comments that seem to be posted by a machine will be [reported] and [blocked].
ex)
Thank you for the good post, OOO.
OOO was here. Please visit my blog too.
Thank you for the good information.
Thank you for the information about OOO.
Such thanks and greetings are not needed. I reject bot-like posts that are almost certainly for the purpose of blog visits rather than genuine greetings or communication.
Please leave greetings in the guestbook.
