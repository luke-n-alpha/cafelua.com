아이폰 출시가 눈앞에 다가온 듯합니다. 그동안 스마트폰 유저로 시장과 통신사에게 소외를 받던 설움을 아이폰이 한방에 날려주는군요. 전 여러가지 이유로 아이폰을 사용할 생각이 없습니다. 그러나 그동안 아이폰보다 막강한 스마트폰을 가지고서도 절름발이 상태이던 현 상황을 아이폰이 한방에 해결해 주셨네요.

**(하지만 제 이용 패턴에 맞춰보면 아이폰을 구매할 생각은 전혀 안듭니다.)**

It seems the iPhone launch is just around the corner. The iPhone is instantly sweeping away the resentment that smartphone users have felt, being marginalized by the market and carriers. I have no intention of using an iPhone for several reasons. However, the iPhone has instantly resolved the current situation, which was crippled despite having smartphones more powerful than the iPhone.

**I am truly grateful for the iPhone launch.**
**As an existing smartphone user, I'd like to share a few benefits I've gained from the iPhone launch.**
**(However, looking at my usage patterns, I have no intention of buying an iPhone.)**

**Benefit 2 from iPhone: Full-fledged Wifi allowance for domestic smartphones**
The iPhone instantly quelled the Wifi controversy surrounding smartphones, including the BM500, ultimately informing carriers that Wifi is the mainstream, and now all recently released smartphones are allowed to have Wifi. Thanks to this, the equation 'smartphone = Wifi' is now perfectly established, and furthermore, with KTF even introducing 'Cook&Show' for Wifi convergence, the iPhone's role in bringing Wifi to the forefront is immense.

**Benefit 3 from iPhone: Emergence of realistic wireless plans**
In line with KTF's iPhone plan, which I envied the most at the time of the iPhone launch, SKT also released its own plans. The moment I saw these plans,** I was truly moved. It was the first time a realistic pricing plan, which I had longed for during my 10+ years of using smartphones, had appeared.**

The so-called All-in-One plans

All-in-One 35: 150 free minutes/150 free texts/100MB = 35,000 won
[All-in-One 45: 200 free minutes/200 free texts/500MB = 45,000 won](http://www.tworld.co.kr/normal.do?serviceId=S_PROD2001&viewId=V_PROD2001&prod_id=NA00002707)
All-in-One 55: 300 free minutes/200 free texts/700MB = 55,000 won

If you only use it in emergencies, All-in-One 35; if you want to use it generously, for a smartphone user, All-in-One 45 is the way to go. 500MB is just the right amount of data.
And these plans are also available to existing 'slave contract' holders... truly grateful.
**(With this, the 3,000 won Me2 option can be removed.)**

If you don't have a 'slave contract' and use minimal basic fees because your usual call and text volume is low,
and you want to use data like crazy on your smartphone,
[Ansim Data 190](http://www.tworld.co.kr/normal.do?serviceId=S_PROD2001&viewId=V_PROD2001&prod_id=NA00002494) also seems valid. This is 2GB.
Standard plan (12,000 won) + 19,000 won (2GB) = 31,000 won

* Of course, there are exceptions, such as using it as a modem for a laptop.
** (I don't quite understand why there are exceptions when you're paying for usage anyway... Ultimately, it might mean they're selling data at a loss and intend to collect fees from users who can't actually use all that data.)**

The reason I envied the iPhone until now was precisely its pricing plans. Now that that problem has been solved, there's actually no reason to envy the iPhone anymore. If you're going to talk about the App Store with its many applications, I'd like to tell you to try Windows Mobile once.

I got to touch a friend's actual iPhone model, and perhaps because it was used so messily, or maybe because of the high-gloss material, it was quite dirty with fingerprints. Moreover, the iPhone's design no longer looks unique because it's been copied so much by the Touch and existing MP3 players. The X1, on the other hand, looks much cooler when it's in its leather case. My cousins' reactions were that the iPhone is an MP3 player, but the X1 is a computer.
 
 Actually, a smartphone is a very useful device if you use it well. I've gone through Cellvic NX, Cellvic XG, Cellvic My Cube, POZ, m6200, m4650, and now the X1, but how well you utilize the device is more important than how good the device is. If you don't use it well, a smartphone can be more inconvenient than a budget phone. I've seen countless people who jumped into smartphones recklessly because they were pretty and new, only to put them up for resale. Honestly, if you're not going to customize it or install new apps, a smartphone is worse than a Lollipop phone. The iPhone probably isn't an exception. **(I agree with those who say that cell phones are becoming unnecessarily complicated.)**
 
 Also, for those who boast about apps on the App Store, I'll reveal my list of applications for WinMo. Honestly, I don't feel the need for anything more. ㅡㅡ;
 
 
**1. MS-SMS**
 : Shows messages in a messenger format per user, much faster than traditional SMS. A grateful program that solved the problem of receiving dozens of system-sent texts daily, driving me crazy. (Disadvantage: MMS is not supported)
**2. G-Alarm **
 : Made me realize how well an alarm program can be made. It even has a feature where you have to solve a quiz to turn off the alarm. ㅡㅡ;
**3. Naver Map**
 : The best. GPS linkage, fast route finding, public transport, satellite map, real-time traffic conditions.
**4. Wingbus**
 : Haven't used it much for finding restaurants. I did have an experience where I visited a restaurant, lamented how bad the food was, and then realized through a Wingbus search that it had a bad reputation.
**5. Me2 Pocket Uploader**
 : Makes the Me2-exclusive data plan (3000 won) unnecessary when using a data plan.
**6. Naver Webtoon**
 : Free comics, Wifi temporary storage.
**7. Power Radio **
 : More useful than Xperia's default radio.
**8. Opera Browser**
 : No words needed, just full browsing. Mainly visits Naver Mobile, Me2day, etc.
**9. Pocket Artist **
 : Reads PSD files, identical to Photoshop, amazing brushes. Can even draw with it.
**10. Astromist**
 : Good for finding constellations. Finds everything when you go stargazing.
**11. Financial/Engineering Calculator**
 : Didn't have much use for it.
**12. Memo**
 : Used the most. Diligently typed with QWERTY, synced for blogging and novel writing.
**13. hTorch**
 : Uses Xperia as a flashlight.
**14. SbpMobile Shell**
 : Beautiful UI, includes weather info by default.
**15. PowerDic 2.1**
 : Contains the entire Prime English-Korean dictionary (says you need to buy a separate language pack for pronunciation. Thinking of buying it later).
**16. Default Calendar, To-Do, Contacts**
 : Identical to Outlook. No explanation needed. Essential for anyone who uses Outlook for work.
**17. CorePlayer**
 : MP3 is standard, plays DivX, can even watch subtitles. Powerful.
**18. Camera**
 : Basic function, image quality is truly art. Good enough to be mistaken for a point-and-shoot camera. Exposure adjustment possible.
