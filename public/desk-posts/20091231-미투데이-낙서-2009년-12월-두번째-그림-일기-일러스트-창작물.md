**December 31, 2009**
{{IMG:3}}
A chronic illness that lingers in the body and torments a person for a long time is generally a terrible experience.
However, if there's an exception, it's lovesickness.
Of course, it can entail as much pain as any other illness.
Still, I hope my love is a chronic illness that remains in my body for a long time, rather than a love that passes with the season.
tag. An illness worth suffering, I love you
