**Title: SMSC 2013 Smart Marketing Strategy Conference**[http://www.inews24.com/smsc/2013](http://www.inews24.com/smsc/2013)
**Subtitle: The Future of Social Mobile Platforms**
**Venue: Korea Science and Technology Center**

**Date: March 5, 2013, 9:30~17:30**
**Organizer: iNews24 Co., Ltd.**

Sharing a review of the event/training I attended the day before yesterday. Some commonly covered or well-known content has been omitted, and I've added some personal notes in bold.
**Session 1. Changes in the Global Mobile Platform Market**
 1) Potential Success Factors for Apple, Google, and MS Platforms
   - Apple: First Mover / iTunes music leverage effect / Securing competitiveness for continuous competition
   - Google: Fast Follow / Open source, strategy to marginalize other platforms with a duopoly strategy against Apple / Allied forces strategy (devices, manufacturers, mobile carriers)
   - MS: Over 10 million VB developers worldwide / Office, business laptop demand / Direct release of low-cost devices
**   Personal Note: I'm not sure if 10 million VB developers is such a big strength. I understand it's based on C# development, so perhaps including C++ developers would have been better.**
** 2) Mobile Platform Issues by Industry Sector**
   - Mobile Carriers: Competition with device manufacturers and market / Lack of killer apps to replace voice calls / Success of their own app stores
   - OS Developers: Competition with Apple / OS market share / Securing 3rd Party developers
   - Device Manufacturers: Competition with Apple / Binary choice between Android and Windows Mobile / Launching their own app stores
   - Developers: Limitations beyond a certain scale / Platform selection and focus / Cross-platform efficiency (HTML5, etc.)
** 3) Expected Changes in Mobile Platforms from 2013 Onwards**
   2009~2012: Apple vs Android
   2013~     : Multimedia Sector [Apple (weak) vs Android (strong)]
               Business Sector [Android vs Windows]
**   Personal Note (Summary): Up until 2012, in the war between Apple and Android, Android caught up with Apple through a fast-follow strategy and gained the upper hand in market share. From 2013, the competition in mobile platforms, expanded into the business sector, is expected to be a battle between Android and Windows.**
 - Apple's market share is expected to drop to 10-20%, with Google and MS splitting the remainder.
 - Service platforms built on top of major platforms, like Line and Kakao, will emerge to lead or disrupt the market.
** 4) Q&A**
   - Will Tizen with HTML5 take off?
         => Negative view. Few businesses other than Samsung are interested (even Intel isn't very active), and Samsung's investment is not as large as expected (about 1 billion KRW in each of 80 countries).
   - How about the Kakao platform?
        => There's a worrying trend of reverting to a vertical platform like MCP.
**   Personal Note (Summary): In the case of the Kakao platform, while I agree it has the characteristics of a vertical platform, I believe it has a complementary relationship with the overly expansive Google Play (to be posted later).**

**Session 2. KT Mobile Platform Strategy**
**1) KT's Future Platform Strategy**
    - Expansion of CP targets: Local/In-house -> Third-party/Global
    - Expansion of products: KT Sellers -> Real Goods (physical) business partnerships
    - Layer independence: Device/Network dependency -> Independence
    - Change in customer approach: Viral/Benefit-centric -> Interactive personalized services (Big Data, personalization)
**Aside: There was nothing particularly special. In summary, KT's platform aims to break its dependence on its own infrastructure-based businesses, expand into physical goods business platforms, and leverage big data.**

**Session 3. SKT Social & Mobile Platform Strategy**
**1) Characteristics of Platform Business**
    - Winner take it all: Monopoly by the winner
     **Aside: I believe niche markets also exist in platform businesses, so I think it's difficult to define platforms solely by "winner take it all."**

**2) Big Data Trend Environment**
    - Number of devices, internet users, data explosion

**3) SKT's Platform Businesses**
    - SKT Digital Marketing Platform: Social network analysis marketing platform
    - Geo Vision Gallery: Region-based marketing platform
    - Big Data Platform:
     Collection of various source data (mobile devices, SK affiliate information, external data) -> Storage (distributed storage) -> Analysis -> Data provision (OLAP, OpenAPI, Download)
    - NFC Platform: Social, location-based
      **Aside (Summary): SKT aims to build a big data platform based on various data it can secure, and sell it as a B2B marketing tool.**
**The example photo shown analyzed a user's Naver web visit history, which I believe violates net neutrality.**

**Legally, processing data not specific to an individual to create rules might not be an issue, but it seems problematic for a network provider to analyze such packets for commercial purposes without consulting the user and service provider.**
**Technically, this constitutes sniffing. It didn't seem much different from a telephone company wiretapping and using call content for marketing.**
**If such attempts increase, I think service providers should encrypt packets. Big data's most sensitive aspect, which needs to be addressed, is user consent.**

-- Lunch, and came in late --
**Session 4. N-Screen and U+AD Advertising Platform**
**1) N-Screen Advertising Platform Presentation**
   - Geo-targeting, big data, high-efficiency advertising
   - Digital Signage Advertising Platform
     : The effectiveness of digital signage advertising is currently judged only by exposure, leading advertisers to doubt its efficacy.
    **Aside (Summary): I arrived late and couldn't hear everything. It was mainly about advertising platforms using location-based push and digital signage, and how these ads increase efficiency through big data-based user analysis. User consent was emphasized more compared to the SKT presenter.**

**Session 5. Online Marketing Strategy and Cases Using YouTube**
**1) YouTube's Current Status and Changes**
    - Where all hot issues exist (e.g., Gangnam Style)
    - Cross-platform encompassing 3 screens
    - Overwhelming No. 1 as a video platform
    - Evolution from a UGC platform to Web TV
    - Strengthening channel features (subscriptions)
    - Where broadcasts can be accessed before TV (This seems related to the effectiveness of the advertising platform mentioned later.)
 **2) YouTube Users, Content Consumption Habits of Generation C**
    - Curators (users who select and recommend content from too much content)
    - Creation (smartphones lower the barrier to creation)
    - Sharing (viral, spread of SNS)
    - Community (mobile, social)
**3) Reasons to Market on YouTube**
    - Sufficient Pool and targeting possible
     : Global platform
     : 15 million users as of 2012 (seems to refer to domestic Android users)
     : Login-based customer segmentation (device-based wasn't mentioned, but seems possible)
    - Precise ad effect analysis possible at low cost
     : For TV, it's difficult to know the effect after execution; YouTube allows real-time, specific user response tracking, reducing trial and error for expensive TV ad campaigns.
      (This seems to be related to the position of being able to access broadcasts before TV.)
    - Platform where virality is possible across all platforms
 **4) TrueView**
     : Delivering personalized ads via ad skipping
     - Delivers relevant ads to users at the beginning of video viewing, allowing them to skip. By analyzing the relevance between skipped ads and users, it identifies suitable target users.
     **\* Aside (Summary): It largely explained YouTube's evolution into broadcasting and its suitability as an advertising platform. The ability to measure video ad effectiveness at low cost, reducing TV ad trial and error, and targeted advertising using user analysis via ad skipping were key. YouTube has already created a higher-level advertising platform compared to current UGC platforms and simple DA-level video ads. There's nothing more to say than "they're doing well."**

**Session 6. Naver App Store, Total Supporters for Domestic App Developers**
**1) Purpose of Naver App Store**
   - Revenue (not very important), search for information users want, win-win with 3rd Parties (extending the lifecycle of apps that quickly disappear from trends and finding win-win points)
   - Naver's own apps are completely excluded from Naver App Store rankings.
   - Plans to provide a total solution including IAP and marketing tools.
   - Multi-platform store supporting iOS and Android.
**Aside: Note-taking stopped during Session 6 due to battery drain ㅜㅜ. Thanks to that, I bought a Windows Atom tablet, W510, which supposedly lasts 18 hours. (Review to follow later)**

**Session 7. Social/Mobile Platform Status and Outlook**
**Aside: I expected Tizen talk, but they talked about other things, so I don't remember what I heard... Tizen is reportedly difficult to mention due to security issues. ChatON, DLNA function, AllShare, S Cloud were presented.**
**Every time I see a manufacturer's presentation, I feel that while they diligently build service lineups, they lack talent in delivering services to users. I think it's fundamentally wrong to expect users to go to [Settings] to do something.**
**In fact, "Settings" is the least clicked menu among all menus, so the very idea that you have to click Settings to do something is already an error. How many people have ever clicked the Settings on their TV?**

**Session 8. Vertical Photo SNS Expansion Strategy Using 'Cymera'**
**1) Plastic Surgery Camera App Based on Face Recognition and Image Processing**
    - Popular among women
    - Secured 5 million users
   **2) Plans to reduce dependence on Cyworld and expand into an independent SNS app.**
**Aside: It seems more difficult than expected, as it doesn't have an overwhelming advantage over Pudding/Naver Camera/LINE Camera in Korea. First, the problem is the lack of storage space like N Drive and an independent SNS. Apps that create effects or specific types of photo images have low technical hurdles and are easy to abandon. The number of photos taken isn't as important as photos saved on the service for lock-in effect, but Cymera hasn't secured this, so its prospects don't look very bright. SK Comms is a bit frustrating... I wonder if something will come out of SKP... The revamped T Store has improved, though.**

**Session 9. Kakao's Marketing Platform Strategy and Plus Success Cases**
**1) Kakao's Current Status**
   - Mobile-specialized company
   - Holds the largest social graph in Korea
   - No. 1 in mobile user time share
**Aside: There were some subtle remarks, presumably targeting Naver, such as statements inferred from PC UVs. Considering Naver hasn't dominated SNS and messaging, I personally think Naver is doing surprisingly well. Comparing time share between search apps and messaging apps, messaging apps are naturally overwhelming... However, it's undeniable that KakaoTalk is an essential app on mobile.**
   - Ecosystem, win-win company: Plus Friend, Gift하기 (Gifting), Emoticons, Story/Story Platform, KakaoLink, Game하기 (Gaming), KakaoPage
   - Rapid growth, aiming for 100 million this year.
   **2) Plus Friend Upgrade**
   - Interactive user participation possible:
    Existing one-way push-based Plus Friend has been modified to allow interaction through chat window functions, enabling event execution.
   **Aside: LINE had such bots from the beginning, and I predicted in an old post that Kakao would definitely follow this feature... It hit the mark again. Good things seem to be copied by each other.**
   - Increased advertiser freedom with a more dynamic and flexible home screen.
**3) Kakao Story Plus**
   **- Brand Account: Unlimited friends, ability to distribute user stories to the news tab, pricing undecided.**
**Aside: What will sell on Kakao Story, which is becoming a parenting forum? I expect mainly household goods and parenting products. I question whether marketing will work well on a closed SNS like Kakao Story, where privacy needs are high. Game messages are already annoying... Seeing ads on the Story timeline would be really irritating. If it does work, it would be a highly effective advertising platform with high costs and high returns. Rewards must be clear for virality to move, and if virality works, it explodes instantly... Also, I have a slight doubt whether KakaoTalk's Plus Channel and Kakao Story Plus, which use the same social graph, might cannibalize each other.**

**End of intense summary. It was a good conference.**
