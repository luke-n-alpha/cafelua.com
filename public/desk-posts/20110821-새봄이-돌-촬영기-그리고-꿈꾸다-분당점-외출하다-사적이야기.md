2011년 8월 19일 회사에서 오후 반차를 쓰고 딸아이의 돌 촬영을 갔습니다.
사실 성장앨범으로 타 스튜디오에서 촬영을 했었는데, 옷이 그다지 맘에 들지 않아 결국 이왕 찍어주는거 돌 촬영만 다시 찍기로 했습니다. 50일, 100일때에 비해서 훨씬 활동량이 많고 잘 웃는 지금은 더 좋은 사진을 찍어줄 수 있을 것 같았거든요.
그래서 선배의 추천에 의해서 가게된 그리고 꿈꾸다 분당점을 골랐습니다.
[http://ndreaming.com/php/ndream_main.php](http://ndreaming.com/php/ndream_main.php)
첫인상은 홈페이지에 있는 일러스트형 사진들이 너무 마음에 들었습니다. 크래용을 들고 있는 예쁜 아이의 사진은 새봄이가 마음껏 걸어다닐때나 찍을 수 있을 법한 사진이긴 했지만 고래배경과 토끼배경사진들도 너무 귀여워보여서 이쪽으로 가기로 했습니다.
성장 앨범을 이미 하나 하기로 한지라, 금전적으로 부담이 되긴했었는데, 전화를 드려보니 백일사진의 단독앨범 가격으로 해주신다고 해서 바로 예약했죠.

**1. 찾아가는 길**
일단 회사에서 너무 가까웠던 것도 좋았구요. (회사에서 10분)

도착한 곳의 입구는 한적한 주택가 한가운데 자리잡고 있는 빨간색 지붕이 인상적인 집이었습니다. 오른쪽의 Enterance를 못보고 아내는 옆에 잠겨 있는 문의 벨을 눌렀습니다. 옆에도 스튜디오이긴 한데, 오른쪽 열려있는 문으로 들어가는 겁니다. 길 바로 건너에는 조그만 주차장이 있어서 주차하기도 손쉽습니다.
{{IMG:1}}

**2. 촬영 시작﻿﻿**
4시 촬영 예정이었는데, 3시 15분정도 도착해서 새봄이의 이유식을 먹이고 촬영이 시작되었습니다.  역시 촬영의 관건은 새봄이 웃기기. 비누방울을 너무 신기해한 나머지 평소처럼 까르르 웃지는 못한편이었지만 그래도 예쁘게 웃어줘서 다행이었습니다. 사실 아이하나를 웃기기 위해서 어른들이 얼마나 많은 노력을 해야하는지.. ^^; 기사님과 언니가 pro정신을 발휘하여 새봄이의 예쁜 표정을 만들 수 있었습니다.

On August 19, 2011, I took a half-day off from work in the afternoon to go for my daughter's first birthday photoshoot.
Actually, we had already done a growth album at another studio, but I wasn't very happy with the outfits, so I decided to re-shoot just the first birthday photos. Compared to when she was 50 or 100 days old, she's much more active and smiles a lot now, so I thought we could get better pictures.
So, based on a senior colleague's recommendation, we chose the And Dreaming Bundang branch.
[http://ndreaming.com/php/ndream_main.php](http://ndreaming.com/php/ndream_main.php)
My first impression was that I really liked the illustrated photos on their website. The picture of a pretty child holding a crayon seemed like something we could only take when Saebom could walk freely, but the whale and rabbit background photos also looked so cute that we decided to go with this studio.
Since we had already committed to one growth album, it was a financial burden, but when I called, they offered to do it at the price of a standalone 100-day photo album, so I booked it right away.

**1. Getting There**
First, it was great that it was so close to my office. (10 minutes from work)

The entrance to the place we arrived at was a house with an impressive red roof, nestled in the middle of a quiet residential area. My wife didn't see the "Entrance" on the right and pressed the bell on the locked door next to it. The door next to it is also a studio, but you enter through the open door on the right. There's a small parking lot right across the street, making parking easy.
{{IMG:1}}

**2. Starting the Shoot**
The shoot was scheduled for 4 PM, but we arrived around 3:15 PM, fed Saebom her baby food, and then the shoot began. As expected, the key to the shoot was making Saebom laugh. She was so fascinated by the bubbles that she didn't giggle as much as usual, but thankfully she still smiled beautifully. Honestly, how much effort adults have to put in just to make one child laugh... ^^; The photographer and assistant showed their professional spirit and managed to capture Saebom's beautiful expressions.

**[ She was so fascinated and loved the bubbles during the shoot that I decided to buy her a bubble machine. ]**

We took photos with 3 concepts and a family photo. As expected, the beautiful illustrated backgrounds I saw in the pictures really stood out.

**Concept 1. Green Fish**
{{IMG:2}}
The fresh green fish concept. I had put a similar hat on her before, and it didn't suit her at all, but this hat looked great on her. The problem was that Saebom dislikes hats and takes them off easily, so we'd stick scotch tape on her arms to distract her, and then quickly put the hat on. She'd forget she was wearing a hat and show us beautiful smiles at the adults' antics.

**Concept 2. Purple Rabbit**
**{{IMG:3}}**
The purple rabbit concept features a long-tailed hat and a rabbit doll attached to the outfit. The long-tailed hat was really cute.

**Concept 3. Apple Hair, Whale Room**
{{IMG:4}}
This "apple ponytail" hair was Mom's idea. The red is the highlight. ^^ The whale in the background isn't very prominent in this photo.

**Last Family Photo.**
{{IMG:5}}
This is the last family photo. ^^ Some places don't really prioritize family photos, but they took good care of us here. We hadn't brought family shirts, but they matched Saebom's outfit well to our clothes. During the shoot, Saebom pinched Mom's nose! At Mom's request, Mom's face is blurred.

**3. Saebom Has Grown This Much.**
It feels like just yesterday Saebom was wiggling around in Mom's tummy like a peanut.

**Becoming a Dad (2010.03.22)** [http://fstory97.blog.me/70082787442](http://fstory97.blog.me/70082787442)

Now she's already standing a little, taking a few steps, and hearing her say "Daddy~ Daddy~" feels so amazing. It hasn't even been 365 days yet. September 30th is Saebom's first birthday, and September 24th is her first birthday party. I can totally relate to all dads being "daughter-fools." I should probably make a new blog skin with the pretty photos taken at And Dreaming. ^^
