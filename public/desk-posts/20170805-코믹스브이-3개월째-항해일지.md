##### 1. 폭풍 같은 7개월

VR웹툰 스타트업 코믹스브이를 정식으로 선장으로 키를 잡은지 이제 딱 3개월이 지났습니다. 정말 1년  같은 3개월이었다는 생각이 드네요. 정확히 코믹스브이 설립일은 올해 1월 10일이었지만, 정식 대표와 공동파운더인 CTO가 부재한 상황에서 진도는 더디기만 했습니다. 1월은 회사 설립하다 흐지부지 날아갔고 (X팔 공인인증서!)

2월은 28일인데다가 설이껴서 녹듯이 사라졌고(사장님들이 2월을 싫어하는 이유를 알것 같아요.)

{{IMG:2}}

3월에 샘플만들고 VR로 초기버전이 간략히 돌아가는 수준이 되었습니다. 이걸 가지고 3월에 부천국제만화축제의 VR웹툰 전시를 맞게된것은 큰 행운이었습니다. 4월부터는 웹쪽 기반을 다지고 5월부터 이제 정식으로 CTO와 제가 합류하면서 속도가 붙게 되었습니다. 이때즘 이름과 아이템만 보고 맞겨주신 초기 엔젤및 공동파운더의 돈이 소진되었고, 이제 정식으로 본 대표가 서면서 신용보증기금에서 돈을 빌리고, 부천국제만화축제의 돈이 나온것도 이때 즈음입니다.
돈이 생긴만큼 저와 CTO의 시니어가 합류했고, 작가들도 섭외하여 고료를 지불했습니다. 계약서 또한 작가님들에게 검증의 검증을 거쳐 시작부터 제대로 가자고 했습니다. 망하면 망했지, 초기 셋팅을 잘못하면 절대로 못 고칠테니까요.
합류후 작업 속도는 화악~! 늘었지만, 돈 나가는것도 같이 크게 증가했습니다. 초기 VC들도 난감해 했던 것이 다른 스타트업에 비해 작가 고료가 나가는데다가 시니어들의 인건비 또한 적지 않았다는 것 같습니다. 일반적인 웹툰 플랫폼에 비하면 몇분의 일 수준으로 아주 배고프게 시작하고 있지만, 반대로 흔히 말하는 대학생 창업팀에 비하면 두배가까이 자본이 빨리 소진된다는 우려가 들 수 밖에 없었습니다. 아무튼 결과적으로 빠르게 보여주는 수 밖에 없다는 생각이 들었습니다.

##### 2. 부천만화축제의 가장 큰 소득은 고객과 만남

그리고, 드디어 부천만화축제가 시작되면서 적지 않은 희망을 보았습니다.

유명 만화가들 ‘청년시절 희귀 원고’ 한자리에!

요즘 또 만화나 게임쪽은 코믹콘으로 뜨거운 듯 하지만, 오히려 저는 한걸음 떨어져서 보게 되었습니다.  BICOF와 SICAF를 거치면서 몇몇 제안과 실제 고객들을 만나면서 우리가 가야할 방향도 다시 생각해보게 되었습니다. IR문서도 싸그리 뜯어 고쳐야 할 것 같습니다.
많은 기관의 창업 양식의 아이디어 단계, 시제품단계, 상용화 단계.. 이런 단계들 별로 작성하는 폼에 맞춰 우리의 제안서를 작성하다보면 이게 맞나 싶습니다. 정부기관의 제안서들은 초기에 약속한 것을 반드시 지켜야만 합니다. 써야하는 돈의 범위나 결과물 또한 정의 되어있습니다. 그러나, 이렇게 고객을 만나고보니 중요하다고 생각했던것이 중요하지 않게 되고, 새롭게 발견되는 중요한 것들을 보면 이러한 제안서가 갖는 경직성은 스타트업의 목을 죌 수도 있겠다는 생각이 들었습니다.

##### 3. 보이는 방향과 시간과 자본의 소중함

할일이 늘고, 실제로 실행하면서 미처 알지 못했던 곳도 돈이 나가면서 생각 보다 더 빠르게 돈이 소진되는 것도 느낍니다. 아직은 적극적으로 관심을 가져주는 VC들은 없지만, 그래도 한번씩 관심을 던져주시는 분들도 있어 희망을 가져보았습니다.
무엇보다도 소중한 경험은 고객을 만났고, 그들을 알았다는데 위안을 삼습니다. 우리가 가야할 방향이 큰 틀에서는 틀리지 않았으며 또 다른 기회가 있다는 것도 발견했습니다. 제법 큰 기업에 있었을 때와 많은 것이 다릅니다. 성장만 고민하던 때와 달리 한편으로는 생존을 같이 고민해야 됩니다. 좋은 반응이 마음이 안편한 이유고, 큰 기업에 있었다면 훨씬 더 반응을 키울 수 있었을텐데 하는 생각도 들어 조금은 아쉽기도 합니다. 그리고 반응을 매출로 만드는건 또 다른 이야기일 테니까요. 모든 것이 조금은 더 조급하고 더 소중합니다.
멘토님은 그러시더군요. 이 때즈음 대표가 더 의연해지고 중심을 잘 잡아야 한다고요.  하지만 그게 쉬운건 아닌것 같습니다. 여름휴가는 거의 못 갈듯 하지만, 내일은 가까운데라도 가보려고 합니다.(그런데 태풍온데요 ㅜㅜ) 회사는 무조건 돈 버는 줄 아는 우리 딸 내미는 가끔 철없이 이렇게 묻습니다. 아빠 얼마 벌어?  (아직 돈 벌때가 아니란다.)

##### 1. Seven Stormy Months

It's been exactly three months since I officially took the helm as captain of the VR webtoon startup ComixV. It truly feels like three months that lasted a year. Although ComixV was officially established on January 10th this year, progress was slow due to the absence of a formal CEO and co-founder CTO. January flew by vaguely with company establishment tasks (damn public certificates!).

{{IMG:1}}

https://blog.naver.com/PostView.naver?blogId=fstory97&logNo=221067665070&redirect=Dlog&widgetTypeCall=true&noTrackingCode=true&directAccess=false#

February, being only 28 days and having Seollal (Lunar New Year) in it, melted away like snow (I think I understand why bosses dislike February).

{{IMG:2}}

https://blog.naver.com/PostView.naver?blogId=fstory97&logNo=221067665070&redirect=Dlog&widgetTypeCall=true&noTrackingCode=true&directAccess=false#

In March, we created samples, and a basic VR version became briefly functional. It was a great stroke of luck that we were able to showcase our VR webtoons at the Bucheon International Comics Festival in March with this. From April, we solidified our web-based infrastructure, and from May, with the official joining of the CTO and myself, things really picked up speed. Around this time, the initial angel and co-founder funds, entrusted to us based solely on our name and item, were depleted. With a formal CEO now in place, we secured a loan from the Korea Credit Guarantee Fund, and the funds from the Bucheon International Comics Festival also came in around this time. With the new funds, senior members, including myself and the CTO, joined, and we also recruited artists and paid their fees. We also had the contracts thoroughly reviewed by the artists, aiming to start properly from the beginning. We figured it was better to fail than to make mistakes in the initial setup that would be impossible to fix later. After we joined, the work speed dramatically increased, but so did our expenses. Early VCs seemed perplexed that, compared to other startups, we had artist fees to pay, and the labor costs for our senior staff were also significant. While we are starting very lean, at a fraction of the cost of typical webtoon platforms, there was also concern that our capital would be depleted almost twice as fast compared to what is commonly referred to as a university student startup team. In any case, I felt that we had no choice but to show results quickly.

##### 2. The Biggest Gain from Bucheon Comics Festival: Meeting Customers

And then, finally, as the Bucheon Comics Festival began, we saw no small amount of hope.

[VR Webtoon Exhibition, Bucheon Comics Festival Site Thank you to everyone who visited the ComixV exhibition held from July 19th to 23rd. A successful ex... comixv.blog.me](http://comixv.blog.me/221062980208)

All development was aligned to this starting point, so the backend was somewhat haphazard. After that, through SICAF, we met customers directly and even secured a permanent exhibition space at the Cartoon Museum.

[Bucheon Manhwa Museum, ComixV VR Webtoon Permanent Exhibition Opening Amidst much praise and interest, ComixV VR webtoons are now on permanent exhibition on the 1st floor of the Bucheon Manhwa Museum! Co... comixv.blog.me](http://comixv.blog.me/221066123876)

We appeared on TV news twice, had several media contributions, and were even featured on a government portal representing Korea.

https://www.youtube.com/embed/d5c-65q43QM?wmode=transparent

Famous Cartoonists' 'Rare Manuscripts from Their Youth' Gathered in One Place!

These days, the comic and game industries seem to be buzzing with Comic-Con, but I've actually taken a step back to observe. Going through BICOF and SICAF, meeting several potential clients and actual customers, made me reconsider the direction we should take. It seems I'll have to completely overhaul the IR documents as well.
When we draft our proposals to fit the forms for each stage of startup development – idea stage, prototype stage, commercialization stage – as required by many institutions, I wonder if this is truly the right approach. Proposals for government agencies must strictly adhere to what was initially promised. The scope of spending and deliverables are also defined. However, after meeting customers, I realized that what I thought was important wasn't, and seeing the new important things that emerged, I felt that the rigidity of such proposals could potentially stifle a startup.

##### 3. The Visible Direction and the Value of Time and Capital

My workload has increased, and as I execute, I realize money is being spent in unexpected areas, depleting funds faster than anticipated. While there aren't any VCs actively showing interest yet, there are a few who occasionally express curiosity, which gives me hope.
Above all, the most valuable experience is having met customers and gotten to know them, which brings me comfort. I've also discovered that our overall direction isn't wrong, and there are other opportunities. Many things are different from when I was at a fairly large company. Unlike when I only worried about growth, now I also have to worry about survival. Good responses make me uneasy, and I also feel a bit regretful thinking that if I were at a large company, I could have amplified those responses much more. And turning responses into sales is another story altogether. Everything feels a bit more urgent and precious.
My mentor told me that around this time, the CEO needs to become more composed and maintain a strong center. But that doesn't seem easy. It looks like I won't be able to go on a summer vacation, but I plan to go somewhere nearby tomorrow (though a typhoon is coming ㅜㅜ). My daughter, who thinks companies always make money, sometimes innocently asks, "Dad, how much do you earn?" (It's not time to make money yet, my dear.)

After the vacation, I need to refocus and push forward again. This year's goal is to pay employee salaries and give my wife the promised amount. My wife's lament, that she wants to send our second child to swimming lessons, broke my heart. It seems there's always a price to pay for realizing a dream. But since I've started, shouldn't I give it a proper try, even if I fail once? Even though I think it was not a bad start, I find myself overthinking. I offer my sincere encouragement to all startup CEOs.
