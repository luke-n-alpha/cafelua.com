Oh, Lord of the star that shone 2000 years ago... I know this is a ridiculous and childish prayer, but please make everyone I know happy. Including that person. And if you have time left over, please make me happy too.
2003. 08. 09

Perhaps, in the end, He did have time left over? But now, the happiness that has become reality seems to carry more weight in its reality than in its happiness. I tell myself not to forget the feelings I had back then, but it's hard. Is it because I've forgotten to pray...?
