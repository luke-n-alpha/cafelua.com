I've revised and written down the blog's inherent purpose and operating principles that align with that purpose. The goal is to reorganize things due to many insights gained from recent posts. Given the nature of blog posts, which are often accessed individually through search, the operating principles below might feel one-sided, but I plan to insert a link to them at the top of future posts.

**Q. Whose opinion is this?**
A. All posts are **my personal opinions**, excluding the stance of any specific company, including previous and current organizations.

**Q. What is the purpose of your blog? What can one do here?**
A. The purpose of this blog in the IT sector is for **readers, including myself, to grow together.** My personal opinions may be wrong, and therefore, **many constructive opinions, including criticism, are welcome.**

*   Based on these operating principles, detailed operating principles are added and revised.
    *   **All posts and comments on this blog will use honorifics.** This is to prevent excessive emotional drain during discussions, and we kindly ask that comments also adhere to this as much as possible.
    *   **Simple promotional or greeting comments that are not opinions will be deleted.**
    *   **Sarcasm, personal attacks, etc., are not allowed. These will also result in a warning or deletion.** (**I have also used sarcasm in the past. I apologize for that. However, I believe this makes it difficult to establish a healthy discussion culture, so I will delete such comments, whether they agree or disagree with my opinion.**)
*   The above operating principles apply to posts written after August 3, 2016.

**Q. Can I share your content?**
A. We generally aim for an open web, and **you are free to share the content according to the CCL.** However, you **must not extract only a portion of the post to use it with a different intent or modify it.** If possible, please include the original URL. **For commercial use, you must obtain my permission.**

**Q. Can I pay to place an advertisement? How much does it cost?**
A. **While I do engage in some sponsored activities, I do not publish one-sided posts that exclude my opinion.** Sponsorship fees are charged per post, and it is a principle to disclose sponsored content within the body of the post. Commissions based on sales volume are not executed, and I cannot guarantee advertising effectiveness. I believe it is better to approach with a purpose closer to consulting than advertising.

**Q. Do you not allow mutual followers (서로이웃)?**
A. I **do not accept mutual followers for one-sided information pushes.** I ask that you apply for mutual followers after sustained interaction and a "face-to-face" connection. I accept mutual followers for those I have met in person offline, and exceptionally, for those who can convince me of a compelling reason to connect as mutual followers.
