**1. 뜨거워진 VR시장과 MS의 홀로렌즈**

최근 인공지능의 등장으로 좀 뜸해졌지만, 2016년 최고의 화두는 VR의 상용화다. 일찍이 삼성전자가 기어VR을 내놓았고, 페이스북이 투자한 오큘러스, 소니의 모피어스, HTC와 Steam의 Vibe까지. 그리고, 얼마전 MS는 3000달러, 국내 돈으로는 약 370만원에 홀로렌즈의 개발자 버전의 예약 판매를 시작했다. 앞서 말한 제품들 중 가장 형식이 다른 것은 홀로렌즈라고 할 수 있다. 가장 고가면서도 VR과는 다른 AR과 홀로그램 기술은 가장 미래지향적인 기술이다. 모바일의 뒤를 잇는 플랫폼 환경으로서 VR과 AR이 손꼽히고 있는 만큼 PC에서 윈도우즈로 생태계를 석권했던 마이크로소프트사의 행보가 기대된다. 아직 홀로렌즈의 배송이 시작된 시점은 아니지만, 이미 공개된 정보로 홀로렌즈의 스펙과 가능성을 알아보겠다.

**2. 홀로렌즈의 핵심 AR과 홀로그램**

VR과 AR은 가상의 물체를 보여준다는 점과, 사용자의 움직임의 정보를 반영한다는 점에서는 동일하다고 할 수 있다. 하지만, AR의 경우, 주변의 풍경위에 가상현실을 겹쳐 보여준다는 점이 크게 다르다. 스마트폰으로 구현한 가장 간단한 AR기술은 GPS 정보와 자이로센서와 중력센서를 이용하여 하늘을 보면 날씨를 보여주고, 주변의 지역정보를 보여주던 것은 익히 보아왔던 것이다. 그보다 좀 더 발전된 AR형태는 영상의 사물을 인식하고, 추가적인 정보를 더 할 수 있다. 아이언맨의 헬멧에서 추가적인 전투정보를 보여주는 것을 기억할 것이다. 실제로 현대전투기의 F-35의 HMD는 이러한 것이 가능한 시스템을 갖추고 있다 ​홀로그램은 빛으로 만든 가상 물체다. 이미 SF를 통해 우리에게는 이미 익숙하지만, 실제 구현에는 여러가지 장애 요인이 많아 제한적으로 사용되고 있었다. MS의 홀로렌즈는 이 홀로그램을 응용한 AR기기라고 할 수 있다. 그러면 홀로렌즈는 어떻게 홀로그램을 우리 눈에 보여줄까? 홀로렌즈는 두개의 Light engine을 가지고 있는데, 초당 수백만번의 빛조각을 방출하여 홀로렌즈의 이미지를 만들고, 이 이미지는 투명 디스플레이에 반사되어 눈에 도착한다. 아래의 그림은 한쪽눈에 가상의 이미지를 보여주는 것을 도식화 한 것 이다. 평판 디스플레이를 이용하는 VR과 크게 다른 점은 수정체에 도착하는 빛의 방향이 다양한 광장(Light Field)를 형성한다는 점이다. 이는 실제 사물처럼 거리에 따른 초점 문제를 해결할 수 있다.

**1. The Heating Up VR Market and MS HoloLens**

Although it has recently quieted down a bit with the advent of AI, the biggest topic of 2016 was the commercialization of VR. Samsung Electronics released Gear VR early on, followed by Oculus (invested by Facebook), Sony's Morpheus, and HTC and Steam's Vive. And recently, MS began pre-orders for the developer version of HoloLens at $3000, or about 3.7 million won in Korea. Among the products mentioned earlier, HoloLens is arguably the most different in form. It is the most expensive, and its AR and hologram technology, distinct from VR, is the most future-oriented. As VR and AR are considered the next platform environment after mobile, Microsoft's moves, which dominated the PC ecosystem with Windows, are highly anticipated. Although HoloLens has not yet started shipping, I will explore its specifications and potential based on already released information.

**2. HoloLens' Core: AR and Holograms**

VR and AR can be said to be similar in that they both display virtual objects and reflect information about the user's movements. However, AR differs significantly in that it overlays virtual reality onto the surrounding scenery. The simplest AR technology implemented on smartphones, which showed the weather when you looked at the sky and displayed local information using GPS, gyroscope, and gravity sensors, is something we are already familiar with. A more advanced form of AR can recognize objects in a video and add additional information. You might remember Iron Man's helmet displaying additional combat information. In fact, the HMD of modern fighter jets like the F-35 is equipped with a system capable of this. A hologram is a virtual object made of light. Although already familiar to us through sci-fi, its actual implementation has been limited due to various obstacles. MS's HoloLens can be described as an AR device that applies this hologram technology. So, how does HoloLens display holograms to our eyes? HoloLens has two light engines that emit millions of light fragments per second to create the HoloLens image, and this image is reflected onto a transparent display and reaches the eye. The figure below illustrates how a virtual image is shown to one eye. A major difference from VR, which uses flat panel displays, is that the light reaching the lens forms a diverse light field. This can solve the focus problem related to distance, just like with real objects.

**3. HoloLens Hardware**

One of the biggest differences between other VR devices and HoloLens is that it is a standalone device that does not require a separate PC or terminal. Simply put, you can think of it as a device that combines a PC and an HMD. The specifications of HoloLens, as revealed on MS's developer site, are as follows:

**1) Display**

- Two light engines capable of projecting 16:9 HD holographic images
- Holographic resolution: 2.3 million pixels
- Holographic density: 2,500 pixels per degree

**2) Processor and Memory**

- One Intel 32-bit CPU
- One HPU (hologram processor unit)
  * It is presumed to be a GPGPU specially designed to process holograms, combining the roles of CPU and GPU.
- 2GB RAM, 64GB storage

**3) Sensors**

- 1 inertial measurement unit
- 4 environmental understanding cameras
- 1 depth camera
- 2-megapixel camera, HD video camera
- 1 ambient light sensor
- 4 microphones

**4) Buttons**

- Volume, brightness adjustment, power

**5) Network**

- 802.11ac Wifi, BLE 4.1

**6) Other**

- Speakers, 3.5mm ear jack, USB 2.0

**7) Weight**

- 579g

**8) Battery**

- 2-3 hours, 2 weeks standby time

However, those who have experienced Google Glass might suspect that there could be a significant difference between the concept and the actual user experience. While it's impossible to be certain as the actual product has not yet shipped, there are reports that it differs quite a bit from the concept videos we've seen. First, there's criticism that the field of view (FOV) is only 30 degrees horizontally and 17.5 degrees vertically.

Furthermore, it is recommended that holograms be displayed within 1.25m to 5m, with 2m being the optimal distance. While we cannot be certain until we see the actual product, it seems unlikely that clear holograms can be seen at all distances.

This shows that, unlike typical VR, HoloLens is designed with Windows' inherent role as a productivity tool in mind.

**5. The Future Computing Environment Proposed by MS**

Above, we have examined the hardware and software of HoloLens. It is still uncertain whether HoloLens will operate as beautifully as shown in the pre-released demo videos. However, it is clear that it will offer a different experience from what current vision-obscuring VR devices provide. Microsoft suggests that HoloLens could be used for military or medical purposes and has plans for its use in spacecraft. It also holds potential as a new creative tool. Its technical completeness might be immature, like Google Glass, and it might fail to open up a commercial market. However, the direction HoloLens proposes for the future computing environment is very clear. The future computing environment presented by Microsoft means the environment we are in itself.
