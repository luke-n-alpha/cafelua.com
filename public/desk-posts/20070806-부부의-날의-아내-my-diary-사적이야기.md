She got angry because I took too long taking pictures. Before getting married, I didn't even know there was such a thing as Couples' Day. Hearing about Couples' Day on the radio, I bought a mini cake on my way home. I thought about buying an ice cream cake, but gave up due to the exorbitant price. And those mini cakes that usually seemed so easy to find, why were they so hard to buy that day? Because I left work late, every place I went said they were sold out, so I think I wandered around for almost 30 minutes. Before getting married, I always carried a camera and posted a lot on Cyworld because I spent a lot of time on the internet at home. Nowadays, it's just me visiting my wife's mini-homepage much later, exclaiming, "Ah~ so there was a photo like this," admiring it, and bringing it over.
