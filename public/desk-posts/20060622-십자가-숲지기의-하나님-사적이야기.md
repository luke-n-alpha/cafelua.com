Among those countless crosses,
Which one belongs to my God?

Are they merely tombstones in a graveyard where dead love is buried?

Lord, who awakens the dead,
Awaken my dead spirit and love,
Show me my path and bring peace to this troubled soul.

2004. 06. 24
