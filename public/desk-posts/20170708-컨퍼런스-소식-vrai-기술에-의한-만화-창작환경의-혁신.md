올해의 화두인 가상현실(VR), 인공지능(AI)과 만난 만화는 어떤 모습일까? 신기술을 적용한 만화 및 애니메이션 콘텐츠를 소개합니다. 가상현실 만화, 웹툰 플랫폼 개발기, 가상현실에서의 만화와 애니메이션, 창작자를 대체하거나 도울 인공 지능 기술을 주제로 관련 분야의 최고 전문가와 VR 유튜버, 만화작가가 함께 참여합니다.

**• 일      시 :****2017. 7. 23.(일) 13:00 ~ 15:30**

• 대      상 : VR/AI에 관심있는 업계 종사자, 만화가, 일반 관람객 등
**
13:00~13:20 Section1.** **가상현실 만화 플랫폼 개발기 **: 코믹스브이 양병석 대표 (20분)

- 만화에서 VR기술이 가지는 의미와 현황

- 인공지능이 만화가의 직업도 위협할까요?
**14:10~14:30 ******** ****가상현실 캐릭터 애니메이션** : 팬더8스튜디오 이배실 대표 (20분)

                         For those curious about ComicsV, the meaning of virtual reality and comics, and how the future comic profession environment will change, a conference will be held. Oh... and I'll be there too.

                         What will comics look like when they meet virtual reality (VR) and artificial intelligence (AI), the hot topics of this year? We introduce comic and animation content applying new technologies. Top experts in related fields, VR YouTubers, and comic artists will participate, discussing virtual reality comics, webtoon platform development, comics and animation in virtual reality, and AI technology that can replace or assist creators.

**• Date      :****2017. 7. 23.(Sun) 13:00 ~ 15:30**
**• Venue      :****Korea Manhwa Museum 5th Floor Seminar Room**
• Target Audience : Industry professionals interested in VR/AI, comic artists, general public, etc.
**
13:00~13:20 Section1.** **Virtual Reality Comic Platform Development**: Yang Byung-seok, CEO of ComicsV (20 min)
  - You can hear about ComicsV's founding background and development strategy.
**13:20~13:40 ****Section2.** **Virtual Reality Comic Content** : Professor Park In-ha, Cheonggang Culture & Arts University (20 min)
 - The meaning and current status of VR technology in comics
**13:50~14:10 ****Section3. ****AI and Artists** : Professor Jung Ji-hoon, Kyung Hee Cyber University (20 min)
 - Will AI threaten the jobs of comic artists too? 
**14:10~14:30 ******** ****Virtual Reality Character Animation** : Lee Bae-sil, CEO of Panda8 Studio (20 min)
 - Let's hear the story of Lee Bae-sil, CEO, the only full-fledged VR animation producer in Korea.

** * Panel Discussion**
 - Presenters plus VR expert YouTuber Meolmiwang, and "Wara! Convenience Store VR" artist Ji Kang-min

To apply, select
