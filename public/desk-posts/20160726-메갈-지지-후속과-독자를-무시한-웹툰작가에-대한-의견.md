                                [**The Future of the Korean Internet, Not the Punishment of Megal** I'd like to re-organize the Megalia issue on my blog. The operating principles announced on my blog on August 3, 2016... blog.naver.com](http://blog.naver.com/fstory97/220782303071)

Through this discussion, I've learned something new. It seems that simply writing 'I support Megal' in the title immediately invites disparagement and criticism. Is it truly anti-hate? Does Megal and Ilbe deserve to be targets of hate?

In my writing, I have never criticized anyone, whether they supported Megal or not. I have never criticized specific individuals or groups in my writing. Criticism from supporters and opponents is different. While I may have responded in a similar tone when feeling upset by sarcastic or unconditionally critical comments, the original text was humble. However, the comments ultimately throw stones at individual 'Megal supporters'.

I was so dumbfounded that I almost spoke more harshly, but fearing the discussion would escalate, I revised the main text to be more polite.

You cannot achieve justice by abandoning reason and self-control. No matter how well it's disguised, it's simply violence.

The current flow of events no longer prioritizes the issue or the truth. Megal has already become a corpse, and violent comments accumulating to criticize Megal are increasing more than the trashy posts left by the Megal camp. Since the internet is search-based, only pages are visible. It's virtually impossible to grasp the full context and authenticity without considerable effort. In other words, the public will ultimately be more exposed to the violent comments from the anti-Megal camp and will inevitably focus on that violence rather than understanding the angry anti-Megal camp. It's natural for progressive figures to increasingly declare their support for Megal.

I hope the anti-Megal camp stops justifying violence. Currently, the actions of the anti-Megal camp even look like mirroring. Megal's mirroring. Megal copied Ilbe, and the anti-Megal camp copied Megal... Acting that way and claiming to be different from Ilbe is useless.

If you wish to debate, please be courteous. If you cannot acknowledge differences, it ultimately leads to fascism.
