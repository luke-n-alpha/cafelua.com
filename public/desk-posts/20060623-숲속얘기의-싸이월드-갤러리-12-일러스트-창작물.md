When I go to the windy forest

Wearing shoes of longing

I wander, searching for you.

Those shoes are made of paper

So they rustle with every step.

The branches and stones of the forest

Pierce my feet

And I am very tired from the long journey

But somewhere in that forest

Where the sad nymph Echo lives

My love for you is buried.

The wind blows, and I put on my shoes again.

Why does it hurt so much?

It hurts because you're loving.

You prayed to love like me, didn't you?

Just as I loved you, you hurt because you're loving.

Love like mine is stained with blood, so it's natural for it to hurt.

It's loving even when rejected.

Your small wounds become bigger wounds for you

And I, too, hurt every day because of you. I pray with tears every day because of you.
