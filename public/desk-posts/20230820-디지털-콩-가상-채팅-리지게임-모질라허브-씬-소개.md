This scene was created for a side project called LizzieGames, serving as a whimsical hangout for fans of the Disney Channel Original Series, Lizzie McGuire. This scene was made in Blender, and the lighting was baked with a few reflection probes at strategic points. Billboarding was used for Cartoon Lizzie's eyes and the lava lamp's user tracking, UV scrolling for the spinning globe, lava lamp, espresso machine, and CD player display, and particle emitters for the soda bubbles, coffee splashes, and CD player music. Cartoon Lizzie's fingers and eyes have light keyframe animation. Currently, only a GIF loops on the TV screen, but YouTube videos might be playable later. The scene also features three custom low-poly character avatars: Lizzie, Gordo, and Miranda, with more characters coming soon. I'd love to get feedback on the space's performance, design, and quality! Please let me know your thoughts, as I might need to adjust tone mapping, increase light brightness, and lower music volume. Unfortunately, this scene does not run on iOS phones (it crashes unless you bypass that screen by clicking the 'Accept' button super fast while selecting an avatar, and the room itself works fine). I've tried several optimizations to fix this but haven't succeeded, so for now, it's just a known limitation. However, it works fine everywhere else (tested on desktop, laptop, tablet, modern Android phones, and Quest 2).
