**Title: "I'll Eat Well :D"**
**** 
**Production Year**: 2005
**** 
**Screenplay.Direction.Cinematography**.-Heo Hye-yoon.
**** 
**Art Director.Animator**.-Kim Jin-young.Kim Ji-yoon.
 
**Sound**.-Elder. :)
**** 
**Running Time**. 4 minutes
**** 
**Filming.** nikon. D-70s.
**** 
**Synopsis:**
The story of a poor lizard family.
With a bad harvest,
Mother lizard
has no food to feed her children.
 
The children grow rapidly day by day,
and as it becomes harder and harder to find food,
 
when food is scarce,
her heart aches and tears fall every time she sees her once good-natured children becoming increasingly violent and fighting over food.
 
The children grow more and more violent,
trying to eat even a little more.
 
The mother grows thinner and thinner, sad and in pain.
As the children continue to grow,
no matter how much food she divides,
it is woefully insufficient.
The more so, the children only scream and fight each other.
So, the mother
prepares the last supper.....
 
 
 
:)
 
 
 
**Production Intention.:**
 
Thoughts on parental devotion.
This is the story of our parents,
who spare nothing for their children.
 
For their rapidly growing children,
she finally begins to cook and offer her own flesh.

But for the children who grow too quickly,
even that is always insufficient.

If a lizard's tail is cut, it grows back.
Therefore,
to feed her children,
she begins to cut off her own tail.
 
However, before a new tail can grow,
the children grow too quickly.
The mother lizard
grows thinner and thinner...
Eventually...
 
Even if she eventually loses her life due to such repetition,
regretting not being able to give more, she prepares the last supper and then
the lonely, quiet death of a parent,
and their desperate love.
 
We also wanted to reflect on our own reality, where we grow up eating our parents' bones and flesh,
yet live without knowing their sacrifice and love.
:)
 
 
**Main Materials:** Jovi clay, stone powder clay, wire, wood, gesso, acrylic paint,
           illustration board, aluminum, lacquer, fabric, cotton...
