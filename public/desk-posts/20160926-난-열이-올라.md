A foolish fever rises. It seems the emotions I had bound have been unleashed again.
Again, I arbitrarily feel a longing.
Again, I arbitrarily feel lonely.
It's still hard,
and there are so many paths to take in this world,
yet why am I just treading water like this?
It must be nice to be so cool-headed.
It must be nice to have someone to hold your hand.
I.. I..
This heat comes from my heart and is still burning me.
Someone, please hold my hand.
2003.08.13

In the end, what I wished for back then was salvation..
Anyone would have been fine, but if it wasn't that specific someone, it was useless.
2016.09.26
