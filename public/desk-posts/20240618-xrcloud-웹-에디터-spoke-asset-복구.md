- On May 31, 2024, some assets on XRCLOUD were down due to the shutdown of Mozilla Foundation's [reticulum.io](http://reticulum.io/) server. These files were uploaded to BELIVVR's GitHub repository and restored on June 18, 2024. [https://github.com/belivvr/files/tree/main/xrcloud/spoke](https://github.com/belivvr/files/tree/main/xrcloud/spoke)
  - glb files of the default project
  - Hub sound pack
  - Architecture pack
  - Rock pack
- During the expansion of storage while deploying XRCLOUD's live service, a server issue occurred, resulting in an outage of approximately 7 hours, from around 03:00 AM to 10:00 AM KST on June 18.
- Please understand that the recovery from the outage is somewhat delayed due to BELIVVR's organizational restructuring.
