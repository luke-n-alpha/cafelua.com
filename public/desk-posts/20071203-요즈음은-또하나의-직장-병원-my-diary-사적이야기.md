집사람이 차사고가 나서 입원을 하고 있는 요즈음은 병원으로 계속해서 출퇴근을 하고 있다.
새벽 일찍 일어나게 된것이 달라졌고,
늘쌍 혼자자는 썰렁한 집이 달라졌다.

12월이 다되가는 날씨는 쌀쌀해졌는데, 같이 있던 사람이 없다는건 왠지 모르게 허전하기도 하다.

환자복을 입고 있는 집사람이 때로는 안쓰럽기도 하고
일에 바쁘다는 핑계로 잘 해주지도 못하고,
그렇다고 일을 특별나게 집중해서 잘하는것도 아니고...

이것도 저것도 아닌것 같아서....
요즘은은 좀... 내가 뭘 하고 있는건가 한다.

인터넷 하는 시간을 줄여야지.
쓸데 없는 말을 줄여야지.

보다 건설적인것과 자기발전에 투자해야지 하면서도
늘쌍 맘처럼 잘안되는게 생활인것 같다.

과연 2007년은 잘 한걸까 ?
1년간 기대했던것은 얻었던걸까 ?

12월 19일 대선같은거 신경쓰지말고, 내 일부터 좀 추스리자.

내가 요즘 우울했던건 어쩌면 대선보단
"올해 니가 한일이 뭐냐?" 에 대답을 찾을수 없는
그런 이유때문일지도..

<!-- en -->
These days, my wife is hospitalized due to a car accident, so I've been commuting to the hospital constantly.
What's changed is that I wake up early in the morning,
and the cold, empty house where I always sleep alone has changed.

As December approaches, the weather has turned chilly, and the absence of the person who was always with me feels strangely empty.

Sometimes I feel sorry for my wife in her hospital gown,
and I haven't been able to treat her well, using the excuse of being busy with work,
nor am I particularly focused and good at my job...

It feels like I'm neither one thing nor the other...
Lately, I've been wondering... what exactly am I doing?

I need to reduce the time I spend on the internet.
I need to reduce unnecessary talk.

Even though I tell myself I should invest in more constructive things and self-improvement,
life often doesn't go as planned.

Did I really do well in 2007?
Did I achieve what I hoped for over the year?

Instead of worrying about things like the December 19th presidential election, I should focus on getting my own affairs in order first.

Perhaps the reason I've been feeling down lately isn't so much the presidential election,
but rather because I can't find an answer to the question,
"What have you accomplished this year?"
