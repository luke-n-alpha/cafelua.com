{{IMG:1}} If Episode 1 supported open-type skins while satisfying the desires of users who like to decorate pretty things like Cyworld, this time, it seems to have evolved towards being faithful to the blog's inherent function of writing posts.
(The much-desired temporary save function!!)
It's funny that I'm actually impressed by a service within my own company... but since the organization is so large... it seems great.
From the DB attachment function that can enhance search... to layout and table functions...
It has brought together Cyworld's charm and convenience all at once. (Cyworld's editor was probably also a benchmark target.)
However, it's a bit disappointing that photo editing uses Active-X, but since Ajax cannot be an alternative to everything, I think we have no choice but to tolerate it for the sake of convenience.
