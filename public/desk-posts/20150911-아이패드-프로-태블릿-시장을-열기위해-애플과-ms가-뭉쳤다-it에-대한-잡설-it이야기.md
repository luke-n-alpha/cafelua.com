** 1) 생산성 도구로서의 아이패드의 가능성** 아이패드는 이미 교육과 산업현장에서 널리 사용되고 있다. 이러한 곳에서는 주로 뷰잉, 무언가를 찾아보거나 보여주는데 많이 쓰인다. 그리고, 이때 '이 상품 어떠세요?' 라던가, 무언가 설명들을 달아 놓을때 터치키보드나 터치로 그림을 그리는 것은 매우 불편하다. 따라서 정전식 펜이라도 사용되고는 한다. 이러한 요구사항들이 등장한 시점에서 이미 아이패드는 펜을 필요로 하고 있었다.   생산성 도구로 쓰기에는 여전히 앱들이 별볼일 없었다. 그런 와중에 전세계 워드프로세서표준인 마이크로소프트의 오피스가 안드로이드와 iOS에 등장했다. 이런 시점에 아이패드가 키보드를 파는것도 당연한 선택이다. 사용자의 요구가 있는데 안만들어줄 이유가 없지 않은가?
2) MS와 애플의 공동 고민, 왜 태블릿을 안쓸까에 대한 해법 MS는 윈도우8 을 출시했으나 여전히 사람들은 데스크탑모드만 고집했고, 시작버튼을 요구했다. 태블릿의 미래를 잡아야 하는 MS로서는 기존의 사용환경이 장애가 되고 있었다. 그리고 윈도우10을 내놓았다. 강제로 윈도우를 업그레이드했으며, 데스크탑모드와 태블릿 모드를 오가는 해법을 내놓았다. 그러나, 여전히 태블릿앱은 부족하다. 서드파티 시장이 열리고 활성화되려면 결국 사용자 볼륨, 곧 규모가 있어야 한다. 태블릿은 기대만큼 팔리지 않았고, 기존의 Mac과 윈도우PC 에 비하면 형편없는 수준이었다. 그런 와중에 애플과 MS가 손을 잡고 유사한 형태의 태블릿을 만들기 시작했다. 다시말하면 동일한 형태의 앱이 동작할 수 있는 환경이 되었음을 의미한다. 윈도우와 아이패드의 시장이 동일시장이 되었으며 앱제작사들에게는 태블릿앱을 만들어야 되는 이유가 늘었다. 실제로 안드로이드의 성장은 iOS와 안드로이드 모두에게 도움이 되었다. 동일한 현상을 태블릿 시장에도 재현을 할 심산인듯하다.
**3. MS는 서피스를 팔까?** 아이패드프로의 출현으로 인해 생산성도구로서의 고민을 오랫동안 해온 MS의 태블릿에서는 맞음을 증명해준 셈이다. 물론, 태블릿도 엔터테인먼트 도구로서는 이미 아이패드가 충분히 잘해오고 있었으며 MS는 이를 따라하려했다. 어찌되었던 이번 발표로 인해 서피스프로를 다시보는 사람들이 늘었다. 하지만 이것이 MS의 하드웨어 매출로 이어지지는 않을것이라고 본다. 최근 중국의 태블릿PC는 놀랍도록 좋아졌다. 이미 서피스나 아이패드 수준의 기기가 나오고 있으면서 가격은 반가격이다. 하드웨어 팔아서 장사할 시기는 아님을 의미한다. 어차피 MS의 본업은 하드웨어 판매가 아니니 나쁠건 없다.
**4. 드디어 태블릿 시대가 열릴 모양이다. 한국에 던지는 과제** 2년전에 태블릿 시대에 대한 기대에 부풀었었으나 생각보다 저조했다. 윈도우8도 망작이었고, MS와 애플의 대표가 바뀌면서 칼을 갈았다. 그리고 칼을 뽑아 서로를 겨눈것이 아니라 함께 시장을 개척하기 시작했다. 태블릿 시대가 좀 더 탄력을 받을 모양이다. 이런 와중에 한국의 Active X는 더 곤란해질것 같다. 키보드 보안은 키보드가 없는 태블릿PC에서는 동작하지 않는다. 아이패드와 MS엣지 브라우져는 NAPI를 지원하지 않는다. 웹기술은 최소한의 표준만을 지원하는 방향으로 이동하고, 앱이 앞으로 나오고 있다. 어디까지 웹으로 하고, 어떤 앱을 만들지 고민을 해야하지 않을까? 그리고 나는 MS의 윈도우10을 쓰고 있는데, 프리로드의 OS사의 서비스 장악력은 PC시대에 비해 압도적으로 올라가고 있다. 한국의 네이버나 카카오같은 토종 인터넷 모바일 회사들한테는 또다른 고민이 될 것 같다.

**1. iPad Pro's Pen and Steve Jobs**
Steve Jobs hated pens. This was a well-known fact.
He said that a pen was a terrible interface on a small mobile phone and introduced the iPhone, an innovative touch device.
And after Jobs' death, Tim Cook brought out the iPad with a pen and keyboard.
Is this a betrayal of Steve Jobs? People seem busy ridiculing the deceased Steve Jobs.
However, what Steve Jobs criticized was actually the resistive stylus of Windows Pocket PCs at the time. Indeed, the resistive stylus was a terrible interface. On such a small device, the stylus forced users to write tiny letters that made their body and mind shrink, and the recognition rate was terrible on a shaky bus. Devices at the time had very low resolution compared to today, so pen lines were very thick compared to today. And the screen was even smaller. In that respect, the iPhone's touch-based interface was revolutionary. And, even today's Windows-based tablets that come with a pen operate by touch before the pen is taken out and palm rejection activates, and an interface combining touch and pen is also being considered. I don't think there's much reason for Steve Jobs to be ridiculed just because a pen came out for the iPad Pro. And, he was someone who would always retract his thoughts depending on the situation. Of course, he also had a stubborn streak where he would never admit he was wrong. Therefore, I believe that even if Steve Jobs were alive, the iPad Pro might have come with a pen. It didn't just come out; it even brought angle detection, which is usually only found on Intuos Pro, so this is quite excellent.

**2. Tim Cook is not Steve Jobs. Why with MS?**
However, what Tim Cook did that went a step further than Steve Jobs was appearing with Microsoft. Due to Steve Jobs' bad relationship with Microsoft, he probably would never have done such showmanship out of pride. So why did he appear with Microsoft?

**1) iPad's Potential as a Productivity Tool**
The iPad is already widely used in education and industrial settings. In these places, it is mainly used for viewing, looking up or displaying something. And, at such times, using a touch keyboard or drawing with touch to say 'How about this product?' or to add explanations is very inconvenient. Therefore, even a capacitive pen is sometimes used. At the point when these requirements emerged, the iPad already needed a pen.
Apps were still unremarkable for use as a productivity tool. Amidst this, Microsoft Office, the global standard for word processors, appeared on Android and iOS. At this point, it's a natural choice for the iPad to sell a keyboard. If there's user demand, why wouldn't they make it?

**2) MS and Apple's Shared Concern, Solution to Why People Don't Use Tablets**
MS released Windows 8, but people still insisted on desktop mode and demanded the start button. For MS, which had to capture the future of tablets, the existing user environment was becoming an obstacle. And then they released Windows 10. They forced Windows upgrades and offered a solution that switched between desktop mode and tablet mode. However, tablet apps are still lacking. For the third-party market to open and become active, there must ultimately be user volume, or scale. Tablets didn't sell as much as expected, and were dismal compared to existing Macs and Windows PCs. Amidst this, Apple and MS joined hands and began creating similar forms of tablets. In other words, it means that an environment where the same type of apps can operate has been created. The Windows and iPad markets have become the same market, and app developers have more reasons to create tablet apps. In fact, the growth of Android helped both iOS and Android. It seems they intend to reproduce the same phenomenon in the tablet market.

**3. Will MS Sell Surface?**
The emergence of the iPad Pro has proven that MS's tablets, which have long pondered productivity tools, were on the right track. Of course, as an entertainment tool, the iPad was already doing well enough, and MS tried to follow suit. Anyway, this announcement has led more people to reconsider the Surface Pro. But I don't think this will lead to increased hardware sales for MS. Recently, Chinese tablet PCs have become surprisingly good. Devices at the level of Surface or iPad are already coming out at half the price. This means it's not the time to make money by selling hardware. Since MS's main business isn't hardware sales anyway, there's nothing bad about it.

**4. It seems the Tablet Era is Finally Dawning. Challenges for Korea**
Two years ago, there was great anticipation for the tablet era, but it was less successful than expected. Windows 8 was also a flop, and with the change of leadership at MS and Apple, they sharpened their knives. And instead of drawing their knives against each other, they began to jointly pioneer the market. It seems the tablet era will gain more momentum. Amidst this, Korea's Active X will become even more problematic. Keyboard security does not work on tablet PCs without keyboards. iPad and MS Edge browsers do not support NAPI. Web technology is moving towards supporting only minimal standards, and apps are coming to the forefront. Shouldn't we consider how much to do with the web and what apps to create? And I'm using MS's Windows 10, and the OS providers' service dominance through pre-loading is overwhelmingly increasing compared to the PC era. This will likely be another concern for Korea's native internet mobile companies like Naver and Kakao.
