전혀 아닙니다.

This work was originally a 4-panel manga that was adapted into an anime.
So this is what happens when a 4-panel manga is made into an anime. ^^
You might think it's a romance or something similar because high school girls appear...
But it's not at all.
An anime where you can't even find a single male character...
Centered around Chiyo, a 10-year-old, perfect-scoring, genius, rich high school girl,
listen to the unique actions and interesting thoughts of characters whose personalities are distinct... no, too eccentric.
It's a fun and bright anime.

What is Azumanga Daioh?
Author's name - Azuma Kiyohiko
Manga - pronounced 'manga'
Magazine name - Dengeki Daioh serialized manga...
Triple combination
Anyway, Azuma Kiyohiko, without much thought with his editor,
named the manga, and then regretted it immensely after the manga became popular...
He revealed this in an interview after the serialization ended.
