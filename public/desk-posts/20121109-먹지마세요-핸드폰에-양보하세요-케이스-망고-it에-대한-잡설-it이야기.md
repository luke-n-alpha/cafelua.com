I proceeded with the Android machine, Galaxy S3. It's a name that makes you want to eat it. Should the meaning of the name be **'Don't eat it. Give it to your phone.'**?

Actually, before I was asked to introduce this product, I saw a couple using it at a local cafe. They had their smartphone placed horizontally, and the design caught my eye so much that I was curious what product it was, and it turned out to be this one when I received it.
**1. Cool Colors and Design**
The core of this product seems to be its eye-catching design and colors.
{{IMG:2}}
**[ Various Colors ]**
Although the design patent battle between Galaxy and iPhone is fierce, the design of smartphones with rectangular touch displays, no matter how much differentiation is attempted, tends to look similar at a distance. Therefore, I think there's no item more suitable than a case for expressing individuality. Since I don't have a Galaxy S3 phone, I took the case to the company to conduct a review with a company test phone, and everyone said it was very pretty. So I gifted them to colleagues who own S3 phones, and they loved them.

**2. Perfect as a Desk Stand**
When placed horizontally, it resembles an old CRT TV. Perhaps like a small TV on your desk? If you place it next to your keyboard or monitor while working, you can immediately check messages or schedules as a phone stand. These days, there are many beautiful lock screen apps, so depending on how you use it, you can utilize it as a mini secretary or a mini photo frame.
{{IMG:7}}
{{IMG:8}}

**3. Grip**
The disadvantage of today's smartphones with larger screens is that they are easy to drop. The inner groove of this case lightens the case's weight while improving grip.
{{IMG:9}}
It's also a good item for women with small hands. Of course, there's the problem that the height increases the moment you put on the case, but the usability alone was excellent. My female colleague next to me said she liked it because it allowed for stable gameplay when playing Anipang. Is Anipang on the decline these days?
{{IMG:10}}
**[ Good for playing Anipang ]**

**4. Phone Protection**
Dropping a smartphone is a disaster in itself, but if it accidentally falls... Anyway, this case is made of soft rubber material. Therefore, it seems to have some protective ability against impact. Of course, if it unfortunately falls screen-first, the damage might be worse due to the added weight of the case, but since it's not my phone, I couldn't conduct a drop test. Brave souls, please try it and let me know the results.

**5. Card Storage**
I didn't realize it when reviewing. It says you can store two cards inside. However, you have to remove the phone to take out the cards, so I wonder if it could be used for storing a bus card.
{{IMG:11}}
**[ Cards in there? ]**

The noticeable drawbacks are the increased height and slightly increased weight. These are disadvantages when in a handbag or pocket, but for the desire to stand out and in terms of usability, it was very good. It's an item recommended for users who spend a lot of time indoors.
