나는 인형이 좋다.

생긴거 답지 않게 인형을 좋아한다고 말할수 있을지 모르겠지만,

내가 인형을 좋아하는 이유는 마치 회화를 좋아하는것과 마찬가지다.

I like dolls.

It might not suit my appearance, but I like dolls.


The reason I like dolls is similar to why I like paintings.
I like dolls that capture fleeting expressions and resemble human emotions.

Some characters represent a product...
{{IMG:1}}

It might be a marketing ploy, but just looking at the generous-looking grandpa character makes me feel full.
(Of course, they don't give them away for free.)

Sometimes, I even find dolls that resemble myself...
{{IMG:2}}

{{IMG:3}}


There are also character dolls from works I was passionate about.

{{IMG:4}}

When I see these dolls, the touching feelings I had while watching the work come back to me, stimulating a desire to buy them.

Anyway...
Thanks to this, my desk, which doesn't quite suit a cognitive developer,

is filled with many figures and dolls, and when I look at them while working, I feel good as if they're talking to me.

Because when I'm racking my brain over complex tasks, looking at their expressions often makes me smile, isn't that good enough?
