**1. 몽환성의 이유, 킨드레라는 존재
**스카이크롤러의 메인 이야기는 킨드레라는 존재입니다. 어른이 되지 못하는 존재, 영원히 살지만 전투에 투입되어 죽기에 어른이 될 필요가 없는 존재. 이것이 바로 킨드레입니다. **그리고 네타가 될수 밖에 없지만 **킨드레는 사실 파일럿의 실력향상을 위해 계속해서 복제되어 전장에 투입되는 복제인간입니다.
그 때문인지 유이치의 시선으로 그려지는 이 세계관은 뭔가 몽환적인 느낌이 납니다. 공각기동대 이노센스([http://fstory97.blog.me/70067891709](http://fstory97.blog.me/70067891709))에서 보여주었던 특유의 현실감이 없는 몽환감, 이는 육체에 갇혀있는 존재를 더욱 집중 할 수 있도록 해주며 끊임없이 내면의 존재와 마주하게됩니다.
또한 이 세계관은 잘보면 시대를 알 수가 없습니다. 2차대전시에 사용되던 프로펠러의 전투기가 등장하지만 lcd tv와 모니터가 걸려있고, 당췌 종잡기 어려운 시대입니다. 그도 그럴것이 킨드레에게는 과거, 현재, 미래가 모두 뒤엉켜있고 계속 반복될 뿐이기 때문입니다.

**2. 가짜 전쟁
두 회사의 실험. 어른이라는 존재들은 이 사실을 모두 알고 있습니다.

**3. 티쳐와 킨드레
**유일한 어른 파일럿, 이길수 없는 존재. 티쳐. 아이와 선생. 가르치는자와 가르침을 받는자. 여기서 다시한번 이 실험의 목적이 드러납니다. 그렇기에 티쳐는 적 회사로 이직이 가능했던거죠. 유이치가 어떻게 적이 될수 있었던거죠? 라고 스이토에게 했던 질문이 그것입니다.

**4. 어른을 이기고 싶은 스이토 - 성장하고 싶은 스이토**
킨드레의 에이스로 오래도록 살아남은 스이토의 심경이 아마 가장 복잡하지 않았나 싶습니다. 복제인간이면서 아이를 낳았습니다. 그리고 스이토는 이 사실을 모두 알고 있습니다.
어쩌면 이때문이라도 스이토는 살아남아야할 의무가 있습니다. 그리고 그때문에 견학오는 무리들을 총으로 쏴죽이고 싶어하는 지도모르죠. 티쳐와 전투에서 죽은 킨드레의 전투기앞에서 울고 있는 이들을 향해 이들은 불쌍한게 아니라 명예로운거라고 외치던것. 이 반복되는 죽음을 알고 있는 스이토로서는 이 의미없는 죽음에 관해서 이러한 의미라도 찾으려고 했던것이 아닐까요?
그리고 궁극적으로 티쳐에대한 집착, 어른을 이기지 못하고서는 언제까지고 아이일 수 밖에 없고 이 실험이 계속되리라는 것을 알고 있습니다.

스이토는 이 의미없는 삶을 죽음으로 끝맺고 싶어했습니다. 그리고 함께했던 유이치의 전임자, 곧 유이치의 전생 역시 마찬가지였고 결국은 스이토에게 죽여달라고 했었죠.
그러나 돌아온 유이치는 스이토에게 살아라라고 말합니다. 사실 반복되는 삶이라고 했으면 스이토를 죽였여야 옳습니다. 그러나 유이치는 그러지 않았습니다. 무언가 한걸음 나갔다는 의미이죠. 그리고 유이치는 티쳐에게 죽임을 당합니다. 이렇게 비극으로 끝나는것 같습니다.

**1. The Reason for the Dreamlike Quality: The Existence of the Kildren
**The main story of The Sky Crawlers revolves around the Kildren. Beings who cannot grow up, who live forever but are deployed in combat and die, thus having no need to become adults. This is what the Kildren are. **And though it's a spoiler,** the Kildren are actually clones, continuously replicated and sent to the battlefield to improve the pilots' skills.
Perhaps because of this, the world depicted through Yuichi's eyes has a dreamlike quality. The unique, unreal dreaminess seen in Ghost in the Shell 2: Innocence ([http://fstory97.blog.me/70067891709](http://fstory97.blog.me/70067891709)) allows for greater focus on beings confined to their bodies, constantly confronting their inner selves.
Furthermore, if you look closely, the era of this world is indiscernible. Propeller fighter planes from World War II appear, but LCD TVs and monitors are present, making it an era that's hard to pin down. This is because for the Kildren, past, present, and future are all intertwined and simply repeat.

**2. The Fake War
**Watching the war in this anime, civilians have no sense of its reality. The base where the Kildren are stationed is operated by only a handful of pilots, with no army to protect it, not even a president. If it were a real war, it seems it could be destroyed at any time if one simply willed it. Another strange point is the village at the joint operation base; it's a large village, but apart from a bowling alley, it's pitch black with only streetlights on. It's like something out of a dream. The battle between the two companies is broadcast live on TV in pubs, and no one in the pubs takes anyone's death seriously. Guardians even come to the company for a tour. In fact, all these awkward elements are explained in the anime's conclusion: it's a fake war. The bombed cities or cities with lights out were probably dummies. The dry, detailed war situation reports on TV were merely explaining this fake war.
An experiment by two companies. The adults, as beings, are all aware of this fact.

**3. The Teacher and the Kildren
**The only adult pilot, an unbeatable being. The Teacher. Child and teacher. The one who teaches and the one who is taught. Here, the purpose of this experiment is revealed once again. That's why the Teacher could transfer to the enemy company. Yuichi's question to Suito, "How could he become an enemy?", was precisely about this.

**4. Suito Who Wants to Defeat the Adults - Suito Who Wants to Grow Up**
Suito, who survived as the Kildren's ace for a long time, probably had the most complex feelings. She is a clone, yet she gave birth to a child. And Suito knows all of this.
Perhaps because of this, Suito has an obligation to survive. And perhaps that's why she wants to shoot the groups who come for tours. When she shouted to those crying in front of the Kildren's fighter plane, killed in battle with the Teacher, that they weren't pitiful but honorable. For Suito, who knows about this repetitive death, wasn't she trying to find some meaning in these meaningless deaths?
And ultimately, her obsession with the Teacher; she knows that unless she defeats the adults, she will remain a child forever, and this experiment will continue.

**5. Yuichi's Return - (Epilogue After Credits) Yuichi's Growth**
Suito wanted to end this meaningless life with death. And Yuichi's predecessor, who was with her, essentially Yuichi's past life, felt the same way and eventually asked Suito to kill him.
However, the returned Yuichi tells Suito to live. In fact, if it were a repetitive life, killing Suito would have been the correct action. But Yuichi did not do that. It means he took a step forward. And Yuichi is killed by the Teacher. It seems to end tragically like this.

 **However, after the credits, the final scene shows Yuichi's reincarnation returning to the base.** Suito says she's been waiting, just as in the first half of the anime. Perhaps she was expecting this life to be different again. They are slowly growing into adults in their own ways. **Perhaps Mamoru's way of confirming existence is through growth, or rather, change.** And so, this anime concludes with another beginning.
