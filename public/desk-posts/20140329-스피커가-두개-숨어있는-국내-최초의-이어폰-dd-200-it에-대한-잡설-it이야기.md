Nevertheless, as I've tried good earphones while receiving review requests, I feel like my ears are gradually opening up. The earphones I'm introducing today are the DD200 from T-PEOS, a domestic company that produces everything from high-end models to cost-effective entry-level ones.

First, when I received these earphones, I honestly wondered why the design was so clunky.{{IMG:2}}{{IMG:3}} Unlike typical sleek earphones, the secret to the sound quality is actually hidden in that bulky part.{{IMG:4}} It's called a dual dynamic unit. Most earphones consist of a single unit, but these earphones have separate units responsible for high and low frequencies, reportedly delivering rich sound quality. Simply put, it means there are separate speakers for treble and bass. The cable is not the flat type that supposedly prevents tangles these days. I was a bit surprised by that, but it's actually a micro-groove design that minimizes tangles and touch noise.{{IMG:5}} I actually compared them to the bundled earphones that came with my Galaxy S3 LTE, and I could feel a clearer and deeper tone. Of course, bundled earphones are much cheaper, but these days, bundles often surpass many entry-level models, so they're not to be dismissed. The components are simple. It includes caps of various sizes, a clip, and a pouch to store the earphones. Honestly, apart from the earphones themselves, I tend to just put things away, so I don't use the extra caps much. {{IMG:6}} For those who might be curious about the detailed specs and information{{IMG:7}} And most importantly, with this performance, it's an entry-level model in the early 30,000 won range!
