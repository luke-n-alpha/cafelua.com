by Forest Story
Regarding Lezhin Comics, which I posted about yesterday, I'm sharing some follow-up news that feels like an antidote.
The content below was posted on Professor Park In-ha's timeline from Chungkang College of Cultural Industries.

​First, the overall content is almost identical to what I argued yesterday. What I newly learned through Professor Park In-ha is that the Korea Communications Standards Commission and the Korea Cartoonists Association are re-examining the review process through a business agreement. Although there was an absurd official response from the person who appears to be in charge of the blocking (I won't introduce it because it might make you sick again), I hope that person's authority can soon be taken away. It's very welcome news that they were already doing what I argued for yesterday. Furthermore, due to this frustrating incident, discussions on self-regulation measures for digital comics and alternative legislation for the Youth Protection Act have become possible, making Lezhin Comics a very positive event.

You must have been very angry after seeing yesterday's content... but the fact that things are slowly improving as much as we express our anger is encouraging, and we should support these actions with applause and strength.

If I have time today, I plan to write about the second "sickening" incident, and I hope a similar "antidote" emerges for that as well.
