I was going crazy, thinking "I want to draw~! I want to draw~!",
then a task like a sudden downpour ended, and I had a brief moment of free time.
In the end, I mostly sketched at work instead of doing actual work, and colored at home.

Lately, a somewhat gloomy and stifling feeling had been gripping me...
It seems drawing is indeed the best remedy for me...

Of course, if it snows, there will be more than one or two inconveniences, starting with commuting to work..
But what does it matter?
The snow makes me happy like a child~
Am I a canid?
