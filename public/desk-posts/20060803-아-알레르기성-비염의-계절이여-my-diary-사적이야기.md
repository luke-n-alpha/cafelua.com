에구..

A cold that even dogs don't catch in summer
always visits me like a regular customer.

As the temperatures in the office, transportation, and outside air differ,
my allergic rhinitis gets even more extremely severe..

Then.. I get tired..
Sigh..

Wonhyo-hyung, who is my senior, went to Canada for a year, breathed clean air, and apparently got rid of it.
Does that mean if you don't have money, you just have to live with it? OTL.
