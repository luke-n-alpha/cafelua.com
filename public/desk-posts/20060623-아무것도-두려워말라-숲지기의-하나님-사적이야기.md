가끔 너무 두려워.

뭐가 달라진거지 ?

무엇이 이렇게 초조한거야.

아무것도 두려워 하지마.

절망적인 상황보다 더 끔찍한건

절망에 빠지는거야.

결과는 그분께 맞기고

신들린 열정으로 노력하는게

네 방식이라고 했었잖아.

절망에 빠져 노력하지 않으면

더 나빠질거야.

믿어봐. 다 잘될거야.

네 가슴의 불씨는 아직 살아있어.

2003. 11. 11

<!-- en -->
Sometimes I'm so scared.

What has changed?

What makes me so anxious?

Don't be afraid of anything.

What's worse than a desperate situation

is falling into despair.

Leave the outcome to Him,

and striving with divine passion

you said that was your way.

If you fall into despair and don't try,

things will get worse.

Believe it. Everything will be alright.

The spark in your heart is still alive.

2003. 11. 11
