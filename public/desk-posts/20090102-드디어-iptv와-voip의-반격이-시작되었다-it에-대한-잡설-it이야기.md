While I agreed that the Lee Myung-bak government had an anti-IT stance based on its past statements, one thing it did well was the real-time broadcasting for IPTV, the number portability system for internet phones, and the recently decided voice integration for Wibro, which means wireless VOIP service.

This leads to a simple conclusion: existing cable operators will disappear, and wired CPs like OnMedia will rise. IPTV had numerous advantages, but real-time broadcasting, especially terrestrial broadcasting, was the only area it hadn't penetrated, and its inclusion is noteworthy. The reason existing cable operators can't compete is that they simply cannot compare to the scale and marketing capabilities of the current IPTV service providers. Furthermore, the disadvantage of having to change broadcasting providers by region when moving house will largely disappear.

I've been using LG's IPTV for 8,800 won on a 3-year contract, and the most convenient features were being able to watch whatever I wanted whenever I wanted, easily playing videos from shared computers, and using the optical terminal if I had a home theater. As of January 1st, it started real-time terrestrial broadcasting, just like cable TV. I called today to switch, and it's 11,000 won for a 3-year contract. The price is very reasonable. I saw an article saying it plans to support about 40 channels.

However, they asked me to wait a week due to an overload of live service subscribers... (It definitely seems like a much-anticipated service.)

Everyone knows 070 calls are cheap. With a wireless plan, it costs 8 won for a 10-second call to a mobile phone, and calls between 070 numbers are free. It's just a shame I can't use 070 at work. Since the number portability system was implemented, subscribers have reportedly increased explosively.

Roughly, combining these three services comes out to the early 30,000 won range. It seems about 10,000 won cheaper than doing them separately. The conclusion is... all devices will be integrated based on IP. Now only mobile phones are left. With voice integration for Wibro decided, isn't it just a matter of time for this market too? 30GB currently costs 20,000 won. If converted to voice, you couldn't use it all even if you talked on the phone all month. Even with video calls, depending on the codec, it might be possible to make DivX-quality calls all month.

Just as electricity powers all devices, the day is not far when all devices requiring data communication will be unified based on IP.
