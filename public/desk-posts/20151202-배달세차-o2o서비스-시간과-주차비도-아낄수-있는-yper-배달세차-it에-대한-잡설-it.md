I've introduced Yper service on my homepage a few times, and now I've had the chance to experience it myself, so I'd like to share it with you. Yper is a leading O2O company in Korea, introduced overseas, and has swept various awards, making other startups envious. Simply put, O2O refers to services where you use online platforms like smartphones to receive services that were traditionally offline, or part of daily life, at your desired location. Yper falls into this category because they come to your location to pick up your car.{{IMG:1}} A characteristic of O2O services is that while you apply for and access them online, the actual service takes place offline. This often leads to limitations in service areas. Yper's only drawback was that its service was limited to the Gangnam area, where its partner companies are concentrated. It's great news that it's recently expanded to Pangyo. After praising Yper's service, I was given the opportunity to try it myself, so I'm writing this review of my experience.
