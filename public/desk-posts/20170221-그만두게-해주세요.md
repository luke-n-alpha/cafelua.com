I should hate you,
It's enough to hate you,
Why do I still like you?
I should hate you.
If I can't erase you,
I should at least hate you,
But why...
Why do I, who am struggling, worry about others
And miss the one who left?
That's why it hurts so much.
That's why I'm dying like this.
Calling and calling,
Are you trying to turn into a stone?
Or become light ash
And fly to their side?
Who would remember you for doing that?
Who would shed a single tear for you for doing that?
You'll just become a laughingstock for passersby.
You'll just become entertainment for the crowd.
The only ones who understand you are the sad clowns who put on makeup every day,
And the fairy tale protagonists who are already dead.
Stop it.
Stop it, I tell you.
This bleeding foolishness.
Please make it stop...
2003. 11. 26

Entering it seemed to be by will,
But leaving it was not by choice – that was love.
At least, according to my experience...
