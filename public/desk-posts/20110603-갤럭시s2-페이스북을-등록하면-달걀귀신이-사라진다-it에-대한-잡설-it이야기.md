When the ringtone rings on my smartphone, an faceless egg ghost always appears. In old feature phones, you could set a ring screen, but on smartphones, the ring screen defaults to the contact's face, which is why this screen appears. To fix this, you need to register photos for your contacts, which is quite a hassle. This time, after switching to a Galaxy S2 and connecting accounts, I discovered a convenient tip that I'd like to share.
**1. What are the benefits of connecting Facebook to your smartphone?**
First, let me tell you what results you can get after performing this tedious task. At first, I thought 'Social Hub' meant Samsung Electronics had created an app to manage multiple SNS platforms in one place, but that wasn't the core idea. You can truly experience the real taste of the Galaxy S2's Social Hub feature when connected to Facebook. While connecting with a Facebook account certainly allows you to utilize Facebook's SNS well, what I want to talk about is the **cloudification of PIMS**. Sounds too difficult when I put it like that, right? Simply put, the contact information of acquaintances registered on Facebook is managed by the acquaintances themselves. In fact, this is one of the significant paradigm shifts that many companies are trying to achieve. Facebook friends don't need to send individual messages when they change their phones. And you don't have to register smartphone photos one by one. You can even change your profile picture from time to time. Even if you change your smartphone, Facebook friends are completely freed from the act of transferring address books, and their address books are updated in real-time. Furthermore, Facebook allows you to know friends' birthdays and share anniversaries by group, and this feature is reflected in your smartphone's calendar. Now, I will explain in detail.

If you look at your contacts, you'll see contacts with Facebook marks and photos, and the egg ghosts have disappeared. What's even better is that it merges with existing contacts based on the contact information. Contacts that didn't have photos in your existing contacts but had the same phone number as on Facebook are merged and now display photos. Now let's go to the calendar. The birthdays of your Facebook friends have been registered.
{{IMG:8}}
[ I'm using jtore for my calendar app. ]
Now that it's the smartphone era, you can send them simple congratulatory messages in addition to SMS. I'm afraid they might ask for gifts.
**3) Organizing Unmerged Contacts**
However, there are definitely Facebook friends whose photos don't appear.
{{IMG:9}}[ My wife, she's on Facebook? Add linked contact ]
This is because the address book name and the Facebook name are not identical. This usually happens with contacts registered by nicknames or with English Facebook names. To fix this, go to contact editing, select the Facebook contact, and link the contacts. Then, a photo will appear for that contact as well.
{{IMG:10}}[ Add link to contact ]
{{IMG:11}}[ My wife appears in recommended contacts, so add her. If she's not here, please find her by viewing all contacts. ]

{{IMG:12}}[ Back in contacts, my wife's contact is now linked to Facebook and her photo is registered. ]
**3. Facebook Service That Makes Your Smartphone Smart**
A service like this, where once the user sets it up, it automatically manages contacts and anniversaries without further intervention, truly makes a smartphone smart. However, I wonder if such powerful built-in features on the phone make it difficult for other competitors to play in this space. If the same feature had been provided by Cyworld, it would have been quite powerful. In fact, when a friend's phone number changes and I need to find it, I visit Cyworld. Among domestic services, there's Me2day for social accounts. {{IMG:13}}
[ Now Me2day is registered too! ]
However, even after adding it, there were no clear changes in the address book or calendar, and even if there were, it seems it would be a lot of trouble to merge them because most Me2day nicknames differ from real names in the address book. But as someone who uses Me2day well, I'm hopeful that it might work here too.
