| {{IMG:2}} http://photom.naver.com/ A few days ago, I went to the East Coast with my aunt and uncle, my wife, and my parents. Although we went to the East Coast for my mother's hometown relative's 70th birthday party. We traveled around a lot for 2 days and 1 night. What I remember is my dear wife finding a wad of cash!!! on the beach at night ~ {{IMG:3}} Her eyesight is good too. To see money rolling around in the middle of the night, a whopping 60,000 won. We all amicably split 10,000 won each. (Though my wife confiscated my share later {{IMG:4}}) The sea, which I saw after a long time, was truly refreshing. The waves were huge... It felt like if I went in these days, I'd be swept away.. As expected, I prefer the sea when there are fewer people, rather than during peak vacation season. |
