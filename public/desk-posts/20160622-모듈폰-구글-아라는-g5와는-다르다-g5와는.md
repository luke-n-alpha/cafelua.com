1. 새로운 스마트폰의 대세로 모듈폰?

국내 모듈폰에 대한 기사들을 보면, 새로운 스마트폰의 대세로 모듈폰을 대하는 느낌이 든다. 단순히 모듈폰을 취향이 선택가능한 확장폰 정도로 보고 있는 느낌이다. 그러나 나는 구글 아라의 프로젝트를 보고 적지 않이 충격을 받았다. 이는 그동안의 구글이 소프트웨어 회사라는 굴레를 벗어던지는 첫 신호탄이 될지도 모른다. 구글이 래퍼런스로 만든 안드로이드 폰의 아키텍처는 뭐가되었던간에 리눅스 데스크탑 머신을 경량화 하여 휴대폰 기능을 좀 얹은 것이다. 컴퓨터구조상 크게 바뀐것은 없다. PC의 파워서플라이가 배터리로 바뀌고, 모니터가 IPS나 AMOLED로 바뀌고, 키보드와 마우스는 정전식 터치로 바뀌었다. 솔직히 말해 컴퓨터와 하등 다를바가 없는 기계다. 그에 반해 애플이 하드웨어로서의 강자라는 이미지는 스마트폰에서 비롯된 것이 아니다. 초기 Apple 부터 이어진 애플의 하드웨어의 실력들은 컴퓨터공학의 교과서에 나오는 기술들이 존재한다. ​

1. Modular Phones as the New Trend in Smartphones?

Looking at articles about modular phones in Korea, it feels like modular phones are being treated as the new trend in smartphones. It feels like modular phones are simply seen as expandable phones where users can choose their preferences. However, I was quite shocked when I saw Google Ara's project. This might be the first signal of Google shedding its long-standing image as a software company. Whatever the architecture of the Android phone Google created as a reference, it was essentially a lightweight Linux desktop machine with some phone functionalities added. In terms of computer architecture, not much has fundamentally changed. The PC's power supply was replaced by a battery, the monitor by IPS or AMOLED, and the keyboard and mouse by capacitive touch. Honestly, it's a machine no different from a computer. In contrast, Apple's image as a hardware powerhouse didn't originate with smartphones. Apple's hardware prowess, which dates back to early Apple, involves technologies found in computer science textbooks.

4. Google's Ara is a Distributed Hardware Module Form Factor.

I'm certain that Ara is not a phone meant for immediate sale. Let's stop comparing it to the G5. They are completely different devices. While it might look like garbage commercially right now, technologically, it's a phone beyond comparison. It's closer to a device created by a research organization aiming to capture future computing technology, rather than a phone made by marketing and product planning departments like LG's. The difference between the G5 and Ara is as vast technologically as the gap between a smartphone and a feature phone. Comparing the two is absurd in itself. And the technology used to create Ara is quite useful. I believe it could become a pretty good platform for IoT in the future.
