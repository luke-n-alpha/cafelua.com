바쁜데두 .. 정리가 안되는듯한 느낌
무언가 할일이 잔뜩 있는데두..
시간에 쫓기는듯한 기분..

소설도 가끔은 써보고 싶고
여행도 다녀보고 싶고
사진도 찍어보고 싶은데..

청첩장 리스트도 정리해야 되는데..
왠지모르게 일에 치여서 엷어져버린 인간관계에 대한 회의와
복잡한 머릿속..

Even though I'm busy... it feels like things aren't getting organized.
Even though there's a ton of stuff to do...
Feeling like I'm being chased by time...

I want to try writing a novel sometimes,
I want to try traveling,
and I want to try taking photos...

I also need to organize the wedding invitation list...
and somehow, a sense of doubt about human relationships that have faded due to being overwhelmed by work,
and a cluttered mind...
