#### [The Flipper](http://book.naver.com/bookdb/book_detail.php?bid=1490661)

Written by Hideo Okuda | Translated by Lee Young-mi
Eunhaengnamu
View Excerpt
**Rating**

Emotional wounds that anyone can fall into, but may not even realize they have.
The way to escape from those wounds is not a complex medical opinion.

It is becoming natural.
Perhaps that's why Irabu is an excellent healer.

The cheerful, short-and-sweet patients are all reflections of ourselves that we can become,
and Irabu is our suppressed, natural self.
