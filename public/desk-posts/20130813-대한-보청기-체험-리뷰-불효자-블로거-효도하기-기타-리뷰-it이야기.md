보청기는 소리를 증폭해주는 증폭기입니다. 그리고 나이가 먹을 수록 청력이 떨어지게 되는데, 이는 소리를 감지하는 청세포들의 능력이 떨어지거나 죽기 때문이라고 합니다. 테스트는 주파수대별로 다른 정도의 크기의 소리를 들을 수 있는지를 테스트 합니다.
높은 소리를 듣는 세포와 낮은 소리를 듣는 세포가 존재합니다. 그리고, 개인에 따라 이 세포들의 능력치가 다른데, 나이가 들 수록 이 세포들의 능력 혹은 개체수가 감소하게 되고 이로 인해 들리지 않게 됩니다. 그리고 보청기는 단순히 소리를 증폭 시키는 것이 아니라, 주파수별로의 개인의 청력에 맞춰 낮은 소리를 얼마나 증폭할지, 높은 소리를 얼마나 증폭할지를 셋팅해두는 겁니다. 그리고, 소리가 들어오면, 미리 셋팅된 값으로 증폭을 하여 청력을 되찾아주는 원리입니다.
기본적으로 전공이 HCI에 관련된게 많아서 사람의 감각에 대해 잘 알고 있다고 생각했는데 소리에 관해 그런 원리를 들으니 새록새록 하더군요.

Being such a 'chic' son, I had lived my life without really doing much for my parents, even past the age of thirty (you can tell by looking at me that having a son is useless). **3. Hearing Test and the Principle of Hearing Aids**
A hearing aid is an amplifier that amplifies sound. As one ages, hearing deteriorates, which is said to be due to the decline or death of the auditory cells that detect sound. The test checks how well one can hear sounds of different magnitudes across various frequency bands.
There are cells that hear high-pitched sounds and cells that hear low-pitched sounds. And, depending on the individual, the capabilities of these cells differ. As one ages, the ability or number of these cells decreases, leading to hearing loss. A hearing aid doesn't simply amplify sound; it's set to amplify low-pitched sounds and high-pitched sounds to different degrees, tailored to an individual's hearing across various frequencies. Then, when sound enters, it's amplified according to the pre-set values, restoring hearing. ﻿
I generally thought I understood human senses well, as my major involved a lot of HCI, but hearing about these principles regarding sound was a fresh revelation.

**4. The Results for a 'Bad' Father**
The problem was that my father's results showed he could hear low frequencies (like men's voices or low sounds) almost as well as an average person, but he couldn't hear high frequencies (like women's voices or high sounds) at all. For reference, my father cannot hear crickets no matter how loudly they chirp. This is worse than not being able to hear anything at all, because if only high-frequency sounds are amplified, low-frequency sounds can also be affected and feel like ringing.

**5. Types and Sizes of Hearing Aids**
The biggest distinction among hearing aid types is between those that hang behind the ear and those that are inserted into the ear, which vary greatly in size. The reason is that while smaller is always better, if they are too small, they become sealed, which can cause more resonance. For severe hearing loss, just as severe myopia requires thicker glasses, the hearing aid inevitably becomes larger. Therefore, while in-the-ear types are aesthetically pleasing, behind-the-ear types are said to offer the best user experience.
{{IMG:8}}

Nevertheless, most people prefer in-the-ear types, and my father also preferred them, so we opted for an in-the-ear model. To reduce the resonance disadvantage of sealed types, a small hole is drilled, which also makes it slightly larger than a fully sealed type, I was told. ﻿
