ADF 기계를 이용하여 OCR을 수행 시, 하나의 책에서는 비슷한 폰트를 사용하고, 비슷한 OCR기계나 환경 값을 가지기 때문에 비슷한 모양의 글자가 계속해서 틀리게 되는 오류의 유형이 비슷하다. 또한 전체 문서에서 틀리는 글자의 비율은 그리 많지 않기 때문에 틀린 글자를 판별하기 또한 쉽지 않다.

- **Title:** Improving Typo Correction Method for Character-Based OCR Documents

- **Background:** In the case of IRIS and Armi, after performing OCR, to correct typos, one must manually correct each incorrect character within the recognized OCR text. When performing OCR using an ADF machine, a single book typically uses similar fonts and similar OCR machines or environmental settings, leading to similar types of errors where characters of similar shapes are consistently incorrect. Furthermore, the proportion of incorrect characters in the entire document is not very high, making it difficult to identify them.

- **Improvements**

- **OCR engine recommends characters it's unsure about.**
The OCR engine sorts candidates based on their distance from the model. A lower distance between the top candidate and the model means the model and the input character are likely the same. However, if the ratio of the distance to the second candidate is low, it means the engine is confused about the input character's answer compared to other models. Using this information, the OCR engine can identify characters it's highly likely to get wrong and request users to correct them.
