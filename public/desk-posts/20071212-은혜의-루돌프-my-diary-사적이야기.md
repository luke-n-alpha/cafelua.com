갑자기 블로그에 집사람의 이름을 적으려니 이상하다.. {{IMG:4}}

오늘 대선 토론에서 문후보님이 아내 이름을 불러봤냐는
말에 뜨끔해서 블로그에서 까지 굳이 "집사람"이라는 표현을 쓰는것이 옳지 않다고 생각했다.
이제는 다시 블로그에 그녀의 이름을 적으리라고 생각하고, 처음 적는다.

내가 그녀를 쫓아다닐때는 무척이나 불러보고 싶었던 이름이고, (생각해보면 무지하게 따라다녔다 정말)
듣거나 부르기만해도 가슴이 뛰던 이름인데도 불과 1년밖에 안되었는데도 생소하게 느껴진다.
이렇듯 버릇이 무섭나보다.

은혜도 이름 불러주는게 좋다고 했었는데....
병서기 오빠라고 부르면 어쩐지 여전히 무흣 하다

2007대선에 휩쓸려, 크리스마스란 것 조차 잊어버리는듯 하다.
이런 때 일수록 많은 사람들은 외롭게 지낸다는것을 잊어버리지 않아야 할텐데.
할아버지 할머니를 모시고 계신 교회인 우리 교회에서는 확실히 명절이 더 조심스러운것 같다. {{IMG:5}}

하지만, 예수님이 오신 것을 기념하는 기쁜 날이지 않은가 ?

무엇보다도 한해를 둘러보고 좌절도 해보고 {{IMG:6}}

겨울에 맞는 음악을 블로그에 걸려고 했더니 가지고 있는게 없다 {{IMG:7}}

Am I supposed to light it up in front of my door when I get home? {{IMG:3}}
I was already thinking I should buy a Christmas tree since Christmas is approaching, but Eunhye beat me to it.

It feels strange to suddenly write my wife's name on the blog... {{IMG:4}}

Today, during the presidential debate, when candidate Moon asked if I had ever called my wife by her name, it struck a chord with me. I realized it wasn't right to insist on using the term "my wife" (집사람) even on my blog. From now on, I've decided to write her name on the blog again, and this is the first time I'm doing it.

It was a name I desperately wanted to call when I was pursuing her (thinking back, I really did follow her around a lot), and a name that made my heart pound just by hearing or saying it, yet it feels unfamiliar after only a year. Habits are truly frightening, it seems.

Eunhye also said she liked being called by her name...
But when she calls me "Byeongseogi oppa," it still makes me smile.

Swept up in the 2007 presidential election, it seems I've even forgotten about Christmas.
At times like these, we shouldn't forget that many people feel lonely.
In our church, which serves many elderly members, holidays definitely seem to be approached with more caution. {{IMG:5}}

But isn't it a joyful day commemorating the arrival of Jesus?
And while we're at it, let's reflect on Jesus's will, look around us,
and most importantly, look back on the year, even experiencing some frustration. {{IMG:6}}
