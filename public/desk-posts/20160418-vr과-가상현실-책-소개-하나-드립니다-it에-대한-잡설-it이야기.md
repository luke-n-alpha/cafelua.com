If you look at the authors... my name is listed sixth. **As you might have guessed, this post is an advertisement. (Of course, I haven't received any separate compensation for this post.)**
I received the manuscript offer a few weeks ago. Given the lack of proper books on the subject, the proposal was to quickly (timing market?) release it as a light, mook-style publication. Six related experts contributed to the writing. I haven't met the other authors in person, so I can't vouch for the overall context of the book. I received it and read a bit, and I think it's a decent book. Haha, especially the part I wrote... (I worked quite hard on it, so I can't say it's a mess, can I?) {{IMG:2}}

The part I wrote delves deeper into the path the VR (virtual reality) market must overcome.
**Chapter 6. Virtual Reality Industry: What Must It Overcome? _ Yang Byeong-seok**
SECTION 01 | Limitations of the Nascent Stage
SECTION 02 | Problems from a **Technological** Perspective
SECTION 03 | Problems from a **Health** Perspective
SECTION 04 | Problems from a **Regulatory** Perspective
SECTION 05 | Problems that will Arise in the **Market**
**Although I usually disclose most of my content on my blog, I've published a book and signed a contract, so I cannot reveal all of its contents.**
To briefly explain, it covers what latency, which everyone vaguely knows about, technically is and what efforts are being made for its improvement... the problems the market must overcome to establish itself, and the health and regulatory issues that are already slowly being discussed. While the actual content might not be as deep as one might expect, I believe approaching it with such a framework will be important.
Besides my part, there are 5 preceding chapters, so I recommend giving them a light read.
What I remember most is Chapter 4, which contains content from Sconec Entertainment's VP Choi Jung-hwan's actual field experiences. Also, Chapter 5, Editor Lee Seok-won's material, was good for reference. Please refer to the table of contents below.
Chapter 1. Why is Virtual Reality Emerging? (Seo Ki-man, Senior Researcher, LG Economic Research Institute)
Chapter 2. How Far Has Virtual Reality Technology Come? (Kim Jung-hyun, Professor, Korea University)
Chapter 3. What is Spatial Augmented Reality Technology? (Lee Ju-haeng, Principal Researcher, ETRI)
Chapter 4. Emerging Virtual Reality Businesses (Choi Jung-hwan, VP, Sconec Entertainment)
Chapter 5. Virtual Reality Devices to Watch (Lee Seok-won, Editor-in-Chief, Techholic)
Chapter 6. Virtual Reality Industry: What Must It Overcome? (Yang Byeong-seok, Researcher, Software Policy Research Institute)
It's nothing grand, but I'm quite proud to have published something offline.
