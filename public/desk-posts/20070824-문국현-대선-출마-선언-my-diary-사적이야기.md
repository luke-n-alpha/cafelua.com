[Moon Kook-hyun](http://people.naver.com/search/people_detail.nhn?id=17662) Economist, Businessman
Born 1949.01.12 (Seoul) Affiliation Yuhan-Kimberly.
This is nothing short of shocking news.
Feeling disillusioned with politicians these days and thinking that I might as well vote for Myung-bak as the lesser of two evils,
then this welcome news...

That 'he' is running for office – the pinnacle of win-win and transparent management, a living legend of the Korean economy...
It seems that someone I can openly support is running.

However, I hope that as he enters politics, he doesn't lose his pure heart, as the saying goes, "Don't let the egret go where the crows play."
Even if he can't turn the crows into egrets, I hope he can at least turn them into magpies.
