LG is actually smaller than Samsung. You might wonder what it means for a large corporation like LG to be 'smaller,' but it simply means that LG and Samsung are not companies competing on a similar scale. Samsung is a company that leads by an overwhelmingly large margin.
Nevertheless, LG is releasing more products. As a result, it has become impossible to tell what LG's flagship phone is, other than the 'Optimus' name. **2. Loss of Competitiveness due to LG Smartphone Fragmentation**
Will the Optimus Big and Black maintain their position as LG's latest phones for three months? Will upgrades truly continue? The biggest concern for Android developers is said to be fragmentation. While having a variety of devices might be a pleasant dilemma for users, if the products aren't properly supported and the software isn't smooth, the advantage of diversity only amplifies the concern of becoming one of the few unsupported phones. And LG has faithfully built up this negative image over time and has not shown any signal to customers that it intends to improve it. Developers have reached a point where they have to worry about fragmentation even among LG devices. While the high-market-share Galaxy S receives priority support, what should Optimus prioritize in development? Although I'm reluctant to make hasty market predictions, LG, having released the Optimus Black and One, will likely have to envy the success of the Galaxy S2. It will be the same even if they release a phone better than the Galaxy S2. Customers are already afraid to buy LG phones.
**3. What matters is not the price, but buyer satisfaction**
62% of US smartphone users reportedly feel their phone is outdated before their 2-year contract ends.{{IMG:1}}**[ Source: **[**http://on.mash.to/lwhpjl**](http://on.mash.to/lwhpjl)** ]**
Galaxy S users would have thought their phone was cutting-edge for at least a year, given that the S2 was widely spreading. For iPhones, this period of satisfaction seems to be about a year. What about LG? Not even 6 months. Is it around 3 months? This means the period of feeling deprived is relatively longer compared to other smartphones, and ultimately becomes a factor in not repurchasing LG smartphones. This Galaxy S2 seems to be a complete victory for SKT and Samsung Electronics. People compare prices for value for money when purchasing, but ultimately, they buy things for the satisfaction they get after the purchase. Maximizing this satisfaction is what makes people not regret spending money. If it were me right now, I would strongly advise against buying an LG smartphone. It seems the Optimus Q's RevB was ultimately blocked.
