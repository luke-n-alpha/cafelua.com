**Cool Summer Sprint**

{{IMG:1}}

2002. 07. 17
Racing between lovers on the beach, a cool summer~! It's already hot, and you two keep sticking together, making it even hotter~!

**2015. 04. 30**
At the time, I had bought a used Tico and was driving it around. Having a car gave me an incredible sense of freedom, but if there was one thing I didn't have, it was probably a girlfriend. And I often expressed the sorrows that single people carry through drawings like this.

I think I was probably anxious and didn't know what to do. It seemed even worse for me, especially since I was a 'born-solo' until my mid-twenties. It's probably a phenomenon all single people experience.
