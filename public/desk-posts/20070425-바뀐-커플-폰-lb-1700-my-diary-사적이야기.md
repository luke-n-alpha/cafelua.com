LG Electronics LB-1700
Terrestrial DMB Phone
Although videos are only 7.5 frames,
after inserting a 1GB memory card, it's reasonably usable.
In particular, listening to DMB radio and converting animations to watch is quite enjoyable.

Plus, it directly links with OUTLOOK, making it convenient for work tasks too~
As a phone I bought for almost free, I'm very satisfied ^^;
