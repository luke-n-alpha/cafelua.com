** 1. 홈미디어 서버란 ?**
홈미디어 서버란 용어가 IT에 안계시는 분들은 생소할지도 모르겠습니다. 단순히 말하면 TV+전축+인터넷이 바로 홈미디어 서버입니다. 컴퓨터에 TV카드 달고, 좋은 스피커 달아놓고 TV도보고 음악도 보고, 인터넷도 하는 그런 기계인데 특히 그 활용도는 방송을 보고 음악을 듣는데 주로 이용되는 기기를 생각하시면 됩니다. 그런데 앞에 왜 홈이란 글자가 붙었느냐면은 그 위치가 개인의 방이 아니라 거실이란 의미입니다. 가족들과 앉아서 TV나 영화를 보고, 음악을 틀어놓고 식사를 하는 장면. 이제 홈미디어 서버가 무슨 의미인줄 아시겠죠? 이 홈미디어 서버는 현재 냉장고, 컴퓨터, IPTV같은 각종 IT기기들이 발전을 해가고 있는 가정형 단말의 최종 모습입니다. SF에서는 이 홈미디어서버에는 홈제어기능이 포함되어 창문도 열고, 보일러도 키는 등 미디어란 글자도 날려버리고 홈서버로 재탄생합니다. 거기에 인공지능이 포함되면, 집은 우리의 친구가 되는것이죠. (대통령이 좋아할것 같은 개념이군요.)

** 2. 홈미디어 서버로서의 IPTV**
그중에 이러한 미래의 모습을 아주 약간이나마 느껴볼수 있는 기술이 우리 곁에 이미 와 있습니다. 바로 IPTV입니다. 저는 myLgtv를 이용중이니 이를 기준으로 말씀 드리겠습니다. 제가 iptv를 이용하기 이전에는 pc와 tv에 연결해서 홈씨어터를 구성해 두었습니다. 5.1채널 브릿즈 스피커와 샐러론급 pc, 무선 키보드를 이용한 구성이었고 주로 p2p사이트의 동영상 플레이 용이었습니다. 그러나 iptv로 바꾸고 나서는 이 pc는 곧 에뮬단지가 되어버렸고 그 자리를 iptv가 차지하게 되었습니다. 제가 홈미디어서버로 IPTV를 활용하며 느낀점들을 말씀드리겠습니다.

** 1) 광단자를 지원하는 IPTV, 홈시어터와 잘 어울림**
mylgtv를 선택했던 가장 큰 이유가 바로 광단자를 지원하는 5.1채널 홈시어터 설치였습니다. 홈시어터를 가지고 있다면 최고의 선택입니다. 설치가 어렵다면 파워콤 프리미안 서비스를 부르면 와서 설치도 해주는것으로 알고 있습니다.
**  더 바라는것 :**** 3.5py 단자 지원과 현금대신 홈씨어터를 주면 어떨까?
** 사실 전 홈시어터 구현을 제대로 하지 못했습니다. 기존의 pc의 5.1채널 스피커들은 3.5파이 크기의 라인 입출력을 지원하는 경우가 많습니다. 제 5,1채널 브릿즈 스피커는 덕분에 애물단지가 되어버렸고, IPTV의 음성은 그냥 TV가 담당하고 있습니다.
또한 현금을 준다는 프로모션이 많은데 그 돈이면 최근엔 홈씨어터 구매도 가능합니다. 그리고, 파워콤에서 대량구매해준다면 단가는 더 낮아지겠죠. 좋은 홈씨어터를 껴준다면 그 파급력은 현금보다 크지 않을까요? 여전히 홈씨어터가 없는 집은 많으며, 사실 몰라서 설치를 잘 못합니다.

** 2) PC와 별도로 연결해주지 않아도 좋다.**
pc의 미디어 파일을 억세스가 가능하다는 점이었는데 윈도우즈의 공유 폴더 기능을 이용한 My Pc란 기능입니다. 이용 방법은 매우 간단합니다. 동영상이 담긴 폴더를 공유하고 선택만 해주면 찾아줍니다.

Dvd플레이어가 없는 집은 흔하지만 수년전 이후 구매한 pc에 dvd가 안달린 경우가 없습니다.

**1. What is a Home Media Server?**
For those not in IT, the term "home media server" might be unfamiliar. Simply put, it's TV + stereo + internet. It's a device where you install a TV card in your computer, connect good speakers, watch TV, listen to music, and browse the internet. Its primary use is for watching broadcasts and listening to music. The word "home" is attached because its location is not a personal room, but the living room. Imagine a scene where a family sits together watching TV or movies, or having a meal with music playing. Now you understand what a home media server means, right? This home media server is the ultimate form of a home terminal, evolving alongside various IT devices like refrigerators, computers, and IPTV. In science fiction, this home media server includes home control functions, opening windows and turning on boilers, shedding the "media" part and being reborn as a "home server." If artificial intelligence is included, the house becomes our friend. (This concept seems like something a president would like.)

**2. IPTV as a Home Media Server**
Among these future visions, a technology that allows us to experience a small part of it is already with us: IPTV. I use myLgtv, so I'll speak based on that. Before I used IPTV, I had a home theater setup connected to my PC and TV. It consisted of 5.1 channel Britz speakers, a Celeron-class PC, and a wireless keyboard, primarily used for playing videos from P2P sites. However, after switching to IPTV, that PC quickly became an emulator hub, and IPTV took its place. I will share my experiences using IPTV as a home media server.

**1) IPTV Supporting Optical Output, a Good Match for Home Theater**
The biggest reason I chose myLgtv was its support for optical output for a 5.1 channel home theater setup. If you have a home theater, it's the best choice. If installation is difficult, I understand that you can call Powercom's Premiun service, and they will come and install it for you.
**More desired: 3.5mm jack support and offering a home theater instead of cash?**
To be honest, I haven't been able to properly implement my home theater. Most existing PC 5.1 channel speakers often support 3.5mm line input/output. My 5.1 channel Britz speakers have become a white elephant because of this, and the IPTV audio is simply handled by the TV.
Also, there are many promotions that offer cash, but with that money, you can now buy a home theater. And if Powercom buys in bulk, the unit cost would be even lower. Wouldn't the impact of including a good home theater be greater than cash? Many homes still don't have a home theater, and in fact, many don't know how to install one.

**2) No need to connect separately to a PC.**
The ability to access media files on a PC was a key feature, using Windows' shared folder function called "My PC." The method is very simple. Just share the folder containing videos and select it, and it will find them.

**More desired: Dedicated S/W is needed, then couldn't PC's DVD-ROM also be used?**
This shared folder function relies on MS solutions, which are often exposed to vulnerabilities, and sometimes the PC cannot be found at all on certain networks. This is less a problem with myLgtv and more with MS's file sharing function sometimes not being configured properly. It would have been much better if they provided dedicated PC software to allow direct control via a different port. This would enable diverse utilization of PC resources. I don't have a DVD player, but my PC has a DVD-ROM. If dedicated software were used to stream DVDs from the PC to IPTV, I would have an excellent DVD player with just one piece of software. It's common for homes not to have a DVD player, but PCs purchased in recent years almost always have a DVD drive.

**4) External USB devices can be connected.**
A similar feature is support for external USB devices. Thus, media files on USB memory sticks or external hard drives can be accessed. If you plug a USB stick containing music, often used in navigation systems, into the IPTV, you can use your home theater for music listening.

**More desired: Please allow use of the hard drive built into IPTV.**
If IPTV's hard disk resources could be utilized, allowing direct copying from external memory to IPTV, its utility would increase further. With the same need for hard disk utilization, I wish IPTV had scheduled recording and time machine functions. Of course, supporting these functions would negatively impact IPTV's VOD business model, but many TVs are already supporting these features. However, from a user's perspective, if TVs have built-in hard drives or memory, it's a duplicate investment, and upgrading would be different in terms of cost and convenience compared to dismantling a wall-mounted TV.

**3. Further Desired Development Directions**
- Besides the categories above, I have some more requests, though I might seem a bit shameless. I believe user demands also affect IPTV's competitiveness, so these aren't idle thoughts.

**1) What about application speed?**
Moving from broadcast to the My PC service requires a considerable waiting time. It's nothing compared to boot time, but it's a bit annoying. A current drawback of IPTV is the loading time when switching applications. This is because it fundamentally uses the same software technology as computers, but its hardware specifications are lower than a typical PC. Please upgrade to a cheap and fast general-purpose CPU like Atom.

**2) Power-conscious IPTV**
{{IMG:7}}

When playing media, if I'm just listening to music, I wish the screen would turn off. If I'm listening to audio directly through the TV without a home theater, I don't want to see the TV screen consuming power and generating heat. Providing at least a black screen output function would help with TV lifespan and power consumption.

**3) Radio broadcasts, not just Live TV...**
{{IMG:8}}
[KBS Internet Radio]

Similarly, I wish for live radio. A home media environment doesn't always require video; needs vary. In our house, during busy mornings, we don't turn on the TV but listen to the radio in the kitchen. This is why radio broadcasting, despite being an old technology, is still loved. There used to be live music broadcasts on IPTV, but they've disappeared. If current radio broadcasts like Gorillia or Kong were offered, similar to how public broadcasts are streamed, it would be an example of utilizing IPTV's inherent interactive technology.

**4) Evolution to 2.0: Network Drive Support.**
{{IMG:9}}
[My photos uploaded to N-drive]

What if network drives like Naver's N-drive or Flickr were supported?

This would allow easy access without using other storage like external USBs. You could imagine viewing countless photos uploaded to Flickr via MMS on Me2day using IPTV, or streaming music and video files uploaded to N-drive. In a future seamless computing environment, such user network storage will gradually replace local storage, and it's natural that IPTV, always connected to the internet, should utilize this storage to set the standard for users' home media environments.
Of course, in the case of Naver's N-drive, it doesn't yet provide an open API, so development might not be easy, but from Naver's perspective, which offers photo albums, such a collaboration request would be attractive and perceived as a synergy-generating demand. Or, recognizing the strong need, Naver N-drive might also provide an open API like Flickr.

Despite my user demands as listed above, IPTV actually supports many functions and can continue to receive software upgrades, making it a truly valuable and still evolving service. I also believe it is the most realistic model among many future IT models to rapidly penetrate the public. Therefore, I hope that more user needs are heard and feedback is implemented. A service befitting the reputation of an infrastructure IT powerhouse. Shouldn't we have one too? And let's sell it overseas.
