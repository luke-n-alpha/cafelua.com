**  - (AI모델의 불법화 가능성은 낮으나 AI저작권 등록은 불가할 것) ** 원본 AI모델의 저작자가 최초 불법의 가능성이 있으나 확실하지는 않음. 논문 작성 및 모델의 무상공개를 했기 때문에 공정이용에 대한 판결 가능성이 있으며, 기존의 인터넷 크롤링된 자료의 분석이 위법이 된 사례는 없기 때문, 현재 검색엔진의 크롤링도 검색엔진의 훈련(데이터마이닝)에 이용되고 있었으며, AI의 이용이 법제화되기 이전에 훈련된 기존 모델들은 새로운 법으로 위법화 할 수 없음 또한 이 모델을 불법으로 규정하기 위해서는 고소를 한 집단 혹은 사람이 입증해야 할 책임이 있으나 기술적으로 불가능함. AI모델을 서비스한 회사에서 양심고백이 있기전에는 입증하기 어려움.  원본 AI모델이 불법이 되더라도 파인튜닝한 AI모델이 불법인지는 더 복잡함. 불법 포토샵을 이용한 창작물이 불법 저작물이 아닌 것처럼 별개의 저작물로 보는 것이 보통이기 때문.  따라서 AI창작이 불법으로 판결될 가능성은 거의 없음.  하지만, AI가 만든 창작물은 인간의 노력이 들어가지 않았으므로 저작권을 취득하지 않게 하는 것은 가능하며, 그렇게 진행 될 것임. ​** - (하지만 AI저작권 등록 불가 정책은 실효성이 낮음) **하지만 실제 이에 영향을 받는 것은 일러스트, 사진 정도 일것이라고 보임.   정보 양이 적은 음악, 짧은 글은 기술적으로 AI와 구분이 어렵고 정보 양이 많은 다른 미디어는 사람이 다시 그리거나 써서 제출 할 수 있기 때문, 저작권 등록에는 대중 매체의 경우 전체의 콘텐츠 데이터를 심사하는 것이 아니라 콘텐츠의 구성을 심사하는 것이기 때문에 최소한의 사람의 노력을 투입하여 기업이 보호 하고자 하는 콘텐츠의 구성의 저작권 등록은 가능하여 쉽게 회피 가능함​** - (대중미디어로 AI콘텐츠 소비와 예술로서 사람들의 창작의 분화가 발생할 것)**  더군다나 장기적으로 AI콘텐츠는 인간이 만든 콘텐츠랑 구분이 어려워 질 것임. 장기적으로는 모든 단점이 사라지고 잘 못만든 콘텐츠가 인간이 만든 것이다라는 인식이 심어질 수 있음.

**This post is about the present and future of the AI content market, intended to explain the 2024 strategy to Belivvr's AI Content Team.****​**

**1. The AI Content Market in 2023**

**    - In 2023, AI technology rapidly evolved beyond webtoons, with further development expected in video and 3D.****    - Webtoons faced strong backlash from readers and creators.****    - Rapid technological changes make it difficult to establish a creative process reliant on specific technologies.****​**

**2. Short-Term Forecast for 2024**

**    - (Full-scale penetration and growth in collective creation, corporate, and outsourced content markets) **AI will be actively adopted in games, video, and animation, where corporate creation is dominant outside of webtoons, and its integration with the metaverse, another axis of technological development in 2024, will accelerate. While music and webtoons, where individual brands are significant, will be the slowest, outsourced brand markets in music and webtoons, where individuals earned income, will transition to AI first. This is because the outsourced brand market is sensitive to cost-efficiency and often does not desire individual artists' brands.****​**
**    - (Initial growth centered on UGC and adult content) **The quantity of UGC will soon lead to an increase in quality. Consequently, AI content on Postype/Pixiv will begin to increase, and growth will first occur in the adult sector, where users may not openly admit to enjoying it. Major platforms with strong authority bear heavy responsibility and find it difficult to handle backlash and issues, whereas UGC platforms can bear risks by shifting responsibility to individuals and creators.****​**
**  - (Low probability of AI creating webtoons in one go without human intervention) **Although some talk about AI technology that can easily create webtoons, developing such technology is not feasible with low costs, making it unrealistic. When creating multiple panel images rather than single panel images, the context between panels is more important than prompts describing a single panel. If we input the context between panels into prompts, even for a simple storyboard, the resolution for SDXL is 1024x1024 = 1.05 million pixels of information. Assuming we need to generate a minimum recognizable resolution for a contextual storyboard of around 30 panels, we would need to generate an image with a resolution of approximately 512 x 25,000 = 12.8 million pixels. This means that 12 times more memory is immediately required just for image processing, not even considering training resources. It seems unlikely that any company would immediately invest such costs and training solely for webtoons, given the larger and more general image generation market. Typically, actual webtoon original work uses image resolutions of 1280~2000px horizontally. For this reason, from a utility perspective, it is much more likely to be used as an assistant for single panel images. In the technology market, investment in multimodal AGI is greater, so it might be faster for AGI to reach the point of creating webtoons.****​**
**    - (The ultimate value of AI content lies in good content that consumers like, not in AI technology) **The ultimate value of AI content is also content, so it resides with the consumer, not the technology. This is because the expected return on investment for AI technology is currently low compared to its input cost. However, due to the nature of technology, becoming a leading player could yield very high expected returns, but with global companies currently pouring vast amounts of capital into AI technology, competition and prediction are difficult. By 2024, AI technology companies will likely start facing pressure for performance (a characteristic of Korea is demanding results excessively quickly). Therefore, when it comes to AI content, the definite value lies in the competitiveness of the content itself, and only when this is proven will the utility of AI content technology also be proven.****​**

**3. Long-Term Forecast: The Future of AI Model Legality and Efforts to Identify It**

**  - (Low probability of AI models being deemed illegal, but AI copyright registration will be impossible) **There is a possibility, though not certain, that the original AI model's creator might initially be deemed illegal. However, because papers were written and models were released for free, there's a possibility of a fair use ruling, and there have been no cases where the analysis of existing internet-crawled data was deemed illegal. Currently, search engine crawling is also used for search engine training (data mining), and existing models trained before AI use was legislated cannot be made illegal by new laws. Furthermore, to deem these models illegal, the accusing group or person would bear the burden of proof, which is technically impossible. It would be difficult to prove unless the company that provided the AI model made a confession. Even if the original AI model is deemed illegal, whether a fine-tuned AI model is illegal is more complex. This is because creations made using illegal Photoshop are usually considered separate works, not illegal copyrighted works. Therefore, it is highly unlikely that AI creation will be ruled illegal. However, it is possible and likely that AI-generated works will not acquire copyright, as they do not involve human effort.****​**
** - (However, the policy of disallowing AI copyright registration has low effectiveness) **However, it is likely that only illustrations and photos will be truly affected by this. Music and short texts, which have less information, are technically difficult to distinguish from AI-generated content, and other media with large amounts of information can be redrawn or rewritten by humans and submitted. For copyright registration, in the case of mass media, it is not the entire content data that is reviewed, but rather the composition of the content. Therefore, by investing a minimal amount of human effort, companies can register copyrights for the composition of the content they wish to protect, making it easily circumventable.****​**
** - (Differentiation between AI content consumption in mass media and human creation as art will occur) **Furthermore, in the long term, AI content will become difficult to distinguish from human-made content. In the long run, all shortcomings will disappear, and there might be a perception that poorly made content is human-made.
