I want to see a rainbow over the street where the rain has stopped.
On the street of my chaotic blue heart,
The rainbow of my dreams that will rise again above it...
Stepping forward in this cold and cruel reality,
Let's raise my own rainbow,
One that no one can mock.
2003. 11. 19
