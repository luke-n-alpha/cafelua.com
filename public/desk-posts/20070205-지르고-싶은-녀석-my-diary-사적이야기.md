DSLR로 훌쩍 건너가고 싶은 마음이 굴뚝이었지만...
두가지가 마음에 걸렸다.

과연 내가 나중에 몇십만원씩 추가지출을 하면서 카메라 가격에
준하는 렌즈의 지름신에 동참할것인가...

그리고, 결정적으로 집사람이 "셀프 가능 카메라" 란 말에.. 결국 DSLR은 대상에서 내려가고 하이엔드 급으로 낙찰..

그래서 찾아본것이 파나소닉 FZ50..

여러가지로 봐서 내가 카메라 가지고 놀기나,
어두운데서 잘 찍히고  (확률이 높아지는것 뿐이지만) 셀카가 가능한
집사람이 원하는 스펙(그러나 LCD가 뒤집히지 않음 ㅡㅡ;)이나.. 가장 맞는듯하당.

문제는 그만한 돈이없다는거..
정품 으로 메모리랑 추가 배터리 갖추면. 57만원~ OTL.

그래봐야 DSLR의 범용 렌즈 2개, 고급렌즈 1개 가격이지만..

My heart was set on jumping straight to a DSLR, but...
Two things weighed on my mind.
 
Would I really, later on, spend hundreds of thousands of won extra, adding to the camera's price,
and succumb to the urge to buy lenses comparable in price?
 
And crucially, when my wife mentioned a "selfie-capable camera," the DSLR was ultimately taken off the list, and a high-end compact was chosen.
 
So what I looked into was the Panasonic FZ50...
 
Considering various aspects, whether for me to play around with the camera,
taking good photos in the dark (though it only increases the probability), or a selfie-capable camera,
or the specs my wife wants (though the LCD doesn't flip ㅡㅡ;)... it seems to be the best fit.
 
The problem is I don't have that much money.
If I get the genuine product with a memory card and an extra battery, it's 570,000 won~ OTL.
 
Even so, that's just the price of two general-purpose DSLR lenses, or one high-end lens...
