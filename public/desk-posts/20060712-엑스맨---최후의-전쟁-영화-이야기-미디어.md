#### [엑스맨 - 최후의 전쟁](http://movie.naver.com/movie/bi/mi/review.nhn?code=43995)

감독 브렛 라트너
제작 2006 미국, 0분
**평점**

마블시리즈를 원래 좋아하는 나로서는..
만족..

다만, 사이클롭스.. 너무 허무하게 죽더라..
자비에 교수는 그나마 멋지게 대항하다 죽었지만.
다시 살아날줄 알았더만..
마지막은 결국 불사신 울버린의 승리. ㅡㅡ

마그네토에게도 꼼짝못했는데
모든 물질을 조정하는 진 그레이는 왜 울버린을 못이기는거지..

아무튼 볼거리가 많아서 즐거웠던 영화.

명대사는 사이클롭스가 울버린에게 말했던
" 누구나 너처럼 빨리 상처가 아물지 않아 "

사실은 울버린이야 말로
상처에 무감각해져버린 그일뿐 상처덩어리 캐릭터이건만..

#### [X-Men - The Last Stand](http://movie.naver.com/movie/bi/mi/review.nhn?code=43995)

Director Brett Ratner
Production 2006 USA, 0 min
**Rating**

As someone who originally likes the Marvel series...
Satisfied...

However, Cyclops... died too meaninglessly...
Professor Xavier, at least, died fighting valiantly.
I thought he would come back to life...
In the end, it was the immortal Wolverine's victory. ㅡㅡ

She couldn't even move against Magneto,
so why couldn't Jean Grey, who controls all matter, defeat Wolverine...?

Anyway, it was an enjoyable movie with lots to see.

The memorable line was what Cyclops said to Wolverine:
"Not everyone heals as quickly as you do."

In fact, Wolverine himself
is just someone who has become numb to his wounds,
but he's a character full of scars...
