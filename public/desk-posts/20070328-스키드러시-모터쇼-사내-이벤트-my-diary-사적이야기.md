Hangame often holds internal events like this.
Like reaching a certain level by a certain date...
And the rare items you occasionally get.

Skid Rush... I tried it for a bit and it was pretty good.
The problem is, for me, when it comes to racing games,
I prefer games that emphasize realism.

That's why I'm not a big fan of KartRider...
Instead, I only enjoyed games like Team Revolution, which most people didn't play much.

Anyway, unlike other companies, these kinds of events
seem to be the 'licorice' (a delightful addition) of company life.

So, by writing this post, is the mission accomplished? ^^;

The fact that Erina and Kaila are coming to the motor show
does that mean cosplay?
Actually, enjoying the models is an indispensable part of a motor show.

As someone who likes cute characters,
the curvy/voluptuous girls are a bit 'meh'... haha
