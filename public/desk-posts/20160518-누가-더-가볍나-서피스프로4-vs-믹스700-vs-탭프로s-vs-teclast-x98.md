I prioritize portability most when purchasing laptops and tablets. I can tolerate slow performance to some extent, but since I travel a lot and use devices outdoors for extended periods, battery life and weight are crucial factors. However, when comparing these products, I noticed that they often don't clearly state the weight including the adapter or keyboard. This made it difficult to know the true weight when I put the keyboard and adapter in my bag for work. So, I decided to research it myself and share the findings. This is not based on actual measurements, but a combination of internet search results.

1. All-in on Portability: Teclast X98 Pro+ (800g)

First, the devices that score best in portability are naturally Atom-based tablets like Cherry Trail. These tablets are under 10 inches, making them excellent for portability. They are also inexpensive, but a bit lacking for everyday work. However, the price is truly attractive. The fact that the keyboard and stand must be purchased separately is both an advantage and a disadvantage. Thanks to this, it's possible to maximize portability under 800g.
