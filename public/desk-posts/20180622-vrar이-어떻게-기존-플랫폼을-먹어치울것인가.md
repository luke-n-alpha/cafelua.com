How VR/AR Will Devour Existing Platforms

Luke ・ 2018. 6. 22. 10:38

Copy URL Add Neighbor

Main Text Other Functions

From the perspective of someone who has closely contemplated the era of mobile platform transformation, the recent emergence of VR/AR devices is very impressive. I feel that they have finally become truly usable.

1.  Mobile-based VR, where comfort is key, starts with 'lean back'

Based on this, if I were to write a novel, mobile VR, represented by Oculus Go, would likely first impact the iPad.

VR Device Oculus Go Virtual/Augmented Reality Portable Headset 2018 New Product 32GB/64GB

Although for indoor use, it's primarily for sofas with armrests rather than desks. If there's anything else, it's a swivel sofa.

View Details

And it seems likely to push out large-screen TVs, which are already being encroached upon by mobile. Considering Netflix's current popularity, this device is surprisingly 'lean back'. This is an area Samsung Electronics and LG Electronics need to consider, as video and comic platforms like ours (ComixV) could rise first.

ComixV - VR Comic / VR Webtoon

2.  Erosion of Mobile Game Engagement Time, and an Even Greater Impact on Revenue Erosion Relative to Engagement Time

Next, it seems likely to affect the mobile market, where usage time is primarily concentrated at night. While mobile is used during commutes and lunch breaks, the peak time is precisely this period, and this is when 'whales' (primarily those who spend money) are active. Gaming is the royal road of all content.

3. The Erosion of Productivity Tools Starts First and Ends Last.

PCs, being productivity tools, might be least affected, but they will cede ground to VR/AR, starting from industrial sectors, spearheaded by AR technology. Because it's B2B, it will start first, but due to its broad scope, the impact will likely last the longest.

Magic Leap is coming in June! Really... But as I always say, don't expect much from the first generation of consumer devices.

And this will accelerate further as the events in points 1 and 2 occur. This is similar to the principle by which mobile popularization once again gained attention as a productivity tool. Around this time, MS will be desperate to acquire Leap Motion, and by then, the large display market might have ended.
