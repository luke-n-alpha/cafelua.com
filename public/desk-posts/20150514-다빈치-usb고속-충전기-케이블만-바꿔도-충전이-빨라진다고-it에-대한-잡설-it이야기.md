Now that I've received it, I'll share the review results. Since the contents are simple and it's just a cable, let's not worry about design or anything like that. It's neither bad nor exceptionally good. It feels slightly thicker around the connector than a standard bundled USB cable.
The important thing is charging speed. To check the charging speed, I first installed an app called Ampare on the Galaxy Zoom 2. The baseline consumption was measured at -450mA. This means it consumes about 450mA of power per hour. With a 2450mA battery, it would be fully depleted in 5.4 hours. This time is based on having the screen on for measurement. This time varies greatly depending on whether the screen is off, browsing, or gaming.
