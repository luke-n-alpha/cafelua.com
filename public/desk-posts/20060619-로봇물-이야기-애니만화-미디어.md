이번엔 로봇물..
로봇이란 SF에서 많이 등장하였는데여..
정확히 이야기하면 더 거슬러 올라갑니다.
무생물에 생명을 불어넣는 환타지의 골렘이나
원래는 석상이었으나 제작자의 아내가된 그리스신화의 누군지
잊어버렸음(--;)
혹은 피노키오등 로봇의 기원은 훨씬 올라가게 됩니다.

만화에서 나오는 로봇은 하나같이 인간형 로봇입니다.
어째서 인간이 인간형 로봇에 대해 집착하느냐..
그것은 일종의 하나님이 인간을 창조한 목적과 비슷합니다.
지구상에 유일한 영장류는 인간밖에 없기에
같이 커뮤니티할 또 다른 객체를 바라는거겠죠.

인간 대신 일을 시키는 노동의 필요성도 있지만,
일단은 인간은 일하기에 적합하게 생기지 않았습니다.
따라서 인간형 로봇은 단가가 올라갈 뿐이죠.
전쟁에도 인간형 로봇은 비싸기만 할뿐 별로 쓸모 없습니다.

그러나, 사람들은 굳이 인간형 로봇을 만들려고 할까요.
그것은 심리적인 요인입니다.
아기를 돌보는 로봇이나 인간의 바로옆에서 시중을
드는 로봇이라면 인간형에 더 친근감을 느끼겠죠.

자.. 그럼 에니 이야기로 돌아오겠습니다.
그럼 최초의 로봇 에니는 무엇인가요 ?
아시는 분은 아시겠지만, 그것은 데스까 오사무씨의
철완아톰입니다.
우리나라에는 우주소년 아톰으로 소개되었습니다.

일반적으로 아이작 아시모프의 로봇의 3원칙을 아십니까 ?
1. 로봇은 인간을 해칠수 없다.
2. 로봇은 인간의 명령에 절대 복종한다.
3. 로봇은 자기자신을 보호한다.
우선권은 위에 나열한 순서대로이구요..
로봇이 지능을 가졌을때를 대비한 원칙같은 거죠.
왜 여기서 이얘기를 했느냐 하면요..

왜냐구요 ?
그것은 대부분 .. 무기로 등장하기 때문인 이유도 있지만,
그보다는 보다 인간같기 때문입니다.
인간은 다른 인간을 죽일수 있죠.

감정을 지니고 있습니다.
- 약간 벗어난 용자씨리즈도 지능형로봇에 들어가는데..
이것의 경우에는 조금 다릅니다. --;

그에 반해 2. 조종형 로봇은 대부분

파괴를 하는 거대한 무기일뿐입니다.
대부분 진행 방향도 로봇중심이 아니라..

보너스로 슈퍼 로봇과 리얼로봇을 크게 구분하는 방법을 알려드리겠습니다.

1. 무기 발사시 소리
슈퍼로봇은 무기를 쓰거나 동작할때 주인공이나 오퍼레이터가 소리를 지르며 알려줍니다. --;
그러나 리얼로봇은 스위치만 동작시킵니다.
2. 조종석
슈퍼로봇의 조종석은 대체적으로 무언가 알수없는 듯한 장치들과 계기판이 잔뜩 붙어있고 조종석이 넓습니다.
리얼로봇의 조종석은 좁아터졌으며 시계도 제안되어있고, 주로 비행기의 콕핏형태이고, 전투기처럼 Hud를 가지고 있습니다.
3. 타격시
리얼로봇은 찌그러지거나 쉽게 부서집니다. 슈퍼로봇은 광택만 약간 상하거나, 아예 잘려나가거나 마구 금이갑니다.
4. 머리
리얼로봇 계열은 인간처럼 눈코입이 있다기보다는 머리의 흔적만있습니다. 주로 카메라가 장치되어있습니다.
슈퍼로봇 계열은 완전한 인간형이 주로 머리에 조종석이있습니다.
5. 무기
리얼로봇의 무기는 미사일과 총탄입니다. 장탄에도 제한이있습니다.
슈퍼로봇은 주로 빔계열의 무기가 많으며 무진장 나가거나 제한도 탄제한이 아닌 에너지 제한입니다.
6. 근성
리얼로봇은 망가지면 끝이지만..
슈퍼로봇은 망가질수록 강해지며.. 소리지르면 더 강해집니다.
7. 합체/변신
리얼로봇은 합체라기보다는 장비를 교체하는 수준.
슈퍼로봇은 꼭 합체를 해야 하며 변신은 슈퍼로봇만의 로망입니다.
8. 에너지원
리얼로봇 - 전기, 수소, 가스 등 주로 연구되고 있는 에너지나 실용에너지 입니다.
슈퍼로봇 - 원자력, 핵융합, 광자력등 듣기만해도 무시무시한 에너지원을 에너지로 사용합니다.
9. 파일럿
리얼로봇 - 주로 군인, 경찰 등 공무원

Somehow, I got dragged to a PC bang again, and since I have some free time today...
I'm going to talk about anime again. ^^
This time, it's about robots.

Robots have appeared frequently in SF, but to be precise, their origins go back even further.
From the Golem in fantasy, which breathes life into inanimate objects,
or the figure in Greek mythology who was originally a statue but became the creator's wife (I forgot who it was (--)),
or Pinocchio, the origins of robots go back much further.

The robots that appear in comics are all humanoid robots.
Why do humans obsess over humanoid robots?
It's somewhat similar to God's purpose in creating humans.
Since humans are the only primates on Earth, they probably desire another entity to communicate with.

While there's a need for labor, having robots do work instead of humans,
humans are not inherently designed for work.
Therefore, humanoid robots only increase costs.
In war, humanoid robots are just expensive and not very useful.

However, why do people insist on creating humanoid robots?
It's a psychological factor.
If it's a robot that takes care of babies or serves humans right beside them, people would feel more familiarity with a humanoid form.

Now, let's get back to anime.
So, what was the first robot anime?
As those who know would be aware, it's Osamu Tezuka's *Tetsuwan Atom* (Astro Boy).
In Korea, it was introduced as *Space Boy Atom*.

Generally, are you familiar with Isaac Asimov's Three Laws of Robotics?
1.  A robot may not injure a human being.
2.  A robot must obey orders given it by human beings.
3.  A robot must protect its own existence.
The priority is in the order listed above...
They are like principles prepared for when robots gain intelligence.
The reason I brought this up here is...

First, in anime, these principles are mostly ignored.
Why?
Partly because they mostly appear as weapons,
but more so because they are more human-like.
Humans can kill other humans.

Well, first off, robot anime can be divided into intelligent robots that can think, and robots that are piloted by humans.

Now, let's first look at 1. Intelligent Robots.
Generally, there are many philosophical anime about intelligent robots that ask fundamental questions about whether humans and robots can truly coexist. There are even anime that show class conflicts between robots and humans. They are very futuristic, and the anime often progresses from the robot's perspective. Most of these robots are warm and possess human-like emotions.
- (The Brave Series, which is a bit of an outlier, also falls under intelligent robots... but in its case, it's a bit different. --;)

In contrast, 2. Piloted Robots are mostly weapons.
They hold value as weapons that kill people and are merely giant instruments of destruction.
Most of the time, the narrative isn't robot-centric... it's about human stories or how splendidly the forces of evil can be crushed using cool weapons.

Asurada from Cyber Formula and Evangelion, which fall into both categories, can be said to be a bit different because they move by communicating with their pilots.

These robots are products of advanced science.
This reflects humanity's perspective on science.
Japanese children and American children view robots differently.
In cartoons American children grew up watching, robots attack humans,
whereas in cartoons Japanese children grew up watching, robots and humans coexist.
Therefore, Japanese children are more tolerant of robots and science, and develop serious thoughts about robots and other life forms much earlier.

Next, 2. Piloted Robots can be further divided.
Into 1) Real Robot and 2) Super Robot series.
Super Robot series primarily feature giant robot heroes defeating evil forces.
Real Robot series mainly deal with sharp political situations among humans, competition or transactions between companies, or prevalent issues in human society.
Real Robot series are primarily targeted at more mature adults,
and while most Super Robots focus on a boy's growth, Real Robots pay more attention to social issues.

In the case of Gundam and Evangelion, they have elements of both Real Robot and Super Robot,
so it's hard to say exactly, but since it's a subjective boundary, it's difficult to define precisely.

As a bonus, I'll tell you how to broadly distinguish between Super Robots and Real Robots.

1.  **Sound when firing weapons**
    *   Super Robots, when using weapons or performing actions, have the protagonist or operator shouting to announce it. --;
    *   However, Real Robots only activate a switch.
2.  **Cockpit**
    *   Super Robot cockpits generally have many mysterious-looking devices and gauges, and are spacious.
    *   Real Robot cockpits are cramped, have limited visibility, are usually shaped like an airplane cockpit, and have a HUD like a fighter jet.
3.  **When hit**
    *   Real Robots get dented or easily broken.
    *   Super Robots only get slightly damaged in their finish, or are completely cut off, or crack wildly.
4.  **Head**
    *   Real Robot series have only traces of a head rather than human-like eyes, nose, and mouth. They usually have cameras installed.
    *   Super Robot series are fully humanoid, and the cockpit is usually in the head.
5.  **Weapons**
    *   Real Robot weapons are missiles and bullets. Ammunition is also limited.
    *   Super Robots mostly have beam-type weapons, which fire endlessly or have energy limits rather than ammunition limits.
6.  **Guts/Tenacity**
    *   Real Robots are finished if they break...
    *   Super Robots get stronger the more they are damaged... and even stronger if they shout.
7.  **Combination/Transformation**
    *   Real Robots are more about equipment replacement than combination.
    *   Super Robots must combine, and transformation is a Super Robot's unique romance/dream.
8.  **Energy Source**
    *   Real Robots - Electricity, hydrogen, gas, etc., mainly energies under research or practical energy sources.
    *   Super Robots - Atomic power, nuclear fusion, photon energy, etc., using energy sources that sound terrifying just to hear.
9.  **Pilot**
    *   Real Robots - Mainly soldiers, police, and other public servants.
    *   Super Robots - Young boys.
10. **Teamwork**
    *   Real Robots - Teamwork.
    *   Super Robots - Solo action.
11. **Ending**
    *   Real Robots - End with a scene of something broken.
    *   Super Robots - End with the robot holding a sword, with something massively exploding in the background.
12. **Creator**
    *   Real Robots - Mainly corporations or eccentric engineers.
    *   Super Robots - Doctors (whose major cannot be predicted).
