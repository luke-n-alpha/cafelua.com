카카오톡의 검찰 수사협조 거부, 그리고 비밀 채팅방 개설 그리고 이은 검찰이 이석우 대표를 불러세운 청소년 성보호법 위반 혐의. 일련의 행위들은 보복수사의 느낌을 짙게 들게 합니다. 김진태 검찰 총장의 아래와 같은 발언이 있은지 두달여만입니다.
**<김진태 / 검찰총장> "쉽게 말씀드리면 문을 안 열어주면 수사기관에서 결국 가서 열쇠공을 불러다가 문을 따는 것처럼 그렇게 할 수 밖에 없죠."**
일반적으로 국민의 안전을 위해 허가된 폭력인 공권력은 오프라인에서는 어느정도 허가가 되어왔던 것은 분명히 사실입니다. 그러나, 물리적으로 보이지 않는 소프트웨어에서의 과도한 정보개입은 오프라인보다 훨씬 위험하고, 조심스러운 일이란 것을 알아야 합니다. 카카오톡의 감청은 범죄자로 추정되는 이들의 문을 따고 들어가는 행위와는 완전히 다릅니다. 카카오톡의 감청논란의 위험은 감청자가 감청여부에 대해 알 수가 없으며 이로인해 유출된 정보가 어떻게 이용되는지 모른다는데 있습니다. 바로 해킹의 위협과 동일한것이죠. 소프트웨어서 해킹의 위협이 대단히 위험한 이유는 그 범위가 매우 광범위하며 뚫렸을때 흔적을찾기도 힘들고, 유출된 정보가 어떻게 사용될지 모른다는 것에 그 위험성이 있습니다. **그런데, 검찰이 카카오톡의 서버에서 슈퍼바이저 권한을 달라는 것과 마찬가지이며, 사용자들의 프라이버시 침해를 방지하기 위해 만들어놓은 장치마저 풀라고 하는것입니다. **
공인인증서에 대한 만능키를 누군가가 가지고 있다고 상상해보십쇼. 공인인증서를 신뢰할 수있을까요? 검찰의 행동을 보니 정말 카카오톡을 탈퇴해야겠나 봅니다.   **검찰은 우체사업본부장을 소환하십쇼. 우체국을 통해 수많은 음란 CD와 비디오가 유통되고 있습니다. 우체국은 모든 우편물을 관리 감독할 필요가 있습니다. 검찰은 도로교통공단장도 소환하십쇼. 음란CD가 도로를 통해 유통되고 있는 혐의가 있습니다.**
다음카카오의 상황이 현재 그리 녹록한상황은 아닙니다. 벤처기업이 끝장나는 순간은 대표가 법원으로 불려다닐때라고 합니다. 특허의 시비가 걸리거나 법적시비가 걸리면 기민하고 열심히 뛰어야될 시점에 법적 사태만 대응하다 시장을 놓치고 만다는 의미죠.** 대한민국 검찰은 청소년을 보호한다면서 향후 그 청년들을 고용할 대한민국의 유망 소프트웨어 기업 하나를 망하게 할 작정인가 봅니다.  **대한민국의 현재 상황을 이야기하자면, 젊은이들이 나이든 꼰대를 엎고뛰고 있는데, 그 꼰대가 젊은이의 목을 조르고 있습니다. 그 젊은이가 넘어지면 당신들도 미래도 끝입니다.

**정부나 검찰을 시민들이 욕하는게 두렵습니까? 저는 한국의 미래가 넘어지는 것이 훨씬 두렵습니다. **그리고, 그것을 인지하지 못하고 있는것자체가 더 암울하고 화가납니다. 다음 카카오가 사회적 역할을 많이 한 착한기업이라서가 아닙니다. 그저 다음 카카오를 비롯한 모든 소프트웨어기업에 대한 선전포고라고 여겨지기 때문입니다.

KakaoTalk's refusal to cooperate with the prosecution's investigation, the subsequent creation of secret chat rooms, and the prosecution's summoning of CEO Lee Seok-woo on charges of violating the Youth Protection Act. This series of actions strongly suggests retaliatory investigation. This comes just two months after Prosecutor General Kim Jin-tae made the following statement:
**<Kim Jin-tae / Prosecutor General> "Simply put, if they don't open the door, the investigative agency will eventually have no choice but to call a locksmith and break it open."**
It is certainly true that public authority, which is authorized violence for the safety of the public, has been somewhat permitted offline. However, it must be understood that excessive information intervention in physically invisible software is much more dangerous and requires more caution than offline. Intercepting KakaoTalk is completely different from breaking into the homes of suspected criminals. The danger of the KakaoTalk wiretapping controversy lies in the fact that the person being intercepted cannot know whether they are being intercepted, and therefore does not know how the leaked information will be used. This is the same as the threat of hacking. The reason why the threat of hacking in software is extremely dangerous is that its scope is very broad, it is difficult to find traces once breached, and there is a risk in not knowing how the leaked information will be used. **However, the prosecution is essentially asking for supervisor privileges on KakaoTalk's servers and demanding that even the mechanisms designed to protect users' privacy be disabled.**
Imagine someone having a master key to all public authentication certificates. Would you be able to trust public authentication certificates? Seeing the prosecution's actions, I really think I should leave KakaoTalk. **The prosecution should summon the head of the Post Office Headquarters. Numerous obscene CDs and videos are being distributed through the post office. The post office needs to manage and supervise all mail. The prosecution should also summon the head of the Road Traffic Authority. There are suspicions that obscene CDs are being distributed via roads.**
Daum Kakao's current situation is not easy. It is said that the moment a venture company collapses is when its CEO is called to court. This means that if there are patent disputes or legal disputes, at a time when they should be agile and working hard, they end up only dealing with legal issues and lose out on the market. **The Korean prosecution, while claiming to protect youth, seems intent on destroying a promising Korean software company that would employ those very youth in the future.** To describe the current situation in Korea, young people are carrying old conservatives on their backs, but those conservatives are strangling the young people. If those young people fall, your future is also over.

**Are you afraid of citizens criticizing the government or the prosecution? I am much more afraid of Korea's future collapsing.** And the fact that this is not being recognized makes me even more gloomy and angry. It's not because Daum Kakao is a good company that has played many social roles. It's simply because this feels like a declaration of war against all software companies, including Daum Kakao.
Please disclose the real-time location and call traffic records of all mobile phones and smartphones used by the prosecution on the internet. Then the public will also be willing to open up KakaoTalk. If you intend to strip the public, then you should also strip yourselves to that extent. It seems we might have to start a "No Cut" movement like webtoons. Do you know why internet companies don't trust the government and don't cooperate when the government asks them to do something? It's due to a lack of understanding of software, and because they are already incredibly busy, stressed, and struggling to compete with global giants, yet the government keeps calling them with absurd demands and bothering them. The backbone of a software-centric society is software companies. And the concerns of software companies are directly the concerns of national competitiveness. I sincerely hope that the voices from the field are not ignored. **The President advocates for a software-centric society, but the prosecution is leading the charge to kill software companies.** In such a situation, can the public and businesses trust the government? If you ask software companies or related professionals what the government should do, the number one answer is **"Just leave us alone."** I have seen many people in the government making strenuous efforts to foster software. However, the reason even their strenuous efforts lose their luster and market trust is likely due to a lack of understanding of software, leading to actions that demand everything from software companies.
