Prologue
A love story. How long has it been since I wrote one. Phew~ Thinking back, my first short story was
[A Small Story of Children] in '94. A love story about a middle-school boy and girl. After that I scribbled a great many
things. The long ones — [The Legend of Dragonia], [Windy the Little Witch] — and a number
of short pieces. Most of my stories contain a love story somewhere, but the only one that was
purely a love story seems to be [The Glass Bead] in '95. The protagonists of the love stories in my fiction have mostly
died. A friend pointed this out to me, and in fact I know
it myself. Almost all my protagonists meet a tragic end.. (there were exceptions of course, but tragedy
is overwhelmingly the rule) [A Small Story of Children], the first, had a happy ending, and yet most of them..
 That sad love is beautiful, or an aesthetics of death? ... --; Well.. probably not.
 Or rather, it may be the envy of someone who has never once been truly in love..
 So I mean to break that a little. Not light the way [Windy] was, and not sad...
 I mean to write another Fstory. Whether it is a Fine Story, or Funny..
 It may turn out Fatal..
 - Kang Ilseok, 24, male -
 Love? Well, hmph, I don't know. That sad love is beautiful? That is a luxury for those who have not
been through it. Sad love is only suffering; it leaves nothing behind but regret. Love seems to be fate.
It comes when you do not want it and leaves however it likes, no matter how much you want it to stay...
 - Yu Hyemi, 20, female -
 Love? Isn't it being honest with yourself? Of course you get hurt too. One broken heart in middle school
and two in high school, three in all. Two of the three ended before they had even begun, absurdly,
with " I don't like you! ", before I could even hurt properly —
but out of that experience I had two loves. Both of those have left now,
of course.. The first I went looking for, and the other.. strangely, we simply drifted apart and then
broke up. Saying it like this makes me sound like a female Casanova.
 Three proper relationships in all, and all of them fell apart, but
one win, one draw, one loss.. I've done the dumping and the being dumped.
 Love is something you make. Doesn't a record as glorious as this prove that I worked
that hard at it? My search will go on until the person who truly suits me
appears. Real love has not come to me yet.
When will my true first love be? Someone once said to me,
 ' A woman's present love is always her first love. '
